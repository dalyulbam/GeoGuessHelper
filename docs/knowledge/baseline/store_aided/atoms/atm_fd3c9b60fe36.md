# Canary Islands package-tourism monoculture economy

`atm_fd3c9b60fe36` · **economy** / region · cell `esvzdqh`

Much of the Canary Islands' coastal development consists of post-1980s all-inclusive resort complexes catering to Northern European (German, British, Scandinavian) package tourism, built along a single coastal highway corridor over unfarmable volcanic desert terrain. Local Spanish/Canarian culture persists inland while the coast functions as an imported-wealth, mono-functional resort economy with rental-car fleets and supermarkets serving both tourists and resort staff.
