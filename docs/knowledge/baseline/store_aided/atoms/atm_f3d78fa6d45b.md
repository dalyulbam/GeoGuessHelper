# Iceland's regional health service districts (Heilbrigðisstofnun) as administrative boundary markers

`atm_f3d78fa6d45b` · **economy** / region · cell `ge9h53s`

Iceland's healthcare system is organized into regional institutions such as Heilbrigðisstofnun Vesturlands (West Iceland) and Heilbrigðisstofnun Norðurlands (North Iceland), whose branding appears on clinic buildings. These administrative health-district boundaries do not always match intuitive geographic groupings — a town on a North Iceland fjord may fall under the West Iceland health authority — making clinic signage a useful but sometimes counterintuitive clue for pinpointing municipal affiliation.
