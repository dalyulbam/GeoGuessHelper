# Bolivian telecom brand cues: ENTEL, Tigo

`atm_4ca3496cbb50` · **economy** / country · cell `6s9edy7`

In Bolivia, ENTEL (state telecom) uses a blue-oval logo commonly painted on wall advertisements, and Tigo (including Tigo Money mobile-money kiosks) is a major private carrier. These brands are Bolivia-specific and distinguish it from Peru (Entel Peru uses orange-magenta branding), Ecuador (CNT/Claro) and Argentina (Personal/Movistar).
