# UAE landline area codes map to emirates

`atm_3442ee1ad8cb` · **language** / country · cell `thrxzsf`

In the United Arab Emirates, fixed-line telephone numbers carry an emirate-specific area code printed on shopfronts and signage: '02' Abu Dhabi/Al Ain hinterland areas actually use '03' for Al Ain, '04' Dubai, '06' Sharjah (including its interior towns), '07' Ras Al Khaimah, and Umm Al Quwain/Ajman/Fujairah have their own prefixes. This lets a signboard's landline prefix alone identify which emirate a location is in, even when the town itself is inland and small.
