# Kariega (Uitenhage) as South Africa's automotive manufacturing satellite

Kariega, formerly Uitenhage, in the Nelson Mandela Bay Metro (Eastern Cape, South Africa) is a historic rail-and-motor industrial town hosting Volkswagen South Africa's assembly plant. Its urban stock includes 1930s-era red-brick, corrugated-iron industrial buildings distinct from the coastal CBD morphology of nearby Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth). The town's economy centers on blue-collar light industry: building-material suppliers, industrial gas distributors, tyre fitment, and aluminium fabrication.
