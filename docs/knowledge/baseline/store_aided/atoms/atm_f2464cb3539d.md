# Iceland's treeless landscape and planted spruce shelterbelts

**시기**: 800 ~ ?

Iceland was largely deforested of its native birch woodland after Norse settlement (9th century onward), leaving a treeless grass/heath tundra landscape across most of the island. Modern conifer stands (spruce, pine) seen today are almost always deliberately planted shelterbelts or afforestation projects, easily distinguished from native woodland by their regular rows and isolated plots.
