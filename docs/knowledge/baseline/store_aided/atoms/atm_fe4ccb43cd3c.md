# Visual cues distinguishing rural Japan from Korea/China/Taiwan

`atm_fe4ccb43cd3c` · **culture** / polity · cell `xn22nqj`

Rural Japanese houses use amado (sliding wooden storm shutters), hollow-core decorative concrete-block plinth walls, kawara clay ridge tiles with ceramic end caps, red plastic fire-bucket boxes on poles, and orange-striped delineator posts. Korean rural houses instead use flatter slab roofs or colored metal panels with masonry walls; Chinese villages show brick/whitewash rows with red tile and rooftop water tanks; Taiwanese villages use RC boxes with rooftop tanks. These architectural and street-furniture vocabularies reliably separate the four countries in East Asia.
