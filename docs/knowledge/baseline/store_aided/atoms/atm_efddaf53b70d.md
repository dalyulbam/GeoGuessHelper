# Vietnamese Northern Midlands (Trung Du) transition zone

`atm_efddaf53b70d` · **geography** / region · cell `w7g64pd`

The Trung Du (midlands) belt lies between the flat Red River Delta and the mountainous Viet Bac/Northeast highlands of Vietnam, characterized by rounded, low forested hills (often planted with eucalyptus or acacia) interleaved with irrigated paddy fields. This mosaic distinguishes it from the dead-flat delta (no hills, canal grid) and from the Central Highlands (red basalt soil, coffee/rubber plantations rather than paddy).
