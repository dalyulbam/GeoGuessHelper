# Small North Atlantic service-town economic profile (Iceland coastal towns)

`atm_e541bfeb0278` · **economy** / region · cell `ge9h53s`

Small Icelandic coastal towns of a few hundred people typically combine sheep farming from surrounding countryside, small-scale fisheries, and Ring Road (Route 1) tourism traffic including wildlife-watching (e.g. seals) as economic pillars. Despite tiny populations, such towns maintain Nordic welfare-state infrastructure standards: municipal swimming pools, regional health clinics, paved streets, and well-kept detached housing.
