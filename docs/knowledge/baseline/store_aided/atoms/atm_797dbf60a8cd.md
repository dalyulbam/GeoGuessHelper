# Botswana's eastern hardveld settlement corridor

`atm_797dbf60a8cd` · **geography** / country · cell `keghd2v`

Roughly 80% of Botswana's population lives along the developed eastern 'hardveld' corridor running north-south between Gaborone and Francistown (the A1 trunk road), characterized by red-brown sandy soil, tarred streetlit roads, dense acacia woodland, and continuous settlement. This contrasts with the sparsely populated Kalahari west (white-grey sand, camelthorn, gravel roads, e.g. Ghanzi/Kgalagadi District) and the wetter Okavango/Ngamiland and Chobe regions in the north with mopane woodland and riverine palm vegetation.
