# Ustyurt Plateau / Caspian Depression arid belt vegetation

`atm_c91fbe903481` · **geography** / region · cell `v0ny37g`

Western Kazakhstan's Caspian Depression and Ustyurt Plateau region is flat sand-and-clay desert dominated by saxaul and tamarisk shrubs with wormwood steppe, lacking cultivation, shelterbelts, or trees. This contrasts with northern Kazakhstan's black-soil wheat/birch forest-steppe, eastern Kazakhstan's hilly conifer terrain, and southern Kazakhstan's irrigated poplar/cotton oases.
