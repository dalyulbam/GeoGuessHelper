# Kitayama forestry landscape north of Kyoto

`atm_f7c9410af07d` · **geography** / region · cell `xn22nqj`

The Kitayama-Tanba uplands north of Kyoto City are characterized by narrow, only a few-hundred-metre-wide flat valley floors hemmed by steep ridges under uniform coniferous (sugi/hinoki cedar and cypress) plantation forestry, distinct from the broad open alluvial basins of the Nara/Osaka plain or the lake-basin horizon and Omi-style white-plaster warehouses of the Shiga/Lake Biwa area. Sawmill timber stacks and dark heavy-gabled Tanba-style minka mark this cultural-economic sphere, historically a major supplier of high-grade Kitayama cedar timber to Kyoto.
