# Húnaflói fjord: low tableland shorelines vs. alpine fjords elsewhere in Iceland

`atm_2221d637527e` · **geography** / region · cell `ge9h53s`

Húnaflói, a wide bay/fjord system in Northwest Iceland, is bordered by low, flat-topped grassy plateaus (e.g. the Vatnsnes peninsula) rather than the steep 800-1000m alpine walls typical of Eyjafjörður or the Eastfjords. This gentler topography, combined with the absence of lava fields (as in Reykjanes) or glaciers/outwash plains (as in South Iceland), is a distinguishing regional signature for Northwest Iceland fjord towns.
