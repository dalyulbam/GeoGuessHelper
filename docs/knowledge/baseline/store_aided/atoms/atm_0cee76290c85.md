# Finnish company suffixes distinguish Finland from Sweden/Estonia/Norway

Finland is officially bilingual in many municipalities, with road and regulatory signs (e.g. 'AUTOMAATTI / AUTOMAT') showing both Finnish and Swedish text. The order of languages on signs indicates local majority: Finnish-first signage marks Finnish-majority bilingual municipalities (most of the southern coast), while Swedish-first or Swedish-only signage marks Swedish-majority areas like Åland and parts of Ostrobothnia.
