# Post-Soviet road furniture: GOST signage and striped delineator posts

Former Soviet states retain GOST-derived triangular warning signs, white centre-line road markings, and black-and-white striped concrete delineator posts along road shoulders. This visual vocabulary distinguishes post-Soviet Eurasia from MUTCD-based North America (green guide signs, yellow centre lines) or Australia (yellow-on-black shields, no concrete delineators).
