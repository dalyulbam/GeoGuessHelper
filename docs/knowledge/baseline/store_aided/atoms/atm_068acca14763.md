# South African municipal branding distinguishes metros

`atm_068acca14763` · **culture** / country · cell `k9fpcu8`

South African metropolitan municipalities each carry distinct civic branding and dialling codes: City of Johannesburg/Tshwane in Gauteng (011/012), City of Cape Town in Western Cape (021, green-and-white livery), eThekwini in KwaZulu-Natal (031, lush subtropical vegetation), and Nelson Mandela Bay in Eastern Cape (041, dry Albany thicket vegetation). These combined with regional vegetation biomes allow reliable identification of the metro from municipal signage alone.
