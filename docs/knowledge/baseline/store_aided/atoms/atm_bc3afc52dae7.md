# Fuerteventura's low eroded volcanic ridges without forest belt

`atm_bc3afc52dae7` · **geography** / region · cell `esvzdqh`

Fuerteventura is geologically older and more heavily eroded than the western Canary Islands, presenting low, rounded, ochre-brown volcanic ridges lacking any pine or laurel forest belt. This contrasts with Tenerife (Teide massif, pine forests), Gran Canaria (forested highlands), and Lanzarote (younger black lapilli/scoria fields with legally restricted white-and-green architecture).
