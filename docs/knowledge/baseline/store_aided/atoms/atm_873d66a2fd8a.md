# Northern Apennine river valleys draining to the Po vs Adriatic-draining Marche torrents

`atm_873d66a2fd8a` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Rivers of the Emilian Apennine foothills (e.g. Taro, Ceno, Trebbia, Secchia, Enza) flow north into the Po Valley and typically show very wide, pale gravel-braided beds that run nearly dry in late summer, flanked by light-industrial estates on the flat terraces. This contrasts with Marche's Adriatic-draining torrents, which are narrower with different settlement patterns, and with Tuscan valleys marked by cypress rows and stone farmhouses, or Piedmont's Langhe with terraced vineyard monoculture on marl ridges.
