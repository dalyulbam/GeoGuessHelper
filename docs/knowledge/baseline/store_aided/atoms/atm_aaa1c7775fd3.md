# Vietnamese quoc ngu script as a diagnostic of Vietnam

`atm_aaa1c7775fd3` · **language** / country · cell `w7g64pd`

Vietnam uniquely uses a Latin-based script (quoc ngu) with dense tonal diacritics for all public signage, distinguishing it at a glance from Laos, Thailand, and Cambodia (which use their own abugida scripts) and from Indonesia/Malaysia (Latin script without tone diacritics). Presence of stacked diacritic marks on shopfronts is a strong single-frame indicator of Vietnam specifically within Southeast Asia.
