# Bolivia's three macro-regions: Altiplano, inter-Andean valleys, lowlands

`atm_7ead35a5930c` · **geography** / region · cell `6s9edy7`

Bolivia divides into three broad geographic macro-regions: the high treeless Altiplano (La Paz/El Alto/Oruro, ~4000m, cold, Aymara cultural presence), temperate inter-Andean valleys (Cochabamba, Sucre, Tarija, warmer with leafy deciduous trees and moderate slopes), and the hot flat eastern lowlands (Santa Cruz, Chaco-Amazon vegetation with palms). Vegetation, tree size, and building density reliably distinguish these zones from satellite/street imagery.
