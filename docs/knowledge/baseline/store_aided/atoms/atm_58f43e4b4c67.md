# Táborsko pond-and-plateau landscape, South Bohemia

`atm_58f43e4b4c67` · **geography** / region · cell `u2f2t6k`

The Tábor district (Táborsko) in South Bohemia is characterized by a rolling plateau at roughly 450-500 m elevation dissected by stream valleys frequently dammed into fishponds, a legacy of medieval and early modern pond-building for fish farming. This landscape sits lower and gentler than the Vysočina highlands to the east, which run 550-650 m with steeper relief and heavier snow-country roof pitches.
