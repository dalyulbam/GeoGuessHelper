# Regional river parks named after their river in Emilia-Romagna

`atm_f74be1a8388d` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Emilia-Romagna designates regional fluvial parks (Parco Fluviale Regionale) named directly after their river, such as the Parco Fluviale Regionale del Taro in Parma province. Such signage is a strong, province-specific discriminator since neighboring provinces (e.g. Piacenza's Trebbia/Nure basin, Reggio Emilia's Secchia/Enza basin) have differently named parks tied to their own river systems.
