# Northern Jordanian highlands: olive terraces on terra rossa limestone

`atm_0ca411f13c11` · **geography** / region · cell `sv9x8tu`

The Ajloun-Jerash-Irbid highland belt of northern Jordan receives roughly 400-600mm annual rainfall, supporting mature olive groves on stony terra-rossa terraces amid rolling limestone ridges and white hilltop villages. This contrasts with the black basalt steppe of the Zarqa/Mafraq region, the sub-sea-level palm/banana plantations of the Jordan Valley and Aqaba, and the redder sandstone and sparser settlement of Karak/Tafilah further south.
