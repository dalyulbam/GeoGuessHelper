# Regional beer brand advertising as a sub-national marker in Germany

`atm_9d8cb0a4a832` · **culture** / region · cell `u284p2k`

German regions are identifiable by dominant local brewery gable advertising: Erdinger Weissbräu (wheat beer) marks Upper Bavaria, Radeberger/Krombacher or Veltins mark Saxony/NRW, and Rothaus/Fürstenberg mark Baden-Württemberg. Painted gable brewery advertisements on building facades are a durable, region-specific street-level cultural cue across Germany.
