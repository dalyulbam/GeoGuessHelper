# Sharjah's inland Central Region vs Hajar-backed east coast enclaves

`atm_32db37decb23` · **geography** / region · cell `thrxzsf`

Sharjah emirate is territorially fragmented: its coastal capital is a dense mid-rise city on the Gulf, its inland Central Region (including Al Dhaid) is flat, low-rise, and oasis-agricultural with no mountain backdrop, while its east coast exclaves (Khor Fakkan, Kalba) sit directly against the Hajar Mountains. Flat horizon versus mountain backdrop is thus a strong visual discriminator between Sharjah's inland and east-coast territories.
