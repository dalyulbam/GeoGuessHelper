# German StVO signage and pharmacy branding markers

`atm_e1eb736eb2bb` · **culture** / country · cell `u284p2k`

Germany's road signage (StVO) uses distinctive supplementary plates like 'werktags Mo-Fr', a blue 'Fahrradstraße' (bicycle street) sign, and red/white diagonally striped RSA works barriers. German pharmacies are marked with a stylized red 'A' emblem on a red 'APOTHEKE' sign, distinct from Austria's green cross pharmacy marking or Switzerland's bilingual 'Apotheke/Pharmacie' signage and blue-white route plates.
