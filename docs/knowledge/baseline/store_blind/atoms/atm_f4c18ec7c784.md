# Post-Soviet road furniture: black-and-white stolbiki and GOST signage

`atm_f4c18ec7c784` · **culture** / polity · cell `v0ny37g`

Former Soviet states share a distinct roadside vocabulary: black-and-white striped concrete delineator posts (stolbiki), GOST-standard triangular warning signs with narrow red borders, and Andreevsky-cross (diagonal saltire) level-crossing markers. This contrasts with MUTCD yellow centre-lines and diamond signs in North America, and with Arabic-script bilingual signage and painted kerbs in the Middle East/North Africa.
