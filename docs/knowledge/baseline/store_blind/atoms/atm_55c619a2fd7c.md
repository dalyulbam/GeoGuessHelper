# Regional student-restaurant branding as a Finnish city discriminator

`atm_55c619a2fd7c` · **culture** / country · cell `u6xzgj3`

Finnish university cities have distinct student catering operators tied to local student unions: UniCafe/HYY serves Helsinki, while Unica serves Turun yliopisto and Åbo Akademi in Turku. Smaller university towns typically have their own local operator. Branded canteen signage on campus buildings can therefore pinpoint which Finnish university city a scene belongs to.
