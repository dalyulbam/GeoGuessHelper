# Czech tradesman van signage convention (TRUHLÁŘSTVÍ, ZAKÁZKOVÁ VÝROBA, .cz domain)

Small Czech craft businesses (e.g. joinery/carpentry, 'truhlářství') commonly advertise on vehicles with the trade name, phrase 'zakázková výroba' (custom-made production/made-to-order), a hometown, and a .cz email or web domain. Such van livery can reveal the business's home village or town, useful for pinpointing rural locations.
