# Turanian desert saxaul-saltbush biome (Ustyurt/pre-Aral)

`atm_6d448096edda` · **culture** / region · cell `v0ny37g`

The Ustyurt plateau and pre-Aral desert belt of western/southwestern Kazakhstan is a dead-flat, cold desert landscape dominated by saxaul shrubs and saltbush on reddish sand, with no cultivation, windbreaks, or relief. This distinguishes it from the black-soil wheat steppe with birch groves of northern Kazakhstan, the rocky granite hummocks of the Sary-Arka uplands, and the irrigated, poplar-lined, mountain-backed landscape of Semirechye in the southeast.
