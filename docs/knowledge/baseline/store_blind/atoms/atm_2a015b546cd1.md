# South Bohemian Tábor/Soběslav basin fringe vs Vysočina highlands

`atm_2a015b546cd1` · **geography** / region · cell `u2f2t6k`

The Tábor/Soběslav basin fringe in South Bohemia is characterized by rolling arable land at roughly 450 m elevation with mixed pine/oak woodlots, contrasting with the neighboring Vysočina highlands to the east, which have denser spruce forest cover and steeper relief. This vegetation/relief contrast helps distinguish sub-regions within South Bohemia at similar latitudes.
