# Jordanian vernacular owner-built concrete-and-limestone housing

`atm_3ed1a91ef487` · **culture** / country · cell `sv9x8tu`

Common Jordanian residential vernacular is owner-built in stages: reinforced concrete frames left with protruding rebar stubs awaiting a future added floor, clad in cheap local pale limestone (the dominant regional building stone). Rooftop blue plastic water tanks are ubiquitous, reflecting municipal water rationing/intermittent supply rather than affluence.
