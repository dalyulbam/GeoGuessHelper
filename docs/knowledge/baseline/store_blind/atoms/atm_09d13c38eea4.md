# Kinai/Sanyo satoyama clustered hamlet pattern

`atm_09d13c38eea4` · **culture** / region · cell `xn22nqj`

In the Kinai and Sanyo (western Honshu) regions, traditional rural settlement takes the form of compact, clustered hamlets sited at the toe of low wooded hills, with walled house compounds featuring clipped niwaki garden pines and lacquered lattice fencing, fronting onto shared irrigation channels that serve flat-bottomed paddy blocks on the valley floor. This contrasts with Hokkaido's settler-era grid-planned farmsteads with wide setbacks and large rectangular fields.
