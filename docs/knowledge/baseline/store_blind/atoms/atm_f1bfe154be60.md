# Finland's bilingual coastal municipalities (Finnish/Swedish)

`atm_f1bfe154be60` · **culture** / country · cell `u6xzgj3`

Finland has a constitutionally recognized Swedish-speaking minority concentrated along the south and southwest coast (Uusimaa, Varsinais-Suomi, Ostrobothnia). Officially bilingual municipalities post municipal signage—including parking and street signs—in Finnish with Swedish as a co-official second line, whereas monolingual interior Finnish cities (Tampere, Jyväskylä, Kuopio, Oulu) use Finnish-only municipal signage.
