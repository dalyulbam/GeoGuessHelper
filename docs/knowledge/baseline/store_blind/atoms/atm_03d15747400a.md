# Munich MVG grass-track tram radials distinguish it from other Bavarian cities

`atm_03d15747400a` · **culture** / city · cell `u284p2k`

Munich's tram network (MVG) is characterized by wide radial avenues with landscaped grass-bedded track and side-mounted catenary, contrasting with Nuremberg's asphalt-paved tram track and sandstone-toned older fabric, and Augsburg's narrower avenues with 'Königsplatz'-style stops. Heavy ongoing construction/scaffolding density is also a marker of Munich's high property-pressure inner-ring corridors.
