# Snow-belt vs Pacific-side Honshu roof and road infrastructure divide

`atm_8ed448618a1a` · **geography** / polity · cell `xn22nqj`

The Sea-of-Japan side of Honshu (Hokuriku/Tohoku snow belt) and Hokkaido show steep steel or half-hipped snow-shedding roofs, reflective snow poles on nearly every roadside post, and centre-line roadside snow-melting sprinkler (shosetsu) nozzles. Warmer Pacific-side and inland basin areas of central/western Honshu lack these features, instead showing widespread clay kawara tile roofing with shallow eaves and no snow poles, marking a clear macro-regional climate/infrastructure boundary usable to separate snow-country from non-snow-country Japan.
