# Turku's Yliopistonmäki/Kerttuli/Martti belt: campus meets wooden quarter

`atm_fe84b8e08af6` · **culture** / city · cell `u6xzgj3`

**시기**: 1800 ~ ?

Turku's university and hospital hill (Yliopistonmäki) sits on a wooded bedrock ridge east of the Aura river, immediately adjacent to intact 19th-century wooden residential quarters (Kerttuli, Martti/Port Arthur) that slope down toward the city center and its domed cathedral silhouette. This juxtaposition of Aalto-idiom campus modernism, concrete institutional slab blocks, and preserved wooden streetscape is characteristic of Turku specifically, distinguishing it from Helsinki's Kumpula/Viikki campuses or Vaasa/Porvoo, which lack comparable hill campuses.
