# Bolivia's three macro-terrain zones: altiplano, inter-Andean valleys, lowlands

`atm_9ef7af912dd0` · **geography** / region · cell `6s9edy7`

Bolivia divides into three distinct settlement zones: the treeless altiplano (La Paz/El Alto/Oruro) at 3800-4100m with adobe/brick blocks and aluminium-clad 'cholet' facades; inter-Andean valley cities (Sucre, Cochabamba, Tarija, Potosi) at 2500-3000m with broadleaf shade trees, tiled colonial-era roofs and milder climate; and the flat, hot eastern lowlands (Santa Cruz) with palms, galleried arcades and heavy motorbike traffic.
