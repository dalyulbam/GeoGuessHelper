# Fennoscandian shield bedrock outcrops in urban streetscapes

`atm_5858623b487c` · **geography** / region · cell `u6xzgj3`

In Finland and much of Sweden/Norway, glacially scoured granite bedrock (often moss-covered) frequently protrudes at street level within city limits, a legacy of Fennoscandian Shield geology and Quaternary glaciation. This is rare in Central Europe, where terraced plastered streetfronts dominate and exposed bedrock is not typically visible in urban cores.
