# Macaronesian islands: volcanic Atlantic archipelagos share flora signatures

`atm_91929140289a` · **nature** / polity · cell `esvzdqh`

Macaronesia (Canaries, Madeira, Azores, Cape Verde) comprises volcanic Atlantic archipelagos of similar origin but divergent climates. The Canaries and Cape Verde include hyper-arid desert islands with black basaltic scoria and Phoenix canariensis palms, while Madeira and the western Canaries (La Palma, La Gomera, El Hierro) are lush, laurel-forested and mountainous due to greater orographic rainfall.
