# Irimoya kawara tiled minka as Japan-specific rural roof marker

`atm_595b46dd9dad` · **architecture** / country · cell `xn22nqj`

Traditional Japanese farmhouses (minka) commonly use the irimoya (hip-and-gable) roof form with deep eaves, clad in clay kawara tiles and topped with ornamental ridge-end oni-gawara tiles. This combination, together with wooden amado sliding shutters and bamboo-fence garden screens, distinguishes Japanese rural housing from Korean, Taiwanese or Chinese rural vernacular, which use lighter slate/steel roofs, painted concrete-block or red-brick walls, and lack oni-gawara.
