# Afrikaans-English commercial layer over isiXhosa-majority Eastern Cape population

`atm_32a347b0ab5e` · **culture** / region · cell `k9fpcu8`

In Eastern Cape towns like Uitenhage/Kariega, commercial signage and family-business names commonly reflect an Afrikaans/English layer (surnames, chain branding) even though the surrounding metro population is isiXhosa-majority. This linguistic stratification between commercial/civic branding and demographic majority is typical of many former Cape Colony and Eastern Cape towns.
