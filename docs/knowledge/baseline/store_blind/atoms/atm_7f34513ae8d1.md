# European vs North American vs East Asian arterial road cues

`atm_7f34513ae8d1` · **culture** / region · cell `u284p2k`

Central/Western Europe city arterials typically show continuous perimeter-block apartment buildings flush to the pavement, white lane markings, and catenary tram wires on span suspension. North American arterials instead show overhead low-voltage utility poles, wide building setbacks, and yellow lane centre markings. East Asian arterials show vertical signage and different pole clutter, distinguishing them at a glance from European streetscapes.
