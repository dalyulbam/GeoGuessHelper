# Aymara vs Quechua highland dress cues in Bolivia

`atm_940b5be8e5c4` · **culture** / polity · cell `6s9edy7`

Aymara-sphere women (La Paz/Oruro) typically wear bowler hats set high on the head with heavier layered aguayo shawls and Wiphala municipal iconography is common. Quechua-sphere women (Chuquisaca/Potosi/Cochabamba) more often wear short polleras with pale broad straw/felt hats and single or double braids. These dress conventions help distinguish Aymara from Quechua cultural zones within the Bolivian highlands.
