# Icelandic coastal landform contrast: south coast vs east fjords vs northwest shallow fjords

`atm_2dd6bcd16393` · **geography** / region · cell `ge9h53s`

Iceland's south coast features black volcanic sand beaches and steep glaciated escarpments (e.g. near Eyjafjöll/Vík); the East Fjords have steep, scree-flanked walls of 800-1000m dropping directly into the sea; the Northwest (Húnaflói/Skagafjörður area) has broader, shallower fjords flanked by low, rolling, grass-covered plateaux without glaciers or black sand. These landform signatures allow regional placement within Iceland from a single photograph.
