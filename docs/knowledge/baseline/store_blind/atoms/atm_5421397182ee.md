# Northwest Jordan rain-fed highland belt (Ajloun-Jerash-Irbid)

`atm_5421397182ee` · **geography** / region · cell `sv9x8tu`

The Ajloun-Jerash-Irbid highlands of northwest Jordan receive enough rainfall to support continuous olive orchards, pine and oak cover, and dispersed white-limestone hillside villages in deep valleys. This contrasts with the Amman-Zarqa metro (dense multi-storey urban fabric, signalised junctions), the southern plateau around Karak/Ma'an (treeless, red-brown soil, too dry for olive canopy), and the eastern Badia (basalt desert steppe, no orchards).
