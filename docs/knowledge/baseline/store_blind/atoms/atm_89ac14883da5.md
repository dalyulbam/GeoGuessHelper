# Tswana lolwapa homestead and rondavel housing typology

`atm_89ac14883da5` · **culture** / polity · cell `keghd2v`

Tswana (Bangwato and related Tswana-speaking groups) homesteads traditionally use the 'lolwapa' walled courtyard layout combined with thatched conical rondavels alongside modern block houses. This mixed housing typology signals a Tswana cultural sphere in eastern Botswana, distinct from Kalanga areas (different toponymy, near Francistown) and San/Basarwa resettlement villages (minimal infrastructure, no brigades or bottle stores).
