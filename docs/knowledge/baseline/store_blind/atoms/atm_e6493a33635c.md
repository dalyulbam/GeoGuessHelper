# Tamba belt dark ibushi-gawara tile and dark sedimentary stone palette

`atm_e6493a33635c` · **geography** / region · cell `xn22nqj`

The Tamba-Sasayama/Nantan basin belt spanning inland Hyogo and Kyoto is characterized by overwhelming use of dark grey ibushi (smoked/burnished) roof tiles and dark sedimentary Tamba-belt stone for retaining walls, contrasting with the orange Sekishu-gawara tile and pale granite (masa) stone typical of Okayama-Hiroshima inland Chugoku basins. Low rounded hills of 150-300 m with heavy moso bamboo encroachment further distinguish this belt from larger, higher-rimmed basins like Shiga or Gifu.
