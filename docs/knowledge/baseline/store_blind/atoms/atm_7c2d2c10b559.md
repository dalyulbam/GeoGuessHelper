# Eastern hardveld corridor of Botswana (Gaborone-Francistown A1)

`atm_7c2d2c10b559` · **geography** / region · cell `keghd2v`

The eastern hardveld corridor along Botswana's A1 trunk road (Gaborone to Francistown) is characterized by rocky, gravelly reddish soil with granite outcrop stones, dense linear ribbon settlement of block houses along a tarred, streetlit two-lane highway, and truck traffic. This contrasts with the deep white Kalahari sand and sparse settlement of Ghanzi/Kgalagadi in the west, and the mopane/riverine woodland with elephant fencing of the Okavango/Chobe northwest.
