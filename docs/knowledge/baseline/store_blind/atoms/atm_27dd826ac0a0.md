# Czech recycling bell labels and phrasing distinguish Czech from Slovak/Polish

`atm_27dd826ac0a0` · **language** / country · cell `u2f2t6k`

Czech municipal recycling containers are labeled PAPÍR, SKLO (směs), KOVOVÉ odpady, with the courtesy phrase 'DĚKUJEME VÁM, ŽE TŘÍDÍTE' (thank you for sorting). This differs from Slovak equivalents (PLASTY/PAPIER/SKLO, 'ĎAKUJEME, ŽE TRIEDITE'), Polish (PLASTIK/PAPIER/SZKŁO), German Austrian (ALTPAPIER/ALTGLAS), and Hungarian (PAPÍR/ÜVEG/MŰANYAG), making recycling signage a reliable country-level discriminator among Central European neighbors.
