# Local business self-branding with town/sub-locality names in rural Jordan

`atm_632345065d73` · **economy** / region · cell `sv9x8tu`

Small rural Jordanian businesses (building-materials suppliers, food chains) commonly brand signage with their own town or sub-locality name (e.g. a materials firm named after Jerash, a shawarma branch labeled with its wadi/neighborhood). This local-toponym branding on commercial signage is a strong, reusable discriminator for pinpointing sub-district location within a governorate.
