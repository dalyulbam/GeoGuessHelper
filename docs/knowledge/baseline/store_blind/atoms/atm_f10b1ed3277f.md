# Aalto-school institutional modernism on Finnish campuses

`atm_f10b1ed3277f` · **architecture** / country · cell `u6xzgj3`

**시기**: 1930 ~ 1980

Mid-20th-century Finnish institutional architecture (the 'Aalto school', after Alvar Aalto) favors white-rendered or brick low-rise buildings set on lawns/wooded ridges, often paired with later 1960s-70s concrete faculty or hospital slab blocks. This idiom is common on Finnish university and hospital hill campuses and helps distinguish them from generic postwar European campus architecture.
