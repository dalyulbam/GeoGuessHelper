# Emilia vs Romagna river basin identity

`atm_88962bc03b9e` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Emilia-Romagna splits culturally along river basins: the Emilian half (west) drains via Apennine torrents like the Taro, Trebbia, Nure, Enza and Secchia toward the Po, while Romagna (east) is defined by the Rubicone, Savio and Marecchia basins draining to the Adriatic with a stronger Adriatic tourism economy. Regional park signage and river names are a direct giveaway of which half of the region one is in.
