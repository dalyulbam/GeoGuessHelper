# Emilian foothill town economic profile: SME manufacturing plus heritage tourism

`atm_32b3e5bf4107` · **economy** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Small provincial towns in the Emilian Apennine foothills (Parma, Piacenza, Reggio Emilia provinces) typically combine light-industrial SME clusters (food-processing machinery, forklifts, tyre and welding shops) sited on river floodplains with heritage-based tourism branding tied to regional river parks and the Via Francigena pilgrim route. Mid-century render apartment blocks stacked on hillsides are a common housing typology in these towns.
