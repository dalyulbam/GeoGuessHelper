# Eastern vs western Canary Islands: aridity gradient

`atm_14083abe94c9` · **geography** / polity · cell `esvzdqh`

Within the Canary Islands, the eastern islands (Fuerteventura, Lanzarote) are low, flat, treeless and hyper-arid with no major volcanic massif, while the central/western islands (Tenerife, Gran Canaria) have steep barrancos and high volcanic massifs (Teide, Roque Nublo) and dense resort high-rises. The far western islands (La Palma, La Gomera, El Hierro) are forested and green, lacking large-scale resort motorway infrastructure.
