# Canary Islands resort monoculture economy pattern

`atm_7a43df241d1e` · **economy** / region · cell `esvzdqh`

Fuerteventura and similar Canary resort zones exhibit a tourism-monoculture economy: motorway-grade access roads serve only walled all-inclusive resort clusters, rental-car fleets dominate traffic, and surrounding desert land is unfarmed with water and food imported. This produces low permanent population density but high seasonal service employment, typical of peripheral EU outermost regions catering to northern European package tourism.
