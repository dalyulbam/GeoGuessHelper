# UAE landline area codes map to emirates

`atm_8cb15971f2d4` · **culture** / country · cell `thrxzsf`

In the United Arab Emirates, landline telephone prefixes are emirate-specific: 02 Abu Dhabi, 04 Dubai, 06 Sharjah/Ajman/Umm Al Quwain, 07 Ras Al Khaimah, 09 Fujairah. Mobile numbers use nationwide 050/052/055/056/058 prefixes regardless of emirate. This makes printed landline prefixes on shopfronts a reliable discriminator of which emirate a UAE location sits in.
