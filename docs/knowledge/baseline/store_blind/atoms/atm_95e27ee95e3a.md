# Bilingual Arabic-English signage as Gulf littoral marker

`atm_95e27ee95e3a` · **language** / region · cell `thrxzsf`

Across Gulf Arab states, commercial and municipal signage systematically pairs Arabic script with English transliteration, distinguishing the Arabian Peninsula littoral from North Africa (French co-signage), the Levant (Arabic often unpaired or paired with less consistent English), and Iran (Persian script, no English duplication). This bilingual convention reflects large expatriate workforces and state administrative practice.
