# Cerdanyola del Vallès, Catalonia (Vallès Occidental, Barcelona province), Spain

보고서: `report_es_cerdanyola-del-valles_41.4835_2.1349_260829_094215_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[sp3eb]]

Cerdanyola del Vallès is a mid-sized suburban city of about 58,500 people on Barcelona's northern metropolitan fringe, in the Vallès Occidental comarca. Its identity is defined by knowledge industries rather than heavy industry, anchored by the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona's main campus in Bellaterra and the ALBA Synchrotron, alongside associated science and technology parks. The town blends quiet, family-oriented suburban living with a youthful university-town atmosphere, and is well connected to Barcelona by rail (about 30 minutes).
