# Ban Champa Thong, Nong Lat subdistrict, Warichaphum district, Sakon Nakhon Province (Isan), Thailand

보고서: `report_th_ban-champa-thong_17.3071_103.5802_260910_235535_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2

Ban Champa Thong is a small farming hamlet in Nong Lat subdistrict, Warichaphum district, Sakon Nakhon Province, in Thailand's Isan region. Its economy is smallholder-agrarian, centered on wet-season rice paddy supplemented by cassava, sugarcane, and cooperative-linked cattle raising, with no industrial or wage-employment base. The village has no distinct tourism, cultural, or biographical profile, functioning as one of many ordinary agricultural hamlets within a district better known for rubber, dairy, and silk production than for any single village's identity.
