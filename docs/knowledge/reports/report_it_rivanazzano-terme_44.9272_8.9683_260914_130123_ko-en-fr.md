# Casalsaglio / Casalvecchio, near Rivanazzano Terme–Voghera, Lombardy (Oltrepò Pavese, Province of Pavia), Italy

Casalsaglio/Casalvecchio is a tiny rural hamlet within the comune of Rivanazzano Terme, in the hilly Oltrepò Pavese district of Pavia province, Lombardy. The area blends a historic thermal-spa and medieval heritage (Terme di Rivanazzano, the fortified Nazzano hamlet) with a rural economy built on vineyards, farming, and small-scale manufacturing, all set within Lombardy's leading wine region. Life here is quiet and slow-paced, anchored by nearby Voghera and defined by an aging, slowly shrinking population balanced by a trickle of newcomers drawn to wine tourism and rural authenticity.
