# Quebrada de los Choros / Chacho Martínez (Huasco valley area), Atacama Region (Huasco / Copiapó province), Chile

보고서: `report_cl_quebrada-de-los-choros_-29.3752_-70.9551_260910_234553_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2

Quebrada de los Choros / Chacho Martínez is a tiny, sparsely populated rural hamlet in the arid upper Huasco valley of Chile's Atacama Region, subordinate to the nearby hub of Vallenar and the rural commune of Alto del Carmen. Its economy blends subsistence goat-herding and forage/olive/pisco-grape agriculture inland with artisanal fishing, shellfish harvesting, and nature tourism (Humboldt Penguin National Reserve) toward the coast at Punta de Choros. The area carries deep pre-Hispanic Copiapó and Diaguita cultural roots overlaid with Spanish colonial traditions of pisco and olive cultivation, situated within a broader province anchored by iron-ore mining and irrigated fruit/viticulture around Vallenar.
