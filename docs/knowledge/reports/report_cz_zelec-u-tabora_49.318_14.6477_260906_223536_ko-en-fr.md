# Želeč (Želeč u Tábora), South Bohemian Region, Tábor District, Czechia

보고서: `report_cz_zelec-u-tabora_49.318_14.6477_260906_223536_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2

Želeč u Tábora is a small, stable South Bohemian village of about 1,000 residents in Tábor District, first documented in 1397 and once seat of a Renaissance chateau built for the Rožmberk family, demolished in 2015 after a 1927 fire. Today it functions as a quiet agricultural and forestry-oriented commuter satellite of nearby Tábor (14 km away), notable economically for hosting a large regional landfill operated by Rumpold s.r.o. since 1996. Village life is anchored by active civic institutions — a volunteer fire brigade, Sokol sports club, school, and cultural house — set amid forests and fishponds typical of the region, with modest tourism appeal via cycling and hiking routes and the nearby Kozlov natural monument.
