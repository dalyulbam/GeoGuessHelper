# Vlore, Vlore County, Albania

보고서: `report_al_vlore_40.4753_19.4765_260810_015707_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srjy7]]

Vlorë is a port city on the southwestern coast of Albania and the capital of Vlorë County, one of the country's largest cities. It sits where the Adriatic meets the Ionian Sea, at the northern gateway to the Albanian Riviera. Historically it is celebrated as the place where Albanian independence was declared in 1912, and today it is a major maritime, commercial, and fast-growing tourism hub.
