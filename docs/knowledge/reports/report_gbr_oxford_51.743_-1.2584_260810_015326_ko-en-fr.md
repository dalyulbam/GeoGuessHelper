Oxford is a historic city in Oxfordshire, South East England, best known as the home of the University of Oxford, the oldest university in the English-speaking world. Nicknamed "the city of dreaming spires," it combines world-class higher education and research with a diverse modern economy that includes car manufacturing, publishing, and biomedical science. With a population of roughly 160,000, it is a compact, cosmopolitan and student-heavy city that draws millions of visitors annually.
