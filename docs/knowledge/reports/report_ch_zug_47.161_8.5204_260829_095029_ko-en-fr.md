# Zug, Canton of Zug, Switzerland

보고서: `report_ch_zug_47.161_8.5204_260829_095029_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[u0qh3]]

Zug is a compact, lakeside Swiss town that transformed from the poorest canton in the country into one of its wealthiest, thanks to ultra-low corporate and personal taxes that attracted commodity traders, multinational holdings, and a thriving "Crypto Valley" blockchain/fintech cluster. It pairs a beautifully preserved medieval Old Town — with the Zytturm clock tower, Zug Castle, and St. Oswald's Church — with a hyper-international, affluent, and transient professional population drawn by finance and tech. Local identity still centers on centuries-old cherry-growing traditions (Zuger Kirschtorte, Chriesisturm festival) even as the town has become a global hub for trading, biotech, and cryptocurrency firms.
