# Lebedinovka (Chüy Region, Bishkek suburb), Chuy Region, Kyrgyzstan

보고서: `report_kg_lebedinovka_42.88_74.6989_260829_091757_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[txt5g]]

Lebedinovka is a sizable Bishkek-adjacent village (pop. ~21,000) in Kyrgyzstan's Chüy Valley, serving as the administrative seat of Alamüdün District. Founded in 1898 by Slavic agricultural settlers, it blends Kyrgyz and Russian/Ukrainian heritage and remains primarily agrarian, specializing in grain, dairy, and livestock, while functioning increasingly as a commuter suburb of the capital. Though not a tourist destination itself, it offers a peaceful rural base near Ala-Archa National Park and reflects Kyrgyzstan's broader pattern of capital-fringe suburbanization.
