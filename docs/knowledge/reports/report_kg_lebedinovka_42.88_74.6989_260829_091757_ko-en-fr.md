# Lebedinovka (Chüy Region, Bishkek suburb), Chuy Region, Kyrgyzstan

보고서: `report_kg_lebedinovka_42.88_74.6989_260829_091757_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p1/p3

Lebedinovka is a sizable administrative village (population 21,118 as of 2021) on the immediate eastern fringe of Bishkek in Kyrgyzstan's Chuy Valley, located at 42.8815°N, 74.6845°E. The settlement is identifiable by its mixed Slavic-Kyrgyz character: Russian street names and late-19th-century settler toponymy (Chapaeva, Michurina, Komsomolskaya) layered over Kyrgyz-language public signage, neighbourhood mosques, and the flat irrigated steppe landscape with leafless poplar shelterbelts typical of the Chuy agrarian zone.
