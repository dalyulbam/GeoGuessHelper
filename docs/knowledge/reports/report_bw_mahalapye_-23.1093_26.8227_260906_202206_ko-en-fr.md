# Mahalapye (Borotsi / Tamocha area, west of A1), Central District, Botswana

보고서: `report_bw_mahalapye_-23.1093_26.8227_260906_202206_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[keghd]]

Mahalapye's Borotsi/Tamocha area is a traditional agro-pastoral ward on the western fringe of Mahalapye, a large Bangwato village in Botswana's Central District straddling the Gaborone–Francistown A1 highway and railway corridor. Life here blends subsistence and small-scale commercial farming (cattle, sorghum, maize, beans) with dependence on Mahalapye's town center for jobs, schools, and services, all set within a deep-rooted Setswana cultural landscape. The wider town serves mainly as a transit hub with railway heritage, a shopping mall, and gateway access to nearby cultural and natural sites, rather than as a major standalone destination.
