# Hvammstangi, Northwestern Region (Norðurland vestra), Húnaþing vestra municipality, Iceland

보고서: `report_is_hvammstangi_65.3981_-20.944_260903_095326_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2

Hvammstangi is a small village of roughly 580–600 people on the Vatnsnes Peninsula in Northwest Iceland, serving as the main service, trading, and administrative hub of Húnaþing vestra municipality. Long rooted in fishing and trade dating to the 1850s, it has diversified into tourism, branding itself the "seal capital of Iceland" thanks to the Icelandic Seal Center and nearby wild harbour seal colonies. The town also hosts Iceland's largest textile factory (KIDKA wool mill) and serves as a gateway stop on the Arctic Coast Way and Ring Road route between Reykjavík and Akureyri.
