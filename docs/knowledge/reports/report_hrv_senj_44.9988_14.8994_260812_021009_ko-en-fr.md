# Senj (Sveta Jelena / Kamp Škver area), Lika-Senj County (Kvarner / northern Adriatic coast), Croatia

보고서: `report_hrv_senj_44.9988_14.8994_260812_021009_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srfxg]]

Senj is an ancient, wind-battered port town on the northern Adriatic in Croatia's sparsely populated Lika-Senj County, renowned for the 16th-century Uskok stronghold of Nehaj Fortress, deep Glagolitic and literary heritage, and Croatia's oldest carnival tradition. Rather than mass tourism, it offers a quieter, "authentic" coastal experience with pebble beaches, dramatic mountain-meets-sea scenery, and access to Northern Velebit National Park, Zavratnica Bay, and nearby islands like Krk and Rab. Its economy remains small-scale — fishing, agriculture, and seasonal tourism — with negligible industry, while its population, like much of the county, is aging and shrinking.
