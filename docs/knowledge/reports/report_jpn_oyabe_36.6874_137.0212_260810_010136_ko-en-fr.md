# Takaoka / Oyabe area, Toyama Prefecture, Japan

보고서: `report_jpn_oyabe_36.6874_137.0212_260810_010136_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[xn92t]]

Takaoka and neighboring Oyabe form an industrial and cultural cluster in the northwestern part of Toyama Prefecture on the Sea of Japan coast of Honshu. Takaoka is Toyama's second-largest city, historically a castle-and-merchant town famous for centuries of metal casting (Takaoka copperware). Oyabe is a smaller neighboring city known for machinery/manufacturing and the whimsical Merhen scenery of its rural landscape. Both sit on the Tonami/Takaoka plain and share the Hokuriku Shinkansen-era transport corridor.
