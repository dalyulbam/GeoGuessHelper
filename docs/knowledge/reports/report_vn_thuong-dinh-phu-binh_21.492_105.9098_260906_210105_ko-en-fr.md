# Thượng Đình / Hanh market area, Phú Bình district, Thái Nguyên province, Vietnam

보고서: `report_vn_thuong-dinh-phu-binh_21.492_105.9098_260906_210105_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[w7g66]]

Thượng Đình/Hanh market area is a rural locality in Phú Bình district, Thái Nguyên province, now merged into Điềm Thụy commune, historically an agrarian Kinh farming community organized around periodic markets like Chợ Hanh and the heritage Đình–Chùa Quan Trang complex. It sits near the fast-industrializing Sông Công–Phổ Yên corridor and the new Phú Bình Industrial Park, positioning it as a rural hinterland increasingly linked to nearby electronics manufacturing (including Samsung's ecosystem) rather than a tourist or urban center. Traditional livelihoods—wet-rice farming, craft villages, and market trade—are gradually giving way to industrial wage labor as younger residents commute to factories.
