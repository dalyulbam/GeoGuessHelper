# Orašac (Dubrovnik Riviera), Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_orasac-dubrovnik_42.7038_18.0064_260813_213107_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsfc]]

Orašac is a small, thousand-year-old hilltop village on the Dubrovnik Riviera between Trsteno and Zaton, defined by centuries-old olive groves, a compact defensively-built historic core, and landmarks like the 1250 St. Nicholas Church and the 1529 Church of Our Lady of Orašac. Its economy blends family-run olive and Mediterranean agriculture with agritourism and seasonal tourism, including the large Sun Gardens Dubrovnik resort, while remaining just a short drive from Dubrovnik's Old Town. The village's best-known cultural export is painter Antun Masle, and it retains a modest year-round population of 742 (2021) sustaining a traditional, agrarian rhythm outside the summer season.
