# Esquinzo / Butihondo (Jandía), Pájara municipality, Canary Islands — Fuerteventura (Las Palmas province), Spain

보고서: `report_es_esquinzo-fuerteventura_28.0717_-14.3123_260906_204807_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[esvzd]]

Esquinzo/Butihondo is a purpose-built beachfront resort corridor within the Jandía tourist strip of Pájara municipality, southern Fuerteventura, built almost entirely around mass tourism. It stretches along a nearly 2-mile golden-sand beach backed by the protected Jandía Nature Park, hosting major international hotel chains and serving as a base for the region's renowned windsurfing and wingfoiling scene, including the Sotavento World Championship nearby. Rather than a traditional town, it functions as a linear hotel enclave between Morro Jable and Costa Calma, staffed largely by rotating hospitality workers and populated seasonally by international tourists and long-stay northern European retirees.
