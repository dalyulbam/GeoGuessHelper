# Zaton Veliki (Zaton, Dubrovnik), Dubrovnik-Neretva County (Dubrovačko-neretvanska županija), Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_zaton-veliki_42.6901_18.0375_260813_222455_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr

Zaton Veliki is a small, sheltered bay village on the Dubrovnik Riviera, about 8 km northwest of Dubrovnik, blending a quiet residential character with seasonal tourism. Once a countryside retreat for Ragusan noble families, it retains Renaissance-era summer villas and traditional stone houses alongside modern apartments, villas and campsites. Its economy rests on small-scale agriculture (olives, wine, fishing) combined with tourism income from private rentals, konoba restaurants and boat/watersport operators, all just a short bus ride from Dubrovnik's Old City.
