# Barnaul, Altai Krai, Russia

보고서: `report_rus_barnaul_53.3793_83.6802_260810_002518_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[vc7x8]]

Barnaul is the capital and largest city of Altai Krai in southwestern Siberia, sited at the confluence of the Barnaulka and Ob rivers. Founded around 1730 as a silver-smelting settlement of the Demidov industrial dynasty, it grew into a major mining, industrial and administrative hub. Today it is a city of roughly 620,000–690,000 people and the fifth-most-populous city in Siberia, serving as the region's industrial, cultural, educational and transport center.
