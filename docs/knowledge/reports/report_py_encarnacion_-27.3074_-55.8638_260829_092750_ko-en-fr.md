This location is the southeastern lakeside edge of Encarnación, Paraguay's premier riverside city, positioned on a causeway crossing a flooded arm of the Paraná River reservoir near Barrio San Pedro. The site is pinpointed by overhead gantry signage identifying the neighbourhood and route numbers, combined with uniquely Paraguayan route-numbering markers, characteristic red lateritic soil, and mid-rise city skyline across the water facing Argentina.
