# Cumaral, Meta Department, Colombia

보고서: `report_co_cumaral_4.2716_-73.4895_260910_234145_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[d2g8r]]

Cumaral is a mid-sized municipality of roughly 21,000–25,000 people in Meta Department, Colombia, whose economy and identity are firmly rooted in Llanos ranching and agriculture (cattle, rice, African oil palm), with a young population and a small-town, agrarian pace of life. It benefits from proximity to Villavicencio (about 40 minutes away) and from Meta's broader oil and gas royalties, while emerging llanero-culture and nature tourism add a growing supplementary income stream.
