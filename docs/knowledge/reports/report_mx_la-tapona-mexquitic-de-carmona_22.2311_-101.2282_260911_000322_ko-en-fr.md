# La Tapona, Mexquitic de Carmona, San Luis Potosí, Mexico

보고서: `report_mx_la-tapona-mexquitic-de-carmona_22.2311_-101.2282_260911_000322_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[9gbn2]]

La Tapona is a small, agrarian hamlet of around 800-810 residents in the municipality of Mexquitic de Carmona, San Luis Potosí, situated at roughly 2,140-2,150 m in the semi-arid Altiplano Potosino about 22 km from the state capital. Life centers on subsistence and small-scale commercial farming (maize, beans, nopal) and goat/cattle ranching, supplemented by wage labor and commuting to San Luis Potosí city's automotive and manufacturing industries. The village has self-built concrete-block housing, limited piped infrastructure, and shares in the broader Mexquitic municipality's Huachichil heritage, Catholic festival traditions, and regional cuisine, but has no tourist infrastructure or notable institutions of its own.
