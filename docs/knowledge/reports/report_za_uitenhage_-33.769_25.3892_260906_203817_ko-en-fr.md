# Uitenhage (Kariega), Eastern Cape (Nelson Mandela Bay Metro), South Africa

보고서: `report_za_uitenhage_-33.769_25.3892_260906_203817_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[k9fpc]]

Kariega (Uitenhage) is a mid-sized industrial town of just over 100,000 people in South Africa's Nelson Mandela Bay Metro, founded in 1804 and nicknamed the "Garden Town" for its setting in the foothills of the Winterhoek Mountains. It is South Africa's premier automotive manufacturing hub, anchored by the Volkswagen Kariega assembly plant — the biggest car factory on the African continent and the only global plant producing the VW Polo for export — alongside Goodyear tyre manufacturing and a component-supplier cluster. The town also carries deep historical weight through well-preserved Cape Dutch and Victorian heritage architecture and the somber legacy of the 1985 Langa Massacre, a pivotal moment in South Africa's anti-apartheid history.
