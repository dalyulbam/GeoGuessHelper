# Añilco / Chihuaico (rural, near Villarrica), La Araucanía Region (Villarrica commune, Cautín Province), at the Los Ríos boundary, Chile

보고서: `report_cl_anilco-villarrica_-39.4654_-72.2604_260916_002050_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2

Añilco/Chihuaico is a small, sparsely populated rural Mapuche community within Villarrica commune (La Araucanía), sitting near the boundary with the Los Ríos Region. Life here centers on smallholder farming, dairy/livestock and depleted logging land, with residents dependent on Villarrica town for services, roads and health care, while their Mapuche heritage and proximity to Lake Villarrica and the Villarrica volcano tie them into the region's broader tourism identity even though the hamlet itself has no tourism infrastructure of its own.
