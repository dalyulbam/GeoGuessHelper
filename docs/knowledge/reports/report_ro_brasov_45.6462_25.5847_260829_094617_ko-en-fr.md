# Brașov, Brașov County, Transylvania, Romania

보고서: `report_ro_brasov_45.6462_25.5847_260829_094617_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[u845w]]

Brașov is Transylvania's leading city, a former Saxon fortified trading town now serving as a major economic, industrial and tourism hub at the foot of the Carpathians. Its medieval Old Town, Gothic Black Church, and Council Square draw huge numbers of visitors, while its diversified manufacturing, automotive, aeronautics, pharma and IT sectors support a strong regional economy. Home to roughly 238,000 people (metro ~372,000), it blends walkable historic charm, mountain recreation, and a multicultural Saxon-Hungarian-Romanian heritage, though it faces the demographic pressures common to mid-sized Romanian cities.
