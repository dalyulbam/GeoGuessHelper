# Potomje / Sreser (Dingač wine district), Pelješac Peninsula, Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_dingac-peljesac_42.9469_17.4434_260813_212246_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srskk]]

Potomje and Sreser are tiny, sparsely populated inland villages on the Pelješac Peninsula that form the historic heart of Croatia's most prestigious wine district, Dingač, alongside neighboring Postup. Life here revolves almost entirely around small-scale, family- and cooperative-based viticulture of the indigenous Plavac Mali grape, grown on extremely steep, sun-drenched slopes and legally protected since 1961. The 1975 Dingač Tunnel, a locally financed feat of self-organization, transformed the isolated district by linking the coastal vineyards to Potomje, spurring both the modern wine industry and a low-key wine-tourism trade centered on cellar visits like Matuško's 50,000-visitor operation.
