# Tirza / Lizums (Pieti area), Vidzeme (Gulbene municipality, Tirza parish), Latvia

보고서: `report_lv_lizums_57.166_26.3655_260812_085333_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[ud4tp]]

Tirza/Lizums is a sparsely populated rural stretch of Vidzeme in Gulbene municipality, defined by small farmsteads, forestry, and a remarkably dense heritage of Latvian writers, artists, and clergy tied to Tirza's historic Lutheran church and parish school. Its economy is modest and agrarian, anchored by the old Tirza-Velēna Society House as a civic hub, while nearby Lizums serves as the area's small industrial center with wood-processing, forestry, and food-processing firms. Tourism here is low-key, built around manor houses, literary memorials, the Tirza River's canoeing and fishing, and quiet countryside trails rather than mass attractions.
