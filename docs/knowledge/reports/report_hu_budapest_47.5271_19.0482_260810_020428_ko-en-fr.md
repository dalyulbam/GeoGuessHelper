# Budapest, Budapest, Hungary

보고서: `report_hu_budapest_47.5271_19.0482_260810_020428_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[u2mw3]]

Budapest is the capital and largest city of Hungary, straddling the Danube River, which divides the hilly, historic Buda side from the flat, commercial Pest side. Home to roughly 1.7 million people (about 3 million in the metro area), it is Hungary's political, economic, and cultural heart and one of Central Europe's most visited cities. It is renowned for its thermal spa culture, grand 19th-century architecture, and UNESCO-listed riverside cityscape.
