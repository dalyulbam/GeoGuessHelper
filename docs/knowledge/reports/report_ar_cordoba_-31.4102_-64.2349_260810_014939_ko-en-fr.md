# Cordoba, Cordoba Province, Argentina

보고서: `report_ar_cordoba_-31.4102_-64.2349_260810_014939_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[6d6m6]]

Córdoba is the capital of Córdoba Province and the second-largest city in Argentina, sitting in the country's central heartland in the foothills of the Sierras Chicas and crossed by the Suquía River. It is a major industrial, educational and technological hub with a metropolitan population of over 1.5 million. Founded in 1573, it is famous for its colonial Jesuit heritage, its historic university, and a large, energetic student population.
