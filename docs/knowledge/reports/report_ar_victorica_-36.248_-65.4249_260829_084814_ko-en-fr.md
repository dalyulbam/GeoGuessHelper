# Victorica, La Pampa Province, Argentina

보고서: `report_ar_victorica_-36.248_-65.4249_260829_084814_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[6993z]]

Victorica is a historic small town in northern La Pampa Province, Argentina, founded in 1882 as the province's first settlement on the site of the former Fortín Resina near Leubucó, the old seat of Ranquel cacique power. With about 6,284 residents, its economy and identity are built almost entirely on extensive cattle ranching in the surrounding caldén woodland, anchored by regular livestock auctions, an agrotechnical school, and the annual Fiesta de la Ganadería. The town markets itself as "where La Pampa was born," blending frontier/Ranquel-criollo heritage, rural tourism, and gaucho culture into its civic identity.
