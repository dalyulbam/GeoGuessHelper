# Victorica, La Pampa Province, Argentina

This is Victorica, a small historic town in northern La Pampa Province, Argentina, positioned at the entrance via Provincial Route 10 near the intersection with RP 105. The location is identifiable by the town's monumental gateway arch reading "VICTORICA - CAPITAL DE LA GANADERIA", green provincial route shields, Spanish-language signage, right-hand traffic, and a natural caldén savanna landscape with scattered flat-crowned Prosopis caldenia trees over dry tussock grass — all marking the semi-arid rangeland core of central Argentina.
