# Rethel, Ardennes, Grand Est, France

보고서: `report_fr_rethel_49.5097_4.3525_260916_001702_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[u0g1e]]

Rethel is a small sub-prefecture town of around 7,400–7,700 residents in the Ardennes department, Grand Est, serving as the market and light-industrial hub for the surrounding cereal-farming "Rethélois" plain. Its identity is anchored by the boudin blanc de Rethel, an IGP-protected culinary specialty celebrated in a centuries-old annual fair, alongside a modest agri-food, construction and retail-based economy. The town blends a semi-rural quality of life with wartime-scarred but restored heritage, such as the Église Saint-Nicolas, and cultural amenities like a museum, theatre, and aquatic center.
