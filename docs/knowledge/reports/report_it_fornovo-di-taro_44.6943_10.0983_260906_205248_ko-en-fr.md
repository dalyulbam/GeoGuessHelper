# Fornovo di Taro, Emilia-Romagna (Province of Parma), Italy

보고서: `report_it_fornovo-di-taro_44.6943_10.0983_260906_205248_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[spznm]]

Fornovo di Taro is a small town of around 6,000 residents in the Parma Apennine foothills of Emilia-Romagna, historically famous as the site of the 1495 Battle of Fornovo and as a medieval waypoint on the Via Francigena pilgrim route. Its economy blends small-scale food-technology and machinery manufacturing, agriculture, and services, with a notable historical footnote as an early Italian oil-extraction center at nearby Vallezza. Today it functions as a quiet rural service town and a springboard for visiting nearby natural and cultural attractions like Monte Prinzera and the Taro River Park, rather than as a major tourist destination in its own right.
