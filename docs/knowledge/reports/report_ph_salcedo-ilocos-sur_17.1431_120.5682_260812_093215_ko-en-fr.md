# Salcedo (Barangay Balidbid / Bago), Ilocos Sur, Ilocos Region (Region I), Ilocos Sur, Philippines

보고서: `report_ph_salcedo-ilocos-sur_17.1431_120.5682_260812_093215_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[wej9x]]

Salcedo is a small, landlocked 4th-class municipality in Ilocos Sur, home to about 11,110 residents (2020 census) spread across 21 barangays including Balidbid/Bago, the town's most populous barangay. Its economy and identity are rooted in smallholder agriculture—rice, corn, and tobacco—along with a growing rambutan industry celebrated through the annual Banat Kadiwa cum Rambutan Harvest Fair. Once known as Baugen and renamed after conquistador Juan de Salcedo in 1957, the town also carries cultural depth as part of the historic territory of the indigenous Bago highland people, and offers niche natural attractions like Baluarte (Agila) Falls and nearby ecotourism parks.
