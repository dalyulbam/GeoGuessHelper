# Monktonhall / Musselburgh, East Lothian, Scotland, United Kingdom

보고서: `report_gb_monktonhall-musselburgh_55.9297_-3.0567_260829_092228_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr

Monktonhall/Musselburgh in East Lothian is a former mining and industrial community that has transitioned from heavy industry to a more diversified, service-oriented economy. Once home to the Monktonhall "super pit" — one of Scotland's last deep coal mines, closing in 1997/1998 — the area also had a strong 19th/20th-century manufacturing base in wire, net, and paper milling. Today it hosts a mix of education (Queen Margaret University), engineering, energy, and food/drink sectors, though pockets of deprivation persist alongside broader regional strengths in tourism and higher education.
