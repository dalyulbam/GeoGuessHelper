# Mokošica (Rijeka dubrovačka), Dubrovnik, Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_mokosica-dubrovnik_42.6739_18.0908_260813_230812_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsfg]]

Mokošica is a dual-character locality at the head of Rijeka dubrovačka within the City of Dubrovnik, split between the historic riverside village of "Old" Mokošica and the dense socialist-era residential extension of Nova Mokošica. Rather than an independent economic center, it functions primarily as a residential commuter suburb for people working in Dubrovnik's tourism, retail, and public-sector economy, while retaining traces of its past as a Ragusan noble summer-villa retreat with surviving ljetnikovci. Nova Mokošica is one of Croatia's most densely populated settlements, giving the combined locality outsized demographic weight within the Dubrovnik area, even as small-scale fishing, marinas, and villa architecture recall its maritime and aristocratic heritage.
