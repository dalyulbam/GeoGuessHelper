# Sucre, Chuquisaca Department, Bolivia

보고서: `report_bo_sucre_-19.0367_-65.2663_260906_203351_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[6s9ed]]

Sucre is Bolivia's constitutional capital and a UNESCO World Heritage colonial city set in the southern highlands, home to about 319,000 residents (2024 census) within a wider Chuquisaca Department of 600,000. Its economy leans on education-driven spending from a large student population, commerce and services for the surrounding agricultural valley, and a few key industrial employers like an oil refinery and cement plant. Known for its relaxed pace, temperate climate, historic university, and Quechua-influenced markets and festivals, Sucre trades economic and political weight (long ceded to La Paz) for status as Bolivia's judicial and symbolic capital.
