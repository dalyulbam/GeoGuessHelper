# Dubrovnik (Lapad / Babin Kuk, Gruž bay), Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_dubrovnik_42.6608_18.0776_260813_231805_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsfe]]

Dubrovnik's Lapad/Babin Kuk and Gruž bay areas form the residential and service backbone of a city whose historic Old Town is being hollowed out by tourism. While the city overall has around 41,000 residents on a slow downward trend, the Old Town's population has collapsed from 5,000 (1991) to about 1,500 today due to short-term rental conversions and cruise-ship overcrowding. Gruž remains a working residential/port district of about 15,000, while Lapad offers a quieter, greener, more affordable neighborhood feel, and Babin Kuk is a resort belt built around luxury and mid-range hotels.
