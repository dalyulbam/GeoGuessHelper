# Fukuyama (Teshiro-cho / Hikino area), Hiroshima Prefecture, Japan

보고서: `report_jp_fukuyama_34.4836_133.3951_260829_093734_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[wynu5]]

Teshiro-chō and Hikino are quiet, dense residential/light-commercial districts in central Fukuyama city, Hiroshima Prefecture, near JR Fukuyama Station. The area has no standalone tourist attractions of its own, functioning instead as an affordable, convenient urban base close to central Fukuyama's key sight, the historic Fukuyama Castle, and the broader Bingo region's food and Shinto shrine culture. Daily life is typical of a mid-sized provincial-city neighborhood, with a population mix of singles, young couples, and families drawn by affordable rents, schools, and manufacturing/retail jobs.
