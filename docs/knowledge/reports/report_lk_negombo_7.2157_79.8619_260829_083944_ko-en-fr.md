# Negombo (Katuwapitiya / Thimbirigaskatuwa area), Western Province, Gampaha District, Sri Lanka

보고서: `report_lk_negombo_7.2157_79.8619_260829_083944_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[tc2c1]]

Katuwapitiya and Thimbirigaskatuwa are dense, working-village fishing neighborhoods within Negombo, a historic coastal town in Sri Lanka's Western Province known as "Little Rome" for its strong Catholic identity. The local economy rests on three pillars — artisanal/small-scale fishing centered on the Lellama fish market, coir/coconut-fiber export processing, and a growing tourism sector built on beaches and colonial heritage. The community is predominantly Karava (Karaiyar) Catholic, preserving distinctive traditions such as the Negombo Fishermen's Tamil dialect and fishing rituals tied to the Catholic calendar.
