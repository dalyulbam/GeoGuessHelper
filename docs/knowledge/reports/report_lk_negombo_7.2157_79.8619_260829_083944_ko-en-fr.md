# Negombo (Katuwapitiya / Thimbirigaskatuwa area), Western Province, Gampaha District, Sri Lanka

장소 노트: [[tc2c1]]

This is an inland fishing neighbourhood in Negombo, Sri Lanka's historic "Little Rome," located at 7.2157°N, 79.8619°E in the Western Province's Gampaha District. The area is identifiable by its dense coconut-garden subdivisions, Catholic institutional naming, Sinhala signage, and red-tile vernacular housing typical of the Catholic Karava fishing communities that define the southwestern coastal belt.
