# Garðabær, Capital Region (Garðabær / Kópavogur), Iceland

보고서: `report_isl_gardabaer_64.0945_-21.904_260810_020042_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr

Garðabær is an affluent suburban municipality in Iceland's Capital Region, on the Álftanes peninsula just south of Reykjavík and between Kópavogur and Hafnarfjörður. With roughly 18,000 residents, it is one of Iceland's wealthiest and fastest-growing municipalities, known for spacious residential districts, low property taxes, and hosting Bessastaðir, the official residence of the President of Iceland. It blends modern suburban living with lava-field nature reserves and coastal scenery.
