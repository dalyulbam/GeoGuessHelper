# Dubrovnik (Gruž / Port area), Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_dubrovnik_42.661_18.0862_260813_232255_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsfe]]

Gruž is Dubrovnik's working port and transport-hub district, about 2 km northwest of the Old Town, serving as the city's ferry, cruise, and bus gateway with a resident population of roughly 15,000. Unlike the tourist-dominated Old Town, it remains a genuinely local, mixed residential-commercial neighborhood built around its harbor, market, and terminal. Its economy is anchored in maritime passenger transport and ancillary tourism services, within a wider Dubrovnik-Neretva County economy overwhelmingly driven by tourism.
