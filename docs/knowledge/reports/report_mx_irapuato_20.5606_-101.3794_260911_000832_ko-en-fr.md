# Colonia El Palomar / Yóstiro (near Irapuato), Guanajuato, Mexico

보고서: `report_mx_irapuato_20.5606_-101.3794_260911_000832_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[9exuv]]

Colonia El Palomar/Yóstiro is a cluster of small rural hamlets (each roughly 150–1,300 residents) on the agricultural fringe of Irapuato, Guanajuato — a municipality famed as Mexico's "World Strawberry Capital." Life here revolves around rainfed/irrigated smallholder farming and day labor in the strawberry and vegetable agroexport chain, with many residents also commuting into Irapuato's growing automotive and industrial-park sector. It is a typical mestizo Bajío peasant community with self-built housing, unpaved streets, and modest connectivity.
