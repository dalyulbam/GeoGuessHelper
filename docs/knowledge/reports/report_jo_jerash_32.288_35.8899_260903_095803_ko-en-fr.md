# Jerash (Wadi Al-Deir / Al-Rayyan area, west of city centre), Jerash Governorate, Jordan

보고서: `report_jo_jerash_32.288_35.8899_260903_095803_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[sv9x8]]

Wadi Al-Deir/Al-Rayyan is a small, semi-rural hillside district on the western fringe of Jerash city in northern Jordan, characterized by terraced olive and fruit farming in forested wadi valleys rather than urban development. It functions as a residential-agricultural buffer zone supporting the nearby world-famous Greco-Roman ruins of Jerash, Jordan's second-most visited tourist site after Petra. Unlike the crowded, economically vulnerable Gaza refugee camp on Jerash's eastern side, this area retains a traditional, low-density agrarian village character.
