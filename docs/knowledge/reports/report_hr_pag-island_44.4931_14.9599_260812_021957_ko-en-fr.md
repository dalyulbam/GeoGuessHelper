# Kolan / Novalja area, Pag Island, Zadar County (island of Pag), Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_pag-island_44.4931_14.9599_260812_021957_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srfth]]

The Kolan/Novalja area on Pag Island combines a traditional pastoral and artisanal economy with one of Croatia's most famous party-tourism destinations. Novalja and its Zrce Beach have become an internationally known "Croatian Ibiza," while inland Kolan anchors the island's renowned Pag cheese and lamb production, notably through the Gligora dairy. Together with the salt works of Solana Pag and UNESCO-listed Pag lace, the area blends heritage food crafts with a booming, youth-driven nightlife economy, creating a sharply seasonal income structure.
