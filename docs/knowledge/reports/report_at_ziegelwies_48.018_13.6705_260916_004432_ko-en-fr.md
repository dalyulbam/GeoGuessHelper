# Ziegelwies (Wolfsegg am Hausruck / Ottnang area), Upper Austria (Hausruckviertel), Austria

보고서: `report_at_ziegelwies_48.018_13.6705_260916_004432_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[u299p]]

Ziegelwies is a tiny hamlet within Wolfsegg am Hausruck (with neighboring Ottnang am Hausruck) in the Hausruckviertel of Upper Austria, a rural area of under 2,000 to 4,000 residents whose identity was forged by roughly 200 years of lignite (brown coal) mining before the industry's closure in the early 2000s. Today the local economy runs on small crafts, agriculture, forestry, and commuting to district towns like Vöcklabruck and Wels, while tourism draws on both a "Luftkurort" Alpine-panorama hiking image and post-industrial coal-heritage attractions such as the Südfeldstollen show mine and Grubengeistweg trail. The area retains a homogeneous, aging, tradition-bound population with deep roots in mining and farming customs, alongside historic parish churches and noble heritage tied to Schloss Wolfsegg.
