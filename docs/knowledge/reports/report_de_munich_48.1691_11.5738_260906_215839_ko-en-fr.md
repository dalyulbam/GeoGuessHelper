# Munich, Bavaria (Schwabing-West, Belgradstrasse / Herzogstrasse), Germany

보고서: `report_de_munich_48.1691_11.5738_260906_215839_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[u284p]]

Schwabing-West around Belgradstraße/Herzogstraße is an affluent, densely built inner-Munich quarter famed for its protected Jugendstil and Wilhelmine-era architecture and its early-20th-century legacy as a bohemian artists' colony (Kandinsky, Münter, Thomas Mann). Street life is quiet and residential, dominated by small bakeries, cafés, boutiques and delis, while the surrounding Schwabing district and nearby Parkstadt Schwabing anchor it to Munich's high-income, tech- and R&D-driven economy (Microsoft German HQ, IBM Watson, Amazon, ARRI, GE Healthcare). Today it draws students, young professionals and heritage tourists alongside long-term wealthy residents, having shifted from bohemian roots toward upscale gentrification.
