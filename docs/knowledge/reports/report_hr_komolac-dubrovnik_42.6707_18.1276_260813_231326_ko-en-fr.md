# Komolac / Rožat (Rijeka Dubrovačka, Dubrovnik), Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_komolac-dubrovnik_42.6707_18.1276_260813_231326_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsfs]]

Komolac and Rožat are two small settlements on the sheltered, fjord-like Rijeka Dubrovačka inlet near Dubrovnik, together home to fewer than 750 permanent residents. Once centered on fishing, olive/vine farming, and boatbuilding, the area has shifted toward nautical tourism, marinas, and yacht-charter services thanks to its calm deep-water inlet. Increasingly it functions as a quiet, semi-rural commuter suburb of Dubrovnik, attracting residents priced out of the historic core, while retaining churches, historic summer villas, and boatyards.
