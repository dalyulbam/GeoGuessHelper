# Morty / Tatarsky Dyum-Dyum junction, Yelabuga district, Republic of Tatarstan, Russia

보고서: `report_ru_naberezhnye-chelny_55.7391_51.7371_260829_084508_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[v1uv1]]

This is a roadside fuel and rest-stop cluster at the junction of two small Tatar agricultural villages on the M7 federal highway between Kazan and Naberezhnye Chelny in Russia's Republic of Tatarstan. The location is pinpointed by a distinctive triple fuel-station arrangement (Lukoil, Gazpromneft/Opti, and Tatneft) beside the natural spring known as Rodnik Gremyashchiy, along a modern 4-lane divided section of the Volga Highway in the Yelabuga district — a corridor of heavy freight traffic serving the KAMAZ truck factory and the Alabuga Special Economic Zone.
