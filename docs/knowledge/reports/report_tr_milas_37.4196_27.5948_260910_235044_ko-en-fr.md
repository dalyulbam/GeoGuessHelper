# Etrenli / Danışment (Milas district), Muğla Province (Milas district), Aegean Region, Turkey

보고서: `report_tr_milas_37.4196_27.5948_260910_235044_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[swesg]]

Etrenli and Danışment are small, quiet rural mahalles (former villages) in the inland hills of Milas district, Muğla Province, tracing their identity to Yörük and Turkmen nomadic-pastoral heritage and, in Danışment's case, to the medieval Danişment Beylik. Life revolves around subsistence farming, animal husbandry, and Milas's celebrated olive-oil economy, with small, slowly shrinking populations (Danışment around 265–282 residents). They have no independent tourism or economic profile of their own but sit near the Milas–Söke road, gateway to the Lake Bafa–Latmos (Beşparmak Mountains) region and the Carian Trail, and are part of a new municipal effort to document and promote rural heritage tourism.
