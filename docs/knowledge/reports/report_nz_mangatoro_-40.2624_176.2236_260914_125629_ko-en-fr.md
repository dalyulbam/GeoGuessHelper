# Mangatoro / Weber area, near Dannevirke, Manawatū-Whanganui / Tararua District (eastern Hawke's Bay border), New Zealand

보고서: `report_nz_mangatoro_-40.2624_176.2236_260914_125629_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[rbv6f]]

Mangatoro/Weber is a sparsely populated inland farming district on the eastern edge of Tararua District, near the Hawke's Bay border, centred on the tiny historic settlement of Weber (population 135). Originally home to Rangitāne and Ngāti Kahungunu, the area was reshaped by early-1900s Crown land purchases and later by Scandinavian settlers who founded nearby Dannevirke; Weber itself grew from a coach-stop into a small farming community that has long since faded from its peak. Today it is defined by pastoral farming (sheep, beef, some dairy), tight-knit rural traditions, and a single notable natural attraction, Waihi Falls.
