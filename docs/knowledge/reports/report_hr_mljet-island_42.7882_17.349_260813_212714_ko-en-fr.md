# Pomena / Polače (Mljet National Park), Dubrovnik-Neretva County (Mljet Island), Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_mljet-island_42.7882_17.349_260813_212714_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srs76]]

Pomena and Polače are the two gateway ports of Mljet National Park, on the island of Mljet in Dubrovnik-Neretva County, centered on the saltwater lakes Veliko and Malo Jezero and the medieval Benedictine monastery on Sveti Marija islet. The economy is dominated by seasonal nature and heritage tourism, supplemented by small-scale fishing, olive growing, and viticulture. Polače also preserves substantial Roman-era ruins, while Pomena's Hotel Odisej is the island's only hotel and its largest employer, reflecting the area's identity as a low-key, nature- and history-driven Mediterranean destination.
