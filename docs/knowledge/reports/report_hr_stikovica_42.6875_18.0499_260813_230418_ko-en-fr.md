# Štikovica (Zaton area, Dubrovnik), Dubrovnik-Neretva County, Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_stikovica_42.6875_18.0499_260813_230418_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srsff]]

Štikovica is a small coastal hamlet in the Zaton bay area, about 8 km from Dubrovnik, whose economy and identity are built almost entirely around seasonal tourism. Once a modest fishing settlement, its stone houses and villas have been largely converted into holiday rentals, with restaurants, konoba taverns, and boat/kayak excursions serving summer visitors drawn to its family-friendly pebble beach. The area retains echoes of Ragusan-era villa heritage and small-scale fishing and olive cultivation, but these are now secondary to hospitality. The settlement, like much of the Dubrovnik littoral, suffered destruction during the 1991–95 war and today has only a small, aging year-round population within the broader shrinking municipality of Dubrovnik.
