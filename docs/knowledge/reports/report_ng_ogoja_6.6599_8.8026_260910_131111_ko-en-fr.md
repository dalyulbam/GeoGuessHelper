# Ogoja, Cross River State, Nigeria

보고서: `report_ng_ogoja_6.6599_8.8026_260910_131111_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr · 적재 p2
장소 노트: [[s1nmb]]

Ogoja is a historic market town in northern Cross River State, Nigeria, serving as the traditional hub of the Ogoja Senatorial Zone since its establishment as a colonial province in 1903. Its economy is predominantly agrarian and informal, centered on subsistence farming, trade in staples like yams, cassava, rice, and palm produce, and timber haulage, with limited manufacturing. The town is culturally rich, home to diverse ethnic groups (Ekoi, Yala, Bekwarra, Obudu, Obanliku and others) who share traditions such as the New Yam Festival, masquerade performances, and communal festivals, making it a gateway for heritage- and nature-based tourism into the wider Cross River hinterland.
