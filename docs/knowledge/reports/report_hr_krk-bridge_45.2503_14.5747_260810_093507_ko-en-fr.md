# Krk Bridge / Omišalj, Krk Island, Primorje-Gorski Kotar County (Kvarner), Croatia

보고서: `report_hr_krk-bridge_45.2503_14.5747_260810_093507_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[u2437]]

Omišalj is a small historic town on the northwestern tip of Krk Island, sitting at the landward end of the iconic Krk Bridge and hosting Rijeka Airport and major energy infrastructure (JANAF oil terminal and Croatia's LNG terminal). With roots stretching back through Illyrian, Roman, and Frankopan-era Glagolitic heritage, it combines a "city-monument" old town with a modern role as Croatia's key energy-transit and transport hub. This blend gives Omišalj a more diversified, year-round economy than the purely tourism- and fishing-based neighboring towns on Krk.
