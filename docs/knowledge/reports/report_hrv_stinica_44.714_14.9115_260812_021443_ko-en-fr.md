# Stinica (Jablanac, near Ferry Stinica–Mišnjak), Lika-Senj County (Velebit Channel coast), Croatia

보고서: `report_hrv_stinica_44.714_14.9115_260812_021443_ko-en-fr.html` · 언어 ko, en, fr
장소 노트: [[srfw7]]

Stinica, alongside neighboring Jablanac, is a tiny hamlet on Croatia's Velebit Channel coast in Lika-Senj County, functioning almost entirely as the mainland ferry gateway to the island of Rab via the Stinica–Mišnjak crossing. Once a forestry and fishing settlement with roots tracing back to the Iron-Age Iapodes and Roman era, it has undergone dramatic depopulation since WWII, shrinking from over 1,300 residents in 1900 to just 61 by 2021. Today its economy rests on ferry traffic, small-scale artisanal fishing, and modest seasonal tourism anchored by the dramatic Zavratnica Cove and Velebit hiking trails.
