# Linear valley settlement patterns in mountain regions

`atm_743c7efc3d36` · **geography** / global

Settlement strung along narrow river valleys constrained by steep forested ridges is a recurring mountain-geography pattern seen in Austria's Alpine valleys (e.g. the Ötztal), Romania's Carpathian valley towns, and Appalachian 'holler' settlements in West Virginia, where flat buildable land is scarce and habitation follows watercourses rather than spreading laterally.
