# Dynasties of Belgium and the Southern Netherlands

`atm_0dc24f1129c2` · **history** / country

Medieval feudal states of the Southern Netherlands (County of Flanders, Marquisate of Namur, Duchy of Brabant, County of Hainaut, Duchy of Limburg, County of Luxembourg) were ruled by the House of Flanders (863-1280), House of Dampierre (1247-1405), House of Reginar (c.770-1406), and House of Burgundy (1384-1482). The Kingdom of Belgium has been ruled by the House of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha (1831-1920), continuing as the extant House of Belgium (1920-present).
