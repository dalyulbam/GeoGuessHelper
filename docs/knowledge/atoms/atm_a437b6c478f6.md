# Post-1989 deindustrialization and monotown decline

`atm_a437b6c478f6` · **economy** / global

**시기**: 1989 ~ 2010

After the fall of communism, many single-industry towns across Central and Eastern Europe suffered mass unemployment when state factories privatized, downsized, or closed, a pattern known as post-socialist deindustrialization. Towns like Ostrava (Czech Republic) and Nowa Huta (Poland) faced decades-long economic restructuring, population decline, and diversification efforts away from the original industry.
