# Hardwood-over-masonry Isan houses vs stilted Malay kampung houses

`atm_a1b98b03e41a` · **architecture** / region · cell `w71dkzn`

In Isan villages the standard house is a two-storey block with an unpainted hardwood upper storey sitting on an enclosed brick/masonry ground floor under corrugated metal, often with a large C-band dish. In Malay/southern kampung areas you would instead see timber boxes fully raised on visible piloti with open underfloor space and mosque domes nearby.
