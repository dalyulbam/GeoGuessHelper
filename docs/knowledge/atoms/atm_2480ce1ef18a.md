# National toponymic suffix systems as script/language identifiers

`atm_2480ce1ef18a` · **language** / global

Just as Turkish street-name suffixes (Sokak, Caddesi, Bulvarı) mark Turkish-language maps, other countries have distinctive toponymic markers: German 'Straße/Platz', French 'Rue/Avenue/Boulevard', Spanish 'Calle/Avenida', and Russian 'Ulitsa/Prospekt'. These lexical markers let map-readers quickly identify a country's dominant language even without translation.
