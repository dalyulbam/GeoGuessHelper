# Single-resort island economies worldwide

`atm_21a020846dcd` · **economy** / global

Small islands within protected areas or with limited infrastructure often depend on one dominant hospitality operator: examples include Ko Tao's dive resorts in Thailand, the Maldives' one-resort-one-island model (each atoll islet leased to a single operator), and Scotland's Isle of Eigg with its handful of small guesthouses. These economies share extreme seasonality, high exposure to a single employer's fortunes, and vulnerability to external shocks like storms or pandemics.
