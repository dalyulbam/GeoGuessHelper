# Takaoka copperware and lacquerware crafts

`atm_cc6189746731` · **culture** / city · cell `xn92tyx`

**시기**: 1609 ~ ?

Takaoka is Japan's leading center of cast copper and bronze ware (Takaoka doki), a nationally designated traditional craft producing Buddhist altar fittings, temple bells, tea utensils, and decorative bronze. It grew from Edo-period castle-town patronage of metal casters, preserved in the historic Kanaya-machi casters' quarter. Takaoka lacquerware (Takaoka shikki) is a second designated craft; both face declining domestic demand and successor shortages.
