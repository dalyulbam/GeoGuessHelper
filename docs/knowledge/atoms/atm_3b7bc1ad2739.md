# Secessionist states that failed or were reincorporated

`atm_3b7bc1ad2739` · **history** / global

States that declared independence but were reincorporated or failed to achieve permanent independence: Carpatho-Ukraine (1939, annexed by Hungary), Cartagena Canton (seceded from First Spanish Republic 1873), Catalan Republic (April 14-17, 1931), Chechen Republic of Ichkeria (independent from Russia from 1996, recognized only by the Taliban, retaken in Second Chechen War after 1999), Confederate States of America (1861-1865/1877), Crimean Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic (declared independence from Ukraine 1992, settled for autonomy), Cruzob (independent from Mexico 1856-1901), Green Ukraine (declared independence from Far Eastern Republic 1920, dissolved 1922), Croatian Republic of Herzeg-Bosnia (separated from Bosnia and Herzegovina 1992, reincorporated 1994), Italian Social Republic (1943-1945), Katanga (seceded from Democratic Republic of the Congo 1960, reincorporated 1963), Republic of Serbian Krajina (seceded from Croatia 1991, reincorporated 1995), South Kasai (seceded from DR Congo June 1960, reincorporated December 1961), Supreme Administration of Northern Region (proclaimed 1918, became Provisional Government of the Northern Region), Republika Srpska (1992-1995, separated from Bosnia and Herzegovina, reincorporated 1995), Principality of Trinidad (declared independence 1893, claimed by UK 1895, incorporated by Brazil), Autonomous Province of Western Bosnia (1993-1995), Novorossiya (2015 confederation of Donetsk People's Republic and Luhansk People's Republic, suspended 2015; both declared independence in 2014 and were purportedly annexed by Russia in 2022, not internationally recognised), and Republic of Artsakh (declared independence from Azerbaijan 1991, reincorporated into Azerbaijan 2024).
