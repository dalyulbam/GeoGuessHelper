# Genkai shuraku: Japan's 'marginal settlements' at risk of extinction

`atm_3f818b5a1b64` · **economy** / region · cell `xn9k2te`

**시기**: 1990 ~ ?

A 'genkai shuraku' (limit settlement/marginal hamlet) is a Japanese demographic classification for a rural community where over half the population is aged 65 or older, making collective functions (festivals, road maintenance, farming cooperatives) unsustainable and risking eventual abandonment. This concept, developed in depopulation studies since the 1990s, frames debates like the Masuda Report and is commonly applied to upland hamlets in regions such as the Noto Peninsula, Shikoku mountains, and Tohoku.
