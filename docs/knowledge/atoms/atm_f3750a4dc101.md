# Ancient Chinese peripheral polities: Beidi, Dongyi, Xirong tribes

`atm_f3750a4dc101` · **history** / region

Ancient tribal polities on the periphery of Huaxia China with unknown dates, ruled by various clan houses. These include Zhoucheng (House of Ji, Huaxia), Baidi (House of Ji, Beidi), Chidi, Changdi (House of Qi, Beidi) with sub-group Souman, Jiuli (House of Jiang, Huaxia), Sanmiao (House of Ji, Huaxia), and the Yi peoples: Yuyi, Fangyi, Huangyi, Baiyi, Chiyi, Xuanyi, Fengyi, Yangyi. Also Rong groups: Maorong, Shenrong, Rong of Yun clan (House of Yun, Xirong) with sub-groups Luhun, Yinrong, Rong of Yiluo, Rong of Jiuzhou, plus Rong of Shihu and Rong of Yitu.
