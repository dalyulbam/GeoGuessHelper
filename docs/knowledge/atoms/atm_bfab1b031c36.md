# Southern Qi dynastic principalities (479-502)

`atm_bfab1b031c36` · **history** / region

Under the Southern Qi dynasty (479-502) of China, agnatic princely fiefs of the House of Xiao (Han descent) included Nan, Guiyang, Shi'an, Yuzhang, Linchuan, Changsha, Wuling, Ancheng, Hengyang, Poyang, Guangxing, Jiangxia, Anlu, Jingling, Sui, Jian'an, Luling, Jin'an, Wuchang, Yidu, Nanping, Nanhai, Baling, Xiyang, Hedong, Jinxi, Shaoling, Badong, Shu, Guanghan, Yongyang, Linhe, Xuancheng, Yi'an, Nankang, Xiangdong, Xin'an, Linhai, Yongjia, Hailing, Shixing, and Liang, all dated within 479-502. Non-agnatic principalities included Lingling (House of Sima, Han descent) and Ruyin (House of Liu, Han descent).
