# Technological/modernity period system

`atm_16c8a39ca9c2` · **history** / global

Technological periodization groups Prehistory (Paleolithic, Mesolithic/Epipaleolithic, Neolithic, Chalcolithic), Ancient history (Bronze Age, Iron Age), Late Middle Ages/Renaissance, Early modern history, and Modern history, the last subdivided into Industrial Age (1760-1970), Machine Age (1880-1945) with Age of Oil (1901-present) and Jet Age (1940s), Nuclear/Atomic Age (1945/1950-present), Space Age (1957-present), and Information Age (1970-present) with Internet Age (1990-present). Related human-form categories include hominids archaeologically/anatomically similar to modern humans (HAASMHs), anatomically modern humans (AMHs), and technologically modern humans (TMHs).
