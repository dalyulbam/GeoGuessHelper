# Bohemian Massif granite plateau vs molasse foreland hill country

`atm_1f233e16a5a5` · **geography** / region · cell `u299pj6`

The Bohemian Massif (including Austria's Mühlviertel north of the Danube) is characterized by granite bedrock producing exposed tors, boulder-strewn field edges, and drystone walls. South of the Danube, the Alpine foreland (e.g. Hausruckviertel, Innviertel) sits on younger molasse (sedimentary basin fill) producing rounded, loamy hills without granite outcrops. This geological contrast is a usable discriminator between the two zones in Upper Austria.
