# River renaturalization movement countering rigid channelization

`atm_a80e8c9540ff` · **nature** / global

Since the 1990s, a river renaturalization movement has pushed back against rigid 19th-20th century channelization, advocating restoration of meanders and floodplains for ecological and flood-buffering benefits, as seen in the Isar River renaturalization in Munich and Rhine floodplain restoration in the Netherlands, contrasting with Vienna's engineered triple-channel approach.
