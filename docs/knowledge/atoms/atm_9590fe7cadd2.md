# Global pattern of shrinking industrial regional cities

`atm_9590fe7cadd2` · **economy** / global

Regional Japanese cities like Takaoka parallel a worldwide phenomenon of aging, shrinking secondary industrial cities losing population to capital regions: Germany's Ruhr Valley towns, Italy's inland Appennine comuni, and South Korea's provincial cities (e.g. around Busan's hinterland) all face youth outmigration, legacy manufacturing decline, and elderly-skewed demographics. National governments in Japan, Germany, and Italy have all launched revitalization subsidy programs targeting these 'shrinking cities'.
