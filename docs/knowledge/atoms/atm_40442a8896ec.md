# Global temperate grain-belt plains

`atm_40442a8896ec` · **geography** / global

The Danubian Plain belongs to a worldwide family of temperate loess/alluvial grain belts: the North European Plain (Poland, Ukraine's chernozem belt), the North American Great Plains and Corn Belt, Argentina's Pampas, and Ukraine's wheat-growing steppe. These regions share flat-to-rolling terrain, deep fertile soils, large contiguous field patterns, and dominance of wheat, maize, and sunflower cultivation.
