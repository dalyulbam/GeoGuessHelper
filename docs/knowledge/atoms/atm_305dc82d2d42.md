# U.S. Steel Košice — Central Europe's largest integrated steelworks

`atm_305dc82d2d42` · **economy** / polity · cell `u2xj588`

U.S. Steel Košice (formerly VSŽ Košice, a Czechoslovak-era steel combine acquired by U.S. Steel in the late 1990s) is Central Europe's largest integrated steel producer, with roughly 4.5 million tonnes/year crude steel capacity and full-cycle ironmaking, steelmaking, rolling, and galvanizing operations focused on flat-rolled steel for automotive, appliance, and construction markets. It is one of Slovakia's largest private employers and has anchored Košice/eastern Slovakia's industrial identity for over 40 years.
