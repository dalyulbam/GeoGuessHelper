# Slovak vs Czech orthographic discriminators (LEKÁREŇ vs lékárna)

`atm_7f1b78158b4f` · **language** / polity · cell `u2v27rh`

Slovak and Czech, though closely related West Slavic languages, are distinguishable on signage: Slovak uses ä, ô, ŕ, ľ, ň and words like 'LEKÁREŇ' (pharmacy) and 'PENZIÓN', while Czech uses ě, ř and corresponding forms like 'lékárna' and 'penzion'. This spelling contrast is a reliable visual discriminator between Slovakia and Czechia in geolocation tasks.
