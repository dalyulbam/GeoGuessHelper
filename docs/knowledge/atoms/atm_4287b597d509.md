# Czech (Bohemian) dynasties

`atm_4287b597d509` · **history** / country

Great Moravia was ruled by the Moymirid dynasty (c.830-906?). The Kingdom of Bohemia was ruled successively by the Přemyslid dynasty (c.870-1198, with interruptions, to 1306), House of Gorizia (1306, 1307-1310), House of Habsburg (1306-1307, 1437-1439, 1453-1457, 1526-1780), House of Luxembourg (1310-1437), House of Poděbrady (1457-1471), and House of Jagiellon (1471-1526).
