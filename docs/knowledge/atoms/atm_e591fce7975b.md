# Parronal table-grape cultivation in Chilean desert valleys

`atm_e591fce7975b` · **economy** / region · cell `66v8ckk`

In Chile's arid Norte Chico oases, table grapes are grown as 'parronales' — vines trained on overhead wire-and-post trellis frames, sometimes with shade netting, irrigated by drip lines rather than flood canals. This export-oriented smallholder viticulture occupies narrow alluvial valley floors and contrasts with pure mining camps (container housing, waste-rock dumps, haul roads) or livestock estancias (open grazing, corrals) that might otherwise occupy similar desert terrain.
