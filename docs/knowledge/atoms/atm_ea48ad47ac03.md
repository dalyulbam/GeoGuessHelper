# Yunnan chiefdoms established under early Ming (1372-1382)

`atm_ea48ad47ac03` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1372 ~ 1956

Includes Xundian Tuzhifu (1372-?), Chiefdom of Biligongwa (1373-?), and numerous chiefdoms established 1381-1382 across Yunnan/Sichuan such as Chiefdom of Baishuiguan, Anning Prefecture, Nan'an Prefecture, Lubiao, Axidongcu, Baimalucu, Beidingcu, Lazacu, Maizacu, Zheduocu, Moulijiecu, Qimingcu, Leducu, Baozangcu, Jinshajiang, Lijiang Tuzhifu (1382-1723, House of Mu, Nakhi descent), Situodian (1382-1912), Lujiang (1382-1956, House of Nang, Dai descent), Mengding (1382-?, House of Dao and House of Han, Dai descent), Yangsiduo, Shaqiaoyi (House of Yang, Bai descent), Lesser Pingfa, Chengjiang Prefecture (House of Dao, Dai descent), Lunan Prefecture (House of Qin, Yi descent), Guangnan Prefecture (House of Nong, Zhuang descent), Luoxiong Prefecture (House of Zhe, Yi descent), Qujing Prefecture (House of Yang, Bai descent), Guanjianshan (House of Wang, Yi descent), Xuanhuaguan (House of Luo, Yi descent), Mishajing (House of Sha), and Guanyinshanyi (House of Guo, Yi descent).
