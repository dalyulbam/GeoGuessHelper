# Squamish, BC: demographic profile of a Vancouver-Whistler gateway town

Squamish, BC had an estimated 2024 population of ~23,819 (+5.6% YoY growth from 19,497 in 2021), with a young median age of 37.9 versus the Canadian national average of 41.9, and 18.7% of residents under 14. It is marketed as 'Canada's Outdoor Recreation Capital' due to world-class rock climbing, mountain biking, hiking, and windsurfing, and functions as a lifestyle-migrant and Vancouver-commuter destination between Vancouver and Whistler.
