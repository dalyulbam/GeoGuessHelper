# Polish small-town (powiat seat) socioeconomic profile: below-average wages, aging, self-employment

`atm_07287096cac3` · **economy** / region · cell `u36t47h`

**시기**: 2017 ~ 2024

Small Polish powiat (county) seat towns of ~10,000-15,000 residents typically show: average wages around 80-85% of the national average, steady population decline (often >10% over two decades) and aging (mean age mid-40s, high dependency ratios), an economy dominated by small manufacturing (metalworking, food processing, furniture) and a large share of self-employed/sole-proprietor businesses registered in REGON, and commuting linkages to nearby larger towns for jobs. This is a common structural pattern across rural West Pomeranian and Lubuskie county towns.
