# Mediterranean self-built holiday-rental vernacular family

`atm_ff812a421048` · **architecture** / global

Pragmatic, self-built concrete-block holiday housing with cheap tile or fibre-cement roofing appears across Mediterranean and Black Sea coastlines wherever mass domestic tourism outpaced formal planning: Spain's Costa Brava 'chalet' sprawl of the 1960s-70s, Bulgaria's Black Sea coast apartment blocks (Sunny Beach hinterland), and Greece's unlicensed 'αυθαίρετα' (arbitrary/illegal construction) zones near beach resorts. All share informal incremental building, family-apartment-rental economics, and contrast with protected historic old towns.
