# Ochre eroded basalt vs black picón: Fuerteventura vs Lanzarote

`atm_2259a51ab69b` · **nature** / region · cell `esvzdqh`

On Fuerteventura the slopes above the resort coast are rounded, ochre-to-brown, deeply gullied Miocene basalt with reddish scree and thin cushion scrub. In Lanzarote you would instead see near-black young lava, malpaís badlands and picón (black lapilli) mulch fields around vines and vegetables. The absence of any fresh black flow is enough to move the guess east-to-south Fuerteventura.
