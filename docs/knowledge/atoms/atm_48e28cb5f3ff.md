# Takaoka heritage sites and Doraemon connection

Takaoka's landmarks include the Great Buddha of Takaoka (a notable daibutsu cast in local bronze) and Zuiryu-ji, an Edo-period Zen temple designated a National Treasure. The city preserves the Kanaya-machi copper-casting district and Yamachosuji merchant street, and holds the ornate Mikuruma-yama float festival. Manga artist Fujiko F. Fujio, co-creator of Doraemon, was born in Takaoka, celebrated with a themed tram and attractions.
