# Ming-era Chinese tusi chiefdoms (15th century)

`atm_ea0ec7052315` · **history** / region

Numerous native chieftain (tusi) offices established under the Ming dynasty across southwest China and Tibetan borderlands, mostly hereditary local rulerships. These include Chiefdom of Dimasa (1406-?), Chiefdom of Jiugang (1407-?, House of Shi, Han), Chiefdom of Cuwa (1407-?), Chiefdom of Sanjin (1407-?), Chiefdom of Lima (1408-?), Chiefdom of Zhenkang (1408-1907, House of Daomen, Dai) with subordinate Chiefdom of Yongde, Chiefdom of Zhuoyou (1409-?), Chiefdom of Dongbuhanhu (1415-?), Chiefdom of Moshale (1416-1665, House of Pu, Yi), Chiefdom of Zhuoni (1418-1949, House of Yang, Tibetan), Chiefdom of Chelijing'an (1421-?, House of Dao), Chiefdom of Diwula (1424-?), Chiefdom of Hemocun (1424-?, House of Wang), Xincheng Aboriginal County (1427-1928), Yizuo County Tuxiancheng (1427-?, House of Sha, Yi), Chiefdom of Ma'erza (1427-?), Xi'e County Tuzhubu (1428-?, House of Wang), Yangli Aboriginal Prefecture (to 1431, House of Zhao), Chongshan Aboriginal County (to 1433, House of Zhao), Chiefdom of Songshaopu (1434-?, House of Li), Chiefdom of Asicu (1435-?), Dahou Prefecture Tuzhizhou (1438-?, House of Feng, Dai), Chiefdom of Guansuoling (1439-?, House of Li), Chiefdom of Mang'erzhe (1440-?), Chiefdom of Qujing (1441-?, House of Gong, Dai), Lijiang Tuzhaomo (1441-?, House of Mu, Nakhi), Chiefdom of Jinningyi (1442-?, House of Lu, Yi), Chiefdom of Mangshi (1443-1955, House of Fang, Dai), Chiefdom of Tieluguan (1443-?, House of Wang), Chiefdom of Longchuan (1444-?, House of Duo), Chiefdom of Sinangri (1446-?), Chiefdom of Dongshankou (1464-?, Houses of Pu and Zhang, Yi), Chiefdom of Nagengshan (1482-?, House of Long, Hani), Chiefdom of Möng Mit (1484-?, House of Si, Dai), Chiefdom of Yongding (1486-1927), Yongshun Zheng Tuzhangguansi (1492-1928, House of Deng), Yongshun Fu Tuzhangguansi (1492-1928, House of Peng), Luhtu (15th century-1920, House of Yulunana, Tsou), Zuozhou Aboriginal Prefecture (to 15th century, House of Huang), Chiefdom of Tangchi (15th century-?, House of Ma, Hui).
