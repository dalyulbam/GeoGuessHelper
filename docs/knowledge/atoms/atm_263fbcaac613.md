# Hierarchical national road classification systems worldwide

`atm_263fbcaac613` · **geography** / global

Many countries use numbered road hierarchies where number ranges or leading digits encode administrative region, similar to Bulgaria's third-class road system: France's route départementale numbering ties into department codes, and the UK's A/B road numbering zones radiate from single-digit sectors centered on London (zone 1 northeast, zone 6 southwest, etc.). Such systems let travelers infer location purely from route-marker numbering.
