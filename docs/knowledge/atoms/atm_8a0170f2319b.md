# Maritime city-state littoral hinterlands

`atm_8a0170f2319b` · **history** / global

**시기**: 1000 ~ 1800

Historic maritime republics projected distinct cultural and settlement patterns into rural hinterlands beyond their fortified urban cores, as seen with Venice's Dalmatian and Istrian coastal holdings, Genoa's Ligurian and Corsican littoral, and Dubrovnik's Primorje. These hinterlands typically preserved older agrarian, dispersed-hamlet patterns distinct from the dense, planned urban centers of the ruling republic.
