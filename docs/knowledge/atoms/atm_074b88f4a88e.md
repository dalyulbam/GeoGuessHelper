# Northern Jordan highland belt (Irbid–Jerash–Ajloun) vs southern/desert Jordan

`atm_074b88f4a88e` · **geography** / region · cell `sv9x8ty`

Jordan's northern highlands (Irbid, Jerash, Ajloun governorates) are characterized by cool winters, terraced olive groves on limestone hillsides, and hilly valley topography, contrasting with the treeless reddish-sandstone steppe of southern Jordan (Karak, Tafila, Ma'an) and the desert/palm terrain of Aqaba and Wadi Rum. Ajloun sits higher and carries pine forest cover in addition to olives, distinguishing it from the purer olive-terrace landscape of Jerash.
