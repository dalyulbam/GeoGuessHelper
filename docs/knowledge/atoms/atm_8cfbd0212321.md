# Iapodes tribal confederation of the Velebit hinterland

`atm_8cfbd0212321` · **history** / region · cell `srfw77f`

**시기**: -500 ~ 100

The Iapodes were an Illyrian-affiliated Iron Age tribal confederation inhabiting the Velebit mountains and their hinterland (including sub-groups like the Parenthines and Orthoplines), predating Roman conquest and Croat settlement in the 7th century. When Croats settled the area they substantially adopted existing Illyrian ways of life, showing settlement continuity rather than population replacement. A Roman-era inscribed boundary stone (attributed to provincial governor Publius Cornelius Dolabella, 1st century AD) near Jablanac/Stinica records Roman arbitration of borders between local Illyrian communities.
