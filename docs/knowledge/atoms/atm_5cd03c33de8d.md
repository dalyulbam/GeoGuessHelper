# Church-over-mosque conversion pattern across former Islamic territories

`atm_5cd03c33de8d` · **architecture** / global

The practice of erecting Christian churches directly atop or by converting former mosque structures recurs across reconquered or formerly Muslim-ruled territories: the Mezquita-Catedral of Córdoba built into a former Umayyad mosque, Hagia Sophia's earlier reverse conversion (church to mosque) in Istanbul, and Sicily's Cathedral of Palermo built over a mosque after the Norman conquest. These conversions typically preserved underlying structural elements like horseshoe arches or minaret bases repurposed as bell towers.
