# Yörük/Turkmen nomadic settlement layer over ancient Carian Anatolia

`atm_0e23428c07fd` · **history** / region · cell `swesguj`

Inland western Anatolia (ancient Caria, roughly modern Muğla and Aydın provinces) has a settlement history layering Turkic Yörük/Turkmen pastoral nomadic groups, who settled from the medieval period onward, over an older Hellenistic/Carian civic landscape. This produces a cultural identity that is solidly Turkish Aegean while retaining ancient place-name and archaeological substrates, without minority-language signage today.
