# Rebar-topped unfinished house family in developing economies

`atm_3fc712b096be` · **architecture** / global

Incrementally-built, tax-deferred unfinished concrete houses with exposed rebar columns are a widespread global pattern, notably in Egypt (informal 'red brick' housing left unplastered to avoid property tax on 'completed' buildings), Mexico (self-built 'auto-construcción' homes), and Greece (unfinished upper floors to avoid the completed-property tax, common in Athens suburbs). These reflect similar informal-economy incentives: taxes triggered by building completion, family land inheritance subdivision, and phased self-financing without mortgages.
