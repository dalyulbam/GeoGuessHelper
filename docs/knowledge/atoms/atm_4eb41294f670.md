# Early glacial-lake geomorphologists

`atm_4eb41294f670` · **geography** / global

The theory that Alpine ribbon lakes like Maggiore, Como, and Garda were carved by glacial overdeepening rather than tectonic subsidence was advanced in the late 19th and early 20th centuries by geologists including Albrecht Penck and Eduard Brückner, whose study of Alpine glaciation ('Die Alpen im Eiszeitalter', 1901-1909) established the classic glacial-lake chronology still referenced today.
