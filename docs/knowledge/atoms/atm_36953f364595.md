# Italian kerbside waste bins: red/white reflective hazard striping

Italian municipal wheeled waste bins placed kerbside on rural roads commonly carry red-and-white reflective hazard striping down their flanks for night-time roadside visibility, a distinct convention from Central European igloo-style EKO-KOM banks or French/Spanish municipal bin designs.
