# Onufry Kopczyński and codification of Polish orthography

Onufry Kopczyński, an 18th-century Piarist grammarian, authored the first systematic Polish grammar (1778-1783) commissioned by the Commission of National Education, helping standardize Polish spelling conventions including diacritic usage. The Polish Language Council (Rada Języka Polskiego), established in 1996, continues to regulate orthographic norms today.
