# Ragusan patrician oligarchy and diplomacy

`atm_d75f452353d9` · **history** / polity · cell `srsfs47`

**시기**: 1358 ~ 1808

The Republic of Ragusa (Dubrovnik) was governed by a hereditary patrician nobility organized around the Rector (Knez) and the Minor and Major Councils, with figures like diplomat and polymath Benedikt Kotruljević (Benedetto Cotrugli) exemplifying Ragusan mercantile scholarship. Ragusan nobles maintained the republic's neutrality and tribute-paying relationship with the Ottoman Empire for centuries.
