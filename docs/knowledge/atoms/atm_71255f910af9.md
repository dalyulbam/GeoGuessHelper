# Spanish-Guaraní bilingual cultural sphere of Paraguay

`atm_71255f910af9` · **culture** / polity · cell `6g0k8uc`

Paraguay is characterized by a mainstream Spanish-Guaraní bilingual cultural sphere, where Guaraní-derived toponyms and neighborhood/business names (e.g., 'Ka'aguy Rory', 'Heterei') coexist with Spanish-language street grids and signage. This differs from Paraguay's other cultural pockets, such as German-speaking Mennonite colonies in the Chaco (Filadelfia, Loma Plata) with distinct colony grid layouts, and 'brasiguayo' Portuguese-speaking zones near the Brazilian border (e.g., Santa Rita, Katueté).
