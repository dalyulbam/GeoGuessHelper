# Expulsion of the Jesuits from the Spanish Americas (1767)

`atm_c3a16dd3c51a` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1767 ~ 1767

In 1767, King Charles III of Spain expelled the Jesuits from all Spanish territories, seizing their missions, estancias, and educational institutions, including those in Córdoba and the Guaraní reductions. This abrupt suppression ended the Jesuit economic and religious network, transferring assets to the Spanish crown and other orders, and is the counter-event that terminated the expansion phase the Manzana Jesuítica represents.
