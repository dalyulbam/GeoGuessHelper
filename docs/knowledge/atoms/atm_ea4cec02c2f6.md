# Frankopan dynasty of Krk

`atm_ea4cec02c2f6` · **culture** / region · cell `u2437ts`

The Frankopan family were medieval Croatian feudal princes who held the island of Krk and much of the Kvarner littoral, building a network of castles (including at Omišalj) from the 15th century onward. Their legacy shapes island identity and heritage tourism (castle concerts, medieval fairs) across Krk's settlements today.
