# National postal-livery and retail-brand geolocation discriminators

`atm_2bd0043d5a35` · **language** / global

Postal services worldwide use distinct national color schemes and logos serving as geolocation cues: France's La Poste (yellow), UK's Royal Mail (red pillar boxes), Germany's Deutsche Post (yellow with black posthorn), Japan's Post (red with 〒 symbol), and USA's USPS (blue eagle logo). Similarly, national fuel-retail chains (Poland's Orlen, Hungary's MOL, Romania's Petrom) are country-specific liveries used to distinguish nearby countries in street-level imagery.
