# Latvia's four historic regions distinguished by terrain and religion

`atm_4c506c031137` · **geography** / country · cell `ud4tpyy`

Latvia is divided into four historic regions: Kurzeme (coastal, pine-heath/dune vegetation), Zemgale (flat, uninterrupted loess plains with large arable fields and long straight paved roads), Vidzeme (upland interior, morainic/drumlin terrain, mosaic of small fields, wood lots and dug ponds, Lutheran-dominated dispersed single-farmstead settlement), and Latgale (hummocky lake-dense terrain, denser village clustering, Catholic wayside crosses/chapels, distinct Latgalian place-name forms). Religion (Lutheran vs Catholic) and settlement pattern (dispersed farmsteads vs clustered villages) are reliable regional discriminators.
