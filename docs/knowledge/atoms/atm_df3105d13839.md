# Siberia steppe and Ob-Ugric polities

`atm_df3105d13839` · **history** / region

Siberian history includes the Luandi/Xiongnu confederation (209 BC-AD 93), Xianbei state (c.93-234), Rouran Khaganate (330-555), Ashina tribe Turkic Khaganates (552-744), the Ajo clan of the Yenisei Kyrgyz Khaganate (840-1207), Khamag Mongol (10th c.-1206), Khanty principalities (14th-17th c.) including Obdorsk, Kod, Bardakovo, Belogorye, Kazym, Mansi principalities including Pelym and Vogul, and the Shaybanid dynasty (1428-1598) ruling the Khanate of Sibir, succeeded by the House of Siberia.
