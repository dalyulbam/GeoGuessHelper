# Ancient and medieval Tunisian polities

`atm_db682447a8c8` · **history** / region

**시기**: -800 ~ 1957

Tunisia's ancient peoples and states include the Gaetuli, Garamantes, Musulamii, Zenata, Houara, Ancient Carthage, Massylii, Kingdom of Capsus, Vandal Kingdom, Jarawa (Berber tribe), and Sanhaja. Medieval Islamic dynasties include the Aghlabid dynasty, Fatimid dynasty, Muhallabids (771-793), Zirid dynasty (972-1148), Awlad Mandil, Banu Hilal, Banu Ghaniya, Banu Kinanah, Khurasanid dynasty (1059-1158), Kingdom of Africa, Hafsid dynasty (1229-1574), Hintata, and Husainid dynasty.
