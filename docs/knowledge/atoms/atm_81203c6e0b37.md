# Early-20th-century Philippine municipal consolidation under Executive Order 64 (1904)

`atm_81203c6e0b37` · **history** / region · cell `wej9x8g`

**시기**: 1904 ~ 1904

In January 1904, the American colonial administration in the Philippines issued Executive Order No. 64 consolidating smaller, fiscally weak municipalities into larger neighboring towns to streamline tax collection and administration. Many small towns (later re-established as independent municipalities) were temporarily annexed piecemeal — sometimes with western and eastern barrios split between different neighboring towns/provinces — a pattern seen repeatedly across small interior municipalities of Ilocos Sur.
