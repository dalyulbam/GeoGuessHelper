# Japanese telephone area codes as fine-grained geographic identifiers

`atm_2893ef2e3a1c` · **economy** / country · cell `wynu5mx`

Japanese landline area codes are assigned at a sub-prefectural level, allowing precise identification of a city or district: e.g. Fukuyama and its immediate satellites use (084), Okayama city uses (086), Hiroshima city uses (082), and Onomichi uses (0848). Area codes visible on shopfront or clinic signage are a strong high-confidence discriminator for narrowing location within a prefecture.
