# Bavarian mansard roofs and ochre stucco facades vs north German brick

`atm_039d544faf25` · **architecture** / region · cell `u284p2g`

Upper Bavarian urban facades typically show warm ochre-toned stucco render with shallow-pitched mansard roofs, contrasting with the steep-pitched clinker-brick gable architecture (Gründerzeit brick detailing) typical of North Rhine-Westphalia, Hesse, and Saxony. Roof pitch and facade material/color are therefore usable regional discriminators within Germany.
