# Papa mudstone cut banks mark eastern North Island, not South Island greywacke

`atm_935d031e2a8f` · **nature** / region · cell `rbv6fj8`

Eastern North Island backroads cut into soft, pale grey-white Tertiary papa mudstone that rills and slumps into rounded, tree-free hill faces. In the South Island you would instead see hard angular greywacke or schist cuts, braided shingle riverbeds and Nothofagus beech forest. A silty, single-channel stream in a steep incised gully with willows reinforces the North Island reading.
