# Coast Salish nations of the Salish Sea region

`atm_607ed4c93082` · **culture** / global

The Squamish Nation is one of many Coast Salish peoples whose traditional territories ring the Salish Sea, including the Musqueam and Tsleil-Waututh in the Vancouver area, the Lummi and Suquamish in Washington State, and the Cowichan on Vancouver Island. These nations share related Salishan languages, longhouse plank architecture, and canoe culture, and many hold overlapping or adjacent territorial claims intersected by the modern Canada-US border.
