# Oceanic/maritime climates as continental climate's opposite

`atm_6d710f051abb` · **nature** / global

Contrasting with West Siberia's extreme continentality, maritime climates like those of the British Isles, coastal Norway, and the Pacific Northwest show small annual temperature ranges and mild, wet winters due to ocean moderation. This oceanic-continental climate divide is a fundamental axis of Köppen classification and explains why coastal Barnaul-latitude cities like Bergen never see Siberian winter extremes.
