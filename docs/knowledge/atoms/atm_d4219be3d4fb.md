# Erdinger Weissbräu and Bavarian regional brewery branding

`atm_d4219be3d4fb` · **culture** / region · cell `u284p2g`

Erdinger Weissbräu is an Upper Bavarian wheat-beer brand whose large painted gable/firewall advertising murals are a regional marker rarely seen outside the Munich/Upper Bavaria area, analogous to how Kölsch (Cologne) or Radeberger/Feldschlösschen (Saxony) branding signals other German regions. Regional brewery advertising on building facades is a useful discriminator for German federal states, since beer branding in Germany is strongly regionalized rather than national.
