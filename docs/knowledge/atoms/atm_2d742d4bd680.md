# Land-title erasure and reserve-system displacement as historical counterforce

`atm_2d742d4bd680` · **culture** / global

The reserve/Indian Reserve system in Canada, alongside similar reservation regimes elsewhere, historically confined Indigenous peoples to a fraction of their traditional territory, often through treaties, the Indian Act, and forced relocations, displacing prior land-use and settlement patterns. Contemporary place-name restoration and land-title litigation (e.g. Tsilhqot'in Nation v. British Columbia, 2014) represent the pushback against this historical displacement.
