# 'Konoba' as marker of Croatian/Dalmatian tavern-eatery culture

`atm_080c7ca4a29f` · **economy** / country · cell `srskkfj`

'Konoba' is a specifically Croatian and Dalmatian term for a small tavern-style eatery, often located in a wine cellar or stone building, serving local wine and traditional food. Its presence on signage is a strong linguistic/cultural marker distinguishing Croatia from neighboring Italy (which uses 'trattoria/osteria'), Montenegro, and Albania in visual geolocation.
