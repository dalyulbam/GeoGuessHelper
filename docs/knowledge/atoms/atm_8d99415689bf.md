# Warring States-era Chinese states (Han, Wei, Zhao, Zeng, and minor states)

`atm_8d99415689bf` · **history** / region

Major Warring States polities include Han (403-230 BC), Wei (403-225 BC), and Zhao (403-222 BC, with its remnant Dai 227-222 BC), all ruled by Huaxia houses (Ji for Han/Wei, Ying for Zhao). Smaller or peripheral states include Zeng (?-5th century BC, House of Ji), Chouyou (?-5th century BC), Ju (368-316 BC), Eastern Zhou state (367-249 BC, House of Ji), Xue (?-298 BC, Houses of Ren and Gui), and Dai (Warring States period, 228-222 BC, House of Ying).
