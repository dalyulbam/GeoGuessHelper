# Depopulation and labor scarcity threatening steep-slope viticulture

`atm_98f1eccf8187` · **economy** / region · cell `srsk6fc`

Steep, hand-worked vineyard regions such as Pelješac, the Douro, and the Mosel face chronic labor shortages and rural depopulation as younger generations move to cities, driving up production costs and threatening abandonment of terraces despite appellation prestige. Some regions respond with seasonal migrant labor or mechanized cable-assisted equipment on moderate slopes, but the steepest plots resist mechanization entirely.
