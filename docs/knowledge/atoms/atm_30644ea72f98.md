# Drought-browned pasture with green gullies = eastern rain shadow, not Taranaki/Waikato

`atm_30644ea72f98` · **nature** / region · cell `rbv6fj8`

On the rain-shadowed eastern North Island, midsummer hill pasture is fully straw-brown while only the gully bottoms stay green, and cut banks are chalky pale grey. In Taranaki or Waikato you would instead see pasture staying green through summer and road cuts exposing dark brown andesitic ash and tephra layers. Combine this with poplar erosion plantings to confirm the eastern papa belt.
