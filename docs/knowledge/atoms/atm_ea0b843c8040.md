# Automotive-cluster cities dependent on assembly plants

`atm_ea0b843c8040` · **economy** / global

Many mid-sized cities worldwide built their economies around automotive assembly and parts manufacturing, mirroring Córdoba's model: Detroit (USA), Wolfsburg (Germany, home of Volkswagen), Toyota City (Japan), Turin (Italy, home of Fiat), and Puebla (Mexico, Volkswagen plant). These cities share the same structural vulnerability: heavy dependence on a single industry's cyclical demand and susceptibility to global auto-sector downturns and offshoring pressure.
