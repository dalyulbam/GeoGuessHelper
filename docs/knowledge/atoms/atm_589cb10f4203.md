# Rainbow pedestrian crossings as Nordic civic inclusivity marker

`atm_589cb10f4203` · **culture** / country · cell `ge9h53s`

Small Nordic towns, including modest Icelandic villages, sometimes paint rainbow-colored pedestrian crossings near civic buildings (churches, schools) as a public gesture of LGBTQ+ inclusivity, despite otherwise plain municipal infrastructure. Presence of such markings signals municipal civic investment rather than commercial or tourist-district signaling.
