# Thirty-six States of the Western Regions (200 BC-AD 1006)

`atm_c7dedae6203e` · **history** / region

A group of small city-states in the Tarim Basin/Western Regions collectively called the Thirty-six States, spanning roughly 200 BC to AD 1006. Notable members include Shule (200 BC-AD 790), Loulan (176-77 BC) succeeded by Shanshan (77 BC-AD 630), Kucha (72 BC-AD 788), Jushi (71 BC-AD 508, split into Nearer Jushi and Further Jushi 67 BC-AD 170), Suoju (66 BC-AD 89), and Khotan (AD 56-1006, ruled by the House of Yuchi of Saka descent, later succeeded internally by Jin AD 851-938, Dabao Yutian AD 938-983, and Jinyu AD 983-?). Also includes Yanqi (AD 58-792, ruled by House of Long) and many smaller undated states: Ruoqiang, Qiemo, Xiaoyuan, Jingjue, Ronglu, Hanmi, Qule, Pishan, Wucha, Yinai, Xiye, Zihe, Puli, Nandou, Dayuan, Taohuai, Juandu, Gumo, Wensu, Quli, Wulei, Yuli, Weixu, Beilu (with Further Beilu), Jie, Pulei (with Nearer and Further Pulei), Qiemi (with Eastern and Western Qiemi), Shan, Huhu, Wulei (Wulei/无雷 distinct from Wulei/烏壘), Weitou, and Xiuxun.
