# Global hippie-trail beach towns

`atm_d114d8bf82fb` · **culture** / global

**시기**: 1965 ~ 1985

The 1960s-70s overland hippie trail and subsequent backpacker circuits transformed fishing villages worldwide into countercultural tourism hubs: Goa's Anjuna and Arambol in India, Kuta in Bali, Indonesia, and Ko Pha Ngan in Thailand (origin of the Full Moon Party). These share a pattern of low-cost guesthouses, beach parties, and long-term Western expatriate residents layered atop original local fishing or farming communities.
