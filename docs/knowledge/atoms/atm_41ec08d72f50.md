# Mljet National Park's saltwater lakes and Benedictine monastery islet

`atm_41ec08d72f50` · **geography** / polity · cell `srs76v3`

**시기**: 1960 ~ ?

Mljet National Park (established 1960, the oldest marine protected area in the Mediterranean) is centered on two interconnected saltwater lakes, Veliko Jezero and Malo Jezero, on the island of Mljet in southern Dalmatia. A 12th-13th century Benedictine monastery of St. Mary, combining Romanesque-Gothic and later Renaissance architectural layers, sits on the tiny islet of Sveti Marija within Veliko Jezero and functioned as the island's religious and economic center for centuries before secularization.
