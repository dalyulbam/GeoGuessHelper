# Philippine administrative vocabulary as country signature

`atm_27356d38e4dc` · **language** / country · cell `wej9x8g`

The Philippines uses a distinctive Latin-script administrative vocabulary — 'Barangay' (basic administrative unit) and 'Sitio' (sub-village unit within a barangay) — that is unique among Southeast Asian nations. This distinguishes it from Indonesia's 'Desa/Dusun/Kecamatan', Vietnam's diacritic-heavy Quốc ngữ, and Malaysia's 'Kampung' terminology, and is a strong country-level identification cue from map labels or signage alone.
