# Thailand (Siam) dynasties

`atm_c6438748e927` · **history** / country

Thai history includes Singhanavati, the Lavachakkaraj dynasty (638-1292, Kingdom of Hiran), Phra Ruang dynasty (1238-1438, Sukhothai Kingdom), Mangrai dynasty (1292-1558, Lan Na), and Ayutthaya Kingdom dynasties: Uthong (1350-1370, 1388-1409), Suphannaphum (1370-1388, 1409-1569), Sukhothai (1569-1629), Prasart Thong (1629-1688), Baan Plu Luang (1688-1767). Also the Inland dynasty of Pattani (1457-1688), Sultanate of Singora (1605-1680), First and Second Kelantanese dynasties of Pattani (1688-1808, 1842-1902), Chet Ton/Thipchak dynasty (1732-1943), Thonburi Kingdom (1767-1782), and the Chakri dynasty (1782-present) ruling the Rattanakosin Kingdom (1782-1932) and Kingdom of Thailand (1932-present).
