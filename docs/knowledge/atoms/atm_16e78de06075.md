# Zug's housing squeeze from low-tax-driven in-migration

`atm_16e78de06075` · **economy** / city · cell `u0qh3ck`

Zug city's extremely low personal income tax (top marginal rate ~22.2% vs ~39.7% in Zurich city) and wealthy international in-migration have produced an acute housing shortage, with rental vacancy well below 1% and some of Switzerland's highest rents, forcing many workers to commute in from Lucerne, Zurich, or rural Zug municipalities. This mirrors a broader pattern where aggressive low-tax policy attracting international wealth can outstrip local housing supply.
