# Jānis Endzelīns, architect of standardized Latvian orthography

`atm_87a8877b3652` · **language** / country · cell `ud15ux2`

Linguist Jānis Endzelīns (1873-1961) was the principal architect of modern standardized Latvian grammar and orthography in the early 20th century, fixing the use of macrons and diacritics (ā, ē, ī, ū, ž, š) that distinguish Latvian from Lithuanian and Estonian. His work at the University of Latvia underpins the orthographic conventions still seen on signage today.
