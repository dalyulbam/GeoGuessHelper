# Rainbow crosswalks as municipal civic marker in Iceland

`atm_e642c5062954` · **culture** / country · cell `ge9h53s`

Small Icelandic towns, even ones with populations under 1,000, frequently feature rainbow-painted pedestrian crossings near civic buildings such as churches or schools, signaling LGBTQ+-inclusive municipal policy. This is consistent with generally strong small-scale public goods provision (paved streets, streetlights, sidewalks, sports fields) typical of Icelandic municipalities regardless of size, funded via national equalization transfers.
