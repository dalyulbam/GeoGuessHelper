# Westfjords vs Húnaflói/north-coast fjord profile

`atm_b3a8761917a2` · **geography** / region · cell `ge9h53s`

Iceland's Westfjords display near-vertical cliffs of 500m+ dropping directly into narrow fjords, whereas the Húnaflói area on the north-central coast has wide, shallow fjords with gentle grass-covered hills and low relief right behind the settlements. Fjord steepness and width is thus a reliable discriminator between these two regions of Iceland.
