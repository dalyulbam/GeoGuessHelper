# Fishing-village-to-surf-tourism transitions worldwide

`atm_8dea6efc26d9` · **economy** / global

Coastal fishing communities converting to backpacker/surf tourism economies is a global pattern seen in Kuta (Bali, Indonesia) from the 1970s, Taghazout (Morocco), and Mancora (Peru), where traditional fishing fleets shrink as guesthouses, surf schools, and seafood restaurants catering to foreign visitors expand. These transitions typically follow the arrival of surfers discovering breaks, followed by budget accommodation and eventually mid-range resort investment.
