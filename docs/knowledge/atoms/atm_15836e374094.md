# Nazi civilian occupation administrations across Europe

`atm_15836e374094` · **history** / global

Nazi Germany established parallel civilian occupation regimes elsewhere modeled on similar hierarchical district structures: Reichskommissariat Ostland (Baltic states and Belarus), Generalgouvernement in central Poland, and Reichskommissariat Niederlande in the Netherlands. Each subdivided occupied territory into General- and Gebietskommissariate run by German civil administrators alongside military and SS authorities, exploiting local resources and populations.
