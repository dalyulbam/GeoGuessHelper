# Krk Bridge, world-class concrete arch (1980)

`atm_14bd04044110` · **architecture** / polity · cell `u2437ts`

Krk Bridge (Krčki most) in Croatia's Kvarner Gulf is a two-span reinforced-concrete arch bridge connecting the island of Krk to the mainland via the small islet of Sveti Marko. Opened in 1980, its longer span (390–416 m) was the third-longest concrete arch in the world and the longest outside China at the time, briefly surpassing Sydney Harbour Bridge's arch length by 85 m. It exemplifies large-scale Yugoslav-era infrastructure engineering built to open remote Adriatic islands to road traffic.
