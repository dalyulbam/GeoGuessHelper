# UAE vs Oman: mobile prefixes and flag bunting on shopfronts

`atm_1b29ea72a049` · **language** / country · cell `thrxzsf`

In the UAE, shop signage carries eight-digit mobiles beginning 050/052/054/055/056/058 and festive bunting in red-green-white-black. In Oman you would instead see 9x/7x eight-digit mobiles, no black band in the flag bunting, and Omani mosques with bulbous or ribbed domes plus a distinct minaret idiom. Arid oasis morphology and Arabic/English bilingual fascias are identical on both sides of the border, so the numbers decide.
