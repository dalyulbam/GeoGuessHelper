# Union republics of the Soviet Union

`atm_088048872ae0` · **history** / region

The USSR (1922-1991) comprised union republics: Armenian SSR (1920-1991), Azerbaijan SSR (1920-1991), Byelorussian SSR (1922-1991), Estonian SSR (1940-1991), Georgian SSR (1921-1991), Karelo-Finnish SSR (1940-1956), Kazakh SSR (1936-1991), Kirghiz SSR (1936-1991), Latvian SSR (1940-1990), Lithuanian SSR (1940-1990), Moldavian SSR (1940-1991, including the Pridnestrovian Moldavian SSR 1990-1991), and Russian SFSR (1922-1991).
