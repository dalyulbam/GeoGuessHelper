# Canary Islands volcanic geology and vegetation zonation

`atm_63230a15ee6e` · **geography** / polity · cell `esvzdq6`

The Canary Islands are shield/stratovolcano archipelago off NW Africa built from basaltic volcanism, ranging from geologically young, near-black lava badlands (Lanzarote) to older, deeply eroded ochre-tan Miocene massifs with sandy beaches (Fuerteventura). Vegetation is xeric scrub with planted Phoenix canariensis (Canary date palm) rather than natural forest, distinguishing the eastern arid islands from the laurel-forest-covered western islands like La Palma or Madeira's laurisilva.
