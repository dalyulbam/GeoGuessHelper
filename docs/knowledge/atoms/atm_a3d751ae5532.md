# Ottoman inland frontier versus Venetian coastal Renaissance towns

`atm_a3d751ae5532` · **architecture** / region

While Venice sponsored Renaissance urbanism along the Dalmatian coast, the Ottoman Empire controlled the immediate hinterland from the 15th-16th centuries onward, producing a sharp architectural and cultural boundary: Islamic-Ottoman forms (mosques, hans, hammams) developed in inland Bosnia and Herzegovina towns like Mostar and Počitelj just kilometers from the Venetian-influenced coast.
