# Ľudovít Štúr and codification of standard Slovak

`atm_b2fe9975ad3d` · **language** / country · cell `u2s1vm7`

Ľudovít Štúr (1815-1856) codified standard written Slovak in the 1840s, based on central Slovak dialects, distinguishing it definitively from Czech literary language despite centuries of shared Czechoslovak linguistic administration. His orthographic reforms underlie the modern Slovak alphabet's distinct diacritics (ä, ô, ľ) still visible on signage today.
