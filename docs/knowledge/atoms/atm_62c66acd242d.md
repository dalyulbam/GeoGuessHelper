# Frontier town founding narratives in post-Conquista Argentina

`atm_62c66acd242d` · **culture** / polity · cell `6993z1r`

**시기**: 1880 ~ ?

Many small towns founded during and after Argentina's 19th-century frontier campaigns market their civic identity around being 'the first' or founding settlement of their province/region, using slogans tying them to indigenous-contact history and frontier military origins (e.g., Victorica's 'Donde Nació La Pampa'). This pattern of heritage branding around conquest-era founding is common across Pampean and Patagonian frontier towns as a tourism and identity strategy.
