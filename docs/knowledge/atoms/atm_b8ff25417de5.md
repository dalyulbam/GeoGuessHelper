# Global automotive nearshoring manufacturing belts

`atm_b8ff25417de5` · **economy** / global

Automotive manufacturing clusters built around foreign-brand assembly plants and export-oriented supplier networks recur worldwide, notably in Mexico's Bajio region (Guanajuato, Aguascalientes, San Luis Potosi with Nissan, Mazda, BMW plants), the US South's 'Auto Alley' (Tennessee, Alabama, South Carolina with foreign OEMs), and Central Europe's Slovakia/Hungary auto corridor (Volkswagen Bratislava, Kia Zilina), all driven by low-cost labor and proximity to major consumer markets.
