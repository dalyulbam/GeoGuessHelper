# Provincial bare-brick merchant houses are pan-Russian, not an Upper Volga fingerprint

`atm_efd274cc5e3b` · **architecture** / country · cell `v47mmjn`

Two-storey unrendered red-brick houses with kokoshnik brick window hoods and stepped attic gablets occur in nearly every pre-revolutionary uyezd centre from the Upper Volga east to the Kama and Cis-Urals. In Yaroslavl/Kostroma you would additionally expect a surviving arcaded gostiny dvor, stuccoed classical facades and a visible church ensemble on a planned regular square; in the Vyatka–Kama belt the brick stock is thinner, isolated, and sits directly among log izbas on an irregular street fork, as here. Treat this typology as a Russia-wide date marker, not a longitude marker.
