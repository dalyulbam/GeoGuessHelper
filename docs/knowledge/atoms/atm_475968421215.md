# Later Israel/Palestine dynasties and Kingdom of Jerusalem houses

`atm_475968421215` · **history** / region

Jarrahids ruled AD 971-1107. In the Crusader Kingdom of Jerusalem: House of Flanders (1099-1118), House of Rethel (1118-1153), House of Chateaudun (1131-1185, 1186-1205), Aleramici (1183-1186, 1190-1192, 1205-1212), House of Lusignan (1186-1192, 1198-1205; branch House of Poitiers-Lusignan 1268-1324), House of Brienne (1210-1228), Hohenstaufen (1225-1268). Later, Burji dynasty (1382-1517) ruled Palestine within the Mamluk Sultanate; Al-Zayadina, Ridwan dynasty (1560-1690, non-sovereign hereditary leaders), and Turabay dynasty (16th century-1677) also ruled locally.
