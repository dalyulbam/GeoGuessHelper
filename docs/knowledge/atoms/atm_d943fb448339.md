# Non-standard national road-sign systems outside the Vienna Convention

`atm_d943fb448339` · **architecture** / country

The United States, United Kingdom, and several other countries never adopted the Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals, retaining distinct national systems: the US uses yellow diamond warning signs and rectangular white speed-limit signs with black text, while the UK uses its own triangular warning-sign and circular-sign conventions dating to the Worboys Report of 1963, creating a visible signage discontinuity at non-EU borders.
