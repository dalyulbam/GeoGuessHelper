# Interwar industrial workers' housing estates across Europe

`atm_786e9b689833` · **architecture** / global

Interwar Europe saw large-scale planned workers' housing built by industrial employers or municipalities near mines, steelworks and railways, including the Ruhr Valley's Zechensiedlungen colliery colonies in Germany, Britain's garden-suburb-influenced council estates, and Belgium's cités ouvrières in the Borinage coal basin. These typically combined functional brick or rendered blocks with communal amenities, reflecting contemporary social-housing reform movements.
