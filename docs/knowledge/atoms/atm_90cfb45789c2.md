# Transitional Tswana housing typology in walled compounds

`atm_90cfb45789c2` · **architecture** / region · cell `keghd35`

In eastern Botswana village settlements, housing typically shows a transitional mix within walled family compounds: conical thatch-roofed rondavels with smoothed earth or mud-plastered walls alongside newer concrete-block, corrugated-iron-roofed structures. This mixed typology reflects gradual modernization while retaining traditional homestead layout and communal walled yards.
