# UAE 06 landline code = Northern Emirates, not Abu Dhabi oasis

`atm_e605fa30bf62` · **economy** / region · cell `thrxzsf`

In the UAE, business signs printing landlines as 06-xxxxxxx place you in Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain or Fujairah; an inland oasis town with 06 numbers is Al Dhaid, Sharjah's Central Region. In Abu Dhabi's comparable oasis (Al Ain) you would instead see 03-xxxxxxx, and in Dubai 04-xxxxxxx. Mobile prefixes (050/052/055/056/058) are nationwide and cannot separate emirates.
