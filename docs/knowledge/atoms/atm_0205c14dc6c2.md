# Regina vs Saskatoon economic specialization within Saskatchewan

`atm_0205c14dc6c2` · **economy** / polity · cell `c8vwgv1`

Within Saskatchewan, Regina (the provincial capital) hosts government administration, Crown corporations (SaskPower, SaskTel, SGI, SaskEnergy), the Co-op Refinery Complex, Evraz steel, and the RCMP's national training academy (Depot Division), giving it a stable, public-sector-driven economy. Saskatoon, by contrast, is resource- and research-driven, home to Nutrien, Cameco, potash/uranium industry service functions, and the University of Saskatchewan. Regina has notably lower housing costs than Saskatoon despite similar climate and quality of life.
