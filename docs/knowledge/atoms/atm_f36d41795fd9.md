# Cambodian royal dynasties (1080–present)

`atm_f36d41795fd9` · **history** / country

Cambodia's monarchy comprises the Mahidharapura dynasty (1080–1336), the broader Varman dynasty (13th century–present), the House of Norodom (1860–1904, 1941–1970, 1993–present, extant), and the House of Sisowath (1904–1941). The Imperial House of Japan nominally oversaw Cambodia 1941–1945 during Japanese occupation.
