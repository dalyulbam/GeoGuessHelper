# Dynasties of Austria

`atm_7e8dab39e149` · **history** / country

Austria was ruled by the House of Babenberg (976-1246) and the House of Habsburg (1278-1780), also called the House of Austria, comprising the Albertinian line (1379-1439, 1440-1457) and Leopoldian line (1379-1493), succeeded by the House of Habsburg-Lorraine (1780-1918/19).
