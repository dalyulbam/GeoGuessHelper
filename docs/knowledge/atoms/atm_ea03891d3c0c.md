# Coastal war depopulation across former Yugoslavia and beyond

`atm_ea03891d3c0c` · **history** / global

Similar wartime depopulation of coastal and rural settlements occurred throughout the 1991-95 Yugoslav wars, notably in the Krajina region of Croatia and villages around Vukovar, where combat and ethnic cleansing emptied communities that only partially repopulated afterward. Comparable postwar coastal abandonment patterns appear in other conflict zones such as the Golan Heights villages after 1967 and Cyprus's Varosha after 1974.
