# Coastal-highway naming as national identity marker in the Mediterranean

`atm_86c1b5091ece` · **language** / global

Mediterranean coastal nations each brand their principal coastal highway distinctly: Croatia's 'Jadranska Magistrala' (D8), Italy's 'Strada Statale' network including the Amalfi SS163, France's 'Corniche' roads, and Spain's 'Costa' associated N-roads. These naming conventions, paired with local words for beach and cave, function as reliable linguistic fingerprints in geolocation from road signage alone.
