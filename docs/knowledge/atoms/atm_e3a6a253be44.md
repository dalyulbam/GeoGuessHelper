# Overtourism and diversification pushback in island economies

`atm_e3a6a253be44` · **economy** / global

Islands dependent on a single tourism operator or narrow high season face criticism for economic fragility and lack of resilience, prompting policy pushes toward diversifying into agritourism, off-season cultural festivals, or capping visitor numbers (e.g. Croatia's Hvar noise/behavior regulations, Venice's day-tripper fees). Some protected islands, like Mljet as a national park, restrict development to prevent overtourism, trading economic growth for conservation.
