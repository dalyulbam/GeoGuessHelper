# Hungarian dynasties

`atm_80d337545fbf` · **history** / country

Hungary's history includes the Hunnic Empire (370s-469), Levedia (750/830-850) and Etelköz (850-895) proto-Hungarian polities, the Árpád dynasty (c.895-1301, including the Aba branch 1038-1044), Přemyslid dynasty (1301-1305), Capetian House of Anjou (1308-1395), House of Luxemburg (1387-1437), Hunyadi family (1458-1490), Habsburg dynasty (1437-1457, 1526-1918), and Zápolya dynasty (1526-1571).
