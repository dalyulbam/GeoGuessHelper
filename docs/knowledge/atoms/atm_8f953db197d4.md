# Zinfandel/Primitivo/Crljenak Kaštelanski: one grape, three names

`atm_8f953db197d4` · **culture** / global

The grape genetically identical to California's Zinfandel and Italy's Primitivo (grown chiefly in Puglia) is the same variety as Croatia's rare Crljenak Kaštelanski, a parent of Plavac Mali. DNA profiling in the early 2000s by UC Davis researchers confirmed this transcontinental identity, linking Dalmatian, Apulian, and Californian viticulture.
