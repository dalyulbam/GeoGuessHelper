# Pacific vs. inland/Gulf Mexico terrain and vegetation contrasts

`atm_0f259f84fb29` · **architecture** / region · cell `9shk1z4`

Mexico's Pacific coastal belt (e.g. Sinaloa, Nayarit) shows flat coastal terrain with dry tropical deciduous vegetation, palms, and flowering trees like tabebuia, distinct from the volcanic highland relief of the Bajío/central Mexico, the humid greener vegetation of the Gulf coast (Veracruz/Tamaulipas), and the flat white limestone scrub landscape with Maya toponyms of the Yucatán Peninsula. These regional vegetation/terrain signatures help distinguish Mexican macro-regions in street-level imagery.
