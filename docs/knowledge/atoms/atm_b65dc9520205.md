# Taj Mahal-derived Mughal mausoleum family

`atm_b65dc9520205` · **architecture** / global

The Taj Mahal in Agra (completed 1653 under Shah Jahan for Mumtaz Mahal) inspired a lineage of later Mughal and Mughal-successor mausoleums replicating its domed-tomb-with-minarets scheme at reduced scale and quality, including Bibi Ka Maqbara in Aurangabad and the tomb of Safdarjung in Delhi (built 1754 for the Nawab of Awadh), both reflecting the empire's declining resources in the 17th-18th centuries.
