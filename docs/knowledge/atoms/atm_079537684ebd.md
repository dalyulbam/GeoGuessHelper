# Glagolitic scribal culture and the Baška Tablet

`atm_079537684ebd` · **culture** / region · cell `srfrzds`

The Baška Tablet (Bašćanska ploča), found on Krk island and dated to around 1100 AD, is one of the oldest surviving Glagolitic inscriptions in Croatian and records a land grant by King Zvonimir; it stands as a key artifact of the Glagolitic priest-scribes (glagoljaši) who maintained Croatian-language liturgy on the Kvarner islands for centuries.
