# Tromso as Arctic research, fisheries, and international-institution hub

`atm_b4e047668ccb` · **economy** / city · cell `ukmkre5`

Tromso (pop. ~78,000, North Norway's largest city) combines a high-latitude university (UiT The Arctic University of Norway, ~16,000 students), a university hospital, and Norway's largest whitefish/cod processing port (~85,000 tonnes/year, ~12% of national cod processing), located ~350km from Barents Sea fishing grounds. It hosts multiple Arctic international bodies (Arctic Council Secretariat, Arctic Frontiers, Norwegian Polar Institute) and benefits from Arctic-zone tax incentives (reduced payroll/social security tax), driving above-average regional economic growth despite housing costs ~23% above the national average.
