# Norwegian Bokmål vs Nynorsk and Danish orthographic splits

`atm_4b3b17e5270b` · **language** / global

Closely related North Germanic written standards diverge on signage similarly to Czech/Slovak: Norwegian Bokmål and Danish share heavy mutual vocabulary but differ in spelling conventions (Norwegian 'apotek' vs Danish softened consonants), while Nynorsk uses distinct rural-dialect-based forms. These small orthographic deltas are used by linguists and geolocators to distinguish otherwise near-identical Scandinavian text.
