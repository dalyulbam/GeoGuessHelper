# Post-independence renaming of colonial-era Indian toponyms

`atm_c9dd7ef02ea9` · **language** / country

Countering inherited toponymic layers, independent India renamed many colonial-era city names to reflect local/regional linguistic identity: Bombay to Mumbai (1995, Marathi), Madras to Chennai (1996, Tamil), Calcutta to Kolkata (2001, Bengali), and Bangalore to Bengaluru (2014, Kannada), asserting regional linguistic pride over Anglicized forms.
