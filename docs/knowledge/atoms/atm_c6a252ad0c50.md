# Khorat Plateau (Isan) defined by red laterite soil and flat terrain

`atm_c6a252ad0c50` · **geography** / region · cell `w71dmn8`

Northeastern Thailand (Isan) sits on the Khorat Plateau, a flat sedimentary highland with reddish laterite soil, dry-season deciduous woodland, and a tropical savanna (Köppen Aw) climate. This contrasts with Northern Thailand's forested mountain ridges, Southern Thailand's evergreen rubber/oil-palm monoculture and coastal Islamic influence, and Central Thailand's grey alluvial clay and canal-side (khlong) settlement patterns.
