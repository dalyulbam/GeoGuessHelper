# Reichskommissariat Ukraine administrative structure in WWII

`atm_0f643119e6e7` · **economy** / polity · cell `u8xsc9x`

During Nazi German occupation of Ukraine (1941–1944), territory was organized under the Reichskommissariat Ukraine, subdivided into General Bezirke (general districts, e.g., Mykolaiv) and further into smaller Gebiet units administered from local town centers. Soviet underground resistance networks operated within these occupied zones, and after liberation, military units involved received honorific town names as battle honors.
