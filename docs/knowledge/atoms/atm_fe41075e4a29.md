# Barnaul industrial and transport economy

`atm_fe41075e4a29` · **economy** / city · cell `vc7x8jq`

**시기**: 1941 ~ ?

Barnaul's core industry is mechanical engineering (boilers, presses, diesel engines, tractor and mining equipment, cargo carriages), much of it rooted in WWII plant evacuations. It also produces chemical fibers, tires, textiles, forest products and ammunition (Barnaul Cartridge Plant). It is a multi-modal transport hub with an airport, an Ob River cargo port, and rail on the Novosibirsk-Barnaul-Semey line toward Central Asia.
