# Telesecundaria/telebachillerato as Mexico-specific distance-education markers

`atm_bfa5269be192` · **economy** / country · cell `9exuvnc`

Mexico's SEP (Secretaría de Educación Pública) operates a nationwide network of 'telesecundaria' (distance/broadcast-based lower-secondary schools) and 'telebachillerato comunitario' (community distance upper-secondary schools) deployed in small rural settlements where a full-size school is not economically viable. These institution names are Mexico-exclusive and do not appear in Andean or Central American school-naming systems, making them a reliable country-level discriminator on maps or signage.
