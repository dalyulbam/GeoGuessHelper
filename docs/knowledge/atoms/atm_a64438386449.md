# Poland's 'Recovered Territories' (Ziemie Odzyskane) post-1945 resettlement

`atm_a64438386449` · **history** / region · cell `u36t47h`

**시기**: 1945 ~ 1950

Following WWII border shifts, former eastern German territories (Pomerania, Neumark, Silesia, parts of East Prussia) were transferred to Poland and their expelled German population replaced by resettled Polish migrants, often from former eastern Poland annexed by the USSR. Towns in this zone retain pre-1945 German-built brick/half-timbered architectural stock (steep tiled roofs, red-and-yellow brick banding, Fachwerk gables) while civic life, signage, and culture are entirely Polish, creating a visible discontinuity between building fabric and current culture. Small towns often carry both a historic German name (e.g. Soldin) and current Polish name (Myślibórz).
