# Illyrian tribal confederations of the western Balkans

`atm_95b8853f8fa0` · **history** / global

Alongside the Iapodes, the Illyrian world comprised numerous named tribal confederations before Roman conquest, including the Dalmatae (giving their name to Dalmatia), the Liburni along the northern Adriatic coast known for fast liburna galleys, the Pannonii inland, and the Ardiaei on the southern Adriatic under king Agron and queen Teuta. Roman conquest absorbed these polities piecemeal between the 2nd century BC and 1st century AD, culminating in the Bellum Batonianum (Great Illyrian Revolt, 6-9 AD).
