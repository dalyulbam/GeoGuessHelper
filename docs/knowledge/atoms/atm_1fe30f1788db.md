# Zaton bay urban cluster naming convention

The Zaton bay area near Dubrovnik is administratively and colloquially split into several named constituent settlements clustered around one bay: Zaton Veliki, Zaton Mali, Štikovica, and Vrbica, together totalling only around 850 inhabitants. This pattern of a single bay hosting multiple separately-named micro-settlements is typical of the fragmented settlement nomenclature along the Dubrovnik littoral.
