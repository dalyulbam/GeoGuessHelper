# Dubrovnik-Neretva County: tourism-dominated, higher-GDP Croatian region with seasonal wage gap

`atm_7583caa0118c` · **economy** / region · cell `srsfew1`

**시기**: 2024 ~ 2026

Dubrovnik-Neretva County is among Croatia's more developed regions by GDP per capita, with an economy overwhelmingly based on tourism as the tertiary-sector employer. Average net tourism-sector salaries (~€1,210/month in 2024, rising to a county-wide average of ~€1,393 by late 2025) remain below the Croatian national average, reflecting a seasonal, tourism-dependent labor market despite strong employment growth and business revenue growth (+15.2% in 2024).
