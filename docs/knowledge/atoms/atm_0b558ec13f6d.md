# Croatian ampelographers and Plavac Mali/Zinfandel lineage research

`atm_0b558ec13f6d` · **economy** / region · cell `u25kesm`

**시기**: 2001 ~ 2001

Croatian scientists at the University of Zagreb, notably Edi Maletić, Ivan Pejić and collaboration with UC Davis researcher Carole Meredith, established via DNA analysis in 2001 that Plavac Mali is a natural offspring of the Crljenak Kaštelanski grape (genetically identical to Zinfandel/Primitivo), resolving a long ampelographic mystery and boosting international recognition of Dalmatian wine appellations like Dingač.
