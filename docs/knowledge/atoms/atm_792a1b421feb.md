# Designers of national road-signage systems

`atm_792a1b421feb` · **architecture** / country

Jock Kinneir and Margaret Calvert designed the UK's modern road signage system in the 1950s-60s, including the Transport typeface and the motorway sign format later adopted or adapted by other countries. Such designers established sans-serif legibility standards that influenced European road-sign harmonization under the Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals (1968).
