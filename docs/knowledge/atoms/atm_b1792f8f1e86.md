# ALBA Synchrotron as anchor of a Barcelona-area science park cluster

`atm_b1792f8f1e86` · **economy** / city · cell `sp3ebxf`

The ALBA Synchrotron in Cerdanyola del Vallès is a third-generation synchrotron light source and the most important such facility in the Mediterranean region, serving academic and industrial users. It anchors the adjacent Barcelona Synchrotron Park (Parc de l'Alba), which hosts aerospace, microelectronics (CNM-IMB, Spain's largest clean room), materials science (ICMAB), and nanoscience (ICN2) institutes, illustrating how a single large scientific infrastructure project can seed a broader applied-research business park.
