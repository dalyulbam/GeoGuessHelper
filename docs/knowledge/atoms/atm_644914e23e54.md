# Great alluvial river plains of the world

`atm_644914e23e54` · **geography** / global

The Gangetic Plain belongs to a global family of vast flat alluvial lowlands built by major river systems, including the Mesopotamian plain of the Tigris-Euphrates, the North China Plain along the Yellow River, and the Nile Valley/Delta in Egypt. These plains share extremely low relief, fertile silt-based agriculture, dense historic populations, and vulnerability to seasonal flooding.
