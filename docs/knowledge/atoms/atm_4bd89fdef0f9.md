# Emilia vs. Romagna vs. Lunigiana watershed and cultural division

`atm_4bd89fdef0f9` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb3`

The Emilian side of the Northern Apennines (Parma, Reggio, Piacenza provinces) drains north into Po tributaries and is culturally/administratively distinct from Romagna to the east (Romagnol torrents like Marecchia and Savio, east of Bologna) and from Lunigiana in Tuscany south of the Cisa Pass, which drains to the Magra river toward Liguria and shows Ligurian-style slate roofing rather than Emilian building traditions.
