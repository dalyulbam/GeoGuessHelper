# Belarusian-Russian diglossia (Trasianka zone)

`atm_c2abec0b30bf` · **language** / country · cell `u9edek1`

In Belarus, Russian dominates urban commercial, media, and everyday speech, while Belarusian is largely confined to rural areas, cultural institutions, and symbolic official use, with a mixed spoken register called Trasianka. This linguistic imbalance parallels the Ukrainian Russian/Ukrainian split but is more pronounced, with Belarusian speakers a small minority even in Minsk.
