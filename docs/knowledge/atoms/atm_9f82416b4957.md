# Nation-state street renaming over religious/imperial layers

`atm_9f82416b4957` · **culture** / global

Many post-imperial or post-revolutionary states overlay secular nationalist street names atop older religious or dynastic urban fabric: Iran's mix of Islamic Revolution-era names near Qajar/Pahlavi landmarks, Egypt's Nasser-era renamings around Cairo's Mamluk mosques, and post-Soviet Central Asian cities naming boulevards after national heroes near Islamic shrines. This layering visually encodes a state's shift from religious/dynastic to secular-nationalist legitimacy.
