# BC provincial highway shield distinct from US state highway markers

`atm_e5882c19fe5d` · **culture** / polity · cell `c2b6twp`

British Columbia uses a plain white rectangular shield for its numbered highways (e.g. Highway 99, the Sea-to-Sky Highway), visually distinct from US state highway shields such as Washington's George-Washington-silhouette marker. On maps this shield shape is a reliable discriminator between BC and adjacent US Pacific states.
