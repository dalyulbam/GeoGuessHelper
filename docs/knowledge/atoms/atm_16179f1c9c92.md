# Commune-committee blue guide signs mark a commune strip, not a district town

`atm_16179f1c9c92` · **geography** / region · cell `w7g64pd`

On Vietnamese provincial/national roads a small blue panel giving a People's Committee at a few hundred metres and the district town many kilometres away means you are on an outlying commune strip. In the district town itself you would instead see continuous shopfronts, traffic signals and a signed administrative complex without such a distance panel.
