# Kanto loam is dark/reddish, not pale brown

`atm_9f4060f146e8` · **geography** / region · cell `xn9k2th`

Cut banks in the Kanto upland typically expose dark humic Kanto loam over orange-brown tephra, a distinctly coloured volcanic profile. Pale brown to grey-brown weathered sedimentary/mudstone banks like these are instead typical of the Sea-of-Japan-side Tertiary hill country including Noto, so pale loam argues against Kanto rather than for it.
