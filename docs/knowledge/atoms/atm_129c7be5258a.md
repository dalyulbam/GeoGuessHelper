# Nordic personal unions and Nordic states

`atm_129c7be5258a` · **history** / region

The Duchy of Schleswig existed 1058-1864. The Kalmar Union linked Nordic kingdoms 1397-1523 (intermittent). Denmark-Norway was a personal union 1524-1814 (intermittent). The Kingdom of Norway (1814) was brief. The United Kingdoms of Sweden and Norway lasted 1814-1905. The Finnish Socialist Workers' Republic existed 1918-1919. Nordic unions were personal rather than unitary.
