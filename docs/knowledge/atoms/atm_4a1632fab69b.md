# Munich perimeter-block Gründerzeit-to-1950s residential fabric

`atm_4a1632fab69b` · **culture** / city · cell `u284p2g`

Central Munich districts like Schwabing-West are characterized by dense, uniform perimeter-block apartment buildings ranging from Gründerzeit (late 19th century) to 1950s-70s postwar infill, typically 5-6 storeys with ground-floor retail. This building-stock layering, combined with ongoing cranes/scaffolding for redevelopment, reflects sustained high housing demand and redevelopment pressure typical of prosperous German metropolitan cores.
