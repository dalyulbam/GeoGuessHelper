# German pharmacy 'Apotheke' emblem as national marker

`atm_e47ce946001b` · **architecture** / country · cell `u284p2g`

Germany's pharmacies are identified by a standardized red 'A' logo depicting a stylized vessel-and-snake (the Bundesvereinigung Deutscher Apothekerverbände emblem), distinct from Austria's green/white cross-and-snake Apotheke sign and Switzerland's green-cross pharmacy mark. This kind of pharmacy-signage divergence is a reliable way to distinguish German-speaking countries in street-level imagery.
