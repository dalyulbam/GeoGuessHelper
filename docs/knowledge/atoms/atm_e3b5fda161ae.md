# Three-way physiographic zonation of Bolivia: altiplano, inter-Andean valleys, lowlands

`atm_e3b5fda161ae` · **geography** / country · cell `6s9edy6`

Bolivia divides into three distinct physiographic and cultural zones: the high, cold, treeless altiplano (La Paz, El Alto, Oruro, above ~3,600-4,000m) with adobe/brick block housing and sparse vegetation; the temperate inter-Andean valleys (Cochabamba, Sucre, Tarija, roughly 1,800-2,800m) with mature shade trees, milder winters, and eroded ochre hillside terrain; and the humid tropical eastern lowlands (Santa Cruz, Trinidad) with palm vegetation, flat terrain, and motorcycle-dominated transit. Each zone has characteristic vegetation, transit fleet, and building typology that allow rapid visual discrimination.
