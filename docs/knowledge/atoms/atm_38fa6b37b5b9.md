# Filipino bahay kubo as national vernacular icon

`atm_38fa6b37b5b9` · **architecture** / region · cell `wdq9709`

The bahay kubo (nipa hut) is the Philippines' iconic stilted bamboo-and-thatch dwelling, raised on posts with a steep thatch roof and woven bamboo (sawali) walls, adapted to tropical heat, rain and flooding. It is a recognized national cultural symbol distinct from Spanish-colonial stone bahay na bato townhouses.
