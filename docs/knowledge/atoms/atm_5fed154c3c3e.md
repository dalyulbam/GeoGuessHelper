# Mudéjar parish churches built on former mosque sites in Granada region towns

`atm_5fed154c3c3e` · **architecture** / region · cell `eyt7v0d`

Following the Reconquista of the Kingdom of Granada (1492), many town parish churches in the region were built in Mudéjar style directly atop the foundations of former mosques, a common conversion pattern across former Nasrid territory. Example: the 16th-century Nuestra Señora de la Encarnación church in Albolote, designed by architect Ambrosio de Vico on a former mosque site, is a protected national historic-artistic monument.
