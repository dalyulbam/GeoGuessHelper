# Post-socialist heritage and renewal reactions to panel estates

`atm_e70740d7190f` · **culture** / global

Since the 1990s, cities across the former Eastern Bloc have responded to panel-estate decline with contrasting strategies: Germany's 'Stadtumbau Ost' program demolished surplus Plattenbau units in shrinking eastern cities, while other estates underwent colorful thermal-insulation retrofits (e.g. in Slovakia and Poland) to extend their life. Some, like sections of Berlin's Karl-Marx-Allee, gained heritage-protected status as valued architectural ensembles rather than being demolished.
