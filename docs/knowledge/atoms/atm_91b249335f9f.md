# Orthography vs terrain: splitting Iceland from the Faroes and other volcanic isles

`atm_91b249335f9f` · **language** / region

Among treeless volcanic landscapes (Iceland, Faroe Islands, Hawaii, Azores), the presence of the letter ð in signage or toponyms narrows candidates to Iceland or the Faroes, since only these living languages retain eth. Between those two, terrain discriminates: Iceland shows bare lava-field plains, whereas the Faroes show grass-covered volcanic hills instead; Faroese toponym suffixes like -fjørður further mark the Faroes.
