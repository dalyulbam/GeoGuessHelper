# House of Nahyan (UAE/Abu Dhabi)

`atm_051e28444450` · **history** / polity

The House of Nahyan is the ruling dynasty of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi, founded by Sheikh Dhiyab bin Isa Al Nahyan, originating in Liwa Oasis. Its head, currently Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, holds the elected office of President of the United Arab Emirates since 1971.
