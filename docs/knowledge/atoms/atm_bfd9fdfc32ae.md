# Republic of China, Mainland period (1912-1949)

`atm_bfd9fdfc32ae` · **history** / country

**시기**: 1912 ~ 1949

Existed until 7 December 1949; its territory is now controlled by the People's Republic of China (Mainland China), Mongolia, portions claimed by India and Japan, and parts of Afghanistan, Bhutan, Pakistan, Russia and Tajikistan. The rump state 'Republic of China' continues on Taiwan and Penghu (acquired from Japan in 1945) plus Kinmen and Matsu.
