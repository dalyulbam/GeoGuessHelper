# Later Iranian dynasties and khanates (14th-20th c.)

`atm_09be58f5b64b` · **history** / region

Iran and surrounding regions saw successive dynasties: Muzaffarid (1314-1393, Arabs), House of Inju (1335-1357, Persians), Chobanids (1335-1357, Taichiud Mongols), Jalairid Sultanate (1336-1432, Jalair Mongols), Afrasiyab dynasty (1349-1504), Marashiyan (1359-1596, Mazanderani), Timurid dynasty (1370-1507, Barlas Mongols), Kar-Kiya dynasty (1370s-1592), Qara Qoyunlu (1378-1468, Yiwa/Baharlu), Aq Qoyunlu (1378-1497, Bayandur Oghuz Turks), Emirate of Hakkari (14th c.-1847, Kurds), Ardalan (14th c.-1868, Kurds), Mukriyan (1400-1800, Kurds), Principality of Mahmudi (1406-1839, Kurds), Zarrinnaal (1448-1925, Kurds), Safavid dynasty (1501-1722, 1729-1736, 1749-1750, Bayandur Oghuz Turks), Emirate of Bradost (1510-1609, Kurds), Principality of Pinyanisi (1548-1823, Kurds), Hotak dynasty (1722-1729, Ghilji Pashtuns), Afsharid dynasty (1736-1749, 1750-1796, Afshar Oghuz Turks), Sarab Khanate (1747-?, Shekak Kurds), Zand dynasty (1751-1794, Zand Kurds), Tabriz Khanate (1757-1799, Donboli Kurds), Qajar dynasty (1789-1925, Qajar Oghuz Turks), Hasan Khan dynasty (1795-1820, Kurds), Emirate of Muhammara (19th c.-1925), Pahlavi dynasty (1925-1979, Mazanderani), and Bazrangi dynasty (dates unknown).
