# United Arab Republic and United Arab States (1958-1961/1971)

`atm_2af5641de17d` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1958 ~ 1971

The United Arab Republic was a union formed by Egypt and Syria in 1958, dissolved in 1961, though Egypt used the name until 1971. Other Pan-Arab unity agreements with Iraq and Jordan in the 1950s failed. The United Arab States was a confederation formed by the United Arab Republic and North Yemen (Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen) in 1958, dissolved in 1961.
