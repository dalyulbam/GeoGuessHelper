# Shared supranational highway numbering systems

`atm_fa72e793a49c` · **geography** / global

Beyond the Central American CA-route system, other regions use shared cross-border route numbering: the Pan-American Highway network spans from Alaska to Argentina, the European E-road network overlays national numbering with green 'E' signs, and the Asian Highway network (AH1, AH2...) links countries from Turkey to Japan. These systems coexist with national numbering, and their signage color/style (e.g. green E-road shields in Europe) is a reliable clue to region even when language is unfamiliar.
