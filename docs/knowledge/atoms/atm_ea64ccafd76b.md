# Municipal fingerpost generics separate Italy from Spain and France

`atm_ea64ccafd76b` · **language** / country · cell `spznmb0`

Italian white municipal fingerposts carry generics like 'Impianti sportivi', 'biblioteca comunale' and 'stazione ecologica' in thin-bordered plates. In Spain you would read 'Instalaciones deportivas'/'Punto limpio' with DGT typography, and in France 'Salle des fêtes'/'Déchetterie' with 'toutes directions' panels instead.
