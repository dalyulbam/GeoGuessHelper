# Oy vs AB/AS/OÜ: company suffix pins Finland among Nordic-Baltic lookalikes

`atm_b227ef35bd59` · **language** / country · cell `u6xzgj3`

On vehicles, silos and shopfronts in Finland you see 'Oy' (or 'Oyj') after the company name, often with Finnish stems like 'liikenne' or 'kuljetus'. In Sweden you would instead see 'AB', in Norway and Estonia 'AS', and in Estonia also 'OÜ' with stems like 'transport'/'liiklus'. A single painted suffix therefore resolves Finland vs Sweden/Estonia even without any road furniture.
