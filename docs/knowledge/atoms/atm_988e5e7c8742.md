# Hausruck coal basin transition to commuter economy

`atm_988e5e7c8742` · **economy** / region · cell `u299pj6`

**시기**: 1900 ~ 2020

The Hausruck region of Upper Austria was historically a lignite (brown coal) mining basin. Following mine closures, the local economy shifted to small-scale family farming combined with commuter employment toward nearby industrial/administrative towns such as Vöcklabruck, Wels, and Attnang-Puchheim, producing prosperous dispersed hamlets of owner-occupied detached housing rather than dense industrial settlement.
