# Malaysian urban high-rise housing displacing kampung vernacular

Rapid urbanization in Malaysia since the 1970s, driven by the New Economic Policy and industrial growth around Kuala Lumpur, Penang and Johor Bahru, has replaced kampung timber houses with concrete low-cost flats and high-rise condominiums. Kampung Baru in central Kuala Lumpur remains a contested enclave of traditional Malay land tenure surrounded by skyscrapers, symbolizing tension between vernacular heritage and redevelopment pressure.
