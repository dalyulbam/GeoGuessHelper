# Glacial ribbon lakes at mountain fronts worldwide

`atm_784d91e88be1` · **geography** / global

Lago Maggiore belongs to a global family of glacially-carved ribbon lakes formed where alpine glaciers debouched onto lowland plains, overdeepening long narrow basins. Parallel examples include the English Lake District's Windermere and Ullswater, New Zealand's Lake Wakatipu at the Southern Alps, and Patagonia's Lago Nahuel Huapi and Lago Argentino at the Andean front. All share a characteristic profile of steep, fjord-like upper reaches narrowing toward mountains and broader, gentler lowland outlets.
