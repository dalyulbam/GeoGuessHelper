# Chüy Valley as Kyrgyzstan's principal breadbasket

`atm_5a1490e2317e` · **economy** / region · cell `txt5gy4`

The Chüy Valley in northern Kyrgyzstan, an irrigated piedmont plain at the foot of the Tian Shan, is the country's largest and most productive agricultural region, supplying grain, vegetables, fruit, eggs, dairy, sugar beets and livestock products nationally. Villages in the valley combine small-scale family-plot farming and livestock with informal trade, and increasingly function as commuter suburbs feeding into nearby Bishkek.
