# Egyptian historical periods

`atm_10052c456892` · **history** / country

Egyptian periodization runs from Prehistoric Egypt (pre-3150 BC) through the Dynastic Period: Early Dynastic/Archaic Period (3150-2686 BC), Old Kingdom (2686-2181 BC), First Intermediate Period (2181-2055 BC), Middle Kingdom (2055-1650 BC), Second Intermediate Period (1650-1550 BC), New Kingdom (1550-1069 BC), Third Intermediate Period (1069-664 BC). Antiquity includes the Late Period (664-332 BC), Argead and Ptolemaic dynasties (332-30 BC), Aegyptus (Roman Egypt, 30 BC-641 AD) including Sasanian Egypt (619-629), and the Coptic period (300-900 AD). Islamic Egypt includes Rashidun (641-661), Umayyad (661-750), and Abbasid (750-868, 905-935) rule. Medieval Egypt includes the Tulunid dynasty (868-905), Ikhshidid dynasty (935-969), Fatimid Dynasty (969-1171), Ayyubid Dynasty (1171-1250), and Mamluk dynasties: Bahri (1250-1382) and Burji (1382-1517). Modern Egypt includes Ottoman Egypt (1517-1867) and the Muhammad Ali dynasty (1805-1953), comprising the Khedivate of Egypt (1867-1914) and Sultanate of Egypt (1914-1922). Contemporary Egypt includes the Kingdom of Egypt (1922-1953) and the Republican Egypt (Arab Republic of Egypt, 1953-present).
