# Eight States of Xishan and other Chinese frontier tribal polities

`atm_b3d50615bd66` · **history** / region

The Eight States of Xishan comprised Buzu, Gelin, Ruoshui, Xidong, Nanshui, Duoba, Qingyuan, and Baigou, all undated. Other related undated frontier polities include Zangke, Kunwu (House of Ji, Huaxia descent), Luodian, Meishan Dong (split into Left Wing under House of Fu and Right Wing under House of Dun), Xi Dong (House of Yang), Yizi (House of Ji), Qiongdu, Xi, Azhe, Dilie, Wugu, and Suo.
