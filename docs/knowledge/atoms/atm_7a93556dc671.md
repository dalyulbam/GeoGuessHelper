# Post-Soviet roadside furniture vocabulary (GOST standard)

`atm_7a93556dc671` · **culture** / polity · cell `v0nxmzr`

Former Soviet states share a distinctive roadside sign and delineator vocabulary derived from GOST standards: black-and-white striped concrete delineator posts, triangular red-bordered warning signs, and A-frame braced wooden power poles along shoulderless two-lane roads. This vocabulary differs sharply from North American (green mileposts, W-beam guardrail, wide gravel shoulders), Australian (yellow diamond warnings), or North/West African (bilingual Arabic-French signage) roadside systems, making it a strong first-pass discriminator for placing a scene in the former USSR.
