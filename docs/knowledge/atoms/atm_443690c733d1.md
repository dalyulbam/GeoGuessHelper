# Quiriguá Maya archaeological site — Motagua School stelae

`atm_443690c733d1` · **culture** / region · cell `d4bb4ty`

Quiriguá, a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1981) near Guatemala's CA-9 highway in Izabal department, contains monumental Maya carved stelae exceeding 8m in height; Stela E stands 10.6m tall and weighs roughly 65 tons, representing the pinnacle of the 'Motagua School' of Maya sculptural art. It is accessed via a short spur road off CA-9 through banana plantations.
