# Sicilian tyrants and dynasty

`atm_9906cb4e59de` · **history** / region

Ancient Syracuse in Sicily was ruled by tyrant dynasties: Deinomenids (485-465 BC), Dionysii (405-344 BC), Timoleon (345-337 BC), Agathocles (317-289 BC), and Hieronids (275-214 BC). The medieval Kalbids ruled Sicily (948-1053).
