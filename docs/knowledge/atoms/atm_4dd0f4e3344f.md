# Low treeline and alpine ridges in Arctic Norway (Troms/Finnmark)

`atm_4dd0f4e3344f` · **geography** / region · cell `ukmkre5`

In North Norway above the Arctic Circle (Troms, Finnmark), the treeline sits remarkably low, around 200-300m elevation, with birch-dominated subarctic forest giving way quickly to bare, often snow-streaked alpine ridges even in mid-summer (July). This contrasts with southern Norway (Vestland/Oslo region), where coniferous forest extends to much higher altitudes and summer snow is rare at comparable latitudes-equivalent terrain.
