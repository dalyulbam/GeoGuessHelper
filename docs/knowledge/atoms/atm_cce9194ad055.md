# Northern Apennine foothill morphology vs. Po plain and Alps

`atm_cce9194ad055` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb3`

The transition zone where the Northern Apennines meet the Po plain in Emilia-Romagna is marked by low rounded hills of marl and clay, hay-cut slopes, and mixed oak woodland, cut by broad white-shingle braided gravel torrent beds. This contrasts with the dead-flat, canal-and-poplar landscape of the Po plain proper and the steeper rock walls, deeper conifer cover, and snow/avalanche infrastructure of the Alps.
