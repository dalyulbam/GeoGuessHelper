# Steep-slope heroic viticulture appellations worldwide

`atm_cff59a9d0f8a` · **economy** / global

Extremely steep, hand-cultivated vineyard terrain defines several prestige wine regions: the Mosel Valley in Germany (slopes up to 65 degrees, Riesling), the Douro Valley in Portugal (terraced schist slopes for Port wine), Priorat in Catalonia (llicorella slate slopes, Garnacha), and the Wachau in Austria. These 'heroic viticulture' zones share mechanization difficulty, terracing or dry-stone walls, and appellation systems protecting low-yield, high-quality wine.
