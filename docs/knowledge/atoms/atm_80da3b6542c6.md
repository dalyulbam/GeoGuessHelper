# Polycentric coal-steel conurbations without a single dominant city

`atm_80da3b6542c6` · **history** / global

The Upper Silesian pattern of a dense conurbation formed by merging separate coal and steel towns without one overwhelming metropole parallels the Ruhr Area in Germany (Essen, Dortmund, Duisburg, Bochum), the Donbas in Ukraine (Donetsk, Makiivka, Horlivka), and the Black Country in England near Birmingham. Each arose from 19th-century coal-iron industrialization producing a fused urban region governed by multiple municipal authorities rather than one city.
