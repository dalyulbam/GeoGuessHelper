# Piedmont colonization church vs Andean colonial church

`atm_a4c8d01b5267` · **architecture** / region · cell `d2g8r9x`

Mid-20th-century Meta/Orinoquia piedmont plaza churches are plastered concrete with slim rectilinear twin towers, flat pilaster strips and bright blue/yellow trim on white. In an Andean interior town (Boyacá, eastern Antioquia) you would instead see stone or heavy adobe masonry, a single deep-set baroque portal, clay-tile roofs and a colonial two-storey balconied plaza around it. Church fabric therefore separates colonization-era lowland towns from colonial highland ones.
