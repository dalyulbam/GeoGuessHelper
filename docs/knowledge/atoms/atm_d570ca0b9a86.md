# Mongolian Plateau steppe polities (330 BCE–1924)

`atm_d570ca0b9a86` · **history** / region

A succession of steppe polities ruled the Mongolian Plateau: Rouran Khaganate (330-555), Ashina tribe/Turkic Khaganates (552-630, 682-744), Xueyantuo (628-646), Tang dynasty rule via Protectorate to Pacify the North (647-682), Türgesh Khaganate (699-766), Uyghur Khaganate (744-840), Yenisei Kyrgyz Khaganate (840-1207) with Western Liao/Qara Khitai (1124-1218), Khamag Mongol (10th c.-1206), Keraite state (11th-13th c.), Naiman state (?-1206). The Mongol Empire (1206-1368) succeeded these, followed by the Yuan dynasty (1271-1368) ruling Mongolia as Lingbei Province, then the Northern Yuan (1368-1635) with successor khanates Altan Khanate (1609-1691), Tüsheet Khanate, Setsen Khanate, Jasaghtu Khanate (1691-1923). The Alliance of the Four Oirats (1399-1634) and the Bogd Khanate of Mongolia (1911-1919, 1921-1924) also arose.
