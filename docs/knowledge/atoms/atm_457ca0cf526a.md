Meta/Casanare piedmont towns show fully paved concrete-slab streets with planted medians, whitewashed tree trunks, trimmed mango and ceiba canopy and painted masonry houses under zinc. In an Amazonian frontier town (Caquetá, Putumayo, Amazonas) you would instead see more unpaved or mud-stained streets, denser continuous rainforest canopy at the town edge, more timber construction and no dry-season leafless trees. Seasonal deciduous trees standing next to evergreen ones is a savanna marker.
