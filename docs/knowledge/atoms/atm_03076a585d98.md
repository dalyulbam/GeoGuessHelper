# Bolivian telecom/finance operator markers as country identifiers

`atm_03076a585d98` · **economy** / country · cell `6s9edy6`

Bolivia's telecom market is dominated by ENTEL (the former state incumbent, often hand-painted on house walls in rural and peri-urban areas) and Tigo, whose 'Tigo Money' cash-in/cash-out agent signage is ubiquitous even in small shops. This operator combination is distinct from neighboring countries: Peru uses Movistar/Claro/Bitel, Ecuador uses CNT/Claro with USD pricing, and Argentina uses Personal/Claro/Movistar. Spotting ENTEL plus Tigo Money together is a strong discriminator for Bolivia specifically.
