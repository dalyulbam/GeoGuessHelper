# Provincial Nigerian market-town core: roundabout, motor park, mission institutions

`atm_643367ff9498` · **culture** / city · cell `s1nmbk3`

Small inland Nigerian administrative/market towns typically organize around a compact central grid featuring a signalised roundabout (often solar-lit), a motor park for shared-taxi/minibus transit, a colonial-era mission institution (cathedral, mission road), and mixed two-storey commercial buildings with a fuel station at the main junction. Paved investment concentrates at this core while side streets remain unpaved laterite.
