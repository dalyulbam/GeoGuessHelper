# Nigerian Pidgin billboard copy vs Krio or Cameroonian Pidgin

`atm_73e57a387697` · **language** / country · cell `s1nmbk6`

In Nigeria, advertising and campaign billboards freely use Pidgin phrasing such as 'no be ... name' rendered in standard English orthography alongside fully English business signage. In Sierra Leone or Liberia you would instead see Krio spellings ('na', 'dem', 'tinap') and far less signalised infrastructure; in anglophone Cameroon French words ('essence', 'Ets.', 'Sarl') would appear even in Pidgin-speaking towns.
