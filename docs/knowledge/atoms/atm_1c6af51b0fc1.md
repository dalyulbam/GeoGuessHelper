# Aegean olive-maquis hinterland vs Taurus/Antalya front

`atm_1c6af51b0fc1` · **nature** / region · cell `swesgsx`

Inland Muğla/Aydın shows rounded granite–limestone knolls under kermes-oak maquis with dry-farmed smallholder olive groves, stone pines and planted river red gum eucalyptus along the highway. On the Antalya/Taurus front you would instead see tall bare karst limestone walls, red terra-rossa soils, citrus orchards and greenhouse plastic. Rounded relief plus olive-maquis therefore favours the Aegean belt.
