# Post-WWII forced population transfers and territorial reassignment in Central/Eastern Europe

`atm_db47f02242c9` · **history** / global

**시기**: 1945 ~ 1950

Poland's Recovered Territories resettlement parallels other post-1945 forced transfers: the expulsion of Sudeten Germans from Czechoslovakia's border regions (Sudetenland) resettled with Czechs and Slovaks, and the Soviet annexation of northern East Prussia (becoming Kaliningrad Oblast) with German population expelled and replaced by Russian settlers. All three cases left German-built architectural stock (Gothic brick churches, Fachwerk houses, Prussian civic buildings) inhabited by populations with no ancestral tie to that building tradition.
