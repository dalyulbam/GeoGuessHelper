# Three-age prehistoric system and general historical eras

`atm_f3e4d31173e9` · **history** / global

Prehistory spans from the appearance of Homo (c. 3 million years ago) to the invention of writing (c. 5000 years ago for the Ancient Near East), divided into Paleolithic (Lower, Middle, Upper), Mesolithic/Epipaleolithic, Neolithic (from c. 10,200 BC), Chalcolithic/Eneolithic/Copper Age, Bronze Age, and Iron Age. Ancient history follows, including Classical antiquity (Greco-Roman world), then Post-classical history (following the fall of Han China 220, Western Roman Empire 476, Gupta Empire 550s, Sasanian Empire 651), the Middle Ages (5th-15th century, subdivided into Early, High, and Late Middle Ages), and Modern history comprising the Early modern period (from c. 1453/1500), Late modern period (from mid-18th century), and Contemporary history (from c. 1945).
