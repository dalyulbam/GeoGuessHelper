# Dynasties and khanates of Central Asia (3rd century BC–1917 AD)

`atm_8c2c15c66118` · **history** / region

Central Asia was ruled successively by the House of Diodotus (255–223 BC), Euthydemid dynasty (230 BC–AD 10), Luandi/Xiongnu (209 BC–AD 93), House of Eucratides (170–130 BC), Xianbei state (c.93–234), Sasanian dynasty (224–651), Afrighid dynasty (305–995), Kidarites (320–500), Ashina tribe/Turkic Khaganates (552–657), Tokhara Yabghus (625–758), Tang dynasty (640–690, 705–790) interrupted by Wu Zhou (690–705), Ikhshids of Sogdia (642–755), Bukhar Khudahs (681–890), Ajo clan/Yenisei Kyrgyz Khaganate (693–1207), Principality of Chaghaniyan, Principality of Khuttal, Principality of Farghana, Karluk Yabghu State (756–840), Oghuz Yabgu State (766–1055), Samanid Empire (819–999), Kara-Khanid Khanate (840–1212, split Eastern 1032–1210 and Western 1041–1212), Banijurids (848–908), Ghurid dynasty (879–1215), Principality of Ushrusana (to 892), Ghaznavid dynasty (977–1186), Ma'munid dynasty (995–1017), Muhtajids (10th–11th century), Seljuq dynasty/Seljuk Empire (1037–1194), Anushtegin dynasty/Khwarazmian Empire (1077–1231), Western Liao (Qara Khitai, 1124–1218), Mongol Empire (1207–1368) with House of Ögedei (1225–1309), Chagatai Khanate (1225–1346, then Western Chagatai to 1402 and Moghulistan 1347–1680 with Yarkent Khanate 1514–1705), Golden Horde (1242–1502) with Shaybanid dynasty (1428–1883) and Manghit dynasty/Emirate of Bukhara (1785–1920) and Nogai Horde (1440s–1634), Kazakh Khanate (1465–1848) with Bukey Horde (1801–1849), Janid dynasty (1599–1747), Ilkhanate (1256–1353), Kart dynasty (1244–1381), Sufi dynasty (1361–1379), Timurid Empire (1370–1507), Khanate of Khiva (1511–1920), Hotak dynasty (1709–1738), Khanate of Kokand (1709–1876), Durrani Empire (1747–1826), Qing dynasty rule (1759–1912), Sarybagysh dynasty/Kara-Kyrgyz Khanate (1842–1854), and House of Holstein-Gottorp-Romanov Russian rule (1867–1917).
