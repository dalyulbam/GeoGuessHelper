# Southeast Asian stilt-house tradition family

`atm_128968f951fe` · **architecture** / global

Stilted timber housing raised above flood, heat and pest risk is a shared vernacular across maritime Southeast Asia: the Malay rumah kampung, Indonesian rumah panggung and Toraja tongkonan, Thai reuan thai, Filipino bahay kubo, and Vietnamese stilt houses of ethnic minorities in the northern highlands. All share raised floors, steep or wide-eaved roofs for monsoon rain shedding, and open under-house space used for storage, livestock or work.
