# Croatian coastal road/place lexicon: Jadranska Magistrala, Plaža, Spilja

`atm_29b9d2dea9c4` · **culture** / country · cell `srsff7q`

Croatian coastal signage and maps commonly use the terms 'Jadranska Magistrala' (Adriatic coastal highway/D8), 'Plaža' (beach) and 'Spilja' (cave), alongside 'Konoba' for taverns. These Latin-script terms with Croatian diacritics (Š, ž, č) are reliable linguistic markers distinguishing Croatia from Italian (SS roads, trattoria), Montenegrin (bilingual Cyrillic, Jadranski put) and Albanian (Rruga) coastal signage.
