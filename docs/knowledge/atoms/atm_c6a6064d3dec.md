# Serbia's bilingual Latin-Cyrillic signage practice

`atm_c6a6064d3dec` · **language** / country

Unlike Bulgaria's Cyrillic-only administrative signage, Serbia officially uses both Cyrillic and Latin (Gaj's Latin alphabet) scripts interchangeably in daily life and often bilingually on signs, a practice rooted in 20th-century Yugoslav-era standardization that diverges from Bulgaria's stricter single-script convention.
