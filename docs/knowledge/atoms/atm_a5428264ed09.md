# North Atlantic island vs continental subarctic terrain discrimination

`atm_a5428264ed09` · **geography** / region · cell `ge9h53m`

At subarctic latitudes, a treeless, glacially-scoured landscape with flat-topped basalt table mountains, planted conifer shelterbelts (not natural forest), and wide shallow fjords indicates a North Atlantic volcanic island setting (Iceland/Faroes) rather than continental Scandinavia (which shows boreal forest and hedgerowed farmland at the same latitude) or North America (which uses yellow centre lines and different sign fonts vs. white dashed lines).
