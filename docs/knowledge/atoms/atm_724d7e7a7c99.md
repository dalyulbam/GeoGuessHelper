# Vietnamese shop signs print full four-level addresses; Laos/Thailand signs do not use Latin diacritics

`atm_724d7e7a7c99` · **language** / country · cell `w7g64pd`

In Vietnam commercial signs habitually end with a hamlet–commune–district–province chain in diacritic-heavy Latin script, which alone fixes the province. In Laos or Thailand you would instead see Lao or Thai abugida lettering and no Latin tone marks, and addresses rarely spelled out in a four-level Latin chain.
