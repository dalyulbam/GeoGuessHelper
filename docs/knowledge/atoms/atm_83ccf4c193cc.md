# Decline of Mughal imperial patronage after Aurangzeb

`atm_83ccf4c193cc` · **history** / polity

After Aurangzeb's death in 1707, Mughal central authority fragmented rapidly, and large-scale imperial architectural patronage of the Taj Mahal/Bibi Ka Maqbara type largely ceased, replaced by regional successor-state patronage (Awadh, Hyderabad's Asaf Jahi dynasty, Bengal) that produced smaller, regionally distinct monuments rather than pan-imperial mausolea.
