# Negombo's 'Little Rome' Catholic Sinhala coastal belt

`atm_395187fe388d` · **culture** / region · cell `tc2c139`

The coastal strip around Negombo, Sri Lanka, is a rare Sinhala-speaking but predominantly Roman Catholic zone, earning the nickname 'Little Rome.' Institutional naming (churches, schools) is Christian rather than Buddhist, distinguishing it from the Sinhala-Buddhist interior even though the spoken language and script (Sinhala) are the same. This is a useful discriminator: Sinhala script plus dense Catholic institutional naming (St. Mary's, Maris Stella, saints' names) signals this specific coastal belt rather than a generic Sinhala-Buddhist village.
