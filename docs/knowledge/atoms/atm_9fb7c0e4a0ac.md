# South African road signage and address conventions as country signature

`atm_9fb7c0e4a0ac` · **geography** / country · cell `k9fpcsx`

South Africa uses English (often bilingual with Afrikaans) road signage with red-bordered inverted-triangle yield signs and a distinctive municipal telephone dialling code system (e.g. 041 for Nelson Mandela Bay/Port Elizabeth area, 011/012 Gauteng, 021/023 Western Cape, 031/033 KwaZulu-Natal). Municipal branding (e.g. 'Nelson Mandela Bay Municipality') and national retail chains like BUCO (hardware), Supa Quick (tyres) and Gascor (gas) are distinctive South African identifiers not found in neighbouring Namibia, Botswana or Zimbabwe.
