# Conquista del Desierto and the Ranquel frontier in La Pampa

`atm_99a388995ff6` · **history** / region · cell `6993z1r`

**시기**: 1870 ~ 1885

During the 19th century, Argentina's Conquista del Desierto military campaigns pushed the national frontier into indigenous Ranquel territory in present-day La Pampa Province, establishing forts (fortines) that later became founding settlements. Leubucó, near Victorica, was the historic seat of Ranquel cacique power (notably cacique Mariano Rosas), and towns like Victorica (founded 1882 on the site of Fortín Resina) trace their origin to this frontier conquest, giving the region a layered Ranquel-criollo cultural identity.
