# Planted spruce shelterbelts on treeless heath mark Iceland, not Norway

`atm_e857853e0979` · **nature** / country · cell `ge9h53s`

In Iceland a subarctic village shows near-treeless grass heath with sharply bounded rectangular blocks of planted spruce and low willow/birch scrub. In mainland Norway at the same latitude you would instead see continuous natural boreal forest reaching the shoreline and fenced hedgerowed farmland. Isolated dark conifer rows against bare grassland are therefore an Icelandic afforestation signature.
