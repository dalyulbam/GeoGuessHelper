# Southwest China tribal chiefdoms under Yuan-Ming rule (1215-1959)

`atm_c6a5b820a02e` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1215 ~ 1959

A large set of hereditary native chieftaincies (tusi) in Yunnan, Sichuan, Guizhou and Hubei/Hunan under Yuan and Ming suzerainty, including Möng Kawng (1215-1796), Kingdom of Mangalai (1220-1877), Laduijiang (1227-1610), Chiefdom of Luomeng (1255-1270, House of Uriankhai), Chiefdom of Menggen/Kengtung (1263-1959, House of Dao, Dai descent), Chiefdom of Wusa (1276-1666), Chiefdom of Wumeng (1276-1727), Chiefdom of Mangbu (1276-1727, House of Long, Yi descent), Chiefdom of Manyi (1276-19th century, House of Wen), Chiefdom of Malong Prefecture (1276-?, House of An, Yi descent), Mengzi County Tuxiancheng (1276-?, House of Li, Yi descent), Chiefdom of Tong'an Prefecture (1277-?, House of Gao, Bai descent), Chuxiong Tuxunjian (13th century-?, House of Yang, Mongol descent), Chiefdom of Rongmei (1310-1735, House of Tian, Tujia descent), Chiefdom of Shunning Prefecture (1327-?, House of Meng, Blang descent), Chiefdom of Mengqing (1327-?), and Babai Dengchu Xuanweisi (1331-?, 1346-?).
