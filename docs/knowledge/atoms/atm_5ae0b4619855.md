# National telephone area-code geography as a locator pattern

`atm_5ae0b4619855` · **geography** / global

Telephone area codes worldwide often map to specific cities or regions and can be used to infer location, similar to Argentina's 351 for Córdoba: Spain's 91 for Madrid, the UK's 0121 for Birmingham, and the US's original 1947 area-code plan assigning codes like 212 to Manhattan. These numbering plans typically reflect population density, with denser areas getting shorter or easily-dialed codes historically.
