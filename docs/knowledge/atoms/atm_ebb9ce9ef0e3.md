# Forty Chiefdoms of Yushu and Neighboring Regions (Qinghai/Tibet)

`atm_ebb9ce9ef0e3` · **history** / region

The Forty Chiefdoms of Yushu and Neighboring Regions was an umbrella grouping of tusi chiefdoms in the Yushu/Kham region, undated. It included the Four Chiefdoms of Yushu (Jiangsai, Zongju, Yala, Rongmo); Upper and Lower Alakeshuo; Béri; Asake; Ayong; Niyamucuo; Gucha; Labu; the Three Chiefdoms of Zhawu (Zhawu, Lada, Buqing); Longbu; Chuilengduo'erduo; Upper, Middle and Lower Ge'erji; Ha'ershou; Upper and Lower Longba; Longdong; Chuohuo'er; Juebala; Su'ermang; Ye'erji; Liewang; Antu; Xingba; La'erji; Sangse'er; Bayannangqian; Dongba; Suluke; Chindu; Menggu'erjin; Yonghepu; Alike; and Banshi.
