# European corporate legal-form suffixes as country identifiers

`atm_fb73bf27bdb5` · **economy** / global

Corporate suffixes function as reliable country markers across Europe: Germany's 'GmbH' and 'AG', France's 'SARL' and 'SA', the UK's 'Ltd' and 'plc', Croatia's 'd.o.o.' and 'd.d.', and Slovenia's identical 'd.o.o.' (a shared Yugoslav-era legal legacy). Distinguishing Croatian 'd.d.' from Slovenian or Bosnian equivalents often requires additional context since the terminology originates from common former-Yugoslav commercial law.
