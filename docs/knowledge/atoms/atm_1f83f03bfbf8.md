# Xianbei tribal states: Duan, Tuoba, Yuwen, Longxi tribes

`atm_1f83f03bfbf8` · **history** / polity

Several Xianbei-descended tribal polities existed in northern China: the Duan tribe (250-356), ruled by the House of Duan, including subordinate Liaoxi (313-338), Duan Qi (350-356) and Duan Zhao (352); the Longxi tribe (265-376) ruled by the House of Qifu; the Tuoba tribe (until 310) and its successor state Dai (310-376), both ruled by the House of Tuoba; and the Yuwen tribe (until 345) ruled by the House of Yuwen.
