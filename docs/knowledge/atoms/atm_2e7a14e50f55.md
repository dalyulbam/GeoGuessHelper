# Aurangzeb and the later Mughal emperors

`atm_2e7a14e50f55` · **history** / polity

Aurangzeb (r. 1658-1707), the sixth Mughal emperor and father of Azam Shah who commissioned Bibi Ka Maqbara, expanded the empire to its greatest territorial extent but strained its finances through prolonged Deccan campaigns, a factor reflected in the smaller scale and cheaper materials of Bibi Ka Maqbara compared to his father Shah Jahan's Taj Mahal.
