# Slovenian and Bosnian road signage overlap with Croatian conventions

`atm_e433d79a595f` · **geography** / country

Slovenia and Bosnia and Herzegovina, both former Yugoslav republics, share Croatia's Latin script, diacritics (č, š, ž), and 'd.o.o.' company suffix, making road-sign-based country discrimination unreliable without additional cues like route-number prefixes ('A' motorways in Slovenia vs 'A' and 'D' in Croatia) or EU/non-EU border markers.
