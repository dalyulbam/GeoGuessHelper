# Dingač Tunnel (1975): community-built infrastructure for isolated vineyards

`atm_322a44091c44` · **history** / region · cell `srskkfj`

**시기**: 1975 ~ 1975

The Dingač Tunnel, a narrow one-lane 400-metre tunnel bored through the Pelješac ridge, opened on 29 November 1975 to connect the village of Potomje with the previously inaccessible Dingač vineyards on the peninsula's southern coast. Before its construction, grapes had to be carried by donkey along steep goat paths from Trstenik, taking over an hour. The project was locally conceived (by winegrower leader Vice Miličić), financed through the local winegrowing cooperative, and designed by a native son (Pavle Andričević) working for the Split-based construction firm Konstruktor — an example of grassroots agricultural-community infrastructure investment in socialist Yugoslavia.
