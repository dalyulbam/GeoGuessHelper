# English dynasties (Norman to Windsor)

`atm_c2dd9ee05b3d` · **history** / country

After Danish rule by House of Knýtlinga (1013-1014, 1016-1042) and House of Godwin (1066), England was ruled by House of Normandy (1066-1135), House of Blois (1135-1154), the Angevins (1154-1216) and House of Plantagenet (1216-1399) with cadet branches House of Lancaster (1399-1461, 1470-1471) and House of York (1461-1470, 1471-1485); House of Capet disputed the throne (1216-1217). Later houses: Tudor (1485-1603), Stuart (1603-1649, 1660-1714), Hanover (1714-1901), Saxe-Coburg and Gotha (1901-1917), and House of Windsor (1917-present).
