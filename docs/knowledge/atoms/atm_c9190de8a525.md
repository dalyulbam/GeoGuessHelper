# Anatolian beyliks, empires and early 20th century states

`atm_c9190de8a525` · **history** / region

Anatolia hosted the Empire of Trebizond (1204-1461), Emirate of Hasankeyf (1232-1524), Beylik of Karaman (1250-1487), Beylik of Candar (1292-1461), Beylik of Alaiye (1293-1471), the Ottoman Empire (c.1299-1923), Beylik of Bafra (14th c.-1460), Beylik of Dulkadir (1337-1522), Ramadanid Emirate (1352-1608), Kara Koyunlu (1374-1468), Aq Qoyunlu (1378-1501), and in the early 20th century: Republic of Van (1915-1918), Erzincan Soviet (1916-1921), Iğdır National Republic (1917-1920), Kars Republic (1918-1919), Oltu Council Government (1919-1920), Government of the Grand National Assembly (1920-1923), and Republic of Ararat (1927-1931).
