# Global lake-effect and sea-effect heavy snowbelt regions

`atm_fb5a69de595c` · **nature** / global

Hokuriku's snow pattern belongs to a global family of lake/sea-effect heavy-snow zones: the Tug Hill Plateau and Great Lakes snowbelt of New York/Ontario (lake-effect off Lake Ontario/Erie), and Hokkaido's Sea of Japan coast, all caused by cold continental air masses crossing relatively warm water and depositing intense orographic or convective snowfall on adjacent land.
