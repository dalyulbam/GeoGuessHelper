# Vienna's Danube flood-management triple-channel system

`atm_142d60e0141b` · **geography** / city · cell `u2edmpb`

Vienna's Danube crossing consists of three parallel water bodies: the main Danube channel (regulated), the Neue Donau (New Danube, an artificial flood-relief channel completed 1988 alongside Donauinsel), and the Alte Donau (Old Danube, a former meander cut off from the main river, now a still-water recreational lake with boathouses and swimming areas). This triple structure is unique among Austrian cities and distinguishes Vienna's riverscape from single-channel Danube cities like Linz or Graz's Mur.
