# River-divided twin-city capitals

`atm_59ecc2c796e2` · **geography** / global

Several European capitals grew from twin settlements unified across a river, mirroring Buda and Pest's 1873 merger: Prague's Malá Strana and Staré Město flank the Vltava, Bratislava sits along the Danube's twin banks, and Budapest itself unified Buda, Pest, and Óbuda. Such cities often preserve a historic hilltop/castle side versus a flat commercial side.
