# Islamist-leaning municipal renaming and re-Islamization of Turkish public space

`atm_062228b64e03` · **culture** / country

Since the 2000s, Turkey's AKP-led municipalities have periodically renamed streets and public spaces to honor Ottoman sultans and Islamic figures, and restored Ottoman-era mosque names and imagery, partially reversing the Kemalist secular naming convention. This reflects a broader push-pull between Kemalist secularism and Islamic-conservative revivalism in Turkish urban identity.
