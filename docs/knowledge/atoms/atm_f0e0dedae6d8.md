# Sri Lanka royal houses

`atm_f0e0dedae6d8` · **history** / country

Sri Lanka's Sinhala Kingdom (543 BC-AD 1597) was ruled successively by House of Vijaya, House of Lambakanna I (66-436), House of Moriya (463-691), House of Lambakanna II (691-1055), House of Vijayabahu, House of Kalinga, and House of Siri Sanga Bo (1220-1597). The Jaffna Kingdom (1232-1619) was ruled by the Savakan Lotus Dynasty, Aryacakravarti dynasty, and House of Siri Sanga Bo. The Kingdom of Kandy (1469-1815) was ruled by House of Siri Sanga Bo, Kandyan dynasty (1590-1739), and Nayaks of Kandy (1739-1815). The Kingdom of Sitawaka (1521-1594) was ruled by House of Siri Sanga Bo. The House of Windsor ruled Ceylon as a Commonwealth realm (1948-1972).
