# Albany thicket / Eastern Cape warm-temperate semi-arid biome

`atm_702faf957ae7` · **culture** / region · cell `k9fpcsx`

The Albany thicket biome of the Eastern Cape, South Africa, is a warm-temperate, semi-arid coastal-to-inland vegetation type characterised by dry, sun-bleached veld grasses interspersed with date palms and eucalypts (the latter introduced), under a typically clear blue winter sky. It differs from the subtropical lushness of KwaZulu-Natal and the Mediterranean fynbos/vineyard landscape of the Western Cape.
