# Nine Clans of Zhaowu (Sogdian city-states) and related Zhaowu-ruled states

`atm_a9de1c07095a` · **history** / region

The Nine Clans of Zhaowu were Sogdian city-states ruled by the House of Zhaowu: Kang, Shi (史), An, Cao (with Eastern, Middle and Western branches), Shi (石), Mi, He, Huoxun, and Wudi. Other polities also ruled by the House of Zhaowu of Sogdian descent include Mu, Eastern An, Bi, Peihan, Nasebo, Wunahe, and Caojuzha. All undated.
