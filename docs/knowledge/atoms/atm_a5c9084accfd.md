# Italian company legal suffixes S.p.A. and S.r.l. as country markers

`atm_a5c9084accfd` · **economy** / country · cell `spyrs4d`

Italian companies use legal-form suffixes S.p.A. (Società per Azioni, joint-stock company) and S.r.l. (Società a responsabilità limitata, limited-liability company), visually distinct from Spanish S.A./S.L. or French SAS/SARL. POI labels bearing these suffixes on maps are a reliable indicator of Italy specifically among Romance-language countries.
