# Snow poles as global cold-climate road-marking convention

`atm_44a0d39c71c2` · **geography** / global

Countries with heavy snowfall and low-density rural road networks use tall roadside markers to indicate road edges and shoulder lines when snow obscures pavement. Norway, Sweden, Finland and northern Japan (Hokkaido) use similar reflective poles, often called 'snow poles' or 'delineator poles,' spaced at regular intervals on rural highways. In North America, Alaska, northern Canada, and states like Minnesota and Wisconsin use comparable flexible fiberglass snow poles along low-traffic roads instead of guardrails.
