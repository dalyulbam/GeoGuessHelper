# Global lignite basin belt: Lusatia, Rhineland, Latrobe Valley

`atm_55c87390ef27` · **economy** / global

Lignite mining basins with similar open-pit landscapes and post-industrial decline exist in Germany's Rhineland (Garzweiler, Hambach) and Lusatia (Lausitz) basins, and Australia's Latrobe Valley in Victoria. All share characteristic open-pit voids later flooded into artificial lakes, large-scale employment collapse after mine closures, and state-owned or formerly state-owned holding companies managing extraction and remediation.
