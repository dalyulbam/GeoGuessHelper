# French Polynesia island kingdoms

`atm_469162f8669b` · **history** / region

Austral Islands: Tamaeva dynasty (?-1900, Kingdom of Rimatara), Teuruarii dynasty (?-1900, Kingdom of Rurutu). Bass Islands: Line of monarchs of Rapa Iti (?-1881). Gambier Islands: Line of monarchs of Mangareva (?-1881). Marquesas Islands: De Thierry dynasty (1835-1837, Kingdom of Nuku Hiva). Society Islands: House of Tapoa (1778-1873, Kingdom of Bora Bora); Pōmare dynasty (1788-1808, 1815-1895, Kingdom of Tahiti and later Bora Bora); House of Tamatoa (1820-1884, 1888-1897, Kingdom of Raiatea); House of Teururai (1852-1895, Kingdom of Huahine and Raiatea).
