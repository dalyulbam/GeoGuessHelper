# Business address commune ≠ camera commune in rural Vietnam

`atm_c4d0a832b76f` · **economy** / region · cell `w7g64pd`

When two shop signs on the same strip name different communes, treat the shared district/province as solid but the commune as uncertain: Vietnamese traders often print their registered home commune. Anchor the commune instead on wayfinding signs or the local market/committee names, otherwise you drift a few kilometres along the corridor.
