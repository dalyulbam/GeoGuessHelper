# Immigrant entry-point districts across European cities

`atm_b57da26de8ba` · **economy** / global

Many European cities have historically industrial, dense tenement districts that serve as low-rent entry points for immigrants: Neukölln and Wedding in Berlin, Kreuzberg's Gastarbeiter heritage, Molenbeek in Brussels, and Belleville in Paris. These districts share Gründerzeit or equivalent 19th-century worker housing stock, lower rents, and successive waves of labor migration.
