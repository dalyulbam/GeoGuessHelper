# Vietnam's commune-level civic infrastructure and party/state signage

`atm_a829b13225ed` · **economy** / country · cell `w7g6609`

Rural Vietnamese communes (xã) display standardized state presence markers: a 'Ủy ban Nhân dân xã' (commune people's committee) signposted building, a commune health station (Trạm Y tế xã), rows of national flags and Communist Party pennants during campaigns, and social-insurance (BHXH) banners strung across roads promoting mandatory enrollment. These recur nationwide as a visual signature of Vietnam's single-party administrative structure at the grassroots level.
