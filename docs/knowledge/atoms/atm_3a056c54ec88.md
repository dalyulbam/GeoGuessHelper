# Dynasties of Champa (192–1822)

`atm_3a056c54ec88` · **history** / polity

Champa's royal dynasties were numbered sequentially: 1st dynasty (192–336), 2nd (336–420), 3rd (420–529), 4th (529–758), 5th (758–854), 6th (854–989), 7th (989–1044), 8th (1044–1074), 9th (1074–1139), 10th (1139–1145), 11th (1145–1190), 12th (1190–1318), 13th (1318–1390), 14th (1390–1458), 15th (1458–1471), and the Dynasty of Po Saktiraidaputih (1695–1822).
