# Low-cost private carrier competition against SAD monopolies

`atm_b748544d5f58` · **economy** / country

Since Slovakia's EU accession, private intercity coach operators such as FlixBus and RegioJet have entered routes historically monopolized by regional SAD companies, undercutting fares on major corridors like Bratislava–Košice. This has pressured SAD operators to modernize fleets and, in some regions, consolidate or receive continued public subsidy to maintain rural routes unprofitable for private entrants.
