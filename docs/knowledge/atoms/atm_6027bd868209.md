# Alpine mountain hut (Alpine Club) network

`atm_6027bd868209` · **culture** / global

Mountain huts (rifugi, Hütten, refuges) built by alpine clubs from the 19th century onward dot the Alps, Pyrenees, and Dinaric Alps, providing shelter for hikers and climbers on multi-day routes. Organizations like the Austrian Alpine Club (ÖAV), Deutscher Alpenverein, and Club Alpino Italiano built and still maintain thousands of such huts, typically at high altitude near passes and peaks.
