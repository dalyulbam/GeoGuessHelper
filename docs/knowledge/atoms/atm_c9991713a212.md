# Czechoslovak socialist-era single-industry towns (ZVL bearing factory model)

`atm_c9991713a212` · **economy** / polity · cell `u2v27rh`

Under Czechoslovakia's planned industrialization policy, many small towns were built around a single large state-owned factory that dominated local employment for decades. Kysucké Nové Mesto exemplifies this with ZVL (Závody presného strojárstva), a precision bearing factory founded in 1950 (later Kinex Bearings), which shaped the town's growth and remains a recognizable industrial legacy landmark today.
