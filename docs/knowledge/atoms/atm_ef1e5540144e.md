# Russian domestic fuel-retail brands as country identifiers

`atm_ef1e5540144e` · **economy** / polity · cell `v1uv16c`

Russia's highway fuel-retail landscape is dominated by domestic brands: Lukoil (with ЭКТО premium fuel line), Gazprom Neft (with 'Опти' truck-stop sub-brand), Tatneft (Tatarstan's regional oil major, flame logo in white-green-red), and Rosneft. Fuel grade boards commonly list ДТ (diesel), 92/95/100 (octane grades). These brand names, absent in Belarus (Belarusneft/A-100), Kazakhstan (KazMunayGas), or Ukraine (OKKO/WOG), let observers distinguish Russia from other post-Soviet Cyrillic states on sight.
