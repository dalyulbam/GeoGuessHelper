# Seasonal Adriatic tourism as replacement economy for lost forestry jobs

`atm_4c333db3423d` · **economy** / region

Since the 1970s-80s, coastal Croatian communities like those along the Velebit littoral and Lika-Senj County have shifted from forestry and cableway-based employment toward seasonal tourism (rental accommodation, hiking guiding in Paklenica and Northern Velebit national parks), though this replacement economy remains precarious due to short summer seasons and continued rural outmigration.
