# Norway's Geirangerfjord and Sognefjord

Geirangerfjord and Sognefjord are UNESCO-listed fjords on Norway's west coast, carved by Pleistocene glaciers into ancient bedrock, with walls rising over 1,000m directly from the water and cruise-ship tourism concentrated at their heads. Sognefjord is Norway's longest and deepest fjord, reaching depths over 1,300m.
