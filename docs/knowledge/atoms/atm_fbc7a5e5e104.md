# Kyoto City's mountain wards look like deep countryside

`atm_fbc7a5e5e104` · **geography** / region · cell `xn22nqj`

Keihoku and other annexed mountain areas of Ukyo Ward, Kyoto City are fully rural: single-lane paddy lanes, no shops, no bus shelters, no route markers, so a scene with zero urban cues can still be inside a designated-city boundary. Do not push such hamlets far out to Tanba or Hyogo just because they look remote; check whether ridges are close-packed forestry slopes typical of the Kyoto Kitayama fringe instead.
