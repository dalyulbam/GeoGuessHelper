# Ćiro narrow-gauge railway to Herzegovina (Austro-Hungarian era)

`atm_e1447da93736` · **history** / region · cell `srsfsxb`

**시기**: 1901 ~ 1996

The 'Ćiro' narrow-gauge railway connected the Dalmatian coast (Dubrovnik area) to the Herzegovinian hinterland, built under Austro-Hungarian administration. It was abandoned/closed in 1976 and its stations, such as Uskoplje, were left derelict and further damaged during the 1990s conflict, now standing as roofless stone ruins overgrown in maquis, symbolizing the contrast between prosperous coastal tourism economies and depopulated karst interior hinterlands.
