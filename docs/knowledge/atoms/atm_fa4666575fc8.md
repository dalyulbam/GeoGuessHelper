# Afro-indigenous Garífuna diaspora along the Central American Caribbean coast

`atm_fa4666575fc8` · **culture** / global

The Garífuna people, descended from shipwrecked/escaped African slaves who intermixed with Island Caribs on St. Vincent and were deported by the British in 1797 to Roatán, now inhabit coastal communities across Belize (Dangriga, Hopkins), Honduras (Trujillo, La Ceiba), Guatemala (Livingston), and Nicaragua, sharing the Garífuna language, punta music and dance, and UNESCO-recognized intangible cultural heritage status (proclaimed 2001).
