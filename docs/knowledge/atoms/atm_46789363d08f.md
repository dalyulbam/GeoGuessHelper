# Roman-era palace and Early Christian basilica ruins at Polače, Mljet

`atm_46789363d08f` · **history** / region · cell `srs76v3`

Polače, a sheltered natural harbor on Mljet island, preserves a large late-Roman palace complex (from which the village's name derives, "palača" meaning palace) along with Early Christian basilica ruins. The site evidences continuous use of the bay as a safe anchorage since antiquity, illustrating how Adriatic islands often layer Roman-era settlement remains beneath later medieval and modern coastal villages.
