# Japanese castle-town craft specialization network

`atm_bd5db3edc7de` · **culture** / global

**시기**: 1600 ~ 1974

Many Japanese cities preserve nationally designated traditional crafts rooted in Edo-period castle-town patronage, comparable to Takaoka's copperware: Kanazawa (gold leaf, Kutani ware), Tsubame-Sanjo (metalwork, cutlery), Nishijin in Kyoto (textile weaving), and Mino (Gifu, ceramics). These crafts share a lineage of daimyo-sponsored artisan guilds later designated as 'Traditional Craft' (Dentō Kōgei) under Japan's 1974 preservation law.
