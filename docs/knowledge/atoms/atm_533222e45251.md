# Sinaloa as Mexico's agricultural breadbasket

Sinaloa state is regarded as Mexico's most important agricultural region ('breadbasket'), producing a large share of the country's wheat, chickpeas, rice, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane, and tomatoes via irrigated farmland. Despite this agrarian identity, the state's leading income source at the macro level is the service sector (banking, trade, tourism).
