# Spanish 'Salida' vs Portuguese/French/Italian exit signage terms

`atm_ad6ef340394d` · **language** / country · cell `eyf77vt`

Spanish uses 'Salida' for 'exit' on wayfinding signage, distinct from Portuguese 'Saída' (with acute accent), French 'Sortie', and Italian 'Uscita'. This lexical difference is a reliable discriminator for identifying the language/country of interior signage in Iberian and Romance-language public buildings or exhibition venues.
