# Ban/Nong toponyms vs Khmer Prasat/Ta- toponyms inside Isan

`atm_9a93da5618de` · **language** / region · cell `w71dkzn`

In the Lao-Isan north of the Khorat Plateau, village and subdistrict names on signs use 'Ban' and 'Nong' elements. In the Khmer-influenced southern Isan belt (Surin, Buriram, Si Saket) you would instead see 'Prasat', 'Ta-' and 'Chorak' names alongside Angkorian laterite ruins.
