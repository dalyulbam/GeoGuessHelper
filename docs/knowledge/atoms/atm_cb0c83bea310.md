# Muğla Büyükşehir Belediyesi branding on rural bus shelters

`atm_cb0c83bea310` · **culture** / region · cell `swesguj`

**시기**: 2014 ~ ?

Following Turkey's 2014 metropolitan municipality (büyükşehir) reform, provinces like Muğla absorbed villages as mahalle under unified metropolitan administration. This is visible on the ground as standardized municipal bus shelters and street furniture branded 'T.C. [PROVINCE] BÜYÜKŞEHİR BELEDİYESİ' with a provincial rosette seal, appearing even in remote hamlets far from the provincial capital, reliably identifying the specific province.
