# Myanmar (Burma) dynasties and kingdoms

`atm_4546211f9594` · **history** / country

Burmese history includes the Pyu dynasty (c.3000 BC-AD 400), First and Second Tagaung dynasties (legendary, c.850 BC and c.600 BC), Thaton Kingdom (4th c. BC-AD 1057), Kingdom of Pong (1st c.-1479), Sarekhitara dynasty (c.400-1044), Early Pagan Kingdom (c.650-1044) and Pagan Kingdom (1044-1287, 1289-1297), Hanthawaddy kingdom (1287-1539, 1550-1552), Myinsaing (1297-1313), Pinya (1313-1365), Sagaing (1315-1365), Kingdom of Ava (1364-1555) with Innwa dynasty (1365-1486), Kingdom of Mrauk-U (1429-1785), Prome Kingdom (1482-1542), Toungoo dynasty (1510-1752) with Nyaungyan dynasty (1599-1752), Restored Hanthawaddy Kingdom (1740-1757), Konbaung dynasty (1752-1885), and the Wikyama and Warman dynasties of the Sri Ksetra Kingdom.
