# Eastern Andean piedmont / Llanos Orientales transition zone

`atm_8eff91373f39` · **geography** / region · cell `d2g8r9w`

The Llanos Orientales piedmont in Colombia (Meta, Casanare, Arauca departments) is a hot, humid lowland belt where the Andean cordillera rises abruptly to the west, contrasting with the cooler high-altitude Andean interior (Boyaca, Antioquia) and the drier Caribbean coastal plain. Vegetation includes oil palm, acacia, and heliconia, with forested foothills visible immediately behind piedmont towns.
