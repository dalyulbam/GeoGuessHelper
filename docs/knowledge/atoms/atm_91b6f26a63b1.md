# Budapest: Danube-straddling primate city of Hungary

`atm_91b6f26a63b1` · **geography** / city · cell `u2mw3kn`

Budapest is Hungary's capital and largest city, home to roughly 1.7 million residents (about 3 million metro-wide). The Danube River divides the hilly, historic Buda side (west) from the flat, commercial Pest side (east). It dominates the country as a primate city, dwarfing Debrecen, Szeged, Miskolc, and Pécs in population, wealth, and international profile.
