# Llanero cultural sphere of the Orinoquia piedmont

`atm_3641a82e3cde` · **culture** / region · cell `d2g8r9w`

The Llanero identity, centered on the Colombian and Venezuelan Llanos (Orinoquia plains and piedmont), is distinguished by cattle-ranching heritage, beef-grilling traditions (mamona/asadero-style whole-roasted beef), straw-hat wear, and joropo music/dance. It contrasts with the Caribbean costeno sphere (vallenato, champeta, fried fish) and the Andean cachaco sphere (chicha, almojabana, cooler highland cuisine).
