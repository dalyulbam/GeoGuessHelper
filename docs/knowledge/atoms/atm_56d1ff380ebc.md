# Ferry crossings persisting where bridges are absent on karst coasts

`atm_56d1ff380ebc` · **architecture** / region

Not all ria and strait crossings on karst coasts have been bridged; Croatia's Pelješac peninsula relied on ferry connections via the Ploče corridor for decades before the 2022 Pelješac Bridge opened, and many Dalmatian island channels (e.g., to Korčula, Hvar) still depend entirely on car-ferry service rather than fixed-link bridges, reflecting cost and engineering constraints on very wide or deep straits.
