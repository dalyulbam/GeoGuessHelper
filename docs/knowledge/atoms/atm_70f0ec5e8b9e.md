# National highway signage and marking standardization systems

`atm_70f0ec5e8b9e` · **economy** / global

Standardized national highway marking schemes analogous to Mexico's SCT system exist worldwide: the U.S. MUTCD governs lane markings and shield design for Interstate and U.S. Routes, the UK's Highway Code and Department for Transport standards govern its motorway signage, and Germany's StVO regulates Autobahn markings, each pairing distinct color-coded lines, route-numbering shields, and agency-branded infrastructure like utility poles and guardrails.
