# Post-1989 deindustrialization and coal-mine closures in Upper Silesia

`atm_de27d76e98ed` · **economy** / region · cell `u2vtbyk`

Since Poland's 1989 economic transition, dozens of Upper Silesian coal mines have closed as the sector shed hundreds of thousands of jobs, driving EU-funded restructuring programs (e.g., via the Coal Regions in Transition initiative) and urban regeneration such as Katowice's conversion of the former Katowicka mine into the Silesian Museum and the Spodek arena district. This mirrors deindustrialization and reconversion in the Ruhr and England's Black Country.
