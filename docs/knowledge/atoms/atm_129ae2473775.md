# Karst-derived pale road surfaces as regional geology indicators worldwide

`atm_129ae2473775` · **geography** / global

Regions built on karst limestone platforms often exhibit distinctively pale, chalky unpaved road and soil surfaces, similar to Yucatán's sascab, seen in the Istrian and Dalmatian karst of Croatia, the karst plateaus of Slovenia, and the limestone terrain of the Yucatán's Caribbean counterpart, the Bahamas' oolitic limestone roads, marking flat, sinkhole-dotted terrain shaped by dissolution rather than volcanic or fluvial deposition.
