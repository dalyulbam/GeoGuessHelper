# South African traditional royal houses

`atm_c7e0b5fe9c01` · **history** / region

South Africa's traditional non-sovereign royal families include the Zulu royal family (leaders of the Zulu tribe), the Rain Queen dynasty (leaders of the Balobedu tribe), the Transkeian dynasty (leaders of the Thembu tribe), the AmaNyawuza dynasty (leaders of the Mpondo tribe), the Molotlegi dynasty (Royal Bafokeng Nation), and the Mandela family (Mvezo).
