# Cossack sloboda origins of Dnipro-basin Ukrainian towns (18th century)

`atm_dce47f562810` · **history** / polity · cell `u8xsc9x`

Many towns in the central Ukrainian Dnipro basin, such as Oleksandriia, originated as Cossack settlements (sloboda/pasture camps) in the 1750s under regiments like the Myrhorod Regiment, later formalized as towns and included in Ukraine's register of historic settlements. Such towns often had multi-ethnic populations by the early 20th century — Ukrainian Orthodox/Cossack majority alongside significant Jewish and Russian (including Old Believer/starovery) minority communities with their own synagogues and institutions.
