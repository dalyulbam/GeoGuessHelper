# Dynasties/polities ruling Bahrain (550 BC–present)

`atm_0a2c31470dc0` · **history** / country

Bahrain was ruled by the Achaemenid dynasty (550–330 BC), Arsacid/Parthian dynasty (247 BC–AD 224), House of Sasan (224–590, 591–651), House of Mihran (590–591, 629), House of Ispahbudhan (591–596, 630–631), Umayyad dynasty (661–750), Abbasid Caliphate (750–899), Uyunid dynasty (1076–1253), Usfurids (1253–1320), Jarwanid dynasty (14th–15th century), Jabrids (15th–16th century), House of Aviz-Beja (1521–1580, Portuguese rule), Philippine dynasty (1581–1602, Portuguese rule), Safavid dynasty (1602–1717), Afsharid dynasty (1736–1753), and the House of Khalifa (1783–present, extant sovereign dynasty).
