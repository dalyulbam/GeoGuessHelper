# Yucatán Peninsula karst coast morphology

`atm_7eb3b0d86a01` · **geography** / region · cell `9gqfsnv`

The Yucatán Peninsula is a flat, relief-free limestone karst platform lacking rivers or hills; its Gulf-side coast features pale limestone/shell beaches, turquoise shallow shelf waters, long sand barrier bars enclosing lagoon/estuary systems (e.g. Laguna de Términos), and low thorn scrub with mangrove and chit/coconut palms. This contrasts with the volcanic dark-sand, hilly coasts of Veracruz or the muddy deltaic river plains of Tabasco.
