# Kyoto Kitayama forestry valley vs Tanba farming basin

`atm_50a2614e4a4c` · **economy** / region · cell `xn22nqj`

In Kyoto's Kitayama/Keihoku forestry valleys the valley floor is a ribbon only one or two paddy-widths wide, with dense even-aged sugi/hinoki plantation rows climbing straight from the house backs and log-stacking yards inside the hamlet. In the Fukuchiyama–Ayabe Tanba basins you would instead see a broad flat alluvial plain of large contiguous paddies, distant low horizons and farm co-op silos or grain dryers rather than timber yards.
