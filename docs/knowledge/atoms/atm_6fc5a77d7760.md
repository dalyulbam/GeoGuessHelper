# Post-1945 Polonization of Recovered Territories signage

`atm_6fc5a77d7760` · **culture** / region

After WWII, Poland's 'Recovered Territories' (Upper Silesia, Lower Silesia, Pomerania) underwent systematic renaming of streets and towns from German to Polish, alongside population transfer of Germans and resettlement by Poles, yet much physical German-era infrastructure — plaques, building facades, and cast-iron signage — was left largely intact and reused rather than replaced.
