# Thai Tessaban Tambon (subdistrict municipality) micro-works contracts

`atm_309c39141fcb` · **economy** / region · cell `w71dmn8`

Thailand's lowest tier of local government, the Tessaban Tambon (subdistrict municipality), commonly posts green project signboards for small-budget public works (e.g. concrete drainage, road patching) costing on the order of 100,000-500,000 baht, naming the province (changwat), district (amphoe), and subdistrict (tambon) hierarchy. These standardized boards are a recurring visual marker of grassroots Thai civic administration.
