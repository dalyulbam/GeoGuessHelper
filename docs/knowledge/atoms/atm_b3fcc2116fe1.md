# Punting traditions on English university rivers

`atm_b3fcc2116fe1` · **geography** / global

Punting — propelling a flat-bottomed boat with a pole — is a defining tradition on the Rivers Cam in Cambridge and Cherwell/Isis in Oxford. Both cities developed riverside boathouses and student punting culture in the late 19th century, and the activity remains a tourist and student pastime distinguishing these college towns from other British cities.
