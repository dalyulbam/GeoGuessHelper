# Red laterite shoulders on flat plateau mark Isan, not Central Thailand

`atm_735a7287ad2c` · **geography** / region · cell `w71dkzn`

In Isan (Khorat Plateau) village roads have bright red-orange laterite shoulders and dusty yards on dead-flat ground with part-leafless dry deciduous trees. In Central Thailand you would instead see grey alluvial clay, canal-side ribbon settlement and 'Bang/Khlong' toponyms; in the South, evergreen rubber/oil-palm rows and mosques.
