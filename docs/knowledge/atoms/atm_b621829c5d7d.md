# Communist Albania's state atheism (1967-1990)

`atm_b621829c5d7d` · **history** / country

**시기**: 1967 ~ 1990

In 1967 Enver Hoxha's communist regime declared Albania the world's first officially atheist state, closing or destroying mosques, churches, and Bektashi tekkes and banning religious practice until the regime's collapse in 1990-91, after which religious pluralism re-emerged.
