# Ragusan patrician villa builders

`atm_399453199a88` · **history** / polity

Ragusan Republic nobility such as the Gozze (Gučetić) and Gundulić families built summer villas (ljetnikovci) along the coast at Gruž, Trsteno, and Rijeka dubrovačka in the 15th-16th centuries, reflecting the merchant aristocracy's wealth from Mediterranean trade. Ivan Gundulić, Ragusa's most celebrated poet, belonged to one such patrician family with a summer estate near the city.
