# Łódź's 19th-century textile industrialist dynasties

`atm_534a1ac8ceb2` · **economy** / city · cell `u3jy6wr`

Łódź's original industrial boom was driven by 19th-century textile magnates such as Karol Scheibler, Izrael Poznański, and Ludwik Geyer, whose factory complexes and palaces (Poznański's palace now houses the Museum of the City of Łódź) form the historic core now being adapted for leisure and retail uses like the Manufaktura complex.
