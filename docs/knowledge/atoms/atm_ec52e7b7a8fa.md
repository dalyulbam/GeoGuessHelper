# Global family of socialist-era prefab housing estates

`atm_ec52e7b7a8fa` · **history** / global

Prefabricated concrete panel housing estates were built across the Eastern Bloc to house industrial workers: Soviet Khrushchyovka and Brezhnevka microdistricts, East German Plattenbau (e.g. Berlin-Marzahn, Halle-Neustadt), Polish blokowisko estates (e.g. Nowa Huta in Kraków built around the Lenin Steelworks), and Yugoslav 'blokovi' in Novi Beograd. All shared standardized panel systems, micro-district planning with shared amenities, and post-1989/1991 demographic decline as industries closed.
