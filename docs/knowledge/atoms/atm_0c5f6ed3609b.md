# Ruhr Area, Germany

`atm_0c5f6ed3609b` · **geography** / region · cell `u1huxx1`

The Ruhr Area is Germany's largest urban agglomeration, a polycentric former coal-and-steel region including Essen, Dortmund, Duisburg, and Bochum, with roughly 5 million people. Like Upper Silesia it transitioned from heavy industry to a service and cultural economy, marked by projects such as the Zollverein Coal Mine industrial heritage site in Essen, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
