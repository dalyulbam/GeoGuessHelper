# Company-form suffixes as national linguistic fingerprints

`atm_fcd1da2c1009` · **language** / global

Legal entity suffixes act as quick national identifiers on signage worldwide: France uses 'SARL', Spain 'S.L.', Italy 'S.r.l.', the Netherlands 'B.V.', Russia 'OOO', and Hungary 'Kft.' - all denoting limited liability companies with local orthographic conventions. These abbreviations, combined with script and diacritic patterns, let observers distinguish countries from storefront photos alone.
