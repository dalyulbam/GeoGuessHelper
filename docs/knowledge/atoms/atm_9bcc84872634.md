# Swiss road-restriction signage: 'Zubringerdienst gestattet'

`atm_9bcc84872634` · **architecture** / country · cell `u0qh3ck`

Switzerland uses a distinctive blue-panel road sign reading 'Zubringerdienst gestattet' (access/delivery traffic permitted) beneath a red-ringed no-motor-vehicle pictogram to denote residents/access-only restrictions. This wording and layout differs from Germany's 'Anlieger frei' and Austria's 'Ausgenommen Anrainer', making it a reliable discriminator among German-speaking countries.
