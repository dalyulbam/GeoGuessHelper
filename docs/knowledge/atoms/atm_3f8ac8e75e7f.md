# Post-war Ottoman retreat and Balkan border redrawing after 1878

`atm_3f8ac8e75e7f` · **history** / period

The Russo-Turkish War of 1877–78 ended with the Treaty of San Stefano and the Congress of Berlin, which redrew Balkan borders, created an autonomous Bulgarian principality, and accelerated Ottoman territorial contraction in Europe. This triggered mass population displacement, including Muslim refugee movements out of Bulgaria and the Balkans, a demographic counter-current to the war's celebrated 'liberation' narrative.
