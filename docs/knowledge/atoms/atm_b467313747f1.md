# Oxford's housing affordability crisis as counterpart to knowledge-economy growth

`atm_b467313747f1` · **economy** / city · cell `gcpn7mh`

Oxford's knowledge economy has driven some of the UK's highest housing costs relative to local wages, with affordability ratios exceeding London's in some measures, displacing lower-income residents and service workers to surrounding towns like Bicester and Didcot. This mirrors the affordability backlash seen in other university/tech hubs such as Cambridge and Palo Alto, prompting local 'affordable housing' mandates and green-belt disputes.
