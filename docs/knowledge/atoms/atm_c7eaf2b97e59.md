# New Zealand road-signage and traffic-sign typology

`atm_c7eaf2b97e59` · **geography** / country · cell `rbv6fn6`

New Zealand uses left-hand traffic and a distinctive NZTA sign set: blue rectangular priority/give-way-to-oncoming-traffic plates with white/red arrows, and yellow diamond warning signs including a specific 'narrow/one-lane bridge' diamond. This contrasts with Australia's differently worded one-lane-bridge signage, the UK's red-triangle warning sign convention, and right-hand-traffic Spanish-signed countries like Chile.
