# Italian dynasties (mainland, early to Savoy)

`atm_4b41106db621` · **history** / region

Italy's peninsular dynasties include the Amal dynasty of the Ostrogothic Kingdom (493-553), Lething and Gausian dynasties of Lombard rule, Harodingian dynasty, various southern Italian houses (Boniface, Capua, Spoleto, Docibilan, Boso, Musco Comite, Hucpold, Salerno), House of Canossa and Salian dynasty (11th-12th c.), the Hauteville family and Hohenstaufen in southern Italy, Aleramici, Visconti of Pisa and Sardinia, House of Este, the Capetian dynasty with Angevin, Valois and Bourbon-Anjou branches (including Bourbon-Parma and Bourbon-Two Sicilies), House of Wittelsbach, House of Gonzaga, Albizzi family, Visconti of Milan, House of Medici, House of Habsburg with Habsburg-Lorraine branch, House of Montefeltro, House of Sforza, House of Della Rovere, House of Farnese, House of Guise, House of Bonaparte, House of Murat, and House of Savoy (1713-1946) with Savoy-Carignano branch.
