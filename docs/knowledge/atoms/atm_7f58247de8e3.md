# Metro-satellite signature: brick bloques with garage fronts next to adosado estates

`atm_7f58247de8e3` · **architecture** / region · cell `eyeu17f`

Large Seville-belt satellites (Dos Hermanas, Alcala de Guadaira, Mairena) show 1970s four-storey red-brick blocks with street-level garage doors grading directly into vast uniform gated adosado estates, often including pseudo-Tyrolean half-timbered rows from speculative 80s-90s urbanizaciones. In the historic Seville core you would instead find continuous 3-4 storey facades on narrow streets with no front gardens or garage aprons, and in a genuine rural pueblo the estate uniformity and volume would be absent.
