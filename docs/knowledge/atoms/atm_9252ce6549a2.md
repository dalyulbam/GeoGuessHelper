# Diglossia patterns in post-Soviet and colonial-legacy states

`atm_9252ce6549a2` · **language** / global

Diglossia—where one language dominates official/administrative use and another dominates informal or commercial speech—appears widely in post-Soviet states (Belarusian/Russian in Belarus, Kazakh/Russian in Kazakhstan) and post-colonial contexts (French/Wolof in Senegal, English/Filipino code-switching in Manila). These patterns typically trace to a historic prestige language imposed by a former imperial or administrative center over a vernacular retained in daily life.
