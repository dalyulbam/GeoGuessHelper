# Guangxi and Guizhou aboriginal prefectures and chiefdoms, 14th century

`atm_eb00b0aaecce` · **history** / region

Several aboriginal prefectures (tuzhou) in Guangxi were established in the 14th century under Ming rule: Dongxiangwulu (to 1732, ruled by House of Tan of Tujia descent), Dujie Aboriginal Prefecture (to 1916, House of Nong), Xiangwu Aboriginal Prefecture (to 1917), Dukang Aboriginal Prefecture (to 1917), Anping Aboriginal Prefecture (to 1928, House of Li), and Chiefdom of A (to 1931, House of A of Tu descent). Guizhou-area chiefdoms include Chiefdom of Greater Pingfa (1386-?) and Chiefdom of Danping (1397-1663).
