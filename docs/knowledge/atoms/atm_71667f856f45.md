# Chilean MOP secondary-road lettering scheme

`atm_71667f856f45` · **culture** / country · cell `66v8ckk`

Chile's Ministry of Public Works (MOP) codes secondary and local roads with a single letter tied to the region plus a number (e.g., D-115, C-490), distinct from national 'Ruta' numbering. The letter prefix corresponds to a geographic macro-zone: for example roughly B for Antofagasta, C/D for Atacama-Coquimbo (Norte Chico), progressing through the country. This differs from Argentina's green pentagon provincial route shields and Peru's departmental 'XX-###' codes.
