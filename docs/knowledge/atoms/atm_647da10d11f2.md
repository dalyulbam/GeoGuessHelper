# Czech vs Slovak sorted-waste igloo lettering

`atm_647da10d11f2` · **language** / country · cell `u2f2t6k`

On Czech recycling igloos you read 'PAPÍR', 'SKLO', 'PLASTY', 'KOVOVÉ odpady' and 'DĚKUJEME VÁM, ŽE TŘÍDÍTE' with ě and ř. In Slovakia you would instead see 'PAPIER', 'SKLO', 'PLASTY' with ľ/ô/ä forms elsewhere on municipal signage and .sk contacts. The ě/ř pair is the single fastest CZ/SK split at village scale.
