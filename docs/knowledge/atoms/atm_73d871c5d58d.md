# Urban flood-relief channel systems worldwide

`atm_73d871c5d58d` · **geography** / global

Cities on flood-prone rivers have built artificial relief channels analogous to Vienna's Neue Donau: the Rhine's Bergsche Rheinbahn and various Dutch 'Room for the River' bypasses, London's Thames Barrier flood-control system, and Bangkok's network of khlongs originally built to divert Chao Phraya floodwaters. These engineered parallel channels combine flood protection with urban recreation space, as Vienna's Donauinsel does.
