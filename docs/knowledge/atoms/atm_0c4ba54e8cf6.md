Uitenhage (renamed Kariega), an inland town within Nelson Mandela Bay Metro, Eastern Cape, South Africa, functions as a light-industrial and automotive-manufacturing satellite of the coastal core city Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth). Its economy features builders' merchants, aluminium fabricators, welding-gas depots and cash-and-carry wholesalers along arterial corridors, reflecting a working-class, small-firm industrial base tied to the regional car industry.
