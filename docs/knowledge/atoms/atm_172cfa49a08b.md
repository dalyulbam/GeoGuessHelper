# Croatian dynasties

`atm_172cfa49a08b` · **history** / country

Croatia was ruled by the Trpimirović dynasty (c.845-864, 878-879, 892-1091) over the Duchy of Croatia and Kingdom of Croatia, the Domagojević dynasty (864-878, 879-892), and the Snačić family (1093-1097). The House of Savoy-Aosta ruled the Independent State of Croatia (1941-1943).
