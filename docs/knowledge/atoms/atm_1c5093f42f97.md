# Granada metropolitan area: industrial estates as satellite-town specialization vs. tourism-service core

`atm_1c5093f42f97` · **economy** / region · cell `eyt7v0d`

In the Granada metropolitan area (Área Metropolitana de Granada), the core city's economy is dominated by tourism, hospitality and retail, while manufacturing, logistics and wholesale distribution functions concentrate in a few satellite municipalities (e.g. Albolote, Atarfe, Armilla) hosting large industrial estates (polígonos industriales). Such industrial estates were often originally state-planned in the late 1960s-1970s (e.g. by national housing/industrial institutes) and later transferred to joint inter-municipal management bodies, evolving over time to mix manufacturing with commercial/logistics tenants.
