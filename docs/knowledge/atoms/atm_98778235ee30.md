# Ardennes region small-scale manufacturing decline

`atm_98778235ee30` · **economy** / region · cell `u0g1ehc`

**시기**: 1950 ~ 2020

The Ardennes department and wider Ardennes region (France/Belgium border area) historically supported small-scale metalworking and textile manufacturing, much of which declined through the late 20th century, leaving derelict brick factory buildings scattered through small towns. Contemporary local economies in these towns lean on services, agriculture (cereal), and commuting to larger regional centers such as Reims or Charleville-Mézières.
