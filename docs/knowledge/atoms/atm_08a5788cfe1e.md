# Eastern European post-socialist emigration wave

`atm_08a5788cfe1e` · **culture** / global

Albania's youth emigration parallels similar demographic drains in Bulgaria, Romania, Moldova, and Bosnia and Herzegovina following the collapse of socialist regimes and, for Bulgaria/Romania, EU accession (2007) which opened Western European labor markets. Moldova and Bosnia experienced some of the highest emigration rates in Europe, hollowing out working-age populations in rural regions while capital cities and coastal tourist zones retain relatively younger populations.
