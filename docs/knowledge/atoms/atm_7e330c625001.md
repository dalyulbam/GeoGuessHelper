# Magadha dynasties: Haryanka, Shaishunaga, Nanda, Maurya (c. 544-185 BC)

`atm_7e330c625001` · **history** / polity

**시기**: -544 ~ -185

The Kingdom of Magadha was ruled successively by the Haryanka dynasty (c. 544-413 BC), Shaishunaga dynasty (c. 413-345 BC), followed by the Nanda Empire (c. 345-322 BC) and then the Maurya Empire (c. 321-185 BC), also noting the contemporaneous Pradyota dynasty (c. 541-403 BC).
