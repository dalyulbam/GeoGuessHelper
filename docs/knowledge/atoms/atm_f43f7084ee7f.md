# Liang dynasty principalities and Gaochang rulers (China, Middle Ages)

`atm_f43f7084ee7f` · **history** / region

Under the Liang dynasty of China (502-?), agnatic princely fiefs of the House of Xiao included Linchuan, Changsha, Yongyang, Hengyang, and Guiyang. Gaochang, a Tarim Basin kingdom (460-640), was ruled successively by the Kan family (460-488), Zhang family (488-496, Han descent), Ma family (496-501), and Qu family (501-640, Han descent). Hou Han (551-552) was ruled by the House of Hou of Jie descent.
