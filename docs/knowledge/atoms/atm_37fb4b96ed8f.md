# Loss of Cossack autonomy and destruction of the multi-ethnic sloboda fabric

`atm_37fb4b96ed8f` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1764 ~ 1943

Catherine II of Russia abolished Cossack Hetmanate autonomy in 1764 and destroyed the Zaporozhian Sich in 1775, ending the self-governing military-settler system that had founded towns like Oleksandriia. The multi-ethnic sloboda communities were later devastated in the 20th century by Soviet collectivization, the Holodomor famine (1932-33), and the Holocaust, which destroyed the Jewish communities documented in earlier town records.
