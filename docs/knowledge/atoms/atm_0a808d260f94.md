# Horn of Africa Somali dynasties

`atm_0a808d260f94` · **history** / region

**시기**: 800 ~ 1884

The Adal Sultanate (9th-13th century AD) was an early Horn of Africa polity. The Walashma dynasty (1285-1577) ruled the Sultanate of Ifat and later the Adal Sultanate. The Guled dynasty (1750-1884) ruled the Isaaq Sultanate. The Habr Yunis Sultanate (late 18th century-1884) was a related Somali polity.
