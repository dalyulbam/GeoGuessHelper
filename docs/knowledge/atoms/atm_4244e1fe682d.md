# Italian river park signage: 'Parco Fluviale Regionale'

`atm_4244e1fe682d` · **culture** / country · cell `spznmb3`

Italian regional river parks are marked with distinctive brown tourist-information road signs reading 'Parco Fluviale Regionale' followed by the river name, a standardized Italian convention for protected riparian corridors, paralleling other brown-sign categories used for cultural/tourist sites nationwide.
