# Lago Maggiore pre-Alpine lake basin geography

`atm_8c55d94bc98f` · **geography** / region · cell `u0nh6e5`

Lago Maggiore is a large north-south oriented glacial lake at the foot of the Alps, straddling the Piedmont-Lombardy border (and extending into Switzerland's Ticino canton), with a wide open plain at its southern end contrasting with narrower, cliff-lined northern reaches. This distinguishes it from neighboring pre-Alpine lakes: Lake Como's Y-shaped fork with steep cliff villages, and Lake Garda's broad rounded southern bay with olive terracing.
