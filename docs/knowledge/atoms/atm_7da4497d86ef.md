# Turkic and Kurdish beyliks and emirates of Anatolia (11th-15th c.)

`atm_7da4497d86ef` · **history** / region

Following Byzantine decline, Anatolia saw the Seljuq dynasty (1037-1308, Seljuk Empire and Sultanate of Rum), Principality of Eğil (1049-1864), Danishmendid dynasty (1071-1178), Saltukids (1071-1202), House of Mengüjek (1072-1277, Erzincan and Divriği branches), Rubenids of Cilicia (1080-1226), Beylik of Smyrna (1081-1098), Beylik of Çubukoğulları (1085-1112), Beylik of Dilmaç (1085-1398), Inalids (1095-1183), Hauteville family (Principality of Antioch, 1098-1163), Artuqid dynasty (1101-1409, Mardin, Aleppo, Hasankeyf, Harput branches), Shah-Armens (1110-1207), Zengid dynasty (1128-1183), House of Poitiers (Antioch, 1163-1268), Principality of Bitlis (1182-19th c.), House of Flanders and Capetian House of Courtenay (Latin Empire), Hethumids (Cilicia, 1226-1341), Chobanids (1227-1309), Emirate of Bingöl (1231-1864), Emirate of Hasankeyf (1232-1524), Karamanid dynasty (1250-1487), Ilkhanate (1256-1353), Menteshe (1261-1424), Beylik of Lâdik (1262-1391), Sahib Ataids (1275-1341), Pervâneoğlu (1277-1322), Eshrefids (1280-1326), Candar dynasty (1292-1461), Alaiye (1293-1471), Karasid dynasty (1296-1357), the Ottoman dynasty (1299-1924), Emirate of Çemişgezek, Hamidid dynasty with Tekeoğulları, Sarukhanid dynasty, Germiyanids, Tacettinoğulları, Aydınid dynasty, Sutayids, Hacıemiroğulları, Eretnids, Principality of Zirqan, Beylik of Dulkadir, Kutluşah dynasty, House of Poitiers-Lusignan (Cilicia and Cyprus), Qara Qoyunlu, Bahdinan, Aq Qoyunlu, Beylik of Erzincan, Emirate of Hakkâri, Principality of Mahmudi, Emirate of Palu, Emirate of Pazooka, Principality of Suleyman, and the undated Maeoniae dynasty of Lydia.
