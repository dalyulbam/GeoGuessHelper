# Bilingual line order on Finnish official signs localises the municipality

`atm_126f0a24f257` · **language** / region · cell `u6xzgj3`

Finnish official signage in bilingual municipalities lists the majority language first: 'AUTOMAATTI / AUTOMAT' means a Finnish-majority officially bilingual town (Turku, Helsinki, Porvoo, Kotka class). In Swedish-majority areas such as Åland or parts of Ostrobothnia you would instead see 'AUTOMAT' on the top line, and in monolingual interior cities like Tampere or Kuopio only the Finnish word appears.
