# Bilingual Afrikaans/English commercial culture in Eastern Cape inland towns

`atm_5dea4781e2c0` · **culture** / city · cell `k9fpcsx`

Inland Eastern Cape towns such as Uitenhage/Kariega display bilingual Afrikaans/English commercial signage and business naming conventions, reflecting a historic Afrikaans-surnamed trading and artisan class, distinguishing them from township areas (isiXhosa-dominant branding, informal spaza-shop signage) and from coastal metro cores with more anglicised retail chains.
