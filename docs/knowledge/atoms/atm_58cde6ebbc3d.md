# SAS suffix marks Colombia, not Venezuela or Ecuador

`atm_58cde6ebbc3d` · **economy** / country · cell `d2g8r9w`

In Colombia small and mid-sized firms almost universally carry the 'SAS' (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada) suffix on shopfront signage. In Venezuela you would instead see 'C.A.' or 'S.A.', and in Ecuador 'Cía. Ltda.' or 'S.A.'; Peru uses 'S.A.C.'. A painted or printed 'SAS' after a brand name is one of the fastest single-word country locks in Latin America.
