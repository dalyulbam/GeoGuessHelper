# Kocaeli/İzmit as Turkey's core heavy-industry cluster

`atm_15c884566bad` · **economy** / polity · cell `sxjrck3`

Kocaeli province (seat: İzmit), part of Turkey's Marmara industrial belt, accounts for roughly 10% of national GDP and over 13% of manufacturing output, with more than a third of Turkey's automobile production. Major firms include TÜPRAŞ (refining), Ford Otosan, Hyundai Assan, Honda, Anadolu Isuzu (automotive), Pirelli and Goodyear (tires), and Nuh Çimento (cement), supported by research infrastructure like Kocaeli University, Gebze Technical University, and TÜBİTAK.
