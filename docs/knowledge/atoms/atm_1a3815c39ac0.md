# Velebit Channel: bare karst coast facing Rab/Pag

`atm_1a3815c39ac0` · **nature** / region · cell `srfw77f`

The Velebit Channel separates the mainland Velebit littoral from the islands of Rab and Pag. Its coasts are extremely denuded, near-soil-free white limestone karst with sparse maquis, the result of centuries of bura wind scouring; opposite islands appear as long treeless white-grey ridges. This bare-karst-facing-bare-island signature distinguishes it from more vegetated, terraced coasts further south in Dalmatia.
