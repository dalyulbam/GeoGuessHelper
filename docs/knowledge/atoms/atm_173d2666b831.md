# Medieval feudal island dynasties of the Adriatic and Mediterranean

`atm_173d2666b831` · **history** / global

Comparable noble maritime dynasties held island and coastal fiefs elsewhere in the Adriatic and Mediterranean, such as the Kačić and Šubić (Zrinski) families in Dalmatia, the Genoese Grimaldi and Doria families controlling Mediterranean island holdings, and the Venetian-aligned noble houses governing the Ionian Islands. These families similarly built defensive castle networks and left lasting heritage-tourism legacies on their home islands.
