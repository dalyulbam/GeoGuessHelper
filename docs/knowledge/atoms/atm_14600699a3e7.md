# Post-Soviet rural depopulation and agro-forestry economy in Vidzeme/Gulbene

`atm_14600699a3e7` · **economy** / region · cell `ud4tpyy`

Rural parishes in Vidzeme (e.g., Gulbene municipality) exhibit strong post-Soviet economic asymmetry: municipal centers (towns) concentrate the majority of economic activity while outlying parishes rely on small-scale agriculture, forestry, and wood-processing, alongside surviving Soviet-era kolkhoz (collective farm) infrastructure now largely derelict. Rural depopulation continues due to out-migration and limited services, though some individual parishes can locally buck the regional population decline trend.
