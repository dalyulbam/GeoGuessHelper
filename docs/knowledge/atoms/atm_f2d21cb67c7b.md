# Yemen dynasties and states

`atm_f2d21cb67c7b` · **history** / country

Ancient South Arabian Yemen included Kingdom of Saba' (c.1200 BC-AD 275), Hadramaut, Kingdom of Awsan (800-500 BC), Kingdom of Ma'in and Minaeans (8th c.-100 BC), Kingdom of Ḥaḑramawt (8th c. BC-AD 300), Kingdom of Qatabān (4th c. BC-AD 200), and Himyarite Kingdom (110 BC-AD 525). Medieval and later dynasties include Ziyadid dynasty (818-1018), Yu'firids (847-997), Rassids (897-1596, 1918-1970) with Qasimid successors (1597-1849, Yemeni Zaidi State), Najahid dynasty (1022-1158), Sulayhid dynasty (1047-1138), Sulaymanids (1063-1174), Zurayids (1083-1174), Yemeni Hamdanids (1099-1174) with First Hatimid, Banu'l-Qubaib, and Second Hatimid lines, Mahdids (1159-1174), Ayyubid dynasty (1174-1229), Rasulid dynasty (1229-1454), Kathiri State of Seiyun (14th c.-1967), Tahirid dynasty (1454-1517), and Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen. Numerous 15th-19th century sultanates and sheikhdoms persisted until 1967: Emirate of Dhala, Fadhli Sultanate, Wahidi Sultanate of Balhaf, Wahidi Sultanate of Habban, Emirate of Beihan, Sultanate of Lahej (1728-1967), `Alawi Sheikhdom (1743-1967), Mawsata (1780-1967), Sultanate of Lower Yafa and Upper Yafa (1800-1967), Hadrami Sheikhdom (1820-1967), Wahidi Sultanate of Bir 'Ali (1830-1967), Muflihi Sheikdom (1850-1967), Qu'aiti State in Hadhramaut (1858-1967), Mahra Sultanate of Qishn and Socotra (1886-1967), and `Aqrabi Sheikhdom (18th c.-1967).
