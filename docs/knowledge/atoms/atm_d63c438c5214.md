# Norwegian retail chain signage as a country discriminator

`atm_d63c438c5214` · **language** / country · cell `ukmkre5`

Norway's retail landscape is distinctively marked by grocery chains such as EUROSPAR, Rema 1000, and Joker (part of the NorgesGruppen conglomerate), together with signage words like 'INNGANG' (entrance) and 'VELKOMMEN' (welcome). This combination differs from Sweden (ICA, Coop; 'INGÅNG'), Finland (K-Market, S-Market; 'SISÄÄNKÄYNTI'), and Iceland (Bónus, Krónan; þ/æ characters), making it a reliable visual cue for identifying Norway from street-level imagery.
