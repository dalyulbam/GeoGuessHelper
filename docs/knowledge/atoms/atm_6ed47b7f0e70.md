# Croat settlement and Illyrian substrate continuity debate

`atm_6ed47b7f0e70` · **history** / region

In contrast to models of wholesale population replacement, archaeological and toponymic evidence in the Velebit hinterland points to Croat settlers in the 7th century absorbing and continuing pre-existing Illyrian (Iapodian) settlement patterns, hillfort sites, and possibly pastoral transhumance routes, rather than displacing them outright. This continuity model contrasts with more disruptive settlement narratives seen elsewhere in the post-Roman Balkans, such as the Avar-driven disruptions further north in Pannonia.
