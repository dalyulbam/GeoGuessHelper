# Central European thermal bath culture

`atm_9d7ccc769c8d` · **culture** / region · cell `u2mw3kn`

Thermal and medicinal bathing is a defining cultural practice in the Carpathian Basin, with roots in Roman spa use and reinforced by Ottoman-era Turkish baths. Budapest is especially famous for it, with landmark baths such as Széchenyi, Gellért, and the Palatinus lido on Margaret Island. Spa and medical wellness tourism is a major economic draw.
