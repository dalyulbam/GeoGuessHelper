# Lake eutrophication and shoreline overdevelopment pressure

`atm_132267750ce6` · **geography** / global

Pre-Alpine lakes including Maggiore, Como, and Garda face counter-pressures from post-war tourism and villa development: nutrient runoff causing eutrophication and algal blooms, and dense shoreline construction crowding the narrow lakefront strips. Regional bodies like the International Commission for the Protection of Italian-Swiss Waters (CIPAIS) monitor Lago Maggiore's water quality as a direct response to mid-20th-century pollution peaks.
