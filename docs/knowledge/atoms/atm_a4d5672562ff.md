# Szimpla Kert founders and the ruin-bar pioneers

`atm_a4d5672562ff` · **culture** / city · cell `u2mw1y8`

Szimpla Kert, opened in 2002 in Budapest's District VII, is credited as the original ruin bar and template for the genre, founded by a group of young entrepreneurs who leased a derelict courtyard building slated for demolition. Its success spurred dozens of imitators (Instant, Fogasház, Ellátó Kert) across the Jewish Quarter through the 2000s and 2010s.
