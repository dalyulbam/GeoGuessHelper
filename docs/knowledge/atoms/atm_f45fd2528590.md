# Southern-taiga spruce-birch without oak is a belt, not a point

`atm_f45fd2528590` · **nature** / region · cell `v47mmjn`

A canopy of Picea and drooping birch with willow scrub and no oak, hornbeam or maple element spans a 1500 km east-west band from Vologda to the Cis-Urals. In genuinely Upper Volga locations you would start to see admixed oak, lime and maple in hedgerows and old parks; their total absence argues for staying agnostic across the whole taiga band and quoting a radius of many hundreds of kilometres.
