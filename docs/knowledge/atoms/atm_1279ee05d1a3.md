# Spain ruling dynasties

`atm_1279ee05d1a3` · **history** / country

Spain's dynasties include Balt dynasty (395-531, Visigothic Kingdom), Astur-Leonese dynasty (718-1037, Kingdom of Asturias), House of Íñiguez (824-905, Kingdom of Navarre), House of Barcelona (878-1410), Jiménez dynasty (905-1234), Castilian House of Burgundy (1111-1369), House of Blois (1234-1284, Navarre), Capetian dynasty (1284-1441, Navarre) with House of Capet and House of Évreux, House of Trastámara (1369-1555), House of Foix (1479-1517, Navarre), House of Albret (1484-1513, 1513-1572, Navarre), House of Habsburg (1504-1700, 1705-1714), House of Bourbon (1572-1620, 1700-1868) with House of Bourbon-Vendôme and House of Bourbon-Anjou (1700-1833, 1874-1931, 1975-present), House of Bonaparte (1808-1813), and House of Savoy (1870-1873).
