# Steep-roof snow-load vernacular architecture worldwide

`atm_2f9b481afa28` · **architecture** / global

Regions with heavy snowfall independently developed steep or heavily reinforced roofs and deep eaves to shed snow load: the gassho-zukuri thatched farmhouses of Shirakawa-go in Japan's Gifu Prefecture, the steeply pitched chalet roofs of the Swiss and Austrian Alps, and the tin-roofed A-frame houses of Scandinavia and the northern US snowbelt. These forms share the functional logic of preventing roof collapse and channeling meltwater away from walls and doors.
