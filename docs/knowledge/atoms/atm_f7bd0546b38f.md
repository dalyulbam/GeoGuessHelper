# Definition of historical sovereign state (post-1453 scope)

`atm_f7bd0546b38f` · **history** / global

A historical sovereign state is a state that once existed but has since been dissolved due to conflict, war, rebellion, annexation, or uprising. The associated list article covers sovereign states, countries, nations, or empires that ceased to exist as political entities sometime after 1453, grouped geographically and by constitutional nature. The sovereignty of some listed states was generally unrecognized, with further detail found in a separate list of historical unrecognized states and dependencies. The cutoff between medieval and early modern states for this list is the fall of Constantinople in 1453.
