# Soviet-era shared postal and retail branding across USSR successor states

`atm_532ae8610bb9` · **language** / country

Before independence, Ukraine, Russia, and Belarus shared unified Soviet postal branding ('Почта СССР') and identical state-run retail infrastructure, making visual discrimination between the future countries impossible before 1991. The post-Soviet divergence into distinct national brands like Ukrposhta, Pochta Rossii, and Belpochta is a direct counter-development to this earlier uniformity.
