# French dynasties (early to Bourbon)

`atm_ecb833635a0b` · **history** / country

France's history includes the Gallic Empire (260-274), Kingdom of Soissons (457-486), Merovingian dynasty (509-751), Carolingian dynasty (751-987 with gaps), House of Poitiers (854-1204), Robertian dynasty (888-923), Bosonids (923-936), and the Capetian dynasty (987-1848) comprising House of Capet (987-1328), House of Valois (1328-1589, with Valois-Orléans and Valois-Angoulême branches), and House of Bourbon (1589-1848, with Bourbon-Vendôme and House of Orléans branches). House of Bonaparte ruled 1804-1814, 1815, 1852-1870. The House of Neuhoff briefly ruled the Kingdom of Corsica in 1736.
