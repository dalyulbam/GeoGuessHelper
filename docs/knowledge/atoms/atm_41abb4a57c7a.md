# M7 'Volga' federal highway and Middle Volga-Kama forest-steppe

`atm_41abb4a57c7a` · **geography** / region · cell `v1uv16c`

The M7 'Volga' federal highway runs Moscow–Vladimir–Nizhny Novgorod–Kazan–Ufa, forming a major freight corridor through the Middle Volga and Lower Kama basin. East of Kazan the terrain is humid continental forest-steppe: pine and birch shelterbelts (Soviet-era windbreaks) interspersed with large mechanised grain fields on dark, chernozem-like soils, distinct from the rockier Ural (M5) or taiga Siberian (R-254/255) highway corridors.
