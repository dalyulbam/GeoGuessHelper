# Chihuahuan Desert Altiplano flora signature (Yucca filifera + nopal hedges)

`atm_bcebe14f209f` · **nature** / region · cell `9gbn2rp`

The southern Chihuahuan Desert / Altiplano of central-northern Mexico (San Luis Potosí, Zacatecas, parts of Durango) is marked by arborescent Yucca filifera stands, Opuntia (nopal) hedgerows used as living field boundaries, mesquite scrub, and pale calcareous/limestone-derived soils at ~2000 m elevation. This combination distinguishes it from the Sonoran Desert (saguaro, palo verde, reddish granitic soils) and from the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt (dark andosols, maguey pulquero rows).
