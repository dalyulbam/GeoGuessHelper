# Late Qing-era ethnic chiefdoms (tusi) of Yunnan/Tibet borderlands

`atm_fdc040c53c49` · **history** / region

A cluster of small hereditary chiefdoms (tusi) established or recorded from AD 1891 onward in the Yunnan-Tibet-Yunnan borderland region, including Mengbin (1891-?, House of Han, Dai), Jiufang (1891-?, House of Yang), Banzhong (1891-?, House of Yang), Jianguan (1891-?, House of Shi, Lahu), Manhai (1891-?, House of Shi, Lahu), Upper Mengyun (1891-?, House of Dao, Dai), Lower Mengyun (1891-?, House of Dao, Dai), Munai (1891-?, House of Shi, Lahu), Donghe (1891-?, House of Zhang), Ximingzhao (1891-?, House of Li, Lahu), Mengban (1899-1955, House of Jiang, Han), Geshizha (?-1911), Ganya (?-1949, House of Chi, Dai), Wandian (?-1949, House of Dao and House of Jing, Dai), and Menghai (?-1950, House of Shao, Dai).
