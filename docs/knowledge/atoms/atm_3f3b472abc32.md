# National highway-shield design as a road-network identity marker

`atm_3f3b472abc32` · **geography** / global

Countries and federations distinguish their road networks through unique highway shield iconography: the US Interstate's blue-red shield, individual US states' custom shields (Washington's George Washington silhouette, New York's rectangular marker), Canadian provincial crown/maple-leaf-themed shields, Mexico's tricolor numbered shields, and Australia's route-number badges. This shield design is a standardized global infographic convention (per bodies like AASHTO in the US) that lets drivers instantly identify jurisdiction.
