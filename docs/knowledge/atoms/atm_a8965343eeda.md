# Spanish DGT road signage conventions

`atm_a8965343eeda` · **culture** / country · cell `sp3ebxf`

Spain's DGT (Dirección General de Tráfico) traffic signage uses metric circular speed-limit discs with thin red rims (often without a full black border) and blue circular mandatory/informational discs (e.g., combined moped-and-car symbols for restricted-access residential streets), distinguishing it from French or Italian sign typography and post/collar hardware styles.
