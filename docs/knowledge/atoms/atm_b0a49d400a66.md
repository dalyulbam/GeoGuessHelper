# Post-socialist critique and renovation of panel housing estates

`atm_b0a49d400a66` · **architecture** / global

Since the 1990s, panel-block microdistricts across the former socialist bloc have faced criticism for monotony and disrepair, prompting divergent responses: Germany's Stadtumbau Ost program partially demolished excess Plattenbau in shrinking eastern cities, while Berlin's Karl-Marx-Allee and Warsaw's estates have been heritage-listed and renovated. Contemporary architecture in the West, by contrast, developed New Urbanism and traditional-neighborhood-design movements as a reaction against modernist mass housing altogether.
