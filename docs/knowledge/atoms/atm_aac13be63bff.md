# Dynasties/polities ruling Afghanistan (678 BC–1973 AD)

`atm_aac13be63bff` · **history** / country

Afghanistan was successively ruled by the Median dynasty (678–550 BC), Achaemenid dynasty (550–330 BC), Seleucid dynasty (330–150 BC), Maurya Empire (305–180 BC), House of Diodotus (255–223 BC), Arsacid/Parthian dynasty (247 BC–AD 224), Euthydemid dynasty (230 BC–AD 10), House of Eucratides (170–130 BC), House of Suren (12 BC–AD 130), Kushan Empire (AD 30–375), Sasanian dynasty (AD 230–651), Kidarites (320–465), Alchon Huns (370–670), Hephthalite Empire (440–710), Nezak Huns (484–665), Tokhara Yabghus (625–758), Tang dynasty (657–670), Umayyad dynasty (661–750), Kabul Shahi with Turk Shahis (665–850) and Hindu Shahis (850–1026), Zunbils (680–870), Abbasid dynasty (750–821), Lawik dynasty (750–977), Tahirid dynasty (821–873), Saffarid dynasty (863–900), Samanid Empire (875–999), Ghurid dynasty (879–1215), Farighunids (9th century–1010), Ghaznavid dynasty (977–1186), Seljuq dynasty (1037–1194), Anushtegin dynasty (1215–1231), Qarlughid dynasty (1224–1266), Chagatai Khanate (1225–1370), Kart dynasty (1244–1381), Ilkhanate (1256–1353), Khalji dynasty (1290–1320), Samma dynasty (1351–1524), Timurid dynasty (1370–1738), Safavid dynasty (1510–1709), Arghun dynasty (1520–1554), Tarkhan dynasty (1554–1591), Katoor dynasty (1570–1947), Hotak dynasty (1709–1738), Afsharid dynasty (1738–1747), Durrani dynasty (1747–1826, 1839–1842), and Barakzai dynasty (1818–1973) including the Musahiban (1929–1973).
