# Reykjavík Capital Region municipal structure

`atm_c464359a13cf` · **geography** / region · cell `ge2kts4`

Iceland's Capital Region (Höfuðborgarsvæðið) around Reykjavík comprises several adjacent municipalities including Reykjavík, Kópavogur (Iceland's second-largest municipality), Hafnarfjörður, and Garðabær. These function as an integrated metropolitan labour market where suburban municipalities serve largely as commuter towns. Kópavogur is densely developed with major shopping (Smáralind), while Hafnarfjörður is more industrial and harbour-oriented.
