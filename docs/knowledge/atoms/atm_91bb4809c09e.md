# Aegean Turkey olive-maquis hinterland vegetation signature

`atm_91bb4809c09e` · **nature** / region · cell `swesguj`

The Aegean Turkish hinterland (inland Muğla, Aydın, İzmir provinces) is characterized by smallholder olive groves on stony limestone slopes, kermes-oak maquis scrub, and planted stands of river red gum eucalyptus and stone pine, under a Mediterranean Csa climate. This vegetation and land-use pattern contrasts with the treeless steppe of Central/Eastern Anatolia (too continental for olives) and the hazelnut/tea terraces of the Black Sea coast, making it a reliable macro-region discriminator within Turkey.
