# DPWH-standard rural concrete roads (PCCP) in the Philippines

`atm_52db6220e238` · **culture** / country · cell `wej9x8g`

Rural farm-to-market roads in the Philippines are typically built as unreinforced Portland Cement Concrete Pavement (PCCP) with a single painted centerline and no shoulders, often paired with stone-masonry or gabion retaining walls, funded through the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH). This standard, along with cab-over light 'elf' trucks with tarpaulin canopies and helmetless motorcycle riders, is a strong visual signature of provincial Luzon/Philippine rural infrastructure.
