# Japanese han (feudal domains) system, 16th century-1871

`atm_e1ce403ee527` · **history** / region

From the 16th century until 1871, Japan was divided into hereditary feudal domains (han) ruled by samurai clans under the shogunate. Domains named in this list include Shiroi (1590-1623), Naha (1590-1662), Obata (1590-1868), Yoshii (1590-1610, 1682-1698, 1709-1869), Hirosaki (1590-1871), Takasaki (1590-1871), Tatebayashi (1590-1871), Soja (1592-1633), Morioka (1599-1870), Owari (1600-1871), Sendai (1600-1871), Yamagata (1600-1871), Aizu (1601-1869), Yonezawa (1601-1871), Maebashi (1601-1871), Isesaki (1601-1871), Ueno Toyooka (1602-1626), Kubota (1602-1871), Soma Nakamura (1602-1871), Iwakitaira (1602-1871), Itabana (1603-1636), Tanagura (1603-1871), Ogo (1604-1616), Matsumae (1604-1871), Aoyagi (1614-1619), Annaka (1615-1871), Nanokaichi (1616-1871), Aterazawa (1622-1648), Kubota (1622-1684), Shonai (1622-1871), Shinjo (1622-1871), Kaminoyama (1622-1871), Nikaho (1623-1626), Honjo (1623-1868), Kameda (1623-1871), Shirakawa (1627-1868), Miharu (1627-1871), Nihonmatsu (1627-1871), Dewamaruoka (1632-1653), Izumi (1634-1871), Yashima (1640-1658, 1868-1871), Oyama (1647-1668), Dewa-Matsuyama (1647-1871), Numata (1656-1871), Iwanuma (1660-1681), Ichinoseki (1660-1671, 1681-1871), Asakawa (1662-1681), Ishikawa (1662-1681), Hachinohe (1664-1871), Yunagaya (1670-1871), Fukushima (1679-1871), Okubo (1682-1693), Murayama (1682-1699), Yanagawa Domain Mutsu (1683-1728, 1807-1821), Koori (1688-1767), Nakatsuyama (1695-1868), Moriyama (1700-1871), Kubota Shinden (1701-1732), Iwasaki (1701-1871), Yonezawa Shinden (1719-1869), Shinozuka (1747-1748), Kamisatomi (1748-1767), Takabatake (1767-1830), Mutsu Shimomura (1787-1823), Nagatoro (1798-1871), Shimotedo (1806-1868), Kuroishi (1809-1871), Shichinohe (1819-1871), Tendo (1830-1871), and Tonami (1870-1871).
