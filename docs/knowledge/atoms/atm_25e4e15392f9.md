# Liu Song dynastic principalities (420-479)

`atm_25e4e15392f9` · **history** / region

Under the Liu Song dynasty (420-479) of China, numerous agnatic princely fiefs held by the House of Liu existed, including Yidu, Pengcheng, Luling, Changsha, Linchuan, Yingyang, Jingling, Jiangxia, Hengyang, Nanqiao, Nanfeng, Wuling, Shixing, Nanping, Guangling, Jianping, Yiyang, Donghai, Ruyin, Huaiyang, Sui, Wuchang, Xiangdong, Jian'an, Nan, Dongping, Hailing, Shanyang, Poyang, Shunyang, Xiyang, Baling, Guiyang, Anlu, Xiangyang, Liyang, Jin'an, Xunyang, Xin'an, Yuzhang, Linhai, Yongjia, Shi'an, Jinling, Shaoling, Linhe, Nanhai, Huainan, Ancheng, Jinxi, Shiping, Lujiang, Jinping, Linqing, Nanyang, Xinxing, Shijian, and Suiyang, each with individual reign spans between 420-479. Non-agnatic principalities under the Liu Song included Lingling (House of Sima, Han descent), Chenliu (House of Cao, Han descent), and Qi (House of Xiao, Han descent, 479).
