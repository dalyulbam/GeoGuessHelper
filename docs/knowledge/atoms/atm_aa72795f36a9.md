# Alpine vs foreland roof pitch and eave depth as elevation/relief proxy

`atm_aa72795f36a9` · **architecture** / region · cell `u299pj6`

In high-relief inner-Alpine regions (Tirol, inner Salzburg, Carinthia), vernacular houses show steep 35-45 degree roof pitches, deep snow-load eaves, and wraparound timber balconies to shed heavy snow loads. In the lower northern Alpine foreland (Upper Austria, Lower Austria hill country), roof pitches are shallower (25-30 degrees) with modest eaves, reflecting lower snow loads and softer relief. This contrast helps distinguish inner-Alpine valleys from pre-Alpine foreland hill country from roofline alone.
