# Cotswold-limestone collegiate Gothic architecture

`atm_92b70bd29630` · **architecture** / region · cell `gcpn77e`

**시기**: 1100 ~ ?

Oxford colleges are built largely of golden Cotswold limestone in a collegiate Gothic style, producing the honey-coloured facades characteristic of the region. Christ Church Cathedral displays Norman and Gothic features including fan vaulting and rose windows. This local oolitic limestone is the defining building material across the Cotswolds and Oxfordshire.
