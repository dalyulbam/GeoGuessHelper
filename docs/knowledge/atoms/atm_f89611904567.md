# Post-Mauryan north Indian dynasties (2nd century BC - AD 4th century)

`atm_f89611904567` · **history** / region

**시기**: -185 ~ 400

Following the Maurya Empire, several dynasties arose in northern India: Shunga Empire (c. 185-75 BC), Mitra dynasty of Mathura (c. 150-50 BC), Deva dynasty of Saketa (2nd-1st century BC), Mahameghavahana dynasty (2nd century BC-AD 4th century), Kanva dynasty (c. 73-28 BC), Northern Satraps (60 BC-AD 2nd century), and Datta dynasty (1st century BC-AD 1st century).
