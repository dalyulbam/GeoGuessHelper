# Alpine lakeside resort hierarchies: Lake Como, Lake Geneva, Lake Garda

`atm_39bb6b54a823` · **economy** / global

Italian and Swiss lake tourism repeatedly produces a two-tier hierarchy of glamorous resort towns versus workaday transit hubs: on Lake Como, Bellagio and Como town draw Belle Époque prestige while Colico serves as a rail/transit point; on Lake Garda, Sirmione and Riva del Garda are premier resorts while Desenzano functions more as a transport gateway. This mirrors the Stresa/Verbania vs. Arona pattern on Lago Maggiore.
