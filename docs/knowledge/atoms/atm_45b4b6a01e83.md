# Planners behind Soviet mass-housing standardization

`atm_45b4b6a01e83` · **architecture** / global

Nikita Khrushchev's 1957 housing decree launched mass-produced concrete-panel apartment construction across the USSR to rapidly resolve postwar housing shortages, giving rise to the khrushchyovka type. Soviet architect-engineers such as Vitaly Lagutenko developed the K-7 panel-building series mass-produced from the late 1950s, standardizing prefabricated residential construction later exported as a planning model to allied socialist states.
