# Southern-hemisphere summer phenology as season indicator

`atm_b67d081a61b7` · **nature** / period · cell `rbv6fn6`

In the southern hemisphere, austral summer (Dec–Feb) produces sun-bleached, drought-browned pasture alongside fully green deciduous trees (e.g. poplars) and flowering pampas grass. This combination — brown grass with green deciduous canopy — distinguishes southern-hemisphere late summer from northern-hemisphere spring green-up or autumn colour change, allowing hemisphere determination from vegetation state alone.
