# Guatemalan land reform and labor movements against UFCO dominance

`atm_b596b7eba650` · **history** / country · cell `d4b84g6`

**시기**: 1952 ~ 1954

President Jacobo Árbenz's Decree 900 (1952) attempted to redistribute unused UFCO land to peasants, directly challenging the company's dominance, and was reversed after the CIA-orchestrated 1954 coup. Later, unions such as SITRABI in Izabal emerged to counter exploitative labor conditions on banana plantations, part of a broader Central American labor movement against agribusiness monopolies.
