# German expellee heritage organizations and Heimatvertriebene memory culture

`atm_ad0093de4697` · **history** / country · cell `u1p7jf6`

**시기**: 1945 ~ 1991

Post-war West Germany absorbed roughly 12 million ethnic German expellees (Heimatvertriebene) from former eastern territories, who organized into Landsmannschaften preserving regional memory (Silesian, Pomeranian, East Prussian) through museums, reunions and lobbying, forming a counter-memory to Poland's resettlement narrative and complicating German-Polish reconciliation until treaties in the 1990s (the 1990 and 1991 German-Polish border and friendship treaties) formally closed border revision claims.
