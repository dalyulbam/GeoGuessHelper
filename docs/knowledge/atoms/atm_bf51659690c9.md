# Jandia peninsula corniche resort development, Fuerteventura

`atm_bf51659690c9` · **economy** / region · cell `esvzdq6`

The Jandia peninsula on southern Fuerteventura (Morro Jable, Solana Matorral, Esquinzo/Butihondo) is developed as a corniche-style resort strip where the FV-2 dual carriageway is cut into a steep ~300 m eroded ridge directly above the coast, with gated all-inclusive resort compounds and apartment urbanizaciones stacked on a seaward terrace above long light-sand beaches. This contrasts with the flat dune-field resort area of Corralejo in the north and the low flat airport-plain resort area of Caleta de Fuste in the centre-east of the same island.
