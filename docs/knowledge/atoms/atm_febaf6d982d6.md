# Founders and benefactors of Oxford's college system

`atm_febaf6d982d6` · **culture** / city · cell `gcpn7mh`

**시기**: 1379 ~ 1546

William of Wykeham, Bishop of Winchester, founded New College Oxford in 1379 and Winchester College as a feeder school, establishing a model of linked school-and-college endowment. Cardinal Thomas Wolsey founded Christ Church (originally Cardinal College) in 1546, and William Waynflete founded Magdalen College in 1458 — all Oxford colleges tied to English ecclesiastical and political elites of the medieval and early modern period.
