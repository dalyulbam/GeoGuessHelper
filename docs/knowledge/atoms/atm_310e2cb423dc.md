# Otoyol network as Turkey's controlled-access counterpart to D-roads

`atm_310e2cb423dc` · **culture** / country

Alongside the older 'D-' numbered national trunk roads, Turkey built a separate network of tolled, controlled-access 'O-' numbered otoyollar (motorways) from the 1980s onward, such as the O-1 to O-7, reflecting a shift from at-grade national highways to limited-access expressways as traffic volumes grew.
