# One-Village-One-Product (OVOP) movement and its global descendants

`atm_dcc3f946acb2` · **economy** / global

**시기**: 1979 ~ ?

OTOP is modeled on Japan's One Village One Product (OVOP) movement, launched in 1979 in Oita Prefecture by governor Morihiko Hiramatsu to encourage rural communities to brand a single signature local specialty. The concept spread across Asia into Thailand's OTOP program (launched 2001 under Thaksin Shinawatra), Laos, and similar municipal-branding livelihood schemes, all pairing a single product with local pride and tourism marketing.
