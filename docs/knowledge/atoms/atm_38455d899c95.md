# Post-collectivization successor cooperatives in Czech villages

`atm_38455d899c95` · **economy** / country · cell `u2f2t64`

**시기**: 1948 ~ 1989

Czech rural villages often retain large consolidated cereal field blocks and big courtyard-style estate buildings that are legacies of communist-era collectivized agriculture (JZD - Jednotné zemědělské družstvo). After 1989 many of these were restructured into successor agricultural cooperatives or private farming companies that continue to work the same large field parcels and occupy the same courtyard complexes, a pattern visible across the former Czechoslovak countryside.
