# Kirovohrad Oblast lignite mining belt (Oleksandriia)

`atm_06391f17f753` · **economy** / region · cell `u8xsc9x`

Kirovohrad Oblast (now part of Kropyvnytskyi region administration) contains Ukraine's largest lignite (brown coal) basin, centered on Oleksandriia, historically worked via open-pit mines (rozrizy) and shafts under the state holding Oleksandriiavuhillia. Peak Soviet-era employment reached ~15,000 in the coal complex, declining to ~6,000 by the mid-2000s amid post-Soviet industrial decline, leaving grey-white spoil heaps and flooded artificial lakes as characteristic satellite-visible terrain markers.
