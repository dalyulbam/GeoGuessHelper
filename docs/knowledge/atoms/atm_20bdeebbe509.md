# Colonial-era single-industry manufacturing hubs

`atm_20bdeebbe509` · **economy** / global

British colonial rule fostered specialized industrial cities to supply metropolitan demand: Dundee in Scotland became a jute-processing hub (using Bengal raw jute), Manchester itself was the cotton-textile center supplied by colonial raw cotton, and Kanpur in India became a leather and textile hub nicknamed 'Manchester of the East'. This pattern of colonial resource-processing specialization created durable industrial identities that outlasted colonial rule.
