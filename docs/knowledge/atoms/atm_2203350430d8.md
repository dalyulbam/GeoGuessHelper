# Soviet-bloc monotown (mono-gorod) model

`atm_2203350430d8` · **economy** / global

**시기**: 1929 ~ 1991

Across the USSR and Eastern Bloc, planned economies built 'monotowns' entirely dependent on one factory or industry: Magnitogorsk (steel, USSR), Nowa Huta (steel, Poland), Halle-Neustadt (chemicals, East Germany), and Dimitrovgrad (Bulgaria). These towns shared centralized housing allocation, factory-run social services, and severe post-1989/1991 crisis when the single employer collapsed.
