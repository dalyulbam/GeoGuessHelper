# Critiques of festival/micro-enterprise branding as unsustainable livelihood strategy

`atm_c54cff8dbe41` · **economy** / global

One-product and harvest-festival branding schemes are criticized for producing oversupply of low-margin handicrafts, dependency on seasonal tourism, and limited scalability beyond municipal fairs, contrasting with export-oriented industrial clustering strategies. Critics note such programs often lack sustained market access support after the initial government branding push, leading to short-lived local booms.
