# Seven Dong of Qinzhou and Ten States of Bili, plus other minor undated states

`atm_fac85061d5ba` · **history** / region

The Seven Dong of Qinzhou comprised Boshi, Jianshan, Tielang, Shiluo, Jian'an, Ruxi, and Gusen Dong. The Ten States of Bili comprised Bili, Yangyun, Koumohan, Yiqun, Mounu, Molu, Yulimoli, Pudu, Shengquan, and Shalou. Other undated minor states include Doumolou, Liugui, Huaxu, Tuoli (Takri Kingdom), Wulian, and the Congling Uyghur Kingdom.
