# Pag Island: salt production (Solana Pag)

`atm_7109014d9dbc` · **economy** / region · cell `srfth7t`

Solana Pag, located on Pag island, is the largest sea salt producer in Croatia, with traditional salt pans (solana) that are also open to visitors for tours and harvest workshops. Coastal salt-pan works of this kind are a long-standing Adriatic/Mediterranean karst-coast industry tied to shallow lagoons and strong evaporation.
