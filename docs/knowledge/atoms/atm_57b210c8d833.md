# Gruž as Dubrovnik's working port/ferry-cruise gateway district

`atm_57b210c8d833` · **economy** / city · cell `srsfew1`

Gruž, about 2 km northwest of Dubrovnik's Old Town, is the city's functional transport hub: it hosts the Jadrolinija ferry terminal (routes to the Elaphite Islands — Šipan, Lopud, Koločep — and international service to Bari, Italy), cruise-ship berths, and the main bus station. Its bay name derives from a term meaning 'cargo port', reflecting its historic role as Dubrovnik's commercial harbor, distinct from the walled tourist Old Town.
