# Pantile roofs = Emilian side; slate roofs = Lunigiana/Ligurian side of the Cisa

`atm_c67e18d2f564` · **architecture** / region · cell `spznmb0`

On the Emilian (north) flank of the Northern Apennines, towns show shallow-pitch terracotta pantile roofs over rendered pastel plaster blocks, as here. South of the Cisa Pass in Lunigiana and on the Ligurian slope you would instead see dark grey slate roofing, slate lintels and dry-stone walling. Roof material alone separates the two sides of the same mountain chain.
