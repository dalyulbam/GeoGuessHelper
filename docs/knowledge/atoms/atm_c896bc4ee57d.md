# Serf and forced labor in Ural-Siberian mining, and post-Soviet industrial decline

`atm_c896bc4ee57d` · **history** / region · cell `vc7xd2n`

Demidov-era mines and smelters like Barnaul's relied heavily on state-assigned serf labor ('possessional peasants') bound to factories, a coercive labor system that persisted until the 1861 emancipation. In the late 20th century, many Siberian single-industry mining towns (monogorods) built on this legacy faced severe economic decline after Soviet collapse removed centralized subsidy.
