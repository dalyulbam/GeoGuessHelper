# House of Temenggong (Malaysia/Johor)

`atm_125e62c3667a` · **history** / polity

The House of Temenggong is the ruling dynasty of Johor Darul Ta'zim, founded by Sultan Abu Bakar, originating in Johor. Its head, currently Ibrahim Iskandar, serves as the rotating Yang di-Pertuan Agong (sovereign monarch) of Malaysia, elected by the Conference of Rulers among nine constituent monarchies.
