# Egg harvesting and coastal development as turtle population threats

`atm_1cd2aa06b732` · **economy** / region · cell `w2ce6p3`

**시기**: 1950 ~ 2010

Before conservation programs, commercial and subsistence harvesting of sea turtle eggs was legal and widespread on Malaysia's east coast, alongside coastal resort development and fishing-net bycatch, driving leatherback nesting numbers at Rantau Abang from thousands of nests in the 1950s-60s down to near zero by the 2000s, illustrating the direct economic pressures conservation programs must counteract.
