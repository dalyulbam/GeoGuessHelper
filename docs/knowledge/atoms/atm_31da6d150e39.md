# EarthCheck and Green Destinations certification networks worldwide

`atm_31da6d150e39` · **economy** / global

EarthCheck, founded from Australian ecotourism benchmarking programs, certifies destinations and businesses worldwide, including Australia's Great Barrier Reef tourism operators and Pacific island resorts. Parallel certification schemes include Green Destinations and the Global Sustainable Tourism Council (GSTC) criteria, used by places like Slovenia (certified Green Destination) to brand themselves internationally as sustainable travel choices.
