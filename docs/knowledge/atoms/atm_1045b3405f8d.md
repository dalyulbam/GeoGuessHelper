# Tierra colorada: lateritic red soil belt of the upper Paraná basin

`atm_1045b3405f8d` · **geography** / region · cell `6g0k8uc`

The border region spanning Paraguay's Itapúa and Alto Paraná departments, Argentina's Misiones province, and Brazil's Paraná state shares a distinctive vivid orange-red lateritic soil ('tierra colorada') combined with lush Atlantic Forest (Mata Atlántica) broadleaf vegetation and high rainfall. This soil-vegetation combination is a strong visual marker distinguishing this specific tri-border macro-region from Paraguay's drier Chaco (grey alluvial soil, thorn scrub) or the sandier Central/Asunción area.
