# Oltrepò Pavese plain looks vine-free: vines sit behind the first hill line

`atm_ec115d67939d` · **geography** / region · cell `spyrs4k`

In the Lombard Oltrepò Pavese the flat strip between Voghera and the Apennine front is pure cereal/forage with cascine and poplar-lined ditches, and the vineyards only begin on the inner slopes several kilometres back, invisible from the plain road. If you were genuinely in the Parma–Reggio fodder belt you would instead expect a much wider (10-20 km) plain with very long straight canalised ditches and larger dairy stalle, not a hill front this close. Absence of visible vines from a plain lane therefore does not exclude a wine province.
