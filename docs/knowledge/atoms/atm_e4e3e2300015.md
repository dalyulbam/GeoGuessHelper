# Austrian German street-name suffixes and route numbering vs Germany/Switzerland

`atm_e4e3e2300015` · **language** / country · cell `u2edmpb`

Austria uses German-language street suffixes extremely commonly, especially '-gasse' (lane) alongside '-straße' (street) and 'Brücke' (bridge). Austrian federal roads (Bundesstraßen) are marked with blue-background three-digit 'B' numbers, and Autobahnen use blue 'A' shield signs (e.g., A22), distinguishing Austria from Germany (yellow 'B' road signs, 'A' autobahn numbering without the same shield color) and Switzerland (green highway signage, different route numbering conventions).
