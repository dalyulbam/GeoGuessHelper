# Córdoba economy: automotive, services and tech

`atm_b4521f74ff37` · **economy** / city · cell `6d6m63c`

Córdoba is Argentina's second-largest metropolitan economy, diversified across manufacturing, automotive production, services and higher education. Automotive and auto-parts manufacturing is the flagship industry, hosting major vehicle assembly plants, alongside metalworking, agro-industry tied to the Pampas grain belt, and a fast-growing software/IT sector. Heavy reliance on automotive makes the local economy vulnerable to national recessions.
