# Dubrovnik municipality population decline trend

`atm_2d7f42f52810` · **economy** / polity · cell `srsffep`

**시기**: 2011 ~ ?

The City of Dubrovnik municipality, which administratively includes many small satellite coastal settlements (like Zaton/Štikovica), has shown a demographic decline from 42,615 (2011 census) to 41,562 in the most recent census, continuing to lose population at a rate of dozens to roughly a hundred residents per year. This reflects a broader pattern of aging, shrinking permanent populations in small Dalmatian coastal satellite villages where housing stock is increasingly devoted to tourist rental rather than year-round residence.
