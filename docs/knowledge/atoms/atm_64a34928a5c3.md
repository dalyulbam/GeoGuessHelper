# EU-wide harmonized road signage under the Vienna Convention

`atm_64a34928a5c3` · **architecture** / global

Most European Union member states, alongside many non-EU signatories, follow road-sign conventions rooted in the 1968 Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals, standardizing circular red-bordered speed limit signs, triangular warning signs, and blue informational signage, resulting in near-identical driving signage from Portugal to Poland to Greece despite different languages.
