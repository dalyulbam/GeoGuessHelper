# Vidzeme farmstead settlement pattern (viensēta)

`atm_7439e4c1e09e` · **culture** / region · cell `ud4tpyy`

Vidzeme's rural landscape is historically organized into dispersed individual farmsteads (viensētas) rather than clustered villages, reflecting Lutheran Baltic-German manorial land organization. This contrasts with the clustered village pattern typical of Catholic Latgale, and produces a mosaic of small fields, hedgerows/wood lots, and hay meadows visible in aerial and street-level imagery.
