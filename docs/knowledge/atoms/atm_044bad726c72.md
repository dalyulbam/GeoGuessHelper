# Dynastic principalities under the Han dynasty (205 BC-AD 221)

`atm_044bad726c72` · **history** / region

Numerous vassal kingdoms existed under Han authority (205 BC-AD 9, AD 25-221). Non-agnatic principalities under Western Han (205-157 BC, various non-Liu houses) included Han, Qi, Huainan, Zhao, Chu, Liang, Yan, Changsha, Lü, and Lu. Agnatic Western Han principalities (201 BC-AD 9, all House of Liu) included Jing, Qi, Chu, Dai, Zhao, Huainan, Liang, Wu, Yan, Hengshan, Huaiyang, Langya, Jichuan, Taiyuan, Jibei, Chengyang, Hejian, Jinan, Lujiang, Hengshan, Jiaoxi, Jiaodong, Zichuan, Runan, Linjiang, Guangchuan, Changsha, Lu, Zhongshan, Jiangdu, Qinghe, Changshan, Jiyin, Jidong, Shanyang, Liu'an, Guangling, Zhending, Sishui, Changyi, Pinggan, Guangyang, Gaomi, Dingtao, Dongping, Jiyang, Xindu, Guangde, Guangping, Guangshi, and Guangzong. Agnatic Eastern Han principalities (AD 25-221, House of Liu) included Huaiyang, Guangyang, Sishui, Zichuan, Taiyuan, Zhending, Changsha, Lu, Chengyang, Zhongshan, Zhao, Hejian, Zuopingyi, Shanyang, Chu, Qi, Langya, Jinan, Donghai, Dongping, Pei, Beihai, Guangling, Guangping, Qiancheng, Julu, Runan, Lecheng, Changshan, Xiapi, Jiyin, Fuling, Pingchun, Jiangling, Liang, Xiping, Qinghe, Rencheng, Liu'an, Chen, Pengcheng, Jibei, Guangzong, Le'an, Pingyuan, Guangchuan, Anping, Bohai, Ganling, Chenliu, Hongnong, Nanyang, Donghai, Boling, and Hanzhong. Non-agnatic Eastern Han principalities (AD 40-42, AD 216-220) were Dai (House of Lu) and Wei (House of Cao).
