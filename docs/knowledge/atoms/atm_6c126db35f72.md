# Global outdoor mural gallery movement on post-industrial facades

`atm_6c126db35f72` · **culture** / global

Cities recovering from industrial decline have used large-scale murals on blank building walls as regeneration tools: Philadelphia's Mural Arts Program, the Bristol/Bristol scene tied to Banksy, Berlin's East Side Gallery on Wall remnants, and Valparaíso's hillside street-art district in Chile. These programs convert derelict or plain facades into tourist-drawing outdoor galleries, often coordinated by municipal or nonprofit curatorial bodies.
