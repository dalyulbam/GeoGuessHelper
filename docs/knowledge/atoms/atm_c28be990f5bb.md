# Guardrails and full winter-maintenance roads as the high-budget alternative

`atm_c28be990f5bb` · **economy** / global

Wealthier, higher-traffic road corridors in densely populated cold regions (e.g. Germany's Autobahns, Swiss alpine passes) invest in steel guardrails, heated bridge decks, avalanche galleries and continuous plowing rather than relying on passive markers like snow poles. This reflects a cost-tradeoff: low-traffic rural roads accept minimal passive infrastructure while high-traffic corridors justify capital-intensive active safety systems.
