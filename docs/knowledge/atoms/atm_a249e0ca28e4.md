# Global fjord family: Norway, Chile, New Zealand, Alaska

`atm_a249e0ca28e4` · **geography** / global

Glacially carved fjords with sheer rock walls rising from the sea occur wherever ice-age glaciers met coastal mountain ranges: Norway's Sognefjord and Geirangerfjord, Chile's Patagonian fjords near Puerto Natales, New Zealand's Milford and Doubtful Sounds in Fiordland, and Alaska's Tracy Arm and Glacier Bay. All share deep U-shaped cross-sections, hanging valleys with waterfalls, and glacial silt-laden meltwater at their heads.
