# Barcelona's Vallès Occidental as a knowledge-industry corridor

`atm_4c34ab75768e` · **economy** / polity · cell `sp3ebxf`

The Vallès Occidental comarca northwest of Barcelona (including Cerdanyola del Vallès, Sabadell, and Sant Cugat del Vallès) forms a metropolitan tech and research corridor along the C-58 motorway, distinct from Barcelona's older industrial belt. It combines large public research infrastructure (universities, synchrotrons, science parks) with residential commuter suburbs, reflecting a shift from textile/manufacturing industry toward R&D, biotech, and applied technology employment.
