# One-lane bridge: NZ pictogram+blue arrow plate vs Australian worded sign

`atm_c0028c87af29` · **geography** / country · cell `rbv6fj8`

In New Zealand a single-lane bridge is announced by a yellow diamond showing a black narrowing-bridge pictogram, usually with a blue rectangular plate carrying a large white arrow (your priority) and a small red opposing arrow. In Australia you would instead see a black-on-white or black-on-yellow worded 'ONE LANE BRIDGE' panel and a separate GIVE WAY arrangement without the blue arrow plate. Seeing the blue arrow-priority plate essentially settles NZ.
