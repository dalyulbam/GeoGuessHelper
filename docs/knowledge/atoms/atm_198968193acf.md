# Local mayoral-title diversity across Slavic and European languages

`atm_198968193acf` · **language** / global

Terms for 'mayor' vary sharply across Europe even among closely related languages: Slovak 'starosta', Czech 'starosta', Polish 'wójt' (rural)/'burmistrz' (town)/'prezydent' (city), Slovenian 'župan', Serbian/Croatian 'gradonačelnik', German 'Bürgermeister', French 'maire', and Hungarian 'polgármester'. These titles are reliable linguistic markers for identifying country of origin on election posters and municipal signage.
