# Albanian independence declared at Vlorë in 1912

`atm_6f410dc5745b` · **history** / country · cell `srjy70q`

**시기**: 1912 ~ 1912

On 28 November 1912, Ismail Qemali raised the national flag and proclaimed Albania's independence from the Ottoman Empire in Vlorë, making the city Albania's first capital and seat of the provisional government. This event is central to Albanian national identity, and Vlorë is celebrated as the cradle of independence.
