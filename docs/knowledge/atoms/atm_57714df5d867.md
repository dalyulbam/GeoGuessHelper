# Croatian coastal and Zagreb population magnets offsetting hinterland decline

`atm_57714df5d867` · **economy** / country · cell `u25kesm`

**시기**: 2011 ~ 2021

While Lika-Senj and other interior Croatian counties depopulate, Zagreb and coastal tourist counties like Istria and Split-Dalmatia have experienced comparatively milder population loss or even growth, driven by tourism revenue, university employment, and internal migration from the depopulating hinterland. This internal redistribution mirrors broader Adriatic coast-versus-interior divides seen in Croatia and neighboring Balkan states.
