# Dynasties/khanates of Azerbaijan

`atm_e2a755121847` · **history** / country

Azerbaijan's rulers included the Arsacid dynasty of Caucasian Albania, Mihranids, Mazyadid (Yazidid) dynasty, Shaddadids (951–1199), Rawadid dynasty (955–1116), Seljuq dynasty, Eldiguzids, Javanshir clan (1748–1822, Karabakh Khanate), House of Black Monk (Shaki Khanate), various Khanates of the Caucasus, House of Holstein-Gottorp-Romanov (1813–1917, Russian rule), and the Aliyev family (1969–present, extant).
