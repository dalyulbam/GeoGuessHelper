# Indian city-renaming movement erasing colonial/Mughal names

`atm_b8f95b26bab9` · **culture** / country

India has seen a wave of city renamings asserting regional, Hindu, or pre-colonial identity over Mughal or British-era names: Bombay to Mumbai (1995), Madras to Chennai (1996), Calcutta to Kolkata (2001), and Allahabad to Prayagraj (2018), alongside Aurangabad's 2023 renaming. These moves are often politically contested, framed as decolonization or communal assertion.
