# Italian brown 'Parco Fluviale Regionale' panels name the river and thus the province

`atm_a26ee42e2c47` · **geography** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Italy signs riparian protected areas with brown tourist panels reading 'Parco Fluviale Regionale' plus the river name, which effectively hands you the basin and province. In Spain or France the equivalent protected-area signs use different wording and colour codes and rarely embed the hydronym in the same standardized way, so treat these brown panels as a hard regional anchor rather than generic tourism clutter.
