# Uskoci (Uskoks) of Senj

`atm_5b2b5964e8b3` · **history** / polity · cell `srfxgpc`

The Uskoci were irregular military refugee-fighters who fled the Ottoman conquest of Bosnia and settled at Senj on the Croatian Adriatic coast in the 16th century, forming a frontier militia that defended the town against Ottoman and, later, Venetian forces until roughly the 1620s. Their stronghold, Nehaj Fortress, was completed in 1558 using materials salvaged from churches and monasteries outside the town walls, and remains the enduring symbol of Senj's identity.
