# Borromeo family territorial and religious influence in the Lago Maggiore region

`atm_3f5066a286fc` · **history** / polity · cell `u0nh6e5`

The House of Borromeo, a powerful noble family, held territorial dominion centered on Arona and the Borromean Islands in Lago Maggiore from the late medieval period onward, shaping the region's politics, economy, and religious life. Saint Carlo Borromeo (1538-1584), a major Counter-Reformation figure and Archbishop of Milan, was born into this family at Arona's castle; a colossal early bronze statue (the 'Sancarlone', among the oldest large-scale statues in the world) commemorates him nearby.
