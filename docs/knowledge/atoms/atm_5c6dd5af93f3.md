# Mediterranean karst coastlines beyond the Adriatic

`atm_5c6dd5af93f3` · **geography** / global

Karst limestone coastal morphology similar to Dalmatia's — steep bare ridges close behind narrow coastal terraces with parallel offshore islands — also characterizes Greece's Ionian coast and islands (e.g. Kefalonia's karst caves and sinkholes), the Kras/Karst plateau region on the Slovenian-Italian border that gave karst its name, and parts of coastal Turkey (Taurus Mountains meeting the Mediterranean near Antalya).
