# Lake District tourism spillover into rural Mapuche/campesino corridors

`atm_a76ff2e557d1` · **economy** / region · cell `62ux7xt`

Around Chilean Lake District tourist hubs like Villarrica and Pucón, the lodging and activity economy (cabañas, lodges, artisanal beer, adventure tourism such as husky or trekking tours) increasingly spills into surrounding rural smallholder corridors, appearing alongside subsistence dairy/pasture farming, forestry plantations, and informal roadside minimarkets selling major beverage brands (e.g., CCU, Pepsi) priced in thousands of pesos. This creates a visible layering of modest indigenous/campesino smallholdings with emerging tourism infrastructure.
