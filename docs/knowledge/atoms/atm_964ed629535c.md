# Bessastaðir, residence of the President of Iceland

`atm_964ed629535c` · **culture** / country · cell `ge2kts4`

Bessastaðir is a historic estate on the Álftanes peninsula in Garðabær that serves as the official residence of the President of Iceland, giving the municipality national symbolic significance. Iceland is a parliamentary republic whose head of state is a directly elected president; Ólafur Ragnar Grímsson and Guðni Th. Jóhannesson (2016–2024) are among those who have resided there.
