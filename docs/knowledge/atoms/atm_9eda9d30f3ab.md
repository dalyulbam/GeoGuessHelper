# Pronatalist and repatriation policy responses to Croatian depopulation

`atm_9eda9d30f3ab` · **economy** / country

In response to demographic decline, Croatia has introduced pronatalist child benefits and housing subsidy schemes, while some coastal and island municipalities offer incentives for young families and diaspora return to counteract outmigration. These measures parallel similar pronatalist and repopulation policies in Hungary, Poland, and Japan's rural revitalization programs.
