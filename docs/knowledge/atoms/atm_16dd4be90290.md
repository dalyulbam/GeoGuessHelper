# Criteria for inclusion in list of former sovereign states

`atm_16dd4be90290` · **history** / global

To be included in the list, a polity must have claimed statehood and either had de facto control over a territory, a population, a government, and a capacity to enter into relations with other states, or have been recognised as a state by at least one other state. The list excludes variant governments of a state and variations of countries' official long-form names. Criteria are similar to those used for the list of states with limited recognition.
