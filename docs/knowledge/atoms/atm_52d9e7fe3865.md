# Guinea savanna–rainforest transition zone of Nigeria's Middle Belt/South-South border

`atm_52d9e7fe3865` · **geography** / region · cell `s1nmbk3`

The area spanning northern Cross River State, Benue State and Ebonyi State sits in a tropical wet-and-dry to rainforest transition zone, with humid overcast rainy seasons, laterite soils, and oil-palm vegetation. It functions as a road corridor (e.g. Katsina-Ala–Calabar road) linking the Middle Belt to the Atlantic coast at Calabar, distinct from the drier Sahelian north and the denser Igbo/Yoruba cores further south.
