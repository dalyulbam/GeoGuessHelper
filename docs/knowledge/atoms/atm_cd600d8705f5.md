# Leatherback turtle nesting sites on Peninsular Malaysia's east coast

`atm_cd600d8705f5` · **nature** / region · cell `w29x2w0`

The east coast of Peninsular Malaysia (Pahang/Terengganu) hosts rare leatherback sea turtle nesting beaches, with nesting season roughly April to September. Grassroots turtle conservation programs emerged in the early 1990s in response to declining sea turtle populations, and turtle-watching (especially at night) has become a signature eco-tourism draw for coastal villages in this region.
