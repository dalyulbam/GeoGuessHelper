# Micronesian and other Pacific island dynasties

`atm_36640a3ec7ef` · **history** / region

Kiribati: Butaritari ruling family (mid-late 19th century-1892) on Butaritari and Makin Islands; Kingdom of Abemama (mid-late 18th century-c.1979). Marshall Islands: Kabua dynasty (c.1863-1910). Micronesia: Saudeleur dynasty (c.1100-1628); Gagil dynasty (?-1947, Yapese Empire). Nauru: House of Emea (1888-?), after uniting the 12 tribes of Nauru. Cook Islands: Ariki families (?-present, non-sovereign, Kingdom of Rarotonga). Samoan Islands: Tui Manu'a Confederacy (dates unknown). Tonga: Tuʻi Tonga (c.900-1865), Tuʻi Haʻatakalaua (c.1470-1797), House of Tupou (1845-present, sovereign).
