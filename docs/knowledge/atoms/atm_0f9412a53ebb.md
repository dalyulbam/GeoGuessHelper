# Non-sovereign dynasties by continent

`atm_0f9412a53ebb` · **history** / global

**시기**: 1696 ~ 1918

Numerous sovereign states and territories host non-sovereign dynasties ruling subnational chiefdoms or peoples, sometimes officially recognized (e.g. Botswana's chiefdoms) and sometimes not (e.g. the Te Wherowhero dynasty of the Māori King Movement in New Zealand). Africa: Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, DR Congo, Ivory Coast, Equatorial Guinea, Ethiopia, Gabon, The Gambia, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Kenya, Liberia, Madagascar, Malawi, Mauritania, Namibia, Niger, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Somalia, Somaliland, South Africa, South Sudan, Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. Asia: Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Timor-Leste, United Arab Emirates. Europe: Bailiwick of Guernsey, Montenegro (Petrović-Njegoš dynasty, official ruling dynasty 1696-1918, recognized since 2011 in a cultural role). North America: Canada, Panama, USA. Oceania: Cook Islands, Marshall Islands, Micronesia, New Caledonia, New Zealand, Palau, Samoa, Tuvalu, Wallis and Futuna. South America: Bolivia.
