# Great Powers' partition of Albania after 1912 independence

`atm_fd3cb2045447` · **history** / country

Despite the 1912 Vlorë declaration, the 1913 Treaty of London and the Conference of London, dominated by Austria-Hungary, Italy, and other Great Powers, drastically reduced Albania's proposed borders, ceding Kosovo to Serbia and Chameria-adjacent areas to Greece, leaving roughly half of ethnic Albanian territory outside the new state — a grievance still shaping Balkan politics.
