# Himalayan foothills and Terai as topographic counterpoint

`atm_3c3147af69b0` · **geography** / region · cell `tuuh6y5`

Immediately north of the flat Gangetic Plain, the land rises abruptly into the Terai lowland belt and then the Himalayan foothills of Nepal and northern India, producing a sharp topographic and climatic contrast within short distances. This juxtaposition of flat monsoon plain against mountainous relief shapes distinct settlement patterns, crops, and street tree species (temperate species replacing gulmohar at higher elevation).
