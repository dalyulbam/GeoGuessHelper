# Papa mudstone hill country of eastern North Island, New Zealand

`atm_e0b610416d73` · **geography** / region · cell `rbv6fn6`

Soft, pale grey-white Tertiary papa (mudstone/siltstone) underlies much of the rain-shadowed eastern North Island (Hawke's Bay, Wairarapa, Tararua, Gisborne), producing rounded pasture hills prone to slumping, slip erosion, and cut-bank exposures. This contrasts with the South Island's harder greywacke/schist terrain and alpine relief, and with the andesitic volcanic-ash soils of Taranaki/Waikato, making papa geology a reliable regional discriminator within New Zealand.
