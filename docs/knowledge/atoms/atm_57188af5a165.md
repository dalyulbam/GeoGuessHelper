# Rijeka dubrovačka: drowned-valley ria inlet near Dubrovnik

`atm_57188af5a165` · **geography** / region · cell `srsfg8t`

Rijeka dubrovačka (fed by the Ombla spring) is a narrow, fjord-like drowned-valley (ria) inlet running inland from the Adriatic near Dubrovnik, with steep hills on both banks and calm green water. This ria morphology, hosting boatyards, marinas and Ragusan summer villas along its quays, is distinct from the open-sea coves typical elsewhere on the Dalmatian coast.
