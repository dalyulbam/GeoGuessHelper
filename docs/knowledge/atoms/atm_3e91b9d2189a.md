# Roman governors administering Dalmatia's frontier

`atm_3e91b9d2189a` · **history** / polity

Publius Cornelius Dolabella served as legate governor of the Roman province of Dalmatia in the early 1st century AD under Tiberius, overseeing construction of military roads and boundary arbitration among native communities, as attested by inscriptions across the Velebit and Lika hinterland. His administrative work followed the suppression of the Great Illyrian Revolt (6-9 AD), which had been put down by future emperor Tiberius and Germanicus.
