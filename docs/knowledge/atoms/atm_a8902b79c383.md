# Zug's cherry-growing tradition and Zuger Kirschtorte

`atm_a8902b79c383` · **culture** / region · cell `u0qh3ck`

The Canton of Zug has cultivated cherries (Chriesi) for over 600 years, favored by its mild lakeside climate; the Zuger Chriesi has AOP status and intangible-heritage recognition. The region's signature dessert, Zuger Kirschtorte, is soaked in kirsch (cherry brandy) distilled locally, and cherry culture is celebrated annually through the Chriesisturm (Cherry Race) festival beginning with the ringing of the 'Chriesigloggä' bell.
