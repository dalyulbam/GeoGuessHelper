# Standardized state-funded rural road programs in developing economies

`atm_606ab6ca1071` · **economy** / global

Governments in many developing countries run standardized rural road paving programs analogous to DPWH's PCCP standard, such as India's Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) building all-weather rural roads, and Indonesia's village road grants (Dana Desa), each producing a recognizable uniform visual signature of narrow, minimally engineered concrete or asphalt roads linking farms to markets.
