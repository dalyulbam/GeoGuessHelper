# Marina/boatyard hinterland zones supporting a tourist old-town core

`atm_0b13748cb731` · **economy** / region · cell `srsfsxb`

Sheltered inlets or rias near a major historic tourist city often develop as marina, boatyard, and logistics hinterlands (yacht storage, chandlery, courier depots, car rental, bank branches) precisely because land in the historic core is too expensive or protected for such uses. This produces a functional split where the old town hosts tourism/retail while a nearby sheltered water body hosts the boat-service and logistics economy supporting it.
