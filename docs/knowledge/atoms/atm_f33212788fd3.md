# Yunnan native chieftaincies (tusi) established under Ming, 1382-1394

`atm_f33212788fd3` · **history** / region

A large group of hereditary native chieftaincies (tusi/土司) in Yunnan province were established starting AD 1382 under the Ming dynasty, ruled by various local noble houses of Bai, Yi, Dai, Nakhi, Hani, and other ethnicities. Examples include the Chiefdoms of Yimen County, Dengchuan Prefecture, Dali Prefecture, Caojian, Qingsuobi, Annanpo, Fengyuxiang, Upper Jiangzui, Shijing, Shenmodong, Shundangjing, Shundangyanjing, Langqiong County, Deshengguanyi, Yunnanyi, Erxiyi, Guangtong, Nalouchadian, Kuirongdian, Entuodian, Xichudian, Diexi, Yingwuguan, Zhennanguan, Shayi, and Wazha (all AD 1382-?), plus Heqing Tutongzhi, Heqing Tuzhishi, Huidengguan Tuxunjian (two lines, House of Yang and House of Cheng), Chuxiong County Tuxiancheng, Dingyuan County Tuzhubu, Xi'e County Tuzhixian, Heqing Prefecture Tuyicheng, Zhennan Prefecture Tupanguan, Baoshan Prefecture Tuzhizhou, Shi'erguan Tuxunjian (two lines), and Chuxiong Prefecture Tuzhifu (all AD 1382-?). Further chiefdoms established AD 1383 include Na-shi (to 20th century), Yunlong Prefecture (to 20th century, with subordinate Chiefdom of Daxingdi), Dahou Prefecture Tuxunjian, Axiongguan, Puchang, Luliang Prefecture, Yilongyi, Lower Jiangzui, Jianganchang, Manshenzhai, Shanjingyanjing, Jujin Prefecture, Ami Prefecture, Mishayanjing, Lianxiangguan, Nanpingguan, Pindian, Nidian, Putuokong, Yaozhou, Lanzhou Tuzhizhou, Shimenguan Tuxunjian, Chuxiong Tuxunjian, Yunnan County Tuxiancheng, and Zhennan Prefecture Tutongzhi. Additional chiefdoms founded AD 1384-1400 include Malongtalangdian (1384-1501), Dingxiling, Heqing Tuzhifu, Chuxiong County Tuzhubu, Yunnan County Tuzhubu (1384-?), Gelatang, Shalu, Chishuipeng (1385-?), Greater Pingfa, Jianchuan Prefecture Tuzhizhou (1386-?), Abangxiang (1387-?), Yangyi (1388-?), Nandian (1389-1950), Fengxi (1390-1659), Shimenguan Tuqianfuzhang, Babai Dadian Xuanweisi (1391-?), Lianxiangguan (1393-?), Pingyi County, Mengzi County Tuzhixian (1394-?), Yuezhou (1395-?), Danping (1397-1663), Gengma (1397-1950), Luoci County (1400-?), and Longdawei, Fuzhou, Luokongdian, Zuonengzhai, Shi'erguan Tufuzhangguan, Jianchuan Prefecture Tuqianhu (all 14th century-?).
