# House of Windsor (Commonwealth realms)

`atm_4c5f8c97d93d` · **history** / polity

The House of Windsor, renamed in 1917 by George V from the House of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha, originates from Thuringia and Bavaria in modern Germany. Its current monarch Charles III reigns over 15 Commonwealth realms: Antigua and Barbuda, Australia, The Bahamas, Belize, Canada, Grenada, Jamaica, Realm of New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Solomon Islands, Tuvalu, and the United Kingdom.
