# Colonial-era leather and textile industrialization in Gangetic Plain cities

`atm_36c2f7426aa1` · **history** / polity · cell `tu9necb`

**시기**: 1800 ~ 1947

Under British colonial rule, some Gangetic Plain cities (e.g. Kanpur) developed as major leather-goods and textile manufacturing hubs to supply British military demand (harnesses, saddlery, boots) during the 19th and 20th centuries, earning nicknames like 'Manchester of the East'. This industrial legacy, tied to river access and abundant labor, persists into the modern economy though facing decline from pollution regulation and competition.
