# Modern flat-roof housing replacing snow-shedding vernacular

`atm_5c89fb5d7fd9` · **architecture** / region

In many Japanese snow-belt cities, postwar reinforced-concrete apartment blocks and prefabricated houses increasingly adopt flat or low-slope roofs paired with rooftop or under-floor snow-melting systems (roof-top sprinklers, electric heating cables), displacing the traditional heavy hipped-tile roof as urbanization and land costs favor denser, standardized construction over steep traditional forms.
