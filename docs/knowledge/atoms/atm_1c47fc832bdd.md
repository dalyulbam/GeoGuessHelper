# Rural highway strip economy in northern Vietnam commune centres

`atm_1c47fc832bdd` · **economy** / region · cell `w7g6609`

Commune centres along national highways (quốc lộ, QL) in Vietnam's midlands typically consist of a thin strip of family-run shophouses selling agricultural inputs (fertilizer, piping, water filters, animal feed) rather than a dense continuous shophouse front. Construction is incremental narrow-lot concrete 'tube housing' (nhà ống) fronted by corrugated-steel workshops, with motorbikes as the dominant private transport mode. Denser district or town centres show continuous shopfronts, traffic signals and administrative complexes by contrast.
