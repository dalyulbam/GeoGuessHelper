# Mexican historical periods

`atm_9a78e996a5e2` · **history** / country

Ancient and Pre-Columbian Mexico includes the Olmecs (1500-400 BC), Mayans (3000 BC-600 AD), Teotihuacan (1-500 AD), Toltecs (800-1000 AD), and Aztecs (1000-1512 AD). Colonial Mexico covers the Spanish conquest of Mexico (1519-1521) and New Spain (1535-1821). The Independence Era includes the Mexican War of Independence (1810-1821), First Mexican Empire (1821-1823), First Mexican Republic (1824-1835), and Centralist Republic of Mexico (1835-1846). Liberal Mexico covers the Second Federal Republic (1846-1863), Second French Mexican Empire (1864-1867), Restored Republic (1867-1876), and Porfiriato (1876-1911). Modern Mexico includes Revolutionary Mexico (1910-1917), Maximato (1928-1934), PRI One-Party State (1934-2000), and Contemporary Mexico (2000-present).
