# Grey-market RHD/LHD import mismatches worldwide

`atm_a78bf6ec32f7` · **culture** / global

Countries near a mismatched-drive-side neighbor often develop large secondhand import markets that violate the 'correct' steering side. Right-hand-drive Japanese used cars flood Mongolia, Myanmar, and parts of the Caribbean and East Africa despite those countries driving on the right or having mixed rules. Myanmar in particular has an overwhelming majority of RHD cars despite official right-hand traffic, a legacy of cheap Japanese imports after import liberalization in the 2010s.
