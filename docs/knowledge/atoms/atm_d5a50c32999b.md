# Industrialized bay corridors adjacent to megacities

`atm_d5a50c32999b` · **geography** / global

Narrow coastal industrial corridors backed by hills near a megacity recur worldwide: the İzmit Bay corridor east of Istanbul, the Keihanshin industrial belt around Osaka Bay in Japan, and the Bohai Bay industrial rim near Beijing-Tianjin in China. These corridors combine deepwater port access, flat coastal land for heavy industry, and proximity to a massive urban labor/consumer market.
