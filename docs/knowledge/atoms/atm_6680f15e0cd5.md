# Global wave of new LNG import terminals post-2010

`atm_6680f15e0cd5` · **economy** / global

Following the 2000s-2010s shale gas boom and the 2022 Russian gas cutoff to Europe, countries built or expanded LNG regasification terminals to diversify supply: Poland's Świnoujście terminal, Lithuania's Klaipėda floating storage regasification unit (FSRU) 'Independence', Germany's rapidly built Wilhelmshaven and Brunsbüttel FSRUs, and Italy's Panigaglia and Piombino terminals. These terminals typically pair floating or onshore regasification units with pipeline grids to redistribute gas inland.
