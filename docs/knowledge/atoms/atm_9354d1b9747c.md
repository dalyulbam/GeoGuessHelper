# Treaty 4 territory and Cree/Assiniboine-CPR layered place naming on the Canadian prairies

**시기**: 1874 ~ ?

Southern Saskatchewan, including Regina, lies within Treaty 4 territory, historically inhabited by Cree and Assiniboine (Nakota) peoples. Urban naming layers reflect this history: Indigenous-derived toponyms (e.g. Wascana, Assiniboia) coexist with Canadian Pacific Railway-era settlement names, a common pattern across prairie cities founded during 19th-century railway expansion.
