# Meiji abolition of the han system and daimyo domains

`atm_609755d083a1` · **history** / period

**시기**: 1868 ~ 1871

The Meiji Restoration of 1868 abolished the feudal han (domain) system in 1871 through the haihan-chiken edict, stripping daimyo like the Maeda of political authority and converting their domains into centrally administered prefectures. This ended over two and a half centuries of decentralized daimyo patronage that had shaped castle-town craft economies across Japan.
