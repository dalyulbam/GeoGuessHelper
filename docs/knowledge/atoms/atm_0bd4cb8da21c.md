# FANCESA and Sucre's cement industry footprint

`atm_0bd4cb8da21c` · **economy** / region · cell `6s9edy6`

FANCESA (Fábrica Nacional de Cemento S.A.), headquartered in Sucre, Bolivia, is a major regional cement producer whose branding saturates construction sites and material yards throughout Chuquisaca Department and neighboring valley cities. Its ubiquitous signage on cement bags, banners, and delivery trucks serves as a reliable local-economy marker distinguishing Sucre from other Bolivian valley cities like Cochabamba or Tarija, which rely on different regional cement suppliers.
