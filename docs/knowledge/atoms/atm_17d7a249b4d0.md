# Whistler and Vancouver housing-affordability spillover pressure

`atm_17d7a249b4d0` · **economy** / region

Squamish's rapid growth is partly a counter-effect of housing unaffordability in Vancouver and Whistler, which pushed workers and lifestyle migrants to relocate to a cheaper intermediate town connected by highway commute. This spillover pattern -- affordability-driven migration to secondary towns near expensive resort or metro cores -- recurs near Aspen, Lake Tahoe, and Banff, where nearby towns like Canmore absorb displaced workforce populations.
