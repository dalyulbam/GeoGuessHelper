# Great Maya city-states and their sculptural schools

`atm_3aea28b9b1fd` · **culture** / global

Quiriguá's Motagua School parallels other regional Maya artistic traditions tied to specific city-states: the Petén style of Tikal, the Usumacinta-region lintel carving of Yaxchilan, and the Palenque school known for refined stucco relief. Each Classic Maya center (250-900 CE) developed a distinct sculptural idiom for dynastic monuments while sharing the Long Count calendar and hieroglyphic writing system.
