# Functional catchment vs administrative boundary mismatch

`atm_8083543e02d9` · **geography** / global

Worldwide, settlements near state or national borders often have daily-life catchments (shopping, schooling, jobs) oriented toward a nearer cross-border town rather than their own administrative center, a pattern seen in border towns like Kansas City (Kansas/Missouri) or Basel's tri-border region (Switzerland/France/Germany), and along linear coastal corridors in Southeast Asia. This mismatch reflects road/rail network geometry overriding jurisdictional lines.
