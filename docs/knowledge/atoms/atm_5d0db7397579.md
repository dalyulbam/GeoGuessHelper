# Single-pylon cable-stayed bridges spanning drowned river mouths worldwide

`atm_5d0db7397579` · **architecture** / global

Single-pylon cable-stayed bridges are a favored solution for spanning narrow, deep ria or fjord mouths worldwide, including the Tatara Bridge approach spans in Japan and Portugal's Ponte Vasco da Gama sections; their asymmetric single-tower design suits sites where one bank offers firmer foundation ground or where navigational clearance is needed on one side, similar to the Franjo Tuđman Bridge's role at Rijeka Dubrovačka.
