# Barnaul: Demidov silver-mining origins and industrial capital

`atm_7c89b761a51c` · **history** / city · cell `vc7x8jq`

**시기**: 1730 ~ ?

Barnaul was founded around 1730 as a silver- and copper-smelting settlement of the Demidov industrial dynasty at the confluence of the Barnaulka and Ob rivers. It grew into a major mining, industrial and administrative hub and became the administrative capital of Altai Krai in 1937. Today it is a city of roughly 620,000-690,000 people and the fifth-most-populous city in Siberia.
