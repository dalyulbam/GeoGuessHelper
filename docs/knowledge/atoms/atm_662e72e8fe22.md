# Timber-yard retail strips flag Vyatka–Kama taiga raion towns over Upper Volga ones

`atm_662e72e8fe22` · **economy** / region · cell `v47mmjn`

In Kirov Oblast and Perm Krai small-town main roads commonly carry banner ads for sawn softwood assortments (брус, доска, вагонка) next to a federal discounter, because local economies rest on logging and sawmilling. In Yaroslavl/Kostroma or Ivanovo towns you would more often see textile-mill brick complexes, dairy or machine-plant signage and fewer raw-timber trade yards. A timber-assortment banner should push your estimate east into the taiga belt.
