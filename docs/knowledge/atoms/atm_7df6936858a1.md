# Yuezhi and the Five Xihou (Kushan predecessors)

`atm_7df6936858a1` · **history** / region

The Yuezhi were a Central Asian people divided into the Greater Yuezhi and Lesser Yuezhi (undated). The Five Xihou (Yabgus) were five sub-groups: Xiumi Xihou (capital/ruler Jiabei), Shuangmi Xihou (Zhexuemosun), Guishuang Xihou (Qiandun) — the group from which the Kushan dynasty later arose — Xidun Xihou (Fudisha), and Gaofu Xihou (Yanfuye).
