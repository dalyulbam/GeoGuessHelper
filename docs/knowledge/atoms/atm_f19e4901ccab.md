# Malaysia's left-hand traffic and Jalur Gemilang flag as country markers

`atm_f19e4901ccab` · **geography** / country · cell `w29x2w0`

Malaysia drives on the left and uses the Jalur Gemilang flag: red-and-white stripes with a crescent moon and 14-point star on a blue canton. These features, combined with Malay-language Latin-script signage (no Chinese or Tamil), distinguish Malaysia from neighboring Indonesia (red-white bicolor, right-hand traffic), Thailand (Thai script, tricolor flag), and Brunei (yellow-based flag with Jawi script).
