# Beyneu as western Kazakhstan rail-and-oil transit node

`atm_9a557d0b3688` · **economy** / region · cell `v0nxmzr`

Beyneu, in Mangystau Region, is a rail junction town on the Kazakhstan–Uzbekistan and Caspian-oilfield rail lines, serving as an economic anchor in an otherwise unproductive saline desert. Its economy depends on rail-transit employment and Mangystau hydrocarbon extraction rather than agriculture, producing a boomtown pattern of upgraded town-approach infrastructure (guardrail, lane markings, street lighting) against minimal, unshouldered open-desert highway conditions outside the settlement.
