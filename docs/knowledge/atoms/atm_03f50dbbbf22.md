# FANCESA hoardings mark Chuquisaca; COBOCE marks Cochabamba

`atm_03f50dbbbf22` · **economy** / region · cell `6s9edy7`

Bolivian construction-site banners advertise the regional cement supplier: FANCESA (with CONCRETEC) around Sucre and Chuquisaca. In Cochabamba you would instead see COBOCE branding, and around La Paz/Oruro SOBOCE/Viacha. Reading the cement brand narrows a Bolivian valley city faster than architecture does.
