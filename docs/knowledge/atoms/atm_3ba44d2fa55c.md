# Artisanal fishing economy of Campeche's Gulf lagoon towns

`atm_3ba44d2fa55c` · **economy** / region · cell `9gqfsnv`

Small Gulf-coast lagoon towns in Campeche state (outside the oil-industry hub of Ciudad del Carmen) typically run on artisanal fishing economies — shrimp, snook (robalo), red snapper (pargo), mullet, octopus, crab and oyster caught from fibreglass outboard pangas — supplemented by small-scale domestic beach tourism (palapas, seafood restaurants, boat tours) and informal/family-based trade, rather than industrial or extractive employers. Youth out-migration toward oil-services jobs in Ciudad del Carmen or tourism jobs in the Riviera Maya is common.
