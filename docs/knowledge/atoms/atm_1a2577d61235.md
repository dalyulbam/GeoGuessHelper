# Vítkovice and Upper Silesian industrial dynasties

`atm_1a2577d61235` · **economy** / region · cell `u2v5800`

The Rothschild family financed and controlled the Vítkovice Ironworks in Ostrava from the 19th century, one of the Habsburg Empire's largest steelworks, while the Silesian coal and steel industry around Katowice was shaped by Prussian and later Polish state industrialists following 19th-century German industrialization of Upper Silesia. These dynasties established the coal-steel complex that still defines the cross-border belt's economy.
