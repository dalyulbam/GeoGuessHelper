# Nasrid atalaya watchtowers guarding the Vega de Granada

`atm_1b291689479e` · **architecture** / region · cell `eyt7v0d`

The Kingdom of Granada (Nasrid dynasty, 13th-15th c.) built a network of stone atalaya (watchtowers) on hilltops surrounding the Vega de Granada to provide visual surveillance and early warning across the agricultural plain and approaches to the capital. Example: the Albolote atalaya on Sierra Elvira, a cylindrical tower roughly 5.4 m diameter and 9.5 m tall, offering panoramic views over Granada city.
