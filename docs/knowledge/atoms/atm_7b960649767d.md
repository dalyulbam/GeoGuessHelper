# Sri Lanka's 'Coconut Triangle' and coir export industry

`atm_7b960649767d` · **economy** / region · cell `tc2c139`

The area between Kurunegala, Puttalam, and Negombo forms Sri Lanka's 'Coconut Triangle,' a coconut-growing heartland supporting coir/coconut-fiber export industries (fiber pith, husk chips, coir yarn, grow bags) alongside fishing and tourism. Coastal towns in this belt historically also produced jaggery, toddy/arrack from palm sap, and (formerly) cinnamon as a colonial-era export.
