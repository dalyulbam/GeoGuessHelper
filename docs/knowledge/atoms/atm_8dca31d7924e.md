# Sonoran vs Chihuahuan vs Bajío vs Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt landscape discriminators

`atm_8dca31d7924e` · **geography** / region · cell `9gbn2rp`

Mexico's arid/semi-arid interior can be zoned by diagnostic flora and soil: Sonoran NW (Sonora/Sinaloa) shows saguaro, palo verde, ocotillo on reddish granitic soils; the Bajío (Guanajuato/Querétaro) is greener with irrigated sorghum/maize plains and few yuccas; the central volcanic belt (Hidalgo/Tlaxcala/Puebla) shows maguey pulquero rows on dark volcanic andosol with tepetate cuts; the Chihuahuan Altiplano (SLP/Zacatecas/Durango) shows Yucca filifera and nopal hedges on pale limestone soils.
