# UK dual metric/imperial height restriction signage

`atm_7493c55d0bf4` · **language** / country · cell `gcvy4mw`

UK road bridge/tunnel height restriction signs display both metric and imperial units (e.g. '4.4 m 14'-6"') within a red-ringed circular sign. This dual marking is a distinctive UK convention; the Republic of Ireland uses metric-only height signs despite also driving on the left, allowing the two nations to be visually distinguished.
