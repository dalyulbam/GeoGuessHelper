# Counter-urbanization and rural revival movements

`atm_dad10402d915` · **economy** / global

Against depopulation trends, counter-movements promote rural revival: Japan's 'I-turn' and 'U-turn' migration incentive programs pay urbanites to relocate to shrinking towns, Spain's 'España Vaciada' movement advocates for depopulated interior provinces, and France's rural repopulation subsidies target villages under 3,500 residents. These remain modest in scale compared to the underlying outflow.
