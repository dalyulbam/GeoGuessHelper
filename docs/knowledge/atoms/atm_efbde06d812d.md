# Wilhelmine/Gründerzeit brick townscapes across former Prussian territories

`atm_efbde06d812d` · **architecture** / global

**시기**: 1870 ~ 1930

The red-and-yellow brick, steep-tile-roof townscape of former Prussian Pomerania and Neumark is part of a broader Gründerzeit/Wilhelmine architectural family found across former Prussian and Hanseatic territories, including Kaliningrad (former Königsberg), the Mazury region of Poland, and Baltic port towns like Gdańsk-adjacent Sopot. Similar brick Gothic Revival civic styles also mark Hanseatic League towns such as Lübeck, Rostock, and Riga.
