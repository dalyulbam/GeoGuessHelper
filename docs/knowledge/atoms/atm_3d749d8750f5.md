# Latin STOP on GOST posts means Kazakhstan, not Russia

`atm_3d749d8750f5` · **language** / country · cell `v0ny37g`

In Kazakhstan the octagonal stop sign carries Latin 'STOP' while the rest of the roadside kit stays Soviet-standard (striped concrete delineators, red-bordered triangles). In Russia you would instead see Cyrillic 'СТОП' on the octagon, plus Lukoil/Gazprom Neft/Tatneft branding at any roadside service point.
