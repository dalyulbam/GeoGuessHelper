# Agricultural consolidation pattern: cascine absorbed or abandoned amid mechanized farming

`atm_f947eed4f050` · **economy** / region · cell `spyrs4d`

In the mechanized arable belts of the Po Plain fringe, historic brick courtyard farmsteads (cascine) are frequently either renovated into single-family middle-income housing with modern fencing and paved forecourts, or left semi-derelict beside new concrete-block machinery sheds. This reflects land consolidation where fewer families now farm larger holdings than the pre-war multi-family courtyard system supported, alongside a local dual economy of mechanized cereal farming plus small/medium metalworking and packaging industry along provincial roads.
