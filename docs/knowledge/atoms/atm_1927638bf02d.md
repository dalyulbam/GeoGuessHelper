# Resource-town-to-recreation-economy transition pattern

`atm_1927638bf02d` · **economy** / global

Former resource-extraction towns worldwide have pivoted to outdoor recreation and tourism economies as extractive industries declined: former mining town Park City, Utah became a ski resort; former coal town Nelson, British Columbia turned to tourism; former slate/fishing villages in Wales and Norway rebranded around hiking and climbing. This transition typically leaves legacy industrial infrastructure (mills, rail lines, ports) repurposed or standing derelict alongside new tourism amenities.
