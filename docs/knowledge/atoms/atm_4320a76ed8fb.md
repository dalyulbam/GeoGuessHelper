# 1945 expulsion and Polonization of former German eastern territories

`atm_4320a76ed8fb` · **history** / region · cell `u36rgnm`

**시기**: 1945 ~ 1950

Following the 1945 Potsdam Agreement, the German population of Pomerania, Neumark, and Silesia was expelled and replaced by Polish settlers, many themselves displaced from territories annexed by the USSR further east. This population replacement contrasts with the architectural continuity of the Prussian-era townscapes, which were preserved and reused by new inhabitants under Polish administration rather than demolished.
