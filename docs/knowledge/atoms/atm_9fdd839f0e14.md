# Borromeo family and the shaping of Lago Maggiore's Belle Époque identity

`atm_9fdd839f0e14` · **history** / region · cell `u0nj1cw`

The House of Borromeo, an Italian noble family, built the Baroque palaces and gardens of Isola Bella and Isola Madre on Lago Maggiore starting in the 17th century, creating the aristocratic scenery that later fueled Stresa's Belle Époque hotel boom in the 19th-20th centuries. Their continued private ownership of the Borromean Islands is central to the lake's tourism branding.
