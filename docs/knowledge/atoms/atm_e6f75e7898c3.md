# Icelandic language and orthography

`atm_e6f75e7898c3` · **language** / country · cell `ge2kts4`

Icelandic is a North Germanic language written in the Latin script with distinctive characters ð (eth) and æ, plus þ (thorn) and accented vowels. Place names commonly use compound forms with suffixes like -bær (town), -braut (road/avenue), -holt (hill) and -hraun (lava field). Its orthography and vocabulary make signage instantly recognisable as Icelandic.
