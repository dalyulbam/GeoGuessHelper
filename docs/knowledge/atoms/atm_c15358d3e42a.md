# Post-industrial decline of Upper Silesian coal towns

`atm_c15358d3e42a` · **economy** / region · cell `u2vqqmw`

**시기**: 1990 ~ 2030

Since the 1990s, Upper Silesia's coal and steel industries have undergone major restructuring and mine closures under EU decarbonization pressure, leaving many interwar workers' housing blocks in towns like Bytom facing depopulation, vacancy and poverty, even as EU cohesion funds finance building retrofits. The Polish government's Just Transition Fund targets the region for economic diversification away from coal by the 2030s.
