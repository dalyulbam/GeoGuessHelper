# Extant sovereign dynasties ruling monarchies today

`atm_6d735965121b` · **history** / global

As of the article's writing, 44 sovereign realms are ruled by monarchs, 41 under dynastic control, with 26 currently reigning sovereign dynasties (two ruling more than one realm). The House of Windsor (founded by George V, from Thuringia/Bavaria) rules 15 Commonwealth realms including the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Jamaica, and others. Non-dynastic monarchies include Andorra, the Holy See/Vatican City, and the Sovereign Military Order of Malta.
