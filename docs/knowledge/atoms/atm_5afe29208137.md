# Russification and Soviet-era suppression of Baltic languages

`atm_5afe29208137` · **history** / region

Latvian, Estonian, and Lithuanian faced sustained pressure from Russification under the Russian Empire (late 19th century) and again under Soviet rule (1944-1991), when Russian was promoted as the language of administration and heavy in-migration of Russian speakers altered demographics, especially in cities like Riga and Daugavpils. Post-1991 independence saw language laws restoring Latvian and Estonian as sole state languages, a direct counter-reaction still contested in citizenship and language-testing policy today.
