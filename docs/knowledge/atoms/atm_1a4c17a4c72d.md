# Purépecha/Mesoamerican toponymic residue in mestizo Bajío communities

`atm_1a4c17a4c72d` · **culture** / region · cell `9exuvnc`

Communities on the Bajío/western Mesoamerican fringe (e.g. Guanajuato) can carry place names of Purépecha or other pre-Hispanic Mesoamerican derivation (such as names ending in patterns like -tiro, -cata) even though the present-day population is entirely Spanish-speaking mestizo with no living indigenous-language use, bilingual signage, or distinctive indigenous vernacular architecture (e.g. Purépecha trojes). This toponymic residue marks historical extension of a pre-Hispanic cultural sphere rather than a currently living indigenous community.
