# Icelandic rural service village public infrastructure standard

`atm_ba9c8744e74b` · **culture** / country · cell `ge9h53s`

Small Icelandic villages of a few hundred people (e.g. Hvammstangi) typically show strong public provision relative to size: paved streets, streetlights, sidewalks, a municipal swimming pool, a regional health clinic, at least one hotel/guesthouse, and often a rainbow crosswalk. This reflects national policy of even public-good distribution to rural municipalities rather than purely market-driven scale.
