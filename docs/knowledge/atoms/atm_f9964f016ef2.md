# African Great Lakes kingdoms

`atm_f9964f016ef2` · **history** / region

Great Lakes kingdoms include Kingdom of Busoga (to 1906), Kingdom of Rwanda (11th c.-1885/1962), Bunyoro-Kitara Kingdom (13th c.-1897), Kingdom of Buganda (14th c.-1894), Kingdom of Karagwe (1450-1963), Kingdom of Ankole (1478-1901/1967), Kingdom of Burundi (1680-1890/1962-1966), Tooro Kingdom (1830-1876), Kingdom of Rwenzururu (1962-1982), and Republic of Martyazo (1972). Several, including Busoga, Bunyoro-Kitara, Buganda and Tooro, still exist as traditional kingdoms.
