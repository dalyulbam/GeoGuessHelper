# Orange pantile pyramidal resort roofs allowed on Fuerteventura, banned on Lanzarote

`atm_5c0a27638e98` · **architecture** / region · cell `esvzdqh`

Fuerteventura resorts freely use large orange pantile hipped and pyramidal roofs over whitewashed volumes. In Lanzarote the Manrique-era code yields strictly flat-roofed white cubes with green or blue woodwork and no tiled roofs at all. A skyline of orange tiles over white walls in a Canarian desert therefore reads Fuerteventura (or Gran Canaria south), not Lanzarote.
