# Script simplification and Latinization reforms

`atm_c2c5ced5d1a1` · **language** / global

Counter to diacritic-rich orthographies, several nations simplified or Latinized their scripts for practicality: Turkey under Atatürk replaced Arabic script with a Latin alphabet in 1928, Vietnam adopted the diacritic-heavy but Latin-based Quoc Ngu replacing Chinese characters, and Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan shifted from Cyrillic to Latin scripts after 1991. These reforms traded historical script continuity for perceived modernization and literacy gains.
