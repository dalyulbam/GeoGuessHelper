# Sheltered Dalmatian coast enabling dense resort development

`atm_2318f07c811c` · **nature** / region · cell `srey3dx`

In contrast to the wind-scoured, bare Velebit channel coast, more sheltered stretches of central and southern Dalmatia (e.g. around Split, Makarska Riviera, and the islands of Hvar and Korčula) enjoy milder, more stable maritime climates protected from the strongest bura by intervening mountains and island chains. This shelter allowed lush Mediterranean vegetation, olive and vine cultivation, and large-scale 20th-century tourist resort development that the exposed Velebit littoral never attracted.
