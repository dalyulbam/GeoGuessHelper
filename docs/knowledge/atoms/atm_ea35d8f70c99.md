# Ladino, Q'eqchi' Maya, and Garífuna cultural coexistence in eastern Guatemala

`atm_ea35d8f70c99` · **culture** / country · cell `d4bb4ty`

Eastern Guatemala's Caribbean lowlands (Izabal department) exhibit a distinct ethnic mosaic from the highland altiplano: Spanish-speaking Ladino (mestizo) culture predominates (~70%), coexisting with Q'eqchi' Maya indigenous communities (~28%) and smaller Garífuna Afro-indigenous coastal communities (~1%, concentrated in towns like Livingston). Unlike highland Maya areas, traditional indigenous dress and Mayan-language signage are largely absent from lowland commercial corridors, signaling the Ladino-dominant lowland cultural zone.
