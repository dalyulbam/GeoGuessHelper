# Global self-built incremental concrete housing belt

`atm_4629ce3c8114` · **architecture** / global

Progressive, self-built concrete-block housing with exposed rebar for future upper floors is a widespread pattern across Latin America, the Caribbean, and parts of Southeast Asia and Africa, seen in Mexican colonias populares, Brazilian favelas' 'auto-construção,' and Filipino informal settlements. The unfinished rebar and blockwork reflect incremental household investment tied to remittance income and lack of mortgage financing, rather than abandoned construction.
