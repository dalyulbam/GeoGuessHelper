# Eastern hardveld corridor of Botswana

`atm_4e202ceb9bb6` · **geography** / region · cell `keghd35`

The eastern hardveld corridor along the A1 trunk road and railway between Gaborone and Francistown is Botswana's main settlement and economic axis, hosting linear district towns (e.g. Mahalapye, Palapye, Serowe environs) with denser development than the deep Kalahari west (Ghanzi/Kgalagadi, sandy tracks, camelthorn scrub) or the northern wetland belt (Ngamiland/Chobe, mopane and floodplain vegetation).
