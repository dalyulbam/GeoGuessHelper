# Lusatia lignite district, Germany

`atm_4782de81e402` · **economy** / region · cell `u34jt3k`

The Lusatia (Lausitz) lignite district spans eastern Germany near Cottbus, historically mined by state combine and later LEUAG/LEAG, with post-reunification decline shrinking employment from tens of thousands to a few thousand. Many former pits have been flooded to create the Lausitzer Seenland, a chain of artificial lakes now marketed for tourism.
