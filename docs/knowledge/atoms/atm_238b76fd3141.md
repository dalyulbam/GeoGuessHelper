# Anti-overtourism regulation and short-term-rental caps

`atm_238b76fd3141` · **economy** / global

Cities facing residential hollowing from tourism have introduced countermeasures: Venice's day-tripper entry fee (2024), Barcelona's phase-out of tourist apartment licenses by 2028, Amsterdam's cap on new hotel beds and tourist numbers, and Dubrovnik's own 'Respect the City' program limiting cruise-ship dockings and Old Town foot traffic. These policies aim to preserve residential viability against Airbnb-driven flight of locals.
