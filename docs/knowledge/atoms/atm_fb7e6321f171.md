# Erosion and abolition of medieval town privileges

`atm_fb7e6321f171` · **history** / global

Local municipal law systems such as Magdeburg law and Žilina law were gradually eroded by centralizing monarchies and eventually abolished in the 18th-19th centuries as Habsburg and other absolutist states imposed uniform civil codes (e.g. the Josephine reforms, later the Austrian General Civil Code of 1811), replacing particularist town privileges with standardized state law.
