# Industrial dynasties of Upper Silesia

`atm_9715e7ff4e77` · **economy** / region · cell `u2vqvpq`

Upper Silesia's coal and zinc industry was built by magnate families such as the Prussian Henckel von Donnersmarck dynasty, who controlled vast mining and metallurgical concessions across Tarnowskie Góry, Siemianowice, and Świętochłowice from the 18th through early 20th centuries. Their industrial empire paralleled the role of the Krupp family in the Ruhr, both exemplifying aristocratic-industrial fusion in Central European heavy industry.
