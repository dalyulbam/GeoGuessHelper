# South Arabian Protectorate sheikhdoms and sultanates (18th century–1967)

`atm_a428599cb5b8` · **history** / region

A group of small sheikhdoms and sultanates in South Arabia (present-day Yemen), most existing from the 18th century until 1967 when the Federation of South Arabia dissolved. Members include the Dathina Sheikhdom, `Awdhali Sultanate, Bu`si Sheikdom, Hawshabi Sultanate of Musaymir, Lower Aulaqi Sultanate, Sheikhdom of Shaib, Upper Aulaqi Sheikhdom, Upper Aulaqi Sultanate, and Dhubi Sheikhdom. The Qutaibi Sheikhdom is also listed among these polities, though without given dates.
