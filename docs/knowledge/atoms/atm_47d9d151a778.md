# Norte Chico transverse valley oases of Chile

`atm_47d9d151a778` · **geography** / region · cell `66v8ckk`

Chile's Norte Chico (roughly Region III Atacama to Region IV Coquimbo) is a hyper-arid to semi-arid transition zone where transverse east-west valleys (Copiapó, Huasco, Elqui, Limarí) cut through otherwise barren mineral slopes, each forming a narrow irrigated oasis strip of agriculture (table grapes, pisco vineyards) fed by seasonal rivers/quebradas, while the surrounding hillsides remain essentially devoid of vegetation. Valley size and infrastructure density (paved roads, tourism signage, village density) increase from north to south, with Elqui and Limarí far more developed than smaller interior quebradas near Huasco/Copiapó.
