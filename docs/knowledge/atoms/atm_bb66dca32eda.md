# Legacy mining railway viaducts in Chile's Norte Chico

`atm_bb66dca32eda` · **history** / region · cell `66v8ckk`

Chile's 19th–20th century mining boom in the Atacama/Norte Chico left behind narrow-gauge and standard steel truss railway viaducts on concrete or masonry piers, built to move copper, silver and iron ore from interior mines to coastal ports. Many of these lines are now disused/derelict, with their rusting bridge structures surviving as landmarks over valleys that have since reverted to smallholder agriculture or been abandoned entirely.
