# Altai Krai as Russia's largest agricultural region

`atm_3ecf707f944b` · **economy** / region · cell `vc7x8jq`

Altai Krai is Russia's largest agricultural region, making food processing (grain, meat, dairy, canned goods) a key economic pillar. Regional food staples include dairy, butter, cheese, grain and honey. Barnaul holds about 32% of the krai's population and concentrates government, universities and industry that second-tier cities like Biysk and Rubtsovsk cannot match.
