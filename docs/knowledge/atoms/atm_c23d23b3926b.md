# Zug's post-1960s low-tax transformation into a corporate/crypto hub

`atm_c23d23b3926b` · **economy** / polity · cell `u0qh3ck`

**시기**: 1960 ~ ?

The Canton of Zug, Switzerland, was historically one of the poorest Swiss cantons, but 1960s tax reforms establishing very low corporate and personal tax rates (combined effective corporate tax ~11.7-11.9%) transformed it into a magnet for holding companies, commodity traders, and multinational headquarters. It now hosts over 30,000 registered companies for roughly 130,000 residents and has branded itself 'Crypto Valley' since the 2010s, attracting blockchain and fintech firms and even accepting Bitcoin for tax payments from 2016.
