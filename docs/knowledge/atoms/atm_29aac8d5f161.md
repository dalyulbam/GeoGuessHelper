# Morihiko Hiramatsu, originator of One Village One Product

`atm_29aac8d5f161` · **economy** / country · cell `wvvt4fv`

**시기**: 1979 ~ 2003

Morihiko Hiramatsu, governor of Oita Prefecture, Japan (1979-2003), created the One Village One Product movement to revitalize depopulating rural areas by having each village develop a distinctive specialty good. The model became a template widely copied across East and Southeast Asia, including Thailand's OTOP and the Philippines' OTOP scheme.
