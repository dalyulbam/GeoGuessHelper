# Jesuit mission and reduction architecture in the Americas

`atm_cacf275f7979` · **architecture** / global

The Jesuits built comparable complexes across colonial Latin America, most famously the Jesuit Missions of the Guaranís (Misiones region spanning Argentina, Brazil, and Paraguay), including San Ignacio Miní, and missions in Chiquitos and Moxos, Bolivia. These UNESCO-listed sites share the same combination of church, school, and residential quadrangle plan as Córdoba's Manzana Jesuítica, reflecting the Jesuits' standardized approach to evangelization and education infrastructure.
