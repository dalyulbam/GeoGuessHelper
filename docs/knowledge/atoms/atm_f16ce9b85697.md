# Mountainous subsistence terrain contrast

`atm_f16ce9b85697` · **geography** / region

In contrast to open grain-belt plains, mountainous zones like Bulgaria's Rhodope and Pirin ranges historically supported subsistence agriculture, transhumant sheep herding, and stone-built villages with slate roofs, due to steep slopes unsuitable for mechanized large-field cropping. This lowland-plain vs. highland-subsistence divide recurs across the Balkans and southern Europe.
