# Bulgarian dynasties (13th-20th c.)

`atm_3a3c127345e5` · **history** / country

Bulgaria's ruling houses include the Smilets dynasty (1292-1299), Borjigin clan (1299-1300), and House of Shishman (1323-1422) during the Second Bulgarian Empire. The Ottoman dynasty ruled Bulgaria under Ottoman rule (1396-1878). The Battenberg family ruled the Principality of Bulgaria (1878-1886), followed by the House of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha (1887-1946) over the Principality of Bulgaria and Kingdom of Bulgaria.
