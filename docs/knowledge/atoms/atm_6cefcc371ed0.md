# Religion-based regional discriminators in the Baltics and Central Europe

`atm_6cefcc371ed0` · **geography** / global

Confessional boundaries left by historical political divisions serve as reliable regional markers elsewhere in the Baltics and Central Europe: Lithuania and Poland are predominantly Catholic due to Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth heritage, while Estonia and northern Latvia are Lutheran due to Swedish and German influence. Similarly, in Germany, the historic Catholic-Protestant divide traces the Reformation-era territorial boundaries between Catholic Bavaria/Rhineland and Protestant northern/eastern states.
