# Bilingual Ukrainian/Russian signage pattern in central-eastern Ukraine

`atm_4878a96cdb97` · **culture** / region · cell `u8xsc9x`

In central and eastern Ukraine (Dnipro basin macro-region), commercial storefront signage is often in Russian while administrative, postal, and official map labeling is in Ukrainian, reflecting a historic diglossia. This contrasts with western Ukraine (Galicia/Lviv region), where commercial signage is overwhelmingly Ukrainian, and helps distinguish sub-regions within Ukraine.
