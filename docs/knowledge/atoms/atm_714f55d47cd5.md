# Garðabær affluent low-tax commuter suburb

`atm_714f55d47cd5` · **economy** / city · cell `ge2kts4`

Garðabær is an affluent municipality of roughly 18,000 on the Álftanes peninsula south of Reykjavík, ranking among Iceland's wealthiest and fastest-growing communities. Its service- and knowledge-based economy makes it a commuter town with few local jobs; it attracts high-income households via historically low municipal tax rates, spacious low-density plots and new neighbourhoods like Urriðaholt. In 2013 it merged with the neighbouring town of Álftanes.
