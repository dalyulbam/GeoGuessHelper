# Braided dry gravel torrent + rounded marl hills = Apennine plain margin, not Po plain

`atm_d06a11087118` · **nature** / region · cell `spznmb0`

At the Apennine foothill margin you see wide white-shingle braided riverbeds almost dry in late summer, framed by low rounded marl-clay hills with mown hay and mixed oak. In the Po plain proper you would instead see dead-flat terrain, poplar rows and rectilinear irrigation canals with no gravel torrent; in the Alps you would get rock walls, conifer belts and snow/avalanche hardware.
