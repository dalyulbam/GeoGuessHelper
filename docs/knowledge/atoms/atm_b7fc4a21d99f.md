# List of Dynasties - inclusion criteria and scope

`atm_b7fc4a21d99f` · **culture** / global

This Wikipedia list catalogs defunct and extant monarchical dynasties of sovereign and non-sovereign statuses at national and subnational levels, organized by geographic region. It includes dynastic regimes not explicitly styled as dynasties (e.g. Golden Horde, Qara Qoyunlu), matrilineal dynasties (e.g. Rain Queen/Modjadji dynasty), and de facto hereditary rule within elective monarchies (e.g. House of Habsburg's control of the Holy Roman Empire, 1440-1740). It also includes one-monarch regimes that would otherwise have been dynastic, such as the Thonburi kingdom before being usurped by the Chakri dynasty, and pretender/descendant lines such as the House of Plantagenet's claim to the Kingdom of Sicily (1254-1263) and the Osmanoğlu family descended from the Ottoman dynasty.
