# Highland Maya cultural dominance in Guatemala's altiplano

`atm_877f06d37696` · **culture** / region · cell `9fxhv7w`

In contrast to the Ladino-dominant Caribbean lowlands of Izabal, Guatemala's western highlands (departments like Sololá, Totonicapán, and Quiché) are strongholds of Maya identity where indigenous languages (K'iche', Kaqchikel, Mam) and traditional traje (woven clothing) remain dominant in daily public life and commercial signage, representing the opposite end of Guatemala's ethnic-geographic spectrum from the Ladino lowlands.
