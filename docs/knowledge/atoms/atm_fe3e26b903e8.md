# Ancient Greek dynasties (Athens, Sparta, Macedonia, Epirus, Pontus)

`atm_fe3e26b903e8` · **history** / region

Ancient Greek ruling houses include the Erechtheid (1556-1127 BC) and Melanthid (1126-1068 BC) dynasties of Athens; Agiad (930-215 BC) and Eurypontid (930-206 BC) dynasties of Sparta; Argead dynasty of Macedonia (700-305 BC); the Paeonia Kingdom (to 511 BC); Antigonid (306-168 BC) and Antipatrid (305-276 BC) dynasties of Macedonia; Mithridatic dynasty of Pontus (281-37 BC); and the Aeacid dynasty of Epirus (to 233 BC).
