# Sudanese dynasties and sultanates

`atm_bf8e8dbefc4f` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1100 ~ 1945

Sudan's historical ruling dynasties include the Daju dynasty (12th-15th century) of the Daju Kingdom, the Tunjur dynasty (15th century-1650) of the Tunjur Kingdom, the Keira dynasty (1603-1874) of the Sultanate of Darfur, the Muhammad Ali dynasty (1821-1885, also called the Alawiyya dynasty), and the House of al-Mahdi (1845-1945) founded by Muhammad Ahmad.
