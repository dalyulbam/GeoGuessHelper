# Munich (MVG) grass-track tram medians as a city discriminator

`atm_58f9b9fd8ed8` · **culture** / city · cell `u284p2g`

Munich's tram network (operated by MVG) is characterized by long continuous grass-turf track medians (Rasengleis) along boulevards such as Belgradstrasse and Barerstrasse, with twin-arm catenary masts and Y-shaped brackets. This contrasts with Nuremberg's paved-track VAG system and Augsburg's smaller network lacking such extended green reservations, making tram-track surfacing and mast design a useful discriminator among Bavarian cities.
