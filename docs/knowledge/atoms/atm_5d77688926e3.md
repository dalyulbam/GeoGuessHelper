# Italy's regional demographic aging, north-Italy extreme cases

`atm_5d77688926e3` · **history** / country · cell `u0nh6e5`

Northern Italian towns, even small tourism/commercial lake towns like Arona (Piedmont, ~13,600 residents), can exhibit some of the most extreme population aging in the country: 65+ share around 29%, under-18 share around 13%, negative natural population change offset only by immigration, and steady population decline since the 1970s. Foreign residents (often Albanian, Ukrainian, Romanian, Moroccan, Chinese communities) partially cushion the decline.
