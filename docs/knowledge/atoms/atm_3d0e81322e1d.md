# Global 19th-century terraced/rowhouse working-class housing family

`atm_3d0e81322e1d` · **architecture** / global

Victorian terraces belong to a wider family of dense 19th-century industrial-era rowhousing: Baltimore and Philadelphia brick rowhouses, Brooklyn brownstones, Amsterdam and Berlin Mietskaserne-adjacent terraces, and Dublin's Georgian/Victorian terraces. These typologies all arose to house rapidly growing industrial-era urban workforces at high density using repetitive party-wall construction.
