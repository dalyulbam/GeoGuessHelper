# Single-hotel island tourism economy pattern

`atm_4938ba62e38a` · **economy** / region · cell `srs76v3`

Small, low-population Croatian islands within protected natural areas can have their entire formal hospitality sector anchored by a single hotel (e.g. Hotel Odisej at Pomena, Mljet's only hotel and largest single employer), supplemented by private rental apartments, konoba taverns, and boat/kayak rental operators. This produces a heavily seasonal cash economy concentrated in July-August with a much quieter off-season.
