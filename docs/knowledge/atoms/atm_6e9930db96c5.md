# Ancient collegiate universities and 'town and gown' cities

`atm_6e9930db96c5` · **culture** / global

The collegiate university model with autonomous colleges, exemplified by Oxford and Cambridge in England, is paralleled by Trinity College Dublin in Ireland and, more loosely, by American collegiate systems like Yale's residential colleges and Harvard's houses. All produce a characteristic 'town and gown' urban dynamic where the transient student population significantly shapes local economy, real estate and identity.
