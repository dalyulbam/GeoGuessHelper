# Spain's state industrial-estate planning institutes

`atm_0e03ac7cf71d` · **economy** / country

Spain's postwar industrialization was driven by state bodies such as the Instituto Nacional de Industria (INI, founded 1941) and later regional development agencies, which planned polígonos industriales (industrial estates) around major cities during the 1960s-1970s developmentalist period, a policy pattern reflected in satellite industrial zones around Granada like those in Albolote and Atarfe.
