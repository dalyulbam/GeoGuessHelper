# Fukuyama's steel/manufacturing industrial cluster

`atm_966e456e1d2c` · **economy** / polity · cell `wynu5mx`

Fukuyama, Hiroshima Prefecture's second-largest city, is built around a major steel (JFE Steel West Japan Works), shipbuilding, and textile/denim manufacturing base, distinguishing its industrial economy from neighboring Onomichi (port and film tourism) and Kurashiki/Mizushima in Okayama Prefecture (petrochemical/auto complex). Dense small metal-working and parts-supply plants (e.g. spring manufacturers) cluster around the larger steelworks, typical of a Japanese provincial industrial city's supply chain.
