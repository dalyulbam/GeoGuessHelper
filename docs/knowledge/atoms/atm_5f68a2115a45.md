# Caldenal/Espinal ecoregion of central Argentina

`atm_5f68a2115a45` · **nature** / region · cell `6993z1r`

The Espinal or Caldenal is a semi-arid savanna belt spanning La Pampa, San Luis, and southern Córdoba provinces, dominated by scattered flat-crowned Prosopis caldenia (caldén) trees over dry tussock grass on sandy pale soils. It forms a transitional zone between the humid Pampas' cropland to the east and the treeless Patagonian steppe to the south, historically supporting extensive cattle ranching rather than row-crop agriculture.
