# Dubrovnik Old Town depopulation from short-term rental conversion and cruise overcrowding

`atm_5331d9309824` · **history** / city · cell `srsfeq5`

**시기**: 1991 ~ 2024

Dubrovnik's walled Old Town population collapsed from about 5,000 residents in 1991 to roughly 1,500 today as apartments were converted to short-term tourist rentals, driving up prices and eroding neighborhood identity. On a typical peak day up to eight cruise ships call, each unloading roughly 2,000 passengers, overwhelming a resident population of only about 2,500 within the walls — a stark case of tourism-driven historic-core hollowing-out.
