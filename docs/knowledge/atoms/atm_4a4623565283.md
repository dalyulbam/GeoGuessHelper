# Southern Gas Corridor and transnational pipeline networks

`atm_4a4623565283` · **economy** / global

The Southern Gas Corridor is a chain of pipelines—South Caucasus Pipeline (Azerbaijan-Georgia), Trans-Anatolian Pipeline (TANAP) across Turkey, and Trans Adriatic Pipeline (TAP)—moving Caspian gas from the Shah Deniz field in Azerbaijan to Italy and Europe. It parallels other major transnational corridors like Nord Stream (Russia-Germany under the Baltic Sea) and the Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan oil pipeline, all designed to diversify Europe's energy supply routes.
