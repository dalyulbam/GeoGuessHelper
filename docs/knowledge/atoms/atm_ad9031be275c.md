# Deindustrialization and Rust Belt decline

`atm_ad9031be275c` · **economy** / global

The counter-force to auto-manufacturing boom cities is deindustrialization: cities like Detroit and parts of the US and UK Rust Belt suffered severe population loss, plant closures and economic decay from the 1970s-80s onward as automakers offshored production to lower-cost regions such as Mexico, China, and Eastern Europe. This pattern illustrates the structural risk single-industry manufacturing cities like Córdoba face when national or global economic conditions shift.
