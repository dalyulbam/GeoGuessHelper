# National road-signage families as country identifiers

`atm_811342082c50` · **architecture** / global

Just as Italy's SS-prefixed shields and O-24 parking sub-plates distinguish it from neighbors, other countries have signature signage systems: Germany's Autobahn blue shields with 'A' numbers and Bundesstraße yellow 'B' signs, the UK's green/blue motorway and A-road signage using the Transport typeface designed by Margaret Calvert and Jock Kinneir, and Japan's blue expressway signs with route numbers and hiragana place names. These conventions let travelers identify a country from road furniture alone.
