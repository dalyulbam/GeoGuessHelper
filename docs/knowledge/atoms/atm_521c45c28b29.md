# Cordillera western flank vs. Cordillera highland interior (Luzon)

`atm_521c45c28b29` · **geography** / region · cell `wej9x8g`

The western foothill flank of the Luzon Cordillera (e.g. Ilocos Sur's eastern uplands) shows sub-500m broadleaf/bamboo vegetation, lowland-style hollow-block housing, coconut/banana, and irrigated paddy on river terraces — distinct from the true Cordillera highland interior (Bontoc/Ifugao/Kalinga sphere) above ~800m, which features pine belts, stone-walled rice terraces, and clustered hillside villages with galvanized roofing. Rivers on this flank are typically wide, braided, boulder/gravel-bedded, cutting through steep forested mountains into a narrow coastal plain, reflecting rain-shadow seasonality of western Luzon.
