# Vietnam's northern midlands (trung du) hill-fringe landscape

`atm_3e9eb9eb04a3` · **geography** / region · cell `w7g6609`

The trung du zone between the Red River Delta and the northern highlands features low forested hills interspersed with small bunded paddy pockets in hollows, distinct from the flat, canal-laced Mekong Delta and the coconut/coastal North Central coast. Hamlets here use the term 'xóm' (as opposed to 'ấp' in the south) and are administratively grouped under 'xã' (commune), often with a 'Nhà văn hóa xóm' (hamlet cultural house) as the local civic hub. This terrain supports mixed farming: wet rice in the lowlands plus orchards and tea on the hill slopes.
