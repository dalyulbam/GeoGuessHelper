# Mustafa Kemal Atatürk and Turkish secular nation-building

`atm_4a66ab569c4f` · **history** / country · cell `sxp75tp`

**시기**: 1923 ~ 1938

Mustafa Kemal Atatürk (1881-1938) led the founding of the Republic of Turkey in 1923, abolishing the Ottoman sultanate and caliphate, and implementing sweeping secularization reforms including the Latin alphabet, civil code, and dress reform. His name and image dominate Turkish public toponymy (Atatürk Bulvarı, Atatürk Airport) as the central symbol of Republican identity.
