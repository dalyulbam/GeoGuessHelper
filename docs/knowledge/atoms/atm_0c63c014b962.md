# Closed shade canopy + hills right behind town = northern Meta piedmont, not Casanare plains

`atm_0c63c014b962` · **nature** / region · cell `d2g8r9x`

In the northern Meta piedmont around Villavicencio you see a closed evergreen shade canopy over the streets and forested foothills rising immediately behind the last block, with lush areca/mango planting. In Casanare (Yopal, Aguazul) you would instead see open savanna horizons, sparser tree cover, drier grass and oil-service pickups, with the cordillera far to the west. Canopy closure plus a hill wall in the same frame is the piedmont-proper signature.
