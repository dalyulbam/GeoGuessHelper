# Pharmacy emblem separates Germany from Austria and Switzerland

`atm_96d1691571d2` · **culture** / country · cell `u284p2k`

In Germany a pharmacy shows a red 'APOTHEKE' sign with the stylized red 'A' containing a vessel and snake. In Austria you would instead see a green-and-white cross with serpent-and-bowl and often 'Apotheke zum …' naming; in Switzerland a plain green cross with 'Apotheke/Pharmacie'. Treat the red A as near-conclusive for Germany.
