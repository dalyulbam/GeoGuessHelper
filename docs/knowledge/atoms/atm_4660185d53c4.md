# Global tropical dry deciduous / savanna biome family

`atm_4660185d53c4` · **nature** / global

The Deccan plateau's tropical dry deciduous, monsoon-driven biome parallels other seasonally dry tropical woodlands worldwide: the African miombo woodlands, Brazil's Cerrado savanna, and Australia's tropical savanna woodlands. These biomes share a wet-dry seasonal rainfall regime, deciduous or semi-deciduous tree canopy, and fire-adapted grass understories.
