# New Zealand endemic/naturalised vegetation markers: tree ferns and toetoe

`atm_305c0c3707b0` · **nature** / country · cell `rbv6fn6`

Tree ferns (e.g. Cyathea, Dicksonia) and toetoe (native Austroderia grasses, often confused with introduced pampas) are characteristic of New Zealand's warm-temperate broadleaf bush understorey, frequently intermixed with exotic Pinus radiata shelterbelts and poplar erosion plantings on farmland. This vegetation mosaic is a strong discriminator against eucalypt-dominated Australian bush or Northern Hemisphere temperate flora.
