# Post-2014 Ukrainian-language mandates and de-russification

`atm_0cb98245d4ef` · **language** / country · cell `ub8gtqx`

Following the 2014 Euromaidan and Russian annexation of Crimea, Ukraine passed laws (2019 State Language Law) mandating Ukrainian in services, media quotas, and signage, accelerating a shift away from the Russian-dominant commercial signage historically seen in Dnipro-basin cities like Kharkiv and Dnipro. This represents a state-driven reversal of the diglossia pattern rather than an organic one.
