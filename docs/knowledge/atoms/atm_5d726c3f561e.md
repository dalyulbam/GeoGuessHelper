# Ustyurt Plateau / Caspian depression desert margin (western Kazakhstan)

`atm_5d726c3f561e` · **geography** / region · cell `v0nxmzr`

The Ustyurt Plateau and adjoining Caspian depression in western Kazakhstan (Mangystau and Atyrau regions) form a dead-flat, saline clay-sand desert with saxaul and wormwood shrub cover, reddish-tan soils, and no cultivation or tree cover. This landscape contrasts with the cereal/birch forest-steppe of northern Kazakhstan, the Tien Shan foothill orchards of the southeast, and the rolling granite-outcrop steppe of central Kazakhstan (Karaganda area), making terrain flatness and vegetation type a key regional discriminator within the country.
