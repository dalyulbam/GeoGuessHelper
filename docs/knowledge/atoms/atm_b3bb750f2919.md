# Tang dynasty and Wu Zhou interregnum (618-907)

`atm_b3bb750f2919` · **history** / polity

The Tang dynasty (618-690, 705-907) was ruled by the House of Li of Han descent. It was interrupted by the Wu Zhou dynasty (690-705) under Empress Wu of the House of Wu, Han descent, which then reverted to Tang rule.
