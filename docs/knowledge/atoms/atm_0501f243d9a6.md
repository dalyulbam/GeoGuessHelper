# Dynasties of Anatolia, antiquity to Ottoman rise

`atm_0501f243d9a6` · **history** / region

Anatolia was ruled successively by the Hittite Empire (c.1600-1178 BC), Heraclid dynasty of Lydia (12th c.-687 BC), Adaside dynasty (911-745 BC, Neo-Assyrian Empire), House of Suhi and House of Astiruwa (Kingdom of Carchemish), Sargonid and Pre-Sargonid dynasties (Neo-Assyrian Empire), Mermnad dynasty of Lydia (680-546 BC), Median dynasty (678-549 BC), Achaemenid dynasty (550-330 BC) with the Mithridatic dynasty of Pontus (281 BC-AD 62), Pharnacid dynasty (480-320 BC), Hecatomnids (395-334 BC), Argead, Antigonid and Antipatrid dynasties under Macedonian rule, Ariarathid dynasty of Cappadocia (331-96 BC), Attalid dynasty of Pergamon (282-129 BC), Arsacid (Parthian) rule (247 BC-AD 224), Seleucid dynasty (200-188 BC), House of Ariobarzanes and House of Archelaus (Cappadocia), House of Odaenathus (Palmyrene Empire, AD 270-273), and the Byzantine imperial dynasties in Anatolia: Constantinian, Valentinianic, Theodosian, Leonid, Justinian, Heraclian, Isaurian, Nikephorian, Amorian, Macedonian, Doukid, Komnenos, Angelos, Laskarid (Empire of Nicaea), Palaiologos dynasties (AD 330-1453). Other polities include Bohtan (8th c.-1847) and the Kaysite (860-964) and Sajid (889-929) dynasties.
