# Shinshu Otani-ha (Jodo Shinshu) temple density in Hokuriku

`atm_65d849f7eb83` · **culture** / region · cell `xn9k2te`

The Hokuriku region (Ishikawa, Toyama, Fukui) has an unusually dense concentration of Shinshu Otani-ha (a Jodo Shinshu Buddhist sect) temples, alongside small Hakusan and Hachiman shrines, reflecting the historical strength of Jodo Shinshu ('Ikko-ikki') communities in this area since the medieval period. A high local density of Otani-ha temples combined with Hakusan-dedicated shrines is a reliable marker for narrowing a Japanese rural scene to the Hokuriku macro-region.
