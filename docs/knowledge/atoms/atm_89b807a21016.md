# Redbrick and modern universities as reaction to Oxbridge exclusivity

`atm_89b807a21016` · **culture** / country

England's 'redbrick' universities (Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool, founded late 19th-early 20th century) arose in industrial cities to provide practical, science-oriented, non-collegiate higher education outside the ancient Oxford-Cambridge model, which historically excluded non-Anglicans and women. This counter-movement expanded access and shifted academic prestige toward research-driven, urban, secular institutions.
