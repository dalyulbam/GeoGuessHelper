# Bajío basin field-clearance and soil signature

`atm_2fbd4c769a30` · **geography** / region · cell `9exuvnc`

The Bajío basin (Guanajuato/Querétaro, northern edge of the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt) is marked by dark vesicular volcanic boulders piled at field edges as clearance debris, deep brown-black fertile soils, and a dense mosaic of small irrigated plots, punctuated by low mesa-like cerros. This differs from the pale calcareous soils and Yucca/nopal cover of the Chihuahuan Altiplano and from the maguey-pulquero andosol landscapes further east in the volcanic belt.
