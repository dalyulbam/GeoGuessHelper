# National highway systems resisting regional standardization

`atm_bd2899174a72` · **geography** / region

Mexico's federal highway system (Carreteras Federales, prefixed 'MEX') and the US Interstate Highway System retain fully independent numbering and signage distinct from any shared continental scheme, unlike the CA-route or E-road overlays. This reflects how larger, older, or more sovereignty-conscious highway administrations resist supranational renumbering even while participating in broader corridors like the Pan-American Highway.
