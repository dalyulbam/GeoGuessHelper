# Medieval Greek despotates and Aegean lordships

`atm_add39bea674c` · **history** / region

Medieval Greek territories were ruled by the Emirate of Crete (824-961); in the Despotate of Epirus by the Komnenos Doukas dynasty (1205-1318), Orsini dynasty (1318-1337, 1356-1359), Nemanjić dynasty (1347-1385), Buondelmonti dynasty (1385-1411), and Tocco dynasty (1411-1479); in the Despotate of Arta by the Losha family (1359-1374) and Spata family (1374-1416); the Zaccaria dynasty ruled the Lordship of Chios and Principality of Achaea (1304-1329, 1402-1455); the Giustiniani dynasty ruled the Maona of Chios and Phocaea (1346-1566); and the Crispo dynasty ruled the Duchy of the Archipelago (1383-1566).
