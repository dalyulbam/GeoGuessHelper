# Mongol Empire and successor khanates (1206-1705)

`atm_756611a99fa8` · **history** / polity

**시기**: 1206 ~ 1923

The Mongol Empire (AD 1206-1368) was ruled by the House of Borjigin of Mongol descent. It included the Ögedei Khanate (1225-1309) and Chagatai Khanate (1225-1346), which split into the Western Chagatai Khanate (1346-1402) and Eastern Chagatai Khanate/Moghulistan (1347-1680), the latter giving rise to Beshbalik (1389-1418), Ili Baliq (1418-1508), Turpan Khanate (1487-1660) and Yarkent Khanate (1514-1705). The Yuan dynasty (1271-1368) was another Mongol Borjigin state, succeeded by the Northern Yuan (1368-1635), including the Tatar state (1388-1635) which fragmented into the Tüsheet Khanate, Setsen Khanate, and Jasaghtu Khanate (all 1691-1923), the Altan Khanate (1609-1691), and the Sain Noyan Khanate (1731-1923).
