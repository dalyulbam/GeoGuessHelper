# Heavy-snow rice-farming coastal plains of the Sea of Japan side (Ura-Nihon)

`atm_c113219fd136` · **geography** / global

The Sea of Japan coastal lowlands of Honshu, including Toyama, Niigata's Echigo Plain, and Ishikawa's Kaga Plain, share a climate pattern known as 'Ura-Nihon' (back Japan): heavy winter snowfall from Siberian monsoon winds crossing the sea, combined with flat alluvial plains intensively used for irrigated rice cultivation, historically making Niigata Japan's top rice-producing prefecture.
