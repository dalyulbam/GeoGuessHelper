# Gulf inland oasis town vs coastal emirate strip

`atm_d0b59aaa3c23` · **geography** / region · cell `thrxzsf`

In an inland UAE oasis town like Al Dhaid you see single-storey rendered shop terraces, animal-feed and contractor trades, date-palm plantations and dead-flat horizons with open sand plots between blocks. On the coastal Sharjah/Dubai strip you would instead see continuous mid- and high-rise apartment blocks, Washingtonia and salt-tolerant ornamental planting, and no farm-supply retail. Absence of the Hajar mountain wall on any horizon also excludes the east-coast towns.
