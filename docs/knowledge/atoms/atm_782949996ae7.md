# Overtourism convergence versus niche diversification

`atm_782949996ae7` · **economy** / global

Counter to Pag's niche-diversification strategy, many tourism destinations suffer from overtourism convergence, where most settlements chase the same mass-tourism product (beach and nightlife), leading to overcrowding, infrastructure strain and backlash, as documented in Spain's Costa del Sol and Croatia's own Dubrovnik old town, which prompted visitor caps and cruise-ship restrictions.
