# Socialist-era prefab housing estate family

`atm_1a59425e959b` · **architecture** / global

Across the former socialist bloc, mass-produced concrete apartment blocks were built to house urban and industrial workers: Khrushchyovka and Brezhnevka estates in the USSR, Plattenbau in East Germany, Yugoslav blokovi in cities like Novi Beograd and Split, and panelák estates in Czechoslovakia. These developments typically ring older historic cores and reflect standardized, centrally planned construction methods from the 1960s–1980s.
