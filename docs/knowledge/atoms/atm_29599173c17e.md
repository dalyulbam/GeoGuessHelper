# Ryukyu Islands dynasties

`atm_29599173c17e` · **history** / region

Ryukyu history includes the legendary Tenson dynasty (?-1185), Shunten dynasty (1187-1259), Eiso dynasty (1260-1349), the Sanzan period (1314-1429) with Haniji dynasty of Hokuzan, Satto dynasty of Chūzan, and Ōzato dynasty of Nanzan (all 1314-1419/1429), First Shō dynasty (1406-1469), Second Shō dynasty (1469-1879), and the Imperial House of Japan ruling the islands (1879-1945, 1972-present).
