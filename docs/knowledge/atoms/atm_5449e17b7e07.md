# West Siberian humid continental steppe-forest climate

Southwestern Siberia around the Altai region has a sharply continental humid climate with cold winters, warm summers and large temperature swings driven by Arctic and Central Asian air masses. Vegetation includes birch and poplar in a steppe-forest transition zone. Barnaul is unusually sunny for Siberia, with around 230 sunny days a year.
