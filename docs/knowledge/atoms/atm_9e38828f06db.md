# Kysuce region valley settlement pattern (Western Carpathians)

`atm_9e38828f06db` · **geography** / region · cell `u2v27rh`

The Kysuce region of northwestern Slovakia (Žilina kraj) sits between the Javorníky mountains and Kysucká vrchovina highlands, along the Kysuca river. Settlements are linear, following narrow river valleys, surrounded by spruce-dominated forested ridges often shrouded in low cloud/fog even in summer — a Western Carpathian/Beskid microclimate and terrain signature distinct from Slovakia's flatter Danubian lowlands (Bratislava/Nitra) or the gentler hills of eastern Slovakia.
