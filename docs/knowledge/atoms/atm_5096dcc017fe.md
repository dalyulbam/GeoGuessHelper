# Ruling houses of Albania (12th-20th c.)

`atm_5096dcc017fe` · **history** / region

Albanian principalities were ruled successively by the Progoni family (1190-1216, Principality of Arbanon), Sratsimir dynasty (1346-1372, Principality of Valona), Balšić noble family (1356-1421, Zeta, Valona, Principality of Albania), Thopia family (1358-1392, Principality of Albania), Zenevisi family (1386-1418, Principality of Gjirokastër), House of Kastrioti (1389-1444, Principality of Kastrioti), Muzaka family (1396-1417, Lordship of Berat), House of Wied-Neuwied (1914-1925, Principality of Albania), House of Zogu (1928-1939), and House of Savoy (1939-1943).
