# Barbarian successor-state dynasties of early medieval Europe

`atm_f2dfa98dfc1d` · **history** / region

Germanic and steppe peoples had ruling dynasties including the Agilolfing dynasty of the Bavarii; the House of the Kings of the Burgundians (4th century-534, called Gjúkungar in the Nibelung legend); the Frankish Merovingian dynasty (481-751) succeeded by the Carolingian dynasty (751-843), with the earlier Arnulfings/Pippinids as mayors of the palace ancestral to the Carolingians; the Hunnic Attilid dynasty and the House of Dulo of Bulgaria (390-503, claimed descent from Attila the Hun); the Lombard Lething dynasty (until early 6th century), Gausian dynasty (546-572), Arodingian dynasty (635-653), and Bavarian dynasty (615-635, 653-712); the Ostrogothic Amal dynasty (before 474-536); the Suebic dynasty of the Kingdom of the Suebi (409-585); the Vandal Hasdingi (before 407-534); and the Visigothic Balt dynasty (395-531).
