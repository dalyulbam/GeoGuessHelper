# Administrative-district vs. daily-life catchment mismatch on Malaysia's east coast

`atm_371fbba51c79` · **geography** / region · cell `w29x2w0`

Villages on Peninsular Malaysia's east coast can administratively belong to one district (e.g. Kuantan) while their actual daily commerce, transport, and social life gravitate toward a closer neighboring town across a state border (e.g. Bandar Chukai in Kemaman, Terengganu), due to travel-time asymmetries along the Federal Route 3 corridor. This pattern of administrative boundary vs. functional catchment divergence is common in linear coastal settlement corridors.
