# Tradesman van toponym as district anchor in Czechia

`atm_a4c8bfdf483f` · **economy** / region · cell `u2f2t6k`

In Czech villages small joinery/roofing firms paint their base village name plus a .cz mail address on the van, and such vans usually park at their own yard. Use that name to pick the district rather than the country; where a name is duplicated (e.g. a Bohemian and a Moravian homonym), decide with the terrain — rolling plateau with conifer woodlots for Bohemia instead of a dead-flat black-soil plain for Haná.
