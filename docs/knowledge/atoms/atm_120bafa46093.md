# Katabatic wind family: bora, mistral, foehn, santa ana

`atm_120bafa46093` · **nature** / global

The bura belongs to a global family of katabatic and downslope winds shaped by mountains channeling cold air onto coasts or plains. The Mistral funnels cold air down the Rhône valley to the French Mediterranean coast near the Camargue and Marseille; the Santa Ana winds descend from the Great Basin onto coastal Southern California, driving wildfire risk; the Foehn/Chinook produces warm, dry downslope winds on the lee side of the Alps and Rocky Mountains respectively. Each has shaped local vegetation, architecture (windbreaks, low rooflines) and agriculture.
