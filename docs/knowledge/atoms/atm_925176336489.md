# Non-volcanic Icelandic terrain contrasts with Snæfellsnes's volcanic zone

`atm_925176336489` · **nature** / region

Unlike the young volcanic and geologically active Snæfellsnes peninsula, the Westfjords (Vestfirðir) of Iceland are built of older, deeply eroded basalt plateau lava dating to over 10 million years, entirely outside Iceland's active volcanic zones, producing dramatically different steep fjord-cut topography without cinder cones or recent lava fields.
