# Žilina town law (žilinské právo) in medieval Slovakia

`atm_1b9b30221a10` · **history** / polity · cell `u2v27rh`

Žilina law (žilinské právo) was a medieval municipal legal code granted to towns in the Kysuce/Žilina region of the Kingdom of Hungary, conferring rights such as a hereditary judge and market privileges. Kysucké Nové Mesto received this town law in 1325, illustrating the spread of localized urban legal charters (analogous to Magdeburg law elsewhere in Central Europe) among northwestern Slovak market towns.
