# Sui dynasty and contemporary short-lived Chinese states (581-630)

`atm_9c5236b24b09` · **history** / polity

The Sui dynasty (581-619), ruled by the House of Yang of Han descent, unified China. Rival/successor claimants amid its collapse included Lin Chu (616-622, House of Lin), Xia (617-621, House of Dou), Later Western Liang (617-621, House of Xiao), Liang (617-628, House of Liang), Liang/Liang-Han rival state (618-619, House of Li), Xu (618-619, House of Yuwen, Xiongnu descent), Zheng (619-621, House of Wang), Wu (619-621, House of Li), and Later Sui (620-630, House of Yang). The Chiefdom of Sizhou (582-1413, House of Tian) and its offshoot Chiefdom of Sinan (1364-1414) were native chieftaincies.
