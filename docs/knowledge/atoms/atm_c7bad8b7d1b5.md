# Danish dynasties

`atm_c7bad8b7d1b5` · **history** / country

Denmark's ruling houses include the legendary Scylding and semi-legendary House of Olaf, House of Knýtlinga (916-1042, also House of Denmark/Jelling dynasty) with House of Estridsen branch (1047-1332, 1340-1375, 1376-1412), Fairhair dynasty (1042-1047), House of Bjälbo (1376-1387), House of Griffin (1396-1439), House of Palatinate-Neumarkt (1439/1440-1448), House of Oldenburg (1448-1533, 1534-1863), and House of Schleswig-Holstein-Sonderburg-Glücksburg (1863-present).
