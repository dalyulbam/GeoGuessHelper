# Low-tax affluent commuter suburbs worldwide

`atm_3bb44773624a` · **economy** / global

Wealthy municipalities near major cities that use low local tax rates and spacious zoning to attract high-income commuter households are a global pattern: examples include Westchester County towns near New York, Zug and Wollerau in Switzerland (low cantonal tax), and Leiden-area suburbs in the Netherlands. These places typically lack a strong local job base and function as bedroom communities dependent on the nearby metropolis.
