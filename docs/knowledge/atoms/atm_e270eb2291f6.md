# Spanish island/province road shield prefixes

`atm_e270eb2291f6` · **geography** / country · cell `esvzdq6`

In Spain, regional and insular road route shields carry province/island-specific letter prefixes: FV- for Fuerteventura, GC- for Gran Canaria, TF- for Tenerife, LZ- for Lanzarote. These prefixes function as reliable geolocation fingerprints distinguishing individual Canary Islands from road signage alone, analogous to other national numbering-plan or shield conventions.
