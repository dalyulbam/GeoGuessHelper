# Soviet partisan movement and post-war honorific town names

`atm_c61a7886e287` · **history** / period

In response to Nazi occupation, Soviet partisan detachments and underground cells conducted sabotage, intelligence-gathering, and assassinations behind German lines across occupied Ukraine and Belarus. After liberation, the Red Army and Soviet state commemorated liberating units and partisan formations through honorific unit names referencing captured cities (e.g. 'Kyivska', 'Zhytomyrska' divisions), a practice paralleling battle-honor traditions in other national militaries.
