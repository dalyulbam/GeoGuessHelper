# Valley canopy vs treeless altiplano in Bolivia

`atm_f6ff80704d60` · **nature** / region · cell `6s9edy7`

Sucre-type inter-Andean valley streets show mature jacaranda/molle shade trees and grassy strip parks, dry-brown in winter but with real canopy. On the altiplano (El Alto, Oruro, Potosí) you would instead see no broadleaf canopy, only eucalyptus rows or bare ground, plus colder-weather dress and paler flat horizons.
