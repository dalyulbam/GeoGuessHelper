# Devanagari standardization and Hindi language policy architects

The Constituent Assembly of India, guided by figures like Beohar Rajendra Simha and the linguist Suniti Kumar Chatterjee, debated and adopted Devanagari as the official script for Hindi in 1950 under Article 343 of the Indian Constitution. The Central Hindi Directorate, established in 1960, has since promoted standardized Devanagari usage across the Hindi Belt.
