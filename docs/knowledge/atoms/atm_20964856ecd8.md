# Lowland Scottish housing vernacular vs England/Wales

`atm_20964856ecd8` · **architecture** / region · cell `gcvy4mw`

Lowland Scottish (Central Belt) housing is distinguished from English and Welsh equivalents by buff/cream sandstone-coloured facing brick combined with wet-dash (harled) render gable panels, decorative scalloped bargeboards, and pantile-roofed single-storey stone cottages with harled chimney stacks. English estates of the same era favour red multi-stock brick with shallow eaves and no wet-dash render, while Welsh villages show dominant slate roofing on older terraces.
