# Local/independent retail resisting chain homogenization

`atm_a9d14aedd052` · **economy** / global

Against the spread of national supermarket chains, movements promoting local markets, farmers' markets, and independent kiosks persist as counterweights, such as Norway's Nærbutikk (local shop) support schemes in rural areas where chain stores are unprofitable. Governments in Norway and other Nordic countries subsidize small rural stores (Merkur-programmet) to prevent grocery deserts.
