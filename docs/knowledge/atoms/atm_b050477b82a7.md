# Slavic settlement disruption and abandonment of Roman coastal sites

`atm_b050477b82a7` · **history** / region · cell `srs7nnr`

Many Roman coastal palaces and villas along the Dalmatian coast, including sites near Mljet, were abandoned or fell into ruin following Avar and Slavic incursions in the 7th century, causing a settlement discontinuity before medieval Ragusan and Venetian-era communities re-established coastal life. This contrasts with sites like Split, where the Roman palace was continuously repurposed rather than abandoned.
