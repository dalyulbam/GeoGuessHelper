# French pavillon suburban housing typology (1970s-80s)

`atm_533e2635a15a` · **culture** / country · cell `u0g1ehc`

**시기**: 1970 ~ 1989

French provincial towns are commonly ringed by lotissements of detached single-family 'pavillons' built in the 1970s-80s: beige-rendered facades, roller-shutter (volet roulant) window casings, pierced-concrete claustra screen walls for boundary privacy, and clipped hedges. This owner-occupied suburban housing stock is a widespread marker of small and mid-sized French towns' peripheries.
