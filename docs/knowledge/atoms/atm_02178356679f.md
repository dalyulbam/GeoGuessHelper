# Overtourism backlash and Dubrovnik's visitor caps

`atm_02178356679f` · **economy** / city · cell `srsfs49`

Dubrovnik's old town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1979, faced severe overtourism from cruise ship crowds and Game of Thrones-driven visitor surges, prompting the city in 2017 to introduce daily visitor caps and cruise ship berth limits under mayor Mato Franković. This counter-movement toward tourism management mirrors restrictions adopted in Venice and Barcelona against mass-tourism saturation.
