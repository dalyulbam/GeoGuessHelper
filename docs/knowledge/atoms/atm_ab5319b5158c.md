# Dalmatian vernacular: kupa kanalica tiles and dry-stone terracing

`atm_ab5319b5158c` · **architecture** / region · cell `srsfctz`

Traditional Dalmatian coastal architecture uses dressed or rendered limestone walls with shallow-pitched roofs covered in 'kupa kanalica' terracotta roof tiles, combined with dry-stone (suhozid) retaining walls that terrace hillside gardens and olive/vine plots on thin karst soils. This vernacular is a reliable visual marker distinguishing the eastern Adriatic coast from other Mediterranean shores.
