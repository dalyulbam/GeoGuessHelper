# Food-machinery business fingerposts flag the Parma valley corridors

`atm_b263ea2c5db6` · **economy** / region · cell `spznmb0`

Clusters of roadside business fingerposts advertising food-industry machinery, forklifts and tyre services in a small valley industrial estate are typical of Parma's 'food valley' spilling up the Taro/Cisa corridor. In Piacenza's Trebbia or Reggio's Enza valleys you would instead find more general mechanical/agricultural-machinery and ceramics-related firms without the food-processing machinery emphasis.
