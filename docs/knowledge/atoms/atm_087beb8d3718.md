# Count István Széchenyi and Budapest's unification

`atm_087beb8d3718` · **history** / city · cell `u2mw1q8`

Hungarian reformer Count István Széchenyi championed the Chain Bridge (Széchenyi Lánchíd), completed in 1849, the first permanent bridge linking Buda and Pest, paving the way for their administrative unification with Óbuda into Budapest in 1873. He is widely credited as a driving force of Hungary's 19th-century modernization.
