# Post-2000 tourism investment layering on traditional Dalmatian villages

`atm_0625509c4e13` · **economy** / region · cell `srsfctz`

Small Dalmatian coastal villages near major tourist hubs (e.g. Dubrovnik) show a visible split between older stone houses and dry-stone agrarian terraces (pre-tourism Ragusan/Dalmatian pattern) and new white-rendered villas with concrete retaining walls and manicured hedges built for the post-2000 rental-apartment tourism boom. Household economies blend small-scale olive/vine agriculture with private accommodation, agritourism, and seasonal service work rather than wage employment in a single large firm.
