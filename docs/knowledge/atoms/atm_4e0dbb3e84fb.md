# Classic Maya collapse and abandonment of monumental centers

`atm_4e0dbb3e84fb` · **history** / period

**시기**: 800 ~ 900

Between roughly 800-900 CE, most Classic Maya lowland cities including Quiriguá ceased erecting dated monuments and were largely abandoned, a phenomenon known as the Classic Maya collapse, attributed to combined drought, warfare, and political fragmentation. This ended the tradition of monumental stelae-carving that produced works like Stela E.
