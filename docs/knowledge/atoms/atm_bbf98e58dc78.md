# Pre-Columbian and Colonial American periods

`atm_bbf98e58dc78` · **history** / region

Pre-Columbian America includes the Classic and Postclassic eras of Central America (200-1519) and Peru's Early Intermediate, Middle Horizon, Late Intermediate, and Late Horizon periods (200-1534), encompassing the Huari, Chimú, Chincha, Chanka, Tiwanaku, and Inca cultures. Colonial America includes the Baroque period in the New World (1600-1750) and Spanish hegemony over the Americas (1492-1832).
