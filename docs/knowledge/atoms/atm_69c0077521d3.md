# Noto Peninsula upland plateau vs Toyama/Kaga plains

`atm_69c0077521d3` · **geography** / region · cell `xn9k2te`

The Noto Peninsula (Ishikawa Prefecture) is characterized by a low rolling forested plateau (roughly 200-300m) with ridge-top wind turbines and limited paddy plains, in contrast to the wide alluvial rice plains of neighboring Toyama (Tonami/Takaoka, backed by the Tateyama mountain wall) and the Hakusan-massif-fronted plains of Fukui/Kaga. This landform contrast is a useful discriminator among Hokuriku prefectures.
