# Po Plain vernacular: coppi barrel-tile roofs over lime-rendered brick

`atm_d3afa16fc410` · **architecture** / region · cell `spyrs4d`

Across the Po Plain and its southern fringes (Lombardy, Emilia, Piedmont), traditional farmhouse (cascina) roofs use shallow-pitch curved terracotta barrel tiles (coppi) over lime-rendered brick walls. This contrasts with steeper-pitched, flat-tile or corrugated roofing typical of Central European plains, and with deep-eave snow roofs of Alpine valleys. Wisteria-draped courtyards and unrendered brick with rendered accents are common accompanying cues.
