# Century of Humiliation rebel states and Muslim sultanates

`atm_ca30111f504a` · **history** / region

**시기**: 1851 ~ 1877

During China's Century of Humiliation, several rebel states and separatist sultanates arose: Taiping Heavenly Kingdom (1851-1864, House of Hong, Han); Chiefdom of Nixi (to 1853, House of Wang); Cheng (1855-1864, House of Chen, Han); Pingnan Sultanate (1856-1873, House of Du, Hui) in Yunnan; Yanling (1861-1868, House of Wu, Zhuang); Ili Sultanate (1864-1871); Yettishar Khanate (1865-1867) succeeded by Hongfu Khanate (1867-1877); Chiefdom of Muchuan (to 1868, House of Yue); Chiefdom of Nama (to 1870, House of Huang).
