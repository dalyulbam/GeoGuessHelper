# Malaysian Federal Route 3 as the Peninsular East Coast trunk road

`atm_25d589535f80` · **geography** / region · cell `w29x2w0`

Federal Route 3, marked with yellow hexagonal shields, is the main highway running along the east coast of Peninsular Malaysia through Pahang, Terengganu, and Kelantan. Towns strung along this corridor typically show resort/homestay clusters and sandy beach coastlines, contrasting with the west coast's North-South Expressway/Route 1 corridor bordering the muddier Strait of Malacca with mangrove mudflats.
