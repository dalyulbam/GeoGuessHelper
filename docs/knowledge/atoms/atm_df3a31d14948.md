# Vienna's UNESCO-protected historic center height restrictions

`atm_df3a31d14948` · **architecture** / city · cell `u2edk85`

In reaction to modern high-rise pressure, Vienna's historic inner city (a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2001) enforces strict height and design restrictions to preserve the Ringstraße's historicist skyline, a tension that led UNESCO to place Vienna's center on its endangered heritage list in 2017 partly due to nearby high-rise projects.
