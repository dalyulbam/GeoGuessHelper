# Piedmont town economy tied to cattle, oil palm, rice, and weekend tourism

`atm_c8c9936aabeb` · **economy** / region · cell `d2g8r9w`

Small towns in the Meta piedmont of Colombia function as market centers for a rural economy based on cattle ranching, oil-palm plantations, and rice cultivation, supplemented by weekend tourism from nearby major cities (e.g., Villavicencio, Bogota) traveling along trunk highway corridors. Typical urban form includes single-storey concrete-block shophouses with corrugated roofs and heavy motorcycle dependence.
