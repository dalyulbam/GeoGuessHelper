# Baltic-German manorial network across Estonia, Latvia and beyond

`atm_54a887a29bb7` · **history** / global

The Baltic-German nobility (Baltendeutsche) controlled manorial estates (Gutshöfe/mõisad) across Estland, Livland, and Kurland from the 13th century Livonian crusades through the early 20th century, paralleling other colonial-noble land regimes such as the Junkers of Prussia and the German-Baltic estate networks in what is now Latvia and Estonia. Major manor complexes like Sangaste (Estonia) and Cesvaine (Latvia) reflect the same Historicist manor-building wave described for Vidzeme.
