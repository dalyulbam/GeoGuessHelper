# Deccan Traps volcanic flood-basalt province

`atm_131252aa7616` · **nature** / region · cell `temk4dv`

The Deccan Traps, formed by massive volcanic eruptions roughly 66 million years ago near the Cretaceous-Paleogene boundary, are one of the largest flood basalt provinces on Earth, comparable to the Siberian Traps in Russia and the Columbia River Basalt Group in the northwestern United States. These provinces are linked by geologists to mass extinction events and large igneous province volcanism.
