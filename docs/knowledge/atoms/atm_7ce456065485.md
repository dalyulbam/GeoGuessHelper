# Evangelical/Adventist inroads alongside Catholic parishes in rural central Mexico

`atm_7ce456065485` · **culture** / region · cell `9gbn2rp`

Rural mestizo villages in central-northern Mexico, historically uniformly Catholic, increasingly show small Protestant/Adventist congregations alongside the Catholic parish church, reflecting late-20th-century evangelical missionary growth in rural Latin America. Coexistence of a Catholic parish and a labelled Adventist church in the same small settlement is a common marker of this trend rather than an indicator of a distinct ethnic or linguistic sphere.
