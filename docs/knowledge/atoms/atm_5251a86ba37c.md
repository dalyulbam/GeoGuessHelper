# Tourist-emptied historic cores vs. functioning port/working districts

`atm_5251a86ba37c` · **culture** / global

Many Mediterranean and European heritage cities show the same split as Dubrovnik: a museified historic core hollowed by short-term rentals and cruise tourism, paired with an adjacent working district that retains real residential life. Examples include Venice's Cannaregio versus San Marco, Palma de Mallorca's Santa Catalina market district versus its old town, and Split's Veli Varoš neighborhood beside the touristified Diocletian's Palace core.
