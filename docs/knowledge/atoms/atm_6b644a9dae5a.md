# Mistral wind of the Rhône valley and Provence

`atm_6b644a9dae5a` · **nature** / region · cell `spg3tpz`

The mistral is a strong, cold, northerly katabatic wind that accelerates down the Rhône valley through a natural funnel between the Alps and the Massif Central, reaching the Mediterranean coast around Marseille and the Camargue. It has shaped Provençal architecture (houses oriented away from it, minimal north-facing windows, cypress windbreak rows) and is a recurring subject in regional culture and Van Gogh's paintings.
