# Roadside snow poles (snjóstikur) as cold-climate low-traffic road infrastructure

`atm_ad6f64fbb564` · **geography** / region · cell `g7x9x69`

In Iceland, rural two-lane roads without shoulders or guardrails commonly use thin yellow-tipped snow poles (snjóstikur) planted along the roadside at regular intervals instead of guardrails, marking road edges under snow cover. This is characteristic of low-traffic, low-maintenance-budget road design in a wealthy but sparsely populated country.
