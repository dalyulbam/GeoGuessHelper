# 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake and Monzenmachi's temple-town heritage

`atm_1dc57346ad13` · **history** / region · cell `xn9k2te`

**시기**: 2024 ~ 2024

Monzenmachi in Wajima City, Ishikawa Prefecture, grew historically as a temple town (monzen-machi, 'gate-front town') around the major Soto Zen temple Sojiji, before Sojiji's main hall burned and its head-temple function partly relocated to Yokohama in the 20th century. The coastal Noto Peninsula, including Wajima and Monzenmachi, suffered severe damage in the January 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake, compounding pre-existing rural depopulation.
