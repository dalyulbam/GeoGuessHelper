# Mangystau Kazakh (Adai/Junior Zhuz) desert-town housing typology

`atm_191ca560edf9` · **culture** / region · cell `v0nxmzr`

In Mangystau Region, Kazakh Turkic communities of the Junior Zhuz (Adai tribal confederation) live in towns characterized by newly built single-family brick/masonry villas with solid painted perimeter walls and metal hipped roofs, laid out on freshly surveyed bare-sand grids — funded largely by oil and rail wages. This contrasts with Russian settler towns (whitewashed izba cottages, blue window frames, khrushchevka apartment blocks, Orthodox churches) and Karakalpak/Uzbek mahalla districts (mud-brick walled courtyards with grape trellises, tiled-dome mosques, irrigation channels).
