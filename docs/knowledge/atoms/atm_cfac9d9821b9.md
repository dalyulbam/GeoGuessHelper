# Ivan Kotliarevsky and the Ukrainian language revival

Ivan Kotliarevsky (1769–1838), author of the burlesque epic 'Eneida', is credited as the founder of modern literary Ukrainian, writing in vernacular Ukrainian at a time when Russian dominated administration and print in the Russian Empire. His work anchored a 19th-century Ukrainophile cultural revival centered in Poltava and later Kharkiv.
