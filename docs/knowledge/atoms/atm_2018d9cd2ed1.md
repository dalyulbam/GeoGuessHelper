# Presidio network as Spanish colonial frontier defense pattern

`atm_2018d9cd2ed1` · **history** / global

Spain built networks of small fortified presidios to secure frontier and coastal zones across its empire, from Presidio San Francisco (1776) and Presidio Santa Bárbara in California to Presidio del Carmen in Campeche and coastal Gulf outposts like Sabancuy. These garrison settlements often predate and outlast the larger civilian towns that later grew around or near them.
