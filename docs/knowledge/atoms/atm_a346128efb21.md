# West coast industrial corridor as the counterpart to the east coast's tourism belt

`atm_a346128efb21` · **economy** / region · cell `w283cgq`

Peninsular Malaysia's west coast, along the North-South Expressway/Route 1, hosts the country's industrial and economic core including Kuala Lumpur, Penang's electronics manufacturing hub, and Port Klang, Malaysia's busiest port, in sharp contrast to the east coast's slower-paced fishing and tourism economy. This west-east economic imbalance has persisted despite decades of federal rebalancing initiatives.
