# Štokavian-based standardization of literary Croatian

`atm_aaefed17cab0` · **language** / country

From the 19th-century Illyrian movement onward, Croatian literary language was standardized on a Štokavian basis (shared with Serbian) rather than Čakavian or Kajkavian, marginalizing Čakavian to a spoken regional dialect and reducing Glagolitic script to a heritage/liturgical curiosity rather than an everyday writing system.
