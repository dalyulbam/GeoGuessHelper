# Trans Adriatic Pipeline energy corridor in Albania

`atm_4f6b8bc9af59` · **economy** / country · cell `srjy70q`

**시기**: 2020 ~ ?

The Trans Adriatic Pipeline (TAP) carries natural gas across Albania toward Italy; its Adriatic crossing point is near Vlorë. Combined with a nearby thermal power plant, this energy infrastructure has drawn investment and jobs to the Vlorë area and forms part of the Southern Gas Corridor linking Caspian gas to Europe.
