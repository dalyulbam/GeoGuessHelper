# Global family of socialist prefabricated housing estates

`atm_fc90c4a62930` · **architecture** / global

Mass prefabricated concrete-panel housing was a defining feature of socialist and some capitalist mass-housing programs worldwide: East Germany's Plattenbau, Yugoslavia's blokovi (e.g. Novi Beograd), Czechoslovakia's panelák estates like Prague's Jižní Město, and North Korea's Pyongyang tower blocks all share standardized panel-construction techniques derived from Soviet models developed under Khrushchev from the mid-1950s.
