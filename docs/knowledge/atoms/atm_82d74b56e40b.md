# Bajío linear colonia sits close to big cities, not only deep countryside

`atm_82d74b56e40b` · **geography** / region · cell `9exuvn3`

In the Bajío, dispersed roadside colonias with weedy lots, cinderblock sheds and horses grazing on the verge occur within 10-25 km of large cities like Irapuato, Salamanca or Celaya. Do not read low built density as evidence of a remote western-Bajío location; instead look for frequent truck traffic, telecom masts and paved side turnoffs, which indicate proximity to a metropolitan corridor. Absent a route shield, anchor the guess on the central Bajío corridor rather than its far western end.
