# Tatarstan as a titular Turkic-Muslim republic within Russia

`atm_a5822442b0ee` · **culture** / polity · cell `v1uv16c`

The Republic of Tatarstan is one of Russia's titular ethnic republics, home to the Volga Tatars (Turkic, historically Muslim). It asserts a distinct identity through bilingual/dual-script signage (Cyrillic Russian plus Latin transliteration of place names), a white-green-red republic tricolour flag flown alongside Russian flags, and republic-branded institutions like the oil company Tatneft. Key cities include Kazan (capital) and Naberezhnye Chelny (home of the KAMAZ truck manufacturer).
