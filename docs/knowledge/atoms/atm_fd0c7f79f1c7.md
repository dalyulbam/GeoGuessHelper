# Interwar land reform and nationalization of Baltic-German estates

`atm_fd0c7f79f1c7` · **history** / period

Following Latvian and Estonian independence in 1918, sweeping agrarian reforms (Latvia's 1920 land reform law) expropriated most Baltic-German manorial estates without full compensation, redistributing land to landless peasants and ending centuries of noble economic dominance. Most remaining Baltic-German landowners emigrated in the 1939 'Umsiedlung' resettlement to Nazi Germany, effectively terminating the community's presence in the region.
