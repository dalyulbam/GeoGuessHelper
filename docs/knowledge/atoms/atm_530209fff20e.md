# Argentine provincial route shield and rural infrastructure conventions

`atm_530209fff20e` · **geography** / country · cell `6993z1r`

Argentina's provincial road network uses green pentagon-style route shields (e.g., RP10, RP105) distinct from national 'Ruta Nacional' blue shields, alongside 'Policía Caminera' (highway police) checkpoints at rural junctions and roundabouts. Rural approach roads to small towns typically feature rust-brown oxidised steel light poles, guardrails, and monumental brick entrance arches (portales) naming the town, a common civic branding device across small Argentine interior towns.
