# Sri Lanka's wet-zone southwest coast vs dry-zone north/east contrast

`atm_b9a5c0270203` · **geography** / region · cell `tc2c139`

Sri Lanka's southwest coastal wet zone (Colombo–Negombo–Galle) is flat, sandy, and densely vegetated with coconut, areca, banana, and mango under humid tropical conditions, contrasting with the dry zone (Jaffna, Anuradhapura, Batticaloa) marked by palmyrah palms, red-brown lateritic soil, sparser scrub vegetation, and Tamil-language signage. Hill country (central highlands) differs further with sloping terrain, tea cultivation, and cooler-climate architecture.
