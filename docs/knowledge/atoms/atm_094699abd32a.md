# Tonami/Takaoka plain, western Toyama

`atm_094699abd32a` · **geography** / region · cell `xn92tyx`

The Tonami and Takaoka plains form the flat, heavily rice-farmed lowland of western Toyama Prefecture on the Sea of Japan coast of Honshu. The area is a heavy-snow region (gosetsu) with a humid climate, and its landscape mixes irrigated paddies, scattered farmsteads, and industrial plants. Takaoka is the region's urban center and Toyama Prefecture's second-largest city.
