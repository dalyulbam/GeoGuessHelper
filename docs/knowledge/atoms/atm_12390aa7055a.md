# Competing claims to Homeric sites across the Mediterranean

`atm_12390aa7055a` · **culture** / global

Multiple Mediterranean locations claim identity with places from Homer's Odyssey: Ithaca (Greek Ionian island) and nearby Lefkada both claim to be Odysseus's true home island; Gozo in Malta and Mljet in Croatia both claim to be Calypso's Ogygia; Trapani in Sicily is linked to the Laestrygonians and Cyclops episodes. These competing mythic-geography claims drive local cultural tourism branding.
