# Tourism-dependent Mediterranean regions with seasonal wage gaps

`atm_fdfb75d2c5b0` · **economy** / global

Many Mediterranean coastal regions show the same pattern as Dubrovnik-Neretva: high GDP per capita driven by tourism but below-national-average wages due to seasonal, low-bargaining-power service employment, seen in Spain's Balearic Islands, Greece's Cyclades, and Italy's Amalfi Coast. These economies also face housing-affordability pressure from short-term rental conversion for tourists.
