# Plavac Mali: indigenous Dalmatian red grape variety

`atm_ba70b4476414` · **culture** / region · cell `srskkfj`

Plavac Mali is an indigenous Dalmatian red wine grape, a natural cross of Crljenak Kaštelanski (genetically identical to Zinfandel/Primitivo) and Dobričić. It produces concentrated, high-alcohol, high-tannin wines with flavors of carob, fig, dark cherry, and pepper, and is the sole grape used in the prestigious Dingač and Postup appellations of the Pelješac peninsula.
