# National retail-chain signage as a country-identification method

`atm_769bef8463f0` · **culture** / global

Street-level imagery analysts identify countries by distinctive grocery/retail chain branding: Tesco/Sainsbury's mark the UK, Carrefour/Leclerc mark France, Rewe/Edeka mark Germany, Woolworths/Coles mark Australia, and 7-Eleven density plus kanji signage marks Japan. This chain-recognition method is widely used in geolocation games like GeoGuessr alongside language cues on signage.
