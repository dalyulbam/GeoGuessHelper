# Medieval and early modern Irish kingdoms and lordships

`atm_810f8747fdde` · **history** / region

Ireland was divided among numerous kingdoms and later polities: Magh Luirg (c.956-1585), Thomond (1118-1543), Kingdom of Desmond (1118-1596), Lordship of Ireland (1171-1542), Clandeboye (1283-1605), Kingdom of Ireland (1541-1801), Irish Catholic Confederation (1641-1649), Republic of Connacht (1798), Irish Soviets (1919-1922) including the Limerick Soviet (1919), and the Irish Free State (1922-1937).
