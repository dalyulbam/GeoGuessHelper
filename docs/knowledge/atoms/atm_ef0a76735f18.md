# Highway-corridor satellite hamlets dependent on a nearby hub city

`atm_ef0a76735f18` · **economy** / region · cell `9gr3utj`

Small linear settlements strung along a federal/state highway near a larger hub city (e.g. Escárcega, Campeche) often lack independent economic bases, functioning instead as commuter/service satellites: residents rely on the hub city for schooling, healthcare and commerce, while local income comes from highway-adjacent microenterprise (roadside snack stalls, tire repair, small general stores) serving passing truck and bus traffic. National distribution brands (Bimbo bakery, Coca-Cola) reaching such tiny hamlets indicate good road connectivity despite otherwise weak local infrastructure.
