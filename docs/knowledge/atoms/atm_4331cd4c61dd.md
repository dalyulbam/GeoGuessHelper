# Iceland's regional health authority (Heilbrigðisstofnun) boundaries as a locating clue

`atm_4331cd4c61dd` · **economy** / country · cell `ge9h53m`

Iceland's public healthcare system is organized into regional authorities (Heilbrigðisstofnun) that do not always align with obvious geographic proximity — e.g. a town's clinic may be branded under the 'Vesturlands' (West Iceland) authority even while neighboring towns along the same coast fall under 'Norðurlands' (North Iceland). Signage naming these institutions can pinpoint a specific municipality among otherwise similar small coastal towns.
