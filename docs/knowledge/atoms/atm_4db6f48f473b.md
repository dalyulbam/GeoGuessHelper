# Dinaric-type karst escarpment coastlines worldwide

`atm_4db6f48f473b` · **nature** / global

Steep limestone escarpments dropping directly to a narrow coastal strip, similar to the Dubrovnik littoral, occur along Croatia's entire Dinaric coast, Greece's Mani Peninsula and Ionian islands (Kefalonia), and Vietnam's Ha Long Bay karst towers rising from the sea. These landscapes share bare, thin-soiled limestone slopes supporting only maquis or garrigue scrub rather than forest.
