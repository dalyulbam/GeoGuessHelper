# Mixed farming/factory-commuter economy near industrial parks in northern Vietnam

`atm_6a567f0a848c` · **economy** / region · cell `w7g6609`

Provinces like Thái Nguyên combine traditional wet-rice and hill-orchard farming with proximity to large foreign-invested industrial zones (e.g. Samsung's Điềm Thụy/Yên Bình complex), producing a rural landscape where commune populations commute to factory work while retaining agricultural land use. This mixed economy is visible in worker dormitory clusters near industrial park fence lines alongside conventional paddy and orchard villages.
