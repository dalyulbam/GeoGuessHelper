# United Arab Emirates ruling houses

`atm_ae6ab4de9a82` · **history** / country

The UAE's emirates are ruled by the House of Qasimi (1708-present, Ras Al Khaimah and Sharjah), House of Nahyan (1761-present, Abu Dhabi, provides the UAE President), House of Mualla (1768-present, Umm Al Quwain), House of Nuaimi (1816-present, Ajman), House of Maktoum (1833-present, Dubai), and House of Sharqi (1879-present, Fujairah).
