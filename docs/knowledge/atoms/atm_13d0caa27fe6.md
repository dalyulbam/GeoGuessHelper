# Geological time scale eras

`atm_13d0caa27fe6` · **history** / period

The geologic time scale divides Earth's history into the Precambrian (Hadean, Archean with Eoarchean, Paleoarchean, Mesoarchean, Neoarchean; and Proterozoic with Paleoproterozoic, Mesoproterozoic, Neoproterozoic and their sub-periods) and the Phanerozoic (Paleozoic: Cambrian, Ordovician, Silurian, Devonian, Carboniferous, Permian; Mesozoic: Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous; Cenozoic: Paleogene, Neogene, Quaternary with their epochs and ages down to the Holocene and the rejected Anthropocene proposal).
