# Yacyretá dam reservoir and Paraná River flooding at Encarnación

`atm_9af3c124876b` · **history** / region · cell `6g0k8uc`

**시기**: 1994 ~ 2011

The Yacyretá hydroelectric dam, a binational Paraguay-Argentina project on the Paraná River, raised reservoir levels in the late 2000s/2010s, flooding low-lying areas and creating wide backwater arms and embayments around Encarnación, Paraguay and Posadas, Argentina. This required rebuilding Encarnación's waterfront as a reorganized 'Costanera' promenade and relocating riverside neighborhoods, reshaping the city's urban geography and economy around retail/tourism trade with Posadas across the river.
