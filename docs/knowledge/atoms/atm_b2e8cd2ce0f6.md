# Toetoe plumes and broadleaf bush vs eucalypt woodland

`atm_b2e8cd2ce0f6` · **nature** / country · cell `rbv6fj8`

New Zealand rural roadsides mix dense dark broadleaf bush, flax and cabbage-tree forms, tall toetoe/pampas plumes and exotic Pinus radiata shelterbelts. In comparable Australian hill country you would instead see open eucalypt woodland with stringybark or gum trunks, sparse understorey and no toetoe plumes. The absence of any eucalypt silhouette on a left-hand-drive-free, left-traffic road strongly favours NZ.
