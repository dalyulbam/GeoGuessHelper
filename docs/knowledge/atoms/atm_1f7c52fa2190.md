# Cyrillic glosses on Croatian-Herzegovinian borderland signage

`atm_1f7c52fa2190` · **language** / region · cell `srsfsxb`

In the borderland zone between Catholic Croat Dalmatia and the Orthodox Serb/Bosniak hinterland, some place-name signage and map labels for older infrastructure (e.g. rail stations) carry a secondary Serbian Cyrillic gloss beneath the primary Croatian Latin-script name, reflecting historical multi-ethnic administration of the area, even where local signage is otherwise exclusively Latin script.
