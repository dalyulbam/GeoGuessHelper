# Mongol, Jurchen and Southeast Asian frontier polities, 14th-17th centuries

`atm_2b194559c7ce` · **history** / region

The Oirat state (瓦剌, AD 1399-1634), ruled by the House of Choros of Mongol descent, gave rise to the Dzungar Khanate (1634-1757). The Dughlat tribe ruled 14th-16th centuries. The Hulun alliance (1406-1619), ruled by the House of Nara of Jurchen descent, comprised Ula (1561-1613, House of Ula Nara), Hoifa (16th century-1607, House of Hoifa Nara), Hada (16th century-17th century, House of Hada Nara), and Yehe (?-1619, House of Yehe Nara, split into Beile of Western City of Yehe and Beile of Eastern City of Yehe). The Hor States (五霍爾, 14th-20th centuries) comprised Khangsar, Mazur, Drango, Beri, and Trehor. Southeast Asian frontier polities include Möng Lem (14th-19th centuries) and Möng Yang (14th-19th centuries), Babai Dadian Xuanweisi (1391-?), Miandian Xuanweisi (1403-1526), and Babai Zhenai Xuanweisi (1404-?).
