# Moso bamboo thrives on the Sea-of-Japan coast up to Noto

`atm_18dfb917ecd9` · **nature** / region · cell `xn9k2th`

Healthy Phyllostachys edulis groves interleaved with sugi plantation occur all along the Hokuriku coastal belt, including the Noto Peninsula, because maritime winters keep temperatures mild despite the snow. Only in Hokkaido and high inland basins would you instead find sasa dwarf bamboo and no moso at all, so a moso grove narrows the guess to 'not Hokkaido', not to the Pacific side.
