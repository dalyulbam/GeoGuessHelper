# 'T.C. … BÜYÜKŞEHİR BELEDİYESİ' shelters pin the province in rural Turkey

`atm_e0ddecfde4f8` · **culture** / region · cell `swesgsx`

In Turkey, rural roadside bus shelters carry a black fascia with 'T.C. <PROVINCE> BÜYÜKŞEHİR BELEDİYESİ' and a province-specific rosette or seal, deployed even in hamlets far from the provincial capital after the 2014 metropolitan reform. In Greece or Bulgaria you would instead see unbranded or KTEL/operator-branded shelters with Greek or Cyrillic text and no republic ('T.C.') prefix. Reading the wordmark therefore jumps straight from country to province.
