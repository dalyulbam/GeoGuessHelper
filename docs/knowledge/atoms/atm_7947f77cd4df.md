# Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (1569-1795)

`atm_7947f77cd4df` · **history** / polity

The Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (1569-1795) united the Crown of the Kingdom of Poland (1386-1795) and the Grand Duchy of Lithuania (1236-1795), and encompassed Royal Prussia (1466-1569), the Duchy of Prussia (1525-1657), and the Duchy of Courland and Semigallia (1561-1795).
