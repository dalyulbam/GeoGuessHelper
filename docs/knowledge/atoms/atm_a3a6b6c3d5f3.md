# Soviet coal-industry planners and Donbas/lignite basin developers

`atm_a3a6b6c3d5f3` · **economy** / country

Soviet five-year-plan industrial planners under Gosplan directed the buildout of coal combines like Oleksandriiavuhillia and the larger Donbas coalfields, embedding company-town infrastructure (housing, clubs, hospitals) around mine shafts. Post-Soviet oligarchs and state holding managers then oversaw the 1990s-2000s restructuring and partial privatization of these assets.
