# Fjord-corridor hydro and industrial development pressure

`atm_dfa20e6bf23f` · **geography** / global

The same steep, deep-water fjord corridors prized for scenery are also exploited for hydroelectric dams and industrial ports, as seen with Norway's fjord-based aluminum smelters (e.g. at Sunndalsora) and BC's own Howe Sound, which hosted the Woodfibre pulp mill and Britannia copper mine before their closure and environmental remediation. Conservation and tourism interests now often clash with proposed LNG and industrial projects in these same corridors.
