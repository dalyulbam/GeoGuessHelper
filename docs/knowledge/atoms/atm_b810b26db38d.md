# Austrian Einfamilienhaus render palette vs German rural render

`atm_b810b26db38d` · **architecture** / country · cell `u299pj2`

Austrian post-1980 rural houses commonly carry saturated single-colour render (deep red, ochre, yellow) with crisp white window reveals and a dark plinth band, with garages recessed under the living floor on a slope. In German villages you would more often see white or pale-grey render with dark-red tiles and free-standing or side-attached garages rather than the sunken under-house garage.
