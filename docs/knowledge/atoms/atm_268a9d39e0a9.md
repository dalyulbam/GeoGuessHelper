# Metric-imperial and signage divergence at the Canada-US border

`atm_268a9d39e0a9` · **geography** / country

Despite shared right-hand traffic and similar pole/barrier infrastructure, Canada and the US diverge sharply in measurement systems: Canada posts speed limits and distances in kilometers (adopted fully in 1977 under metrication policy) while the US retains miles, creating an abrupt cognitive shift for drivers crossing the border, e.g. at the Peace Arch crossing between BC and Washington State.
