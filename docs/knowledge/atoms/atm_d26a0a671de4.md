# Former military zones turned marine protected areas

`atm_d26a0a671de4` · **nature** / global

Karaburun-Sazan mirrors a global pattern of Cold War-era restricted military islands and coastlines being repurposed as marine nature reserves once demilitarized, since limited human access preserved habitats: examples include Croatia's Vis Island (former Yugoslav naval base, now popular but with protected coves), and the former Soviet military zones on Estonia's islands (Naissaar, Suurupi) now under conservation.
