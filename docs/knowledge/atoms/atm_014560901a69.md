# UAE vs Saudi Arabia: bilingual English fascias and plate colour

`atm_014560901a69` · **language** / country · cell `thrxzsf`

In UAE small towns nearly every shop fascia is fully bilingual Arabic/English with Latin transliteration and a P.O. Box plus emirate name, and cars carry plain white rectangular plates with coloured emirate lettering. In Saudi Arabia you would instead see many Arabic-only fascias, landlines beginning 01x/013/012, and plates with a distinctive left-hand blue band and Arabic-numeral duplication.
