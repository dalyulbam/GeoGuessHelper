# Palisade fencing and burglar bars as normalised South African security idiom

`atm_b408fc3d3b66` · **culture** / country · cell `k9fpcsx`

Galvanised steel palisade fencing and burglar bars on windows are a near-universal built-environment feature across South African residential, commercial and industrial properties, reflecting normalised private security spending in response to property crime concerns. This visual idiom is distinct from Australia/NZ or Southern Europe, where such fencing is rare or signals unusual paranoia rather than baseline practice.
