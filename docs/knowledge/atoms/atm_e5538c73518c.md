# Po Plain–Apennine transition zone: flat alluvial floor meeting foothills

`atm_e5538c73518c` · **geography** / region · cell `spyrs4d`

The southern margin of the Po Plain (e.g., Oltrepò Pavese, Piacentino, Tortonese) is characterized by a dead-flat alluvial floor of fine grey-brown loam under intensive winter cereal cultivation, with poplar and pollarded willow hedgerows, giving way abruptly to modest 500-900m forested Apennine foothills on the horizon. This differs from the rolling hill country of Tuscany/Umbria (cypress, olive, stone farmhouses) and from Apulian tavoliere (red soil, dry-stone walls, olives).
