# Sri Lankan suburban vernacular housing markers

Lower-middle-income Sri Lankan coastal suburbs show a characteristic mix: single-storey houses with steep half-hipped red clay-tile roofs and deep columned verandahs sit beside incrementally self-built homes with exposed brick and corrugated-sheet fencing. Boundary walls are low, plastered, and feature pierced decorative triangle/grille openings. Private unpaved lanes without lane markings or sidewalks indicate land informally subdivided from former coconut gardens, common where remittance or Gulf-labor income funds gradual home upgrades.
