# Post-war tourism-led reconstruction of the Dalmatian coast

`atm_329f3ad24f9a` · **economy** / region · cell `srsfs47`

Following the 1991-95 war, Dalmatian coastal towns including Dubrovnik pursued rapid tourism-driven reconstruction, rebuilding historic cores (aided by UNESCO support for Dubrovnik's Old Town) and marketing wartime heritage sites alongside beaches, reversing outmigration in some centers while littoral hamlets recovered more slowly. This represents a broader postwar pattern of tourism as the primary vehicle for economic recovery in former conflict zones, as also seen in Beirut and parts of Vietnam.
