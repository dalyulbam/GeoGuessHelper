# National consumer cooperative retail chains in post-socialist Europe

`atm_4be2cb5e9ebf` · **economy** / global

Slovakia's COOP Jednota belongs to a wider family of national consumer cooperative grocery chains rooted in 19th-20th century cooperative movements, surviving into the post-socialist era as recognizable national retail brands: Coop in Czechia (formerly Jednota), Co-op in Hungary (COOP Hungary), Społem in Poland, and the original Rochdale-style Co-operative Group in the United Kingdom. Each nation's cooperative chain acts as a distinct visual and commercial marker of that country's retail landscape.
