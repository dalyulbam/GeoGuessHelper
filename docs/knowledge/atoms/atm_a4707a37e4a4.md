# Pioneering big-wall and granite monolith climbers

`atm_a4707a37e4a4` · **culture** / global

Yvon Chouinard and Warren Harding pioneered big-wall climbing techniques on El Capitan in Yosemite in the 1950s-60s, methods later applied to granite monoliths worldwide including the Stawamus Chief. Fred Beckey was a prolific first-ascensionist across North American granite ranges including the Coast Mountains and Cascades, documenting and opening routes that became foundational to regional climbing culture.
