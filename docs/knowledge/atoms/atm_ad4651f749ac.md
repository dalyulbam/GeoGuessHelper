# Turkish KGM warning triangle + rain sub-plate vs Greek sign style

`atm_ad4651f749ac` · **language** / country · cell `swesgsx`

In Turkey warning triangles carry a broad white inner band inside the red border and are frequently paired with a separate white rectangular supplementary plate (e.g. rain/umbrella pictogram) on the same twin-post galvanised frame, with speed roundels stacked directly below. In Greece you would instead see triangles with a narrower white margin, Greek-language supplementary text plates, and blue-on-white place-name signage in Greek script. Absence of any Greek characters plus this stacking convention settles the border.
