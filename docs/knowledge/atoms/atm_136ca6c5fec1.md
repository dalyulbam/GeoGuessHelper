# Russo-Turkish War (1877–78) battle sites around Pleven, Bulgaria

`atm_136ca6c5fec1` · **history** / region · cell `sx9wsps`

The Siege of Pleven and surrounding engagements (including the Battle of Pelishat-Zgalevo, August 19, 1877, where Osman Pasha's breakout attempt was repelled) were pivotal in the 1877–78 Russo-Turkish War of Liberation. Villages around Pleven province commonly host memorial monuments to fallen Russian and Bulgarian soldiers from this war, and the war's memory forms a core layer of local identity and heritage-tourism itineraries across the region, alongside Pleven's own war museums and the Grivitsa redoubt.
