# Romance-language street-naming conventions as a contrast case

`atm_19e7bb82b228` · **language** / country

In contrast to German compound street-suffix naming, Romance-language countries like France and Italy use a prefixed generic term plus name, e.g. 'Rue de Rivoli' or 'Via Roma', reflecting a fundamentally different toponymic word order and grammatical structure from German's suffixed compound street names like Bahnhofstraße.
