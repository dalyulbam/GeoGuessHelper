# National highway numbering and driving-side as geolocation cues

`atm_0de705b1aa28` · **geography** / global

Countries encode identity in road systems: Germany's Autobahn 'A' network, the US Interstate system, Japan's expressway numbering, and India's National Highway 'NH' prefixes each use distinct signage conventions. Driving side (right-hand traffic in most of the world vs left-hand in the UK, Japan, Australia, India, and former British colonies) is a strong visual proxy for former colonial or trade-sphere affiliation.
