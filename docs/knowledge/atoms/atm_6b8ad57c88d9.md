# Baltic-German cultural figures: Herder, Keyserling, and the Ritterschaften

`atm_6b8ad57c88d9` · **history** / polity · cell `ud15ux0`

Baltic-German noble corporations (Ritterschaften) of Estland, Livland, and Kurland governed local law and church administration for centuries; philosopher Johann Gottfried Herder worked in Riga in the 1760s within this milieu, and Hermann Keyserling, born on a Livonian estate, became a noted 20th-century philosopher. These figures exemplify the intellectual output tied to the manorial estate system.
