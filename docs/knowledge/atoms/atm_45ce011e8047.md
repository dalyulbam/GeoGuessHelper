# Polar day/night seasonal light cycle in high-Arctic Norwegian cities

`atm_45ce011e8047` · **nature** / region · cell `ukmkre5`

Cities north of the Arctic Circle in Norway (e.g. Tromso, ~69.7N) experience extreme photoperiod swings: polar night (no sunrise) roughly mid-November to mid-January, and midnight sun/polar day roughly mid-May to mid-July. Fireweed blooms and lush, compressed summer vegetation growth are visible markers of the short, intense subarctic growing season during this period.
