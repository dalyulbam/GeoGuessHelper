# Global fruit-company enclave economies and 'banana republic' pattern

`atm_3b9faf71c73e` · **economy** / global

**시기**: 1900 ~ 1980

The United Fruit Company model was replicated across Central America and the Caribbean by Standard Fruit Company in Honduras, and similar single-crop corporate enclaves arose in colonial contexts such as sugar plantation economies in Cuba and the Dominican Republic, and rubber plantations of Firestone in Liberia. These enclaves shared company towns, foreign ownership of infrastructure, and heavy political influence over host governments.
