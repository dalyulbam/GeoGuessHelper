Aurangabad is a mid-sized city on the Deccan plateau in Maharashtra, India, historically associated with the Mughal emperor Aurangzeb who made it a base of operations. It was officially renamed Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar in 2023 after the Maratha king Sambhaji. It serves as a base for tourism to nearby heritage sites and combines Mughal-era monuments with modern residential colonies.
