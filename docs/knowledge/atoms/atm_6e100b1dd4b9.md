# Georgian dynasties

`atm_6e100b1dd4b9` · **history** / country

Georgia's ruling dynasties include the Pharnavazid dynasty (299 BC-90 BC, 30 BC-189 AD), Artaxiad dynasty of Iberia (90-30 BC), Arsacid dynasty of Iberia (189-284 AD), Chosroid dynasty (284-580, 627-684) with Guaramid dynasty branch, Nersianid dynasty (748-780), and the long-ruling Bagrationi dynasty (813-1810). Regional houses include the Tsarazon dynasty of Alania, House of Dadiani (1183-1857), House of Jaqeli (1268-1625), House of Shervashidze (1463-1864), House of Mukhrani (1658-1724), and House of Dadeshkeliani (1720-1857).
