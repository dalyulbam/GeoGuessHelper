# Northern and Southern dynasties (386-589)

`atm_f60de8a51efb` · **history** / period

**시기**: 386 ~ 589

The Northern and Southern dynasties period (AD 386-589) split China into Northern dynasties (386-581) — Northern Wei (386-535, House of Tuoba, Xianbei, splitting into Eastern Wei 534-550 and Western Wei 535-557), Northern Qi (550-577, House of Gao, Han), Northern Zhou (557-581, House of Yuwen, Xianbei) — and Southern dynasties (420-589) — Liu Song (420-479, House of Liu, Han), Southern Qi (479-502, House of Xiao, Han), and Liang dynasty (502-557, House of Xiao, Han).
