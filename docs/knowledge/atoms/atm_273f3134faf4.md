# Vatnsnes peninsula seal-watching tourism (Northwest Iceland)

`atm_273f3134faf4` · **economy** / region · cell `ge9h53m`

Small coastal service towns in North and Northwest Iceland typically combine three economic pillars: inland sheep and horse farming, light fishing and marine-service activity, and seasonal nature/wildlife tourism (e.g. seal-watching, whale-watching routes). A local concrete or construction-materials plant often serves regional building demand. This mix is characteristic of Icelandic rural municipalities outside the capital region and major fishing ports.
