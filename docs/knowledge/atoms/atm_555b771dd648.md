# Climbing access restrictions and land-use conflicts on sacred/protected rock

`atm_555b771dd648` · **nature** / global

Iconic climbing monoliths often face access restrictions balancing recreation against indigenous or ecological concerns: Uluru in Australia banned recreational climbing entirely in 2019 due to its sacred status to the Anangu people, and some First Nations groups in British Columbia have raised similar concerns over climbing on culturally significant peaks. This counters the global trend of monolith-based outdoor-recreation branding with conservation and cultural-heritage limits.
