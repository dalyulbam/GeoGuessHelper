# Decisive battles of the Reconquista

`atm_5436844715ef` · **history** / global

The Reconquista's turning points include the Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa (1212), which broke Almohad power in Iberia, and the fall of Granada in 1492 under the Catholic Monarchs Ferdinand II of Aragon and Isabella I of Castile, ending nearly 800 years of Muslim rule in the peninsula. These battles are commemorated in Spanish national iconography much like La Higueruela.
