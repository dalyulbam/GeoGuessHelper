# Pacific side of Honshu: dry winter, wet summer contrast

`atm_e2336b077e82` · **nature** / region

The Pacific coast side of Honshu (Tokyo, Nagoya, Osaka region) experiences the opposite seasonal pattern from Hokuriku: dry, sunny winters as the winter monsoon dumps its moisture on the Sea of Japan side and descends dry over the mountains (foehn-like effect), while summers bring heavy rain and typhoons from the Pacific. This Pacific-side/Sea-of-Japan-side climatic divide, split by the central Honshu mountain spine, shapes distinct regional agriculture and architecture across Japan.
