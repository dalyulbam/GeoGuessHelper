# Chile's MOP letter-block road prefixes map to macro-regions

`atm_0b01719c9eb4` · **geography** / country · cell `62ux7xt`

Chile's Ministry of Public Works assigns secondary/local road letter prefixes in a north-to-south geographic sequence: roughly C/D for Norte Chico (Atacama-Coquimbo), F/G/H/I for Central Chile, N/O/P/Q for Biobío, S/T for Araucanía and northern Los Ríos, and V/W for Los Lagos. Identifying the letter prefix on a signed route (e.g., S-239) narrows a location to that macro-region even without other cues.
