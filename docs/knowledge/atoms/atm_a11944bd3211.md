# Turkic Khaganates (552-745)

`atm_a11944bd3211` · **history** / polity

The First Turkic Khaganate (552-581), ruled by the House of Ashina of Göktürk descent, split into the Eastern Turkic Khaganate (581-630, revived as Later Eastern Turkic Khaganate 639-681) and the Western Turkic Khaganate (581-657, later divided into the Xingxiwang Khaganate of the Left Wing 657-736 and Jiwangjue Khaganate of the Right Wing 657-742). The Second Turkic Khaganate (682-745) later restored Ashina rule. The Basmyl Khaganate (742-744) was also ruled by the House of Ashina. The Tokhara Yabghu State (625-758) was likewise ruled by Ashina descendants of Göktürk stock.
