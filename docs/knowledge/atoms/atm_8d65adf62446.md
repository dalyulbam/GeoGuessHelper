# Post-communist deindustrialization of Łódź

`atm_8d65adf62446` · **economy** / city

After 1989, Łódź's textile industry collapsed under market liberalization and competition from cheaper foreign producers, leading to mass factory closures and unemployment; many former mill complexes, such as Poznański's factory, were later repurposed into shopping and cultural centers like Manufaktura, reflecting a broader post-socialist pattern of adaptive reuse of industrial heritage across Central Europe.
