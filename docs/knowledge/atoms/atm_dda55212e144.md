# Daimyo castle-town craft patronage as a Japan-wide urban pattern

`atm_dda55212e144` · **history** / global

Beyond Kaga's Maeda clan, other Edo-period daimyo similarly seeded castle-town craft economies: the Shimazu clan of Satsuma fostered Satsuma ware ceramics in Kagoshima, and the Maeda's rivals the Date clan promoted textile and lacquerware crafts in Sendai. This daimyo-patronage model of castle towns as craft-industry incubators was a widespread structural feature of Edo-period Japan's decentralized feudal economy.
