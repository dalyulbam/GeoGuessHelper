# Icelandic sagas as landscape toponymy sources

`atm_cad327cb9b65` · **culture** / polity · cell `g7x9x69`

Many Icelandic place names encode narrative episodes from medieval Icelandic sagas (e.g. Eyrbyggja Saga), such as Berserkjahraun ('berserkers' lava field') named for a legendary tale of two Swedish berserker slaves who built a road (Berserkjagata) across a lava field and were then killed and buried there by the farmer who had promised them freedom. These saga-linked sites function today as heritage/hiking attractions with associated grave markers.
