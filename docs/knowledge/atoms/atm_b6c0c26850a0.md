# Sixteen Kingdoms (304-439)

`atm_b6c0c26850a0` · **history** / period

**시기**: 304 ~ 439

The Sixteen Kingdoms period (AD 304-439) comprised numerous successor states in northern China: Han-Zhao (304-329, House of Liu, Xiongnu, with Northern Han and Former Zhao phases), Cheng-Han (304-347, House of Li, Di, with Chengdu, Cheng, Han phases), Later Zhao (319-351, House of Shi, Jie, with Wei phase), Former Liang (320-376, House of Zhang, Han, with Xiping phase), Former Yan (337-370, House of Murong, Xianbei), Former Qin (351-394, House of Fu, Di), Later Yan (384-409, House of Murong, Xianbei), Later Qin (384-417, House of Yao, Qiang), Western Qin (385-400, 409-431, House of Qifu, Xianbei, with Yuanchuan, Henan, Jincheng, Liang phases), Later Liang (386-403, House of Lü, Di, with Jiuquan, Sanhe phases), Southern Liang (397-404, 408-414, House of Tufa, Xianbei, with Xiping, Wuwei, Hexi phases), Northern Liang (397-439, House of Juqu, Lushuihu, with Jiankang, Zhangye, Hexi, and Northern Liang of Gaochang 442-460 phases), Southern Yan (398-410, House of Murong, Xianbei), Western Liang (400-421, House of Li, Han, with Later Western Liang 422-442), Hu Xia (407-431, House of Helian, Xiongnu), and Northern Yan (407-436, House of Feng, Han).
