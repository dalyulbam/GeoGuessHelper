# Upper Silesian Industrial Region (GOP) / Katowice metropolitan area

`atm_ce9d24778d61` · **history** / region · cell `u2vqqje`

The Katowice metropolitan area, historically known as the Upper Silesian Industrial Region (Górnośląski Okręg Przemysłowy, GOP), is Poland's largest conurbation with roughly 2.4 million people across 1,777 km², comprising Katowice, Sosnowiec, Gliwice, Zabrze, Bytom and other coal-mining/steel cities. Katowice serves as the metropolitan hub; other member cities historically had different pre-WWII sovereignties (some under Germany/Prussia, others under Poland), giving the conurbation a mixed cultural-administrative heritage despite shared coal, zinc, and steel industrial specialization.
