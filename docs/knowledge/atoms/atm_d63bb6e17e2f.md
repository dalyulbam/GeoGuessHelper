# Period of rule dating conventions

`atm_d63bb6e17e2f` · **history** / global

Establishment and collapse dates of dynasties may be unknown or disputed; approximate time frames are given in such cases, as with the Deva dynasty, believed to have ruled during the 12th-13th centuries AD with disputed exact dates. A dynasty's overall dates may differ from its rule over a specific realm/region; for example the House of Savoy was founded in 1003 in the County of Savoy and ruled the Kingdom of Italy until 1946, but held the Spanish throne only from 1870 to 1873.
