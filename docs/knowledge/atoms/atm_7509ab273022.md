# Continental steppe-forest transition climates worldwide

`atm_7509ab273022` · **nature** / global

The forest-steppe biome, sharply continental with cold winters and warm summers, recurs across the Eurasian and North American mid-latitude interiors: the Kazakh and Ukrainian steppe-forest belts, the Manchurian Plain, and the North American prairie-parkland transition zone in the Canadian Prairies. These zones share birch/aspen-dominated woodland patches interspersed with grassland, shaped by continental air-mass dominance far from moderating oceans.
