# Kazakhstan's A-series national highway route shields

`atm_296c5163328d` · **culture** / country · cell `v0nxmzr`

Kazakhstan numbers its national automobile routes with an 'A-xx' prefix (e.g., A-33), distinct from Uzbekistan's M-39/4A series, Turkmenistan's M-37 series, and Russia's R-/A-1xx series used in regions like Kalmykia/Astrakhan. Combined with Kazakh-language toponyms in Latin/Cyrillic dual form, the A-series shield is a reliable country-level identifier within the post-Soviet Central Asian desert belt.
