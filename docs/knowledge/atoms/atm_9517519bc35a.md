# Oxford knowledge economy: education, health, Mini plant, OUP

`atm_9517519bc35a` · **economy** / city · cell `gcpn77e`

Oxford has a high-value knowledge economy anchored by the University of Oxford and Oxford Brookes University. Major sectors include healthcare (Oxford University Hospitals NHS Trust, John Radcliffe Hospital), car manufacturing at the BMW-owned Mini plant in Cowley, academic publishing (Oxford University Press), and biomedical/pharmaceutical research with a notable role in vaccine development. Tourism and university spin-out technology companies are also significant, alongside high property costs.
