# Venetian Renaissance urban planning across the Adriatic

`atm_9e35ecc6159d` · **architecture** / global

Venetian-sponsored architects spread Renaissance grid planning, fortified enceintes and civic squares across Adriatic coastal towns beyond Dalmatia, including Koper and Piran in Istria, Kotor in Montenegro, and Corfu town in the Ionian Islands. These towns share loggias, Venetian Gothic-Renaissance palazzi, and campaniles modeled on St Mark's, reflecting a shared Stato da Màr architectural koine.
