# Historic French states and republics/empires

`atm_881a50c8b7f8` · **history** / country

France's precursor and component states include the Viscounty of Béarn (9th century-1620), Kingdom of France (843-1792, 1814/15-1848), Duchy of Burgundy (918-1482), Duchy of Brittany (939-1532), Duchy of Lorraine (959-1766), County of Foix (1010-1607), Kingdom of Corsica (1736), Corsican Republic (1755-1769, 1793-1794), and Anglo-Corsican Kingdom (1794-1796). Revolutionary and imperial French states include the French First Republic (1792-1804), First French Empire (1804-1814/15), Bourbon Restoration (1815-1830), July Monarchy (1830-1848), French Second Republic (1848-1852), Second French Empire (1852-1870), Free Cities of Menton and Roquebrune (1848-1849), Paris Commune (1871), Third French Republic (1870-1940) with the Territory of the Saar Basin (1920-1935), Alsace-Lorraine Soviet Republic (1918), Vichy France (1940-1944), and Fourth French Republic (1946-1958) with the Saar Protectorate (1946-1956).
