# Dynasties of Bulgaria and the Odrysian Kingdom

`atm_d5bcbae8b5ca` · **history** / country

The Odrysian Kingdom (480-30 BC) preceded later Bulgarian states. The First Bulgarian Empire was ruled by the Dulo clan (AD 681-753, 768-997) with Krum's dynasty (803-997), the Vokil clan (753-762, 766), the Ugain clan (762-765), and the Cometopuli dynasty (997-1018). The Second Bulgarian Empire was ruled by the Asen dynasty (1185-1280), its offshoot the Sratsimir dynasty (1346-1417, Principality of Valona and Tsardom of Vidin), and the Terter dynasty (1280-1292, 1300-1322).
