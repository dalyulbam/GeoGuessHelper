# Bajío basalt clearance piles vs Altiplano pale calcareous soil

`atm_f3fb02fa3be5` · **geography** / region · cell `9exuvn3`

In Guanajuato's Bajío you see heaps of dark vesicular basalt boulders shoved to plot edges on deep brown-black soil, with agave and mesquite between them. In the Chihuahuan Altiplano further north you would instead see pale, chalky-tan calcareous soil with yucca, creosote and low nopal, and few volcanic rock piles. Isolated flat-topped cerros on a flat basin floor reinforce the volcanic-belt margin reading.
