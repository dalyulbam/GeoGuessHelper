# Dynasties of Armenia

`atm_aa3a254f6c22` · **history** / country

Armenia was ruled by the Orontid dynasty and its Artaxiad branch (189 BC-12 AD), the Arsacid dynasty of Armenia (54-428), the Siunia dynasty and House of Hasan-Jalalyan, the Bagratuni dynasty (885-1045), the Rubenid dynasty of the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia (1080-1225), the Zakarids-Mkhargrdzeli (1201-1360, Zakarid Armenia), the House of Lusignan of Cilicia (1342-1467), the Kara Koyunlu (1374-1468), the Javanshir clan (1748-1822, Karabakh Khanate), and the House of Holstein-Gottorp-Romanov (1828-1917, Russian Armenia).
