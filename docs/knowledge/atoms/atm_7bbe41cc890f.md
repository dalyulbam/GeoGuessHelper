# Mono-functional international tourism economy on arid volcanic coasts

`atm_7bbe41cc890f` · **economy** / region · cell `esvzdq6`

Arid, agriculturally marginal volcanic coastlines (e.g. southern Fuerteventura) can develop into mono-functional international tourism economies: dense all-inclusive resort complexes and self-catering apartment urbanizaciones, supporting infrastructure limited to golf courses and mainstream supermarket chains (e.g. Mercadona in Spain), total car dependence with large surface parking, and a labor/service culture drawn from the national mainland serving a foreign (often Northern European) clientele.
