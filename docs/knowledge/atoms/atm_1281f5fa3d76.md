# Post-WWII slum clearance and tower-block replacement of terraces

`atm_1281f5fa3d76` · **architecture** / country

From the 1950s-1970s, many English Victorian terraces judged as slums were demolished under comprehensive redevelopment programs and replaced with high-rise council tower blocks (e.g. in Glasgow, Birmingham, London's inner boroughs). This later provoked its own backlash, with a conservation movement from the 1970s onward protecting surviving terraces as heritage assets.
