# Basin-defined macro-regions distinguished by road corridors

`atm_18dbba2c1bb0` · **geography** / global

Many countries divide into distinct macro-regions defined by lowland basins and their associated highway/rail corridors, similar to the Košice Basin pattern: examples include the Pannonian Basin corridor through Hungary, the Po Valley corridor in northern Italy along the A4 autostrada, and the Ebro Basin corridor in northeastern Spain, each anchoring distinct cultural-economic sub-regions.
