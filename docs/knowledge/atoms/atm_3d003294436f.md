# Ottoman and Roman thermal bath legacies across Europe

`atm_3d003294436f` · **culture** / global

Thermal bathing traditions rooted in Roman and Ottoman heritage extend across former Ottoman and Roman territories: Bath in England preserves Roman Aquae Sulis baths, Bursa in Turkey retains historic Ottoman hamams and thermal springs, and Sarajevo and Sofia both preserve Ottoman-era hamam buildings repurposed as cultural sites. Karlovy Vary in the Czech Republic represents the separate Central European tradition of Habsburg-era spa towns.
