# 19th-century Dalmatian and South Slav autonomy movements

`atm_4d957e14b50e` · **history** / region

Following Ragusa's absorption into the Austrian Empire, 19th-century Illyrian and later Croatian national revival movements sought cultural and political autonomy for Dalmatia within the Habsburg framework, foreshadowing eventual South Slav unification. Figures and societies promoting Croatian language and identity emerged partly in reaction to the loss of Ragusan self-governance and subsequent foreign administration.
