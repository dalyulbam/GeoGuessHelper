# Pre-Shang/Xia era clan polities ('shi') of Huaxia and related peoples

`atm_17e446fee5fd` · **history** / region

Numerous clan-based polities designated '-shi' from the Xia dynasty period and earlier, mostly of unknown dates, many ruled by the House of Si (Huaxia): Youmin-shi (House of Yao), Youshi-shi, Zhenxun-shi, Youhu-shi, Youyi-shi, Younan-shi, Tongcheng-shi, Zhenguan-shi, Youxin-shi (all House of Si), Youxiong-shi, Youjiao-shi, Youreng-shi, Bao-shi, Fei-shi, Qi-shi, Zeng-shi, Xin-shi, Ming-shi, Ge-shi, Youqiong-shi, Youge-shi, and Boming-shi (House of Yun, Dongyi).
