# Northern and Central Asian tribal states (Hujie, Jiankun, Merkit, etc.)

`atm_b90f0413ee79` · **history** / region

Various undated tribal states of Inner/Central Asia: Hujie, Jiankun, Luodian, Sumpa, Kingdom of Almalik, and the Merkit state ruled by the House of Uduyid of Mongol descent. Also includes several Mongol/Turkic tribal polities: Dörbet tribe of Borjigin (ruled by House of Borjigin, Mongol descent), Qibi tribe (ruled by House of Qibi, Tiele descent), Tongluo tribe, Bayirqu tribe, and Ikires tribe.
