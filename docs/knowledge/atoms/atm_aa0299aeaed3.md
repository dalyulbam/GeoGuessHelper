# Japanese manga-artist hometown tourism branding

`atm_aa0299aeaed3` · **culture** / global

Several Japanese cities brand themselves around native manga or anime creators, paralleling Takaoka's Fujiko F. Fujio/Doraemon association: Sakaiminato, Tottori, honors Shigeru Mizuki (GeGeGe no Kitaro) with a themed shopping street, and Kuwana or Tokorozawa-linked sites celebrate other creators. This pattern of manga-hometown tourism uses themed trams, statues, and museums to drive visitor economies in mid-sized regional cities.
