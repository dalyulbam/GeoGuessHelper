# Rural depopulation belts in Southern and Eastern Europe

`atm_f1f2aae7e0ab` · **economy** / global

Lika-Senj's pattern of severe rural depopulation parallels Spain's 'España vaciada' (emptied Spain) phenomenon affecting interior provinces like Soria and Teruel, Italy's inner Apennine and Alpine villages losing population to coastal cities, and Bulgaria's rural northwest, Europe's poorest region by GDP per capita. All share aging populations, emigration to capital or coastal regions, and shrinking public services.
