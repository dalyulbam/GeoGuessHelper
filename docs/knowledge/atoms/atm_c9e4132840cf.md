# Ethnic naming patterns in southern Nigerian toponyms signal sub-regional identity

`atm_c9e4132840cf` · **language** / region · cell `s1nmbk3`

Yoruba place and shop names use distinctive orthography and diacritics (southwest Nigeria); Igbo core areas (Enugu, Anambra) favor names with 'Chukwu-' or 'Nna-' prefixes; the South-South/Cross River-Benue transition zone instead shows minority-ethnic toponyms (e.g. Ogoja, Bekwarra, Yala, Idoma, Tiv) that are neither Yoruba nor Igbo. Political billboards naming state senators are a reliable way to pinpoint the specific Nigerian senatorial district.
