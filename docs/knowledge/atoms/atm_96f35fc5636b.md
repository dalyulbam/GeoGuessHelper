# Southern Italy and immigrant-driven demographic cushioning

`atm_96f35fc5636b` · **economy** / country

While northern Italian towns like Arona age due to long-term low fertility offset by immigration, some Italian regions and cities pursue active repopulation incentives — such as southern towns (e.g., Sambuca in Sicily) selling abandoned homes for a symbolic one euro to attract new residents — representing a policy counter-response to the aging pattern.
