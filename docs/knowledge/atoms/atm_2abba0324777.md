# Croatian road signage and administrative naming conventions

`atm_2abba0324777` · **geography** / country · cell `u2437ts`

Croatian road infrastructure uses blue-shielded 'D' (državna cesta/state road) route number signs and separate international 'E' road numbering, with Latin-script signage featuring the diacritics č, ć, š, ž. Company names commonly carry legal-form suffixes 'd.d.' (dioničko društvo, joint-stock company) and 'd.o.o.' (limited liability company), useful markers for distinguishing Croatia from neighboring Slovenia, Italy or Montenegro in visual/textual geolocation.
