# Volcanic treeless landscapes of the North Atlantic and beyond

`atm_f4d0689cb5d2` · **nature** / global

Iceland's lava-field terrain parallels other volcanic, largely treeless landscapes such as Hawaii's Kilauea lava flows, the Azores' basalt fields, and the Faroe Islands' grass-covered volcanic hills. All share subarctic or oceanic climates that limit tree growth despite fertile volcanic soil, favoring shrub, grass, and moss cover instead.
