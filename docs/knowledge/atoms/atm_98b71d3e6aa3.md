# Global 'ruin bar' and adaptive-reuse nightlife scenes

`atm_98b71d3e6aa3` · **culture** / global

The ruin-bar model of repurposing derelict pre-war buildings into informal nightlife venues has parallels worldwide: Berlin's techno clubs occupying former East German industrial and power-plant sites (e.g. Tresor, Berghain in a former power station), Tbilisi's Bassiani in a Soviet-era swimming pool basement, and Beirut's post-war warehouse bar districts. These scenes share a common pattern of cheap derelict real estate, subculture pioneers, and eventual gentrification pressure.
