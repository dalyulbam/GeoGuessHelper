# Akinfiy Demidov and the Ural-Altai industrial empire

`atm_4ac41a5ca83d` · **economy** / region · cell `vd23gqs`

Akinfiy Demidov (1678–1745), son of dynasty founder Nikita Demidov, expanded the family's ironworks from the Urals into the Altai region, establishing silver and copper smelters including Barnaul's Kolyvano-Voskresensky works under a state monopoly granted by Peter the Great. The Demidovs became the Russian Empire's dominant private industrial family for over a century.
