# Remittance-funded rural consumption pattern in Isan villages

`atm_68509cfdf0f6` · **economy** / region · cell `w71dmn8`

Rural Isan villages often show visible signs of remittance income from family members working in Bangkok or abroad overlaid on a subsistence paddy-and-cash-crop base: two-storey hardwood-and-masonry houses, pickup trucks, satellite dishes, and small tarpaulin-shaded grocery shopfronts, even where the underlying livelihood is modest wet-rice, cassava, or rubber cultivation supplemented by aquaculture ponds.
