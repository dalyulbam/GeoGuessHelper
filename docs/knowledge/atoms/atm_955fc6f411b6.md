# Bokmål standardization and centralizing language reforms in Norway

`atm_955fc6f411b6` · **language** / country

Countering the dialect-preserving Nynorsk movement, a series of Norwegian language reforms (notably 1907, 1917, 1938) sought to bring Bokmål (descended from Dano-Norwegian riksmål) closer to spoken eastern-Norwegian usage, reinforcing Oslo-centered linguistic norms such as the '-veien' suffix as the dominant convention in official and urban contexts.
