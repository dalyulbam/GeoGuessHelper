# Roof pitch as a north-south French vernacular discriminator

`atm_be0ab53acd2f` · **architecture** / region · cell `u0g1ehc`

In northern and eastern France (Champagne-Ardenne, Picardy, Grand Est), vernacular roofs are steeply pitched with dark slate-grey or brown tiles and dormer skylights, adapted to wetter, colder winters. This contrasts with the flat-pitched Roman/canal-tile roofs and ochre render of Provence/Occitanie, and the granite-trimmed slate roofs of Brittany or half-timbered/flint construction of Normandy.
