# Founders of the Jesuit Province of Paraguay in colonial Córdoba

`atm_36aed5dbc192` · **architecture** / country · cell `6d6m725`

The Jesuit order established the Colegio Máximo and Universidad de Córdoba in 1613, making it one of the oldest universities in the Americas. Jesuit priests and architects, working within the Society of Jesus's Province of Paraguay administrative structure, designed the Manzana Jesuítica buildings and the surrounding estancias (Jesús María, Santa Catalina, Alta Gracia) as an integrated agricultural-educational economic network.
