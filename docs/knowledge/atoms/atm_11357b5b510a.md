# Novalja/Zrce Beach: youth party-tourism hub ('Croatian Ibiza')

`atm_11357b5b510a` · **economy** / city · cell `srfth7t`

Novalja on Pag island developed from a modest 1950s fishermen's room-letting economy into one of Croatia's leading youth/clubbing destinations, centered on Zrce Beach and large beach-club operators (Papaya, Aquarius, Noa, Kalypso). Nicknamed the 'Croatian Ibiza', it hosts international DJs and EDM festivals, driving a sharply seasonal hospitality economy that contrasts with more upscale, sightseeing-oriented Adriatic nightlife destinations such as Hvar town.
