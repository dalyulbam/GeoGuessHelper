# Budapest ruin bars and Jewish Quarter nightlife

`atm_3340739b4d02` · **culture** / city · cell `u2mw3kn`

**시기**: 2002 ~ ?

Budapest is known for 'ruin bars' (romkocsma) — eclectic bars set up in derelict pre-war buildings and courtyards of the old Jewish Quarter (District VII), a scene that began in the early 2000s. This nightlife, alongside the Sziget Festival (one of Europe's largest), drives party tourism and associated over-tourism pressures in the historic core.
