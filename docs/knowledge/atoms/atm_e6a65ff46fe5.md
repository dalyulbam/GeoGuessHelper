# Glacially exposed granite monolith climbing destinations

`atm_e6a65ff46fe5` · **nature** / global

Fjord-valley or glacially carved granite monoliths that anchor world-class climbing and regional tourism branding occur worldwide: El Capitan and Half Dome in Yosemite Valley, California; the granite walls of Lofoten and Kjerag in Norway's fjords; and Yosemite's counterparts in Patagonia such as Cerro Torre and Fitz Roy (though those are more spire-like than monolithic). These landmarks share glacial over-steepening of valley walls exposing resistant plutonic rock.
