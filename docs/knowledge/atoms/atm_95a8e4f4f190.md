# Lika-Senj County: lowest industrial output, severe depopulation

`atm_95a8e4f4f190` · **economy** / region · cell `srfxgpc`

Lika-Senj County in Croatia has the lowest share of national industrial production of any Croatian county (about 0.5%) and is the country's least populous county (42,748 residents, 2021 census). Rural hamlets in its hinterland have dwindled to a few dozen residents or fewer, reflecting a nationwide pattern of small-town and rural depopulation in Croatia (national population fell 9.64% between the 2011 and 2021 censuses), with population loss milder only in Zagreb and a few coastal/tourist counties.
