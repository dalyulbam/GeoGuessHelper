# End of the Republic of Ragusa, 1808–1814

`atm_483358c79995` · **history** / polity · cell `srsfg8t`

**시기**: 1808 ~ 1814

The Republic of Ragusa (Dubrovnik), an independent maritime city-state since the medieval period, was abolished by Napoleonic French forces in 1808 and formally ended when its nobility held a final council session in 1814 before absorption into the Austrian Empire. Ragusan noble summer villas along the coast (ljetnikovci) served as venues tied to this transition period.
