# Baltic-German manorial estates and the von Tiesenhausen/von Wolff families

`atm_8b6110d669e6` · **history** / polity · cell `ud4tpyy`

Baltic-German noble families (e.g., von Tiesenhausen, von Wolff) held manorial estates across Vidzeme and other Baltic provinces from the medieval period through the 19th century, building Lutheran churches, manor palaces, and agricultural societies that shaped local administration and culture. Manor palaces built or renovated in the 19th century often adopted Historicist styles with Neo-Gothic facades, employing local craftsmen for ornate interior woodwork, and many now serve as schools or heritage sites after 20th-century nationalization.
