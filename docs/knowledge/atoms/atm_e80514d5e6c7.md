# Central Swiss pre-Alpine lake-basin vernacular architecture

`atm_e80514d5e6c7` · **architecture** / city · cell `u0qh3ck`

Central Switzerland (cantons like Zug, Lucerne, Schwyz) around its pre-Alpine lakes shows a characteristic vernacular: whitewashed rendered masonry houses with heavy timber eaves, moderate-to-steep tiled gable roofs, and painted louvred shutters (often red or green), sometimes combined with chalet-style timber elements. This differs from Ticino's Italian-influenced stone-slab roofs and loggias, and from Valais/Grisons' dark timber blockbau chalets set against higher alpine relief.
