# Global artisanal fishing communities near industrial/tourism hubs

`atm_d3727eb4767a` · **economy** / global

Small-scale panga- or dory-based fishing economies persist worldwide alongside larger extractive or tourism industries, often losing youth to those better-paying sectors: Gulf of California fishing villages near Cabo tourism, Senegalese pirogue fishers near Dakar's port economy, Philippine banca fishers near Manila, and Vietnamese Mekong Delta fishers near industrial zones. These communities typically target shrimp, snapper, crab and mollusks with family-owned small boats and informal market chains.
