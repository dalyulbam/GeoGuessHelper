# Colonial reserve system and displacement in British Columbia

`atm_62f40469349a` · **history** / region

The Indian reserve system established by the colonial and later Canadian government in the 19th century confined Coast Salish and other First Nations to small, scattered land parcels (like Squamish Nation's Stawamus and Yekwaupsum reserves) far smaller than traditional territories, a policy administered under the Indian Act (1876) that also banned practices like the potlatch (1885-1951). This dispossession is the direct counter-force to the pre-contact territorial extent described in the seed, and is the subject of ongoing land-claim and treaty litigation.
