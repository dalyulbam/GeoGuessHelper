# Environmental backlash against tannery pollution

`atm_203a14419593` · **economy** / global

Tannery clusters worldwide face recurring environmental crackdowns due to chromium and toxic effluent discharge into rivers: Bangladesh forcibly relocated Hazaribagh's tanneries to Savar in 2017, and Kanpur's tanneries have been repeatedly ordered by courts (including the National Green Tribunal) to reduce operations to protect the Ganges, especially during the Kumbh Mela. This reflects a global tension between labor-intensive export industries and water-pollution regulation.
