# Brazilian imperial houses

`atm_39caa0e3c974` · **history** / country

House of Braganza (1640-1910) ruled Brazil under Portuguese rule (1640-1815), within the United Kingdom of Portugal, Brazil and the Algarves (1815-1822), and the Empire of Brazil (1822-1889). House of Braganza-Saxe-Coburg and Gotha (1853-1910) and House of Orléans-Braganza (claimants since 1921) followed. Palmares (1670-1695) was a quilombo state. Harden dynasty (1893-1895) ruled the Principality of Trinidad.
