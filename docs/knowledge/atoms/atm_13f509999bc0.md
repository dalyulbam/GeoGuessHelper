# Croatia's rapid national demographic aging and youth outmigration

`atm_13f509999bc0` · **history** / country · cell `srsfew1`

**시기**: 2011 ~ 2026

Croatia's population structure is aging rapidly: the under-14 population fell by nearly 23,000 since the 2021 census while the 65+ population grew by over 42,000 to 911,500. This trend, combined with youth outmigration, affects coastal tourism-dependent counties like Dubrovnik-Neretva alongside overall city population decline in urban centers such as Dubrovnik (42,615 in 2011 to ~41,000 mid-2020s).
