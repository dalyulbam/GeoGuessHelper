# Chichimeca frontier and Guachichil/Otomí resettlement zone of the Altiplano Potosino

`atm_424413bdfad8` · **history** / region · cell `9gbn2rp`

**시기**: 1550 ~ 1600

The Altiplano Potosino-Zacatecano was historically the northern frontier of Mesoamerica, inhabited by nomadic Guachichil and Otomí-Chichimeca peoples who resisted Spanish colonization during the 16th-century Chichimeca War. Spanish and allied Otomí/Tlaxcalteca resettlement created mestizo agricultural villages on former Chichimeca territory, producing the Spanish-speaking mestizo peasant culture now dominant across rural San Luis Potosí and Zacatecas, distinct from surviving indigenous-language spheres (Wixárika/Huichol to the northwest, Nahua Huasteca to the southeast).
