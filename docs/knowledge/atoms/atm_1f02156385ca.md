# Eastern Adriatic karst coast morphology

`atm_1f02156385ca` · **geography** / region · cell `srsfctz`

The Dalmatian coast is characterized by a steep, bare karst limestone ridge rising close behind a narrow coastal terrace, with low elongated offshore islands (e.g. Elaphiti Islands) running parallel to the shore in narrow channels. Thin soils on pale limestone support olive groves, pergola-trained vines, cypress and evergreen maquis, distinguishing it from broader Istrian rolling terra rossa hills or the wind-scoured, sparser Kvarner slopes.
