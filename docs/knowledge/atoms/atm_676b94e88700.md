# Muğla dual-carriageway legs: inland Söke road vs Bodrum coastal corridor

`atm_676b94e88700` · **economy** / region · cell `swesgsx`

On the inland dual state highways of Milas district (e.g. toward Söke) the roadside is open olive/maquis with agricultural stalls, HV lattice pylon corridors along the valley floor and no coastal-resort signage. Along the Bodrum-airport corridor you would instead see hotel/marina advertising boards, dense commercial frontage and a broader flat coastal plain. Presence of pure farm-maquis frontage with only agricultural stalls argues for the inland leg, not the coastal one.
