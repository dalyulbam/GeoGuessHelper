# Satellite dishes as proxy indicator of telecom infrastructure gaps

`atm_2494855827a9` · **economy** / global

In regions lacking fiber or cable buildout, satellite television and satellite internet (e.g., DirecTV in Latin America, DStv in sub-Saharan Africa, VSAT dishes in rural India and Mongolia) serve as visible substitutes, letting observers infer weak wired-telecom penetration from dish density even where electrification is otherwise adequate.
