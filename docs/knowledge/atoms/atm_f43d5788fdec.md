# Irish kingdoms and dynasties

`atm_f43d5788fdec` · **history** / country

Ireland's regional kingdoms and dynasties include Laigin, Mide, Dál Fiatach, Osraige, Airgíalla, Uí Maine, Connachta, Ulaid, Uí Chennselaig, Uí Néill, Dál Birn, Uí Fiachrach, Eóganachta, Uí Briúin, Bréifne, O'Conor Don, O'Brien, Fitzpatrick, Dál gCais, MacCarthy, Cenél nEógain, Burke, Cenél Conaill, O'Donnell, O'Neill, and Clanricarde, spanning roughly the 6th century BC to the 17th century AD. The House of Plantagenet (1154-1485), including the Angevin kings of England (1154-1215) and House of Lancaster (1399-1461, 1470-1471), also held claims in Ireland.
