# Single-pylon cable-stayed bridges as ria-mouth landmarks on karst coasts

`atm_2d85430db3fd` · **architecture** / region · cell `srsfeq5`

On steep karstic coasts with drowned river valleys (rias), a single-pylon cable-stayed bridge spanning the ria mouth (e.g. Franjo Tuđman Bridge over Rijeka Dubrovačka) is a common modern infrastructure solution and a distinctive visual landmark, contrasting with concrete arch bridges used at other estuary crossings such as Šibenik. The viewing aspect of such a bridge (framed against open sea vs. framed against an inland settlement) is a reliable discriminator for which side of the bay an observer stands on.
