# Danubian Plain of northern Bulgaria: grain and vineyard agricultural belt

`atm_3688bcb73509` · **geography** / region · cell `sx9wsps`

The Danubian Plain (Дунавска равнина) in northern Bulgaria is characterized by gently rolling hills, large-scale contiguous cropland blocks, and shelterbelt tree lines, contrasting with the more mountainous Rhodope/Pirin terrain (steep slopes, stone-slate roofs) to the south and the flatter, more irrigated Thracian Lowland further south. It is a core grain (wheat, barley, maize) and sunflower cultivation zone, with pockets of viticulture, and forms the economic backbone of provinces like Pleven, Ruse, and Veliko Tarnovo.
